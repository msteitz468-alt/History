# Outstanding War-Strategy Sources — West Point–Grade Military History

Sourcing roadmap for the `wiki/hubs/warfare/` build-out: graduate / staff-college-level
operational and strategic analysis, organized to feed the
`hubs/warfare/(period)/(specific war)/(specific battle)` tree with West Point–style
treatment of tactics, objectives, terrain, command, and outcome.

**Scope:** analytical operational/strategic works (not popular narrative). Tiers 1–3 are the
cross-cutting backbone; Tier 4 is period-by-period campaign and battle depth mapped to the hub tree.

**Status markers:** ✅ in collection · 🟡 partial · ❌ not yet sourced.
**Acquisition flags:** `[BUY]` `[LIBRARY]` `[OUT OF PRINT]`.

*Cambridge History of Strategy* v1–2 · *Cambridge History of War* v2 & v4 · *Cambridge History of
the First World War* v1–3 · *Cambridge History of the Second World War* v1 & v3 · Beevor, *The Second
World War* · Christopher Duffy, *Siege Warfare* · Timothy May, *The Mongol Conquests in World History*
(note: his *Mongol Art of War* below is a **different** book) · Vegetius, *Epitome* · Thucydides ·
Livy, *Hannibal's War* · the CAH volumes · Cold War (Westad; Leffler–Westad v1–3).
