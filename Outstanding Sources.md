# Outstanding Sources — World History Wiki

Sources needed to fill structural gaps in the existing collection.
The collection is exceptionally strong for Greco-Roman antiquity, the medieval
Mediterranean and Europe, and pre-modern East Asia. The gaps below are real
and will limit wiki quality in those areas until addressed.

Status flags:
- `[BUY]` — in print, widely available
- `[LIBRARY]` — best sourced via university library or interlibrary loan
- `[OUT OF PRINT]` — requires secondhand sourcing or library
- `[NO TRANSLATION]` — no complete English translation exists; secondary scholarship is the workaround

---
