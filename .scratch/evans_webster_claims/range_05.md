**Entry:** Intermediate Area: Overview
**Source_lines:** 35327–35395
**Author:** Frederick W. Lange
**Claim:** The Intermediate Area is a largely artificial culture-geographic construct for space between Mesoamerican and Andean “high civilizations,” with indistinct boundaries matching neither natural nor modern political lines; also partly nested in schemes labeled Mesoamerica, Central America, lower Central America, Middle America.
