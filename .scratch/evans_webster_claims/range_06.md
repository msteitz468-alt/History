**Claim:** The southern Maya Lowlands—focal for Mesoamerican civilization—span southern Yucatán between roughly 16°–18° N, Caribbean to Gulf/Isthmus of Tehuantepec; modern coverage includes Chiapas, Tabasco, Campeche, Quintana Roo, Petén, Alta Verapaz, and Belize.
