# Angkor and the Khmer Civilization (Michael D. Coe, 2003)

A concise culture-history of the **Khmer civilization** of Cambodia — the builders of **Angkor**,
the largest pre-industrial urban complex in the world and the supreme monument of the
[[indianization-of-southeast-asia|Indianized]] states of mainland Southeast Asia. This is the wiki's
first dedicated source on a **Southeast Asian** civilization, opening the long-flagged regional gap.

Coe combines the **French EFEO** epigraphic-archaeological tradition with recent **LiDAR** and
excavation evidence, tracing the Khmer from the Mekong delta chiefdoms through the apogee of the
god-kings to the post-Angkorian transformation. His comparative (Mayanist) perspective foregrounds
the **hydraulic city**, the **devaraja** kingship, and the temple-mountain as the organising
institutions of a classic agrarian-ritual state.
