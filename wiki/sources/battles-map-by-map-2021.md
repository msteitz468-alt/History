# Battles Map by Map (DK/Smithsonian, 2021)

This is a comprehensive cartographic survey of battles across human history, presenting each engagement through detailed maps that illustrate deployments, movements, terrain, and key moments. Foreword by Peter Snow; consultant Philip Sabin (author of *Lost Battles*).

The primary value of this source is **visual**. It consists of high-quality, full-color maps for over 200 battles, with accompanying text, keys, timelines, and "at a glance" summaries. Ideal for enriching the wiki's battle analyses with clear spatial context.

## Structure and Coverage (288 pages)

Organized chronologically by major eras, with 2–4 page spreads per battle including:
- Large main battle map with keys for forces, movements, terrain.
- Timeline of phases.
- "At a glance" summary.
- Contextual text on background, commanders, outcome.
- Insets for campaigns or details.
- "Directory" sections summarizing additional battles.

Signature style: clean, colorful, accessible maps with arrows, symbols, labels; emphasis on tactics, decisions, and "why it mattered".

## Historiography / Collection Note

DK atlases are designed for broad accessibility with high production values. Maps are interpretive syntheses based on standard historical accounts; they excel at clarity for educational use. Cross-reference with primary sources and specialist works (e.g., Sabin *Lost Battles*, Goldsworthy, Sears) for depth. The consultant's involvement (Philip Sabin) aligns with the wiki's existing collection.

The Bibliotheca Alexandrina now makes a wide selection of these maps directly available to illustrate the network of historical battles.
