# Southeast Asia in the Age of Commerce, Vol. 2 — Expansion and Crisis (Anthony Reid, 1993)

The second volume of Reid's classic, and the wiki's **economic-political narrative backbone** for
early-modern Southeast Asia. Vol. 1 ([[reid-southeast-asia-age-of-commerce-v1-1988|The Lands Below the
Winds]]) drew the social-cultural portrait of the region; Vol. 2 supplies the **story** — the rise of
the commercial boom, the trading cities, the religious and political transformations it drove, and the
seventeenth-century crisis that ended it. It completes the [[southeast-asian-age-of-commerce|age of
commerce]] page and connects the region to the global early-modern economy and the **general crisis of
the seventeenth century**.
