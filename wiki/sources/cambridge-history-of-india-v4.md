# The Cambridge History of India, Vol. IV: The Mughul Period (1937)

The dynastic narrative of Mughal India, 1526–c. 1750, edited by Richard Burn — the classic (dated)
English reference for the empire's political history, deepening the wiki's [[mughal-empire|Mughal
Empire]], [[akbar]], and [[aurangzeb]] pages and supplying two missing foundational figures.

## What it contributes

- **[[babur]]** — the Timurid founder of the empire (Panipat 1526; the memoirist of the
  [[baburnama|Baburnama]]).
- **[[sher-shah-suri]]** — the Afghan who briefly displaced the Mughals and whose administrative
  "blueprint" (land revenue, the rupee, the road-and-sarai network) the Mughals perfected.
- Deepens [[mughal-empire]] (the later-Mughal decline), [[akbar]], and [[aurangzeb]].
