# The Cambridge History of the Second World War, Vol. I: Fighting the War (2015)

The military-strategic volume of the series — the eight powers' strategies, the war's theatres,
and the comparative anatomy of how the war was fought. Used to deepen [[second-world-war-1939]] and
to anchor a new page on the [[strategic-bombing-campaign|strategic bombing campaign]].

## What it contributes

- **[[strategic-bombing-campaign]]** — the Anglo-American bombing of Germany and Japan (ch. 17),
  with the area-vs-precision and effectiveness debates (new page; also drawn from Vol. III).
- Deepens **[[second-world-war-1939]]** as a **coalition war** decided by the matching of strategy
  to resources across global theatres.

## Reading note

Well-trodden operational history, so light-to-moderate and flagged; the volume's value here is its
comparative strategic framing and the bombing chapter. The economic/social dimension is in Vol. III.

(Vol. I of 3 — the Cambridge History of the Second World War; Vol. II absent from the collection.)
