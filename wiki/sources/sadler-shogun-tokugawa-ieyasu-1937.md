# Shogun: The Life and Times of Tokugawa Ieyasu (Sadler, 1937)

The classic biography of [[tokugawa-ieyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]], founder of the [[tokugawa-shogunate]] and
third of the "three unifiers" after [[oda-nobunaga]] and [[toyotomi-hideyoshi]]. Ingested as a DEEP read
deepening the wiki's [[edo-period]] / [[azuchi-momoyama-period]] coverage and filling the unification
narrative — the [[sengoku-period|Sengoku]] wars, the decisive [[battle-of-sekigahara]], the founding of
the shogunate, the [[siege-of-osaka|Osaka campaigns]] that destroyed the Toyotomi, and the institutions of
Tokugawa rule.

Deployed-subagent strategy, **6 agents** mapped chronologically to Ieyasu's life. Body content: lines
~**1,000–11,800** of `raw/_sadler-wrapped.txt`; later lines are appendices, notes, and index.
