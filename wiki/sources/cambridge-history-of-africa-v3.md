# The Cambridge History of Africa, Vol. 3: From c. 1050 to c. 1600 (1977)

The third CHA volume — Africa's **high-medieval and early-modern florescence**: the gold-and-
salt empires of the western Sudan, the Swahili and Zimbabwe trading worlds, and Christian
Ethiopia. Complements the UNESCO [[gha-vol4-12th-16th-century|GHA]] vol. 4.
