# Warfare in the Western World, Vol. I: Military Operations from 1600 to 1871 (1996)

The U.S. Military Academy's survey of Western warfare from Gustavus Adolphus to the
unification of Germany — a **Tier 1 backbone source** for the `hubs/warfare/` section (see
*Outstanding War Strategy Sources.md*). Its enduring value is an **analytical through-line**:
the [[military-revolution|military revolution]] → the **limited war** of the dynastic age →
the **nation in arms** of the French Revolution → the decisive **[[napoleonic-warfare|
Napoleonic system]]** → industrialized mass and **[[total-war|total war]]** → the
**Prussian/Moltke system** that closed the period.

## Scope of this ingest (new material only)

Two of the book's blocks are already covered in the wiki at full depth and are **not
re-treated** here, only cross-referenced: the **[[american-revolution|War for American
Independence]]** (Ch 4–5; see the [[hubs/warfare/age-of-expansion/american-revolutionary-war/american-revolutionary-war|dedicated hub]] from the *West Point History of the American
Revolution*) and the **[[american-civil-war-1861-1865|American Civil War]]** (Ch 10–14; see
the [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/american-civil-war|Civil War hub]]).
This ingest builds deep analyses for the chapters the wiki **lacked**: the Military
Revolution, the dynastic "limited wars," the Napoleonic Wars, and the Wars of German
Unification.
