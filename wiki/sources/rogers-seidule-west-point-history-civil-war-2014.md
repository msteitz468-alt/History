# The West Point History of the Civil War (Rogers & Seidule, eds.)

Multi-author volume in the West Point History of Warfare series, general editors Clifford J. Rogers and Ty Seidule. Produced under the auspices of the United States Military Academy Department of History. Focuses on the military conduct of the American Civil War (1861–1865) with emphasis on strategy, operations, tactics, leadership, and the transformation of warfare.

This is the canonical "West Point style" treatment — operational depth, clear maps, order-of-battle detail, and lessons for the professional study of war. It is the highest-priority source listed for the American Civil War section of `Outstanding War Strategy Sources.md`.
