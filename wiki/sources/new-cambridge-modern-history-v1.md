# The New Cambridge Modern History, Vol. I: The Renaissance, 1493–1520 (1957)

The opening volume of the **New Cambridge Modern History** (Modern series), ingested to begin
filling the wiki's Early-Modern Europe gap. It opens the modern period at the cusp of 1500 —
the Renaissance at its height, the eve of the Reformation, the Italian Wars, and the first
Iberian overseas expansion.
