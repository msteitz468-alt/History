# The Cambridge History of Africa, Vol. 6: From 1870 to 1905 (1985)

The sixth CHA volume — the **partition of Africa**: the decades in which European powers
overran almost the entire continent, and Africans resisted, adapted, or were conquered.
Complements the UNESCO [[gha-vol7-colonial-1880-1935|GHA]] vol. 7.
