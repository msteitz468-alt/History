# Archaeology of Ancient Mexico and Central America: An Encyclopedia

## Overview

Edited by **Susan Toby Evans** and **David L. Webster** (Pennsylvania State University), first published by Garland (2001) and reissued by Routledge (2010). More than **500 signed articles** covering the archaeology of **Middle America** (Mexico + Central America) with primary focus on the **[[mesoamerica-culture-area|Mesoamerican culture area]]**, plus adjacent **Northern Arid Zone** and **[[intermediate-area|Intermediate Area]]** (lower Central America toward northern South America).

The encyclopedia is the densest single archaeological reference for this region in the collection: sites, regional sequences, culture histories (Aztec, Maya, Olmec, Zapotec, Mixtec, Tarascan/Purépecha, Toltec, Teotihuacan), period overviews, and thematic articles (agriculture, ball game, writing, warfare, obsidian, architecture, settlement patterns, religion).

**Scope distinction (editors):** *Middle America* = modern Mexico + Central America; *Mesoamerica* = the culture area defined by a Kirchhoff-type trait package (maize agriculture, ball game, stepped pyramids, markets, hieroglyphic writing, calendars, distinctive deities/sacrifice complexes, etc.) — boundaries historically mobile, not modern borders.

**Dating methods emphasized in the Introduction:** stratigraphy, ceramic seriation, archaeomagnetism, obsidian hydration, radiocarbon (primary absolute method, typically ±50–100 years); Long Count for Classic Maya day-precision; dendrochronology mainly in the Northern Arid Zone.

## Volume Synthesis Note

This encyclopedia is the densest **archaeological reference layer** for Middle America in
the collection. Unlike CHNPA II (narrative multi-author synthesis) or Snow/Gonlin/Siegel
(textbook survey), it is organized as **500+ signed specialist entries** — sites, regional
sequences, culture histories, and themes — so its wiki footprint is an **UPDATE-and-densify
pass** over an already strong Mesoamerica spine rather than a first-time foundation.

**What it adds structurally:**

1. **Regional scaffolds that CHNPA treated unevenly as chapters.** Full place pages now
   exist for [[west-mexico]], [[gulf-lowlands]], [[intermediate-area]], [[soconusco]],
   [[puebla-tlaxcala]], [[michoacan-region]], [[morelos-region]], [[guerrero-region]],
   [[northern-arid-zone]], and [[tehuacan-region]] — with entry-level environment,
   chronology, and interregional role.
2. **Major site densification.** New or filled capitals and nodes: [[el-mirador]],
   [[mayapan]], [[uxmal]], [[bonampak]], [[quirigua]], [[caracol]], [[cuello]],
   [[cotzumalhuapa]], [[el-manati]], [[la-mojarra]], plus deep EW layers on
   [[tikal]], [[calakmul]], [[copan]], [[chichen-itza]], [[san-lorenzo-olmec]],
   [[la-venta]], [[tenochtitlan]], [[tula-hidalgo]], [[monte-alban]], [[el-tajin]].
3. **Thematic entries as concept/process fuel.** Scarborough on the [[mesoamerican-ball-game]],
   Marcus on [[mesoamerican-writing-systems]], Fedick/MacNeish on [[maize-domestication]],
   Schortman & Urban on [[interregional-interactions-mesoamerica]], Grove on Olmec
   mother/sister positions, Webster on warfare culture-history, Smith on Postclassic
   as a time-block (not evolutionary grade).
4. **Attributed internal tensions preserved, not averaged** — e.g. Tula urban area
   (Healan ~13 km² / ≤60k vs Mastache–Cobean ~16 km²); Cuicuilco population and Xitle
   dating; Toltec “empire” vs Smith’s “no material evidence for empire”; Teotihuacan
   political form (Townsend theocratic / Tate anonymous corporate).

**Relation to already-ingested sources:**

- **CHNPA II.1–2** remains the narrative spine; EW2001 supplies site metrics, regional
  overviews, and specialist nuance (Cyphers on San Lorenzo extent; Pollard on Purépecha
  court; Houston/Webster on Maya).
- **Snow et al. 2020** Mesoamerica chapter is a survey sketch; EW2001 is the gazetteer
  behind it.
- Knowledge cutoff is **late 1990s**: later LiDAR (e.g. Mirador/Calakmul settlement),
  refined AMS maize dates, and post-2000 epigraphy are **not** in this source — flag on
  pages where EW numbers may be superseded.

**Method note:** Deployed two-stage Subagent Strategy (9 Stage-1 claim extractors →
~643 claims; 6 Stage-2 exclusive-page integrators + main-thread Introduction). Minor site
entries folded into parent regions; no attempt to page every encyclopedia headword.
