# The West Point Atlas of American Wars — American Civil War (Griess ed.)

This volume of the West Point Atlas series provides the definitive set of campaign and tactical situation maps for the American Civil War. It is the cartographic backbone referenced throughout the wiki's ACW battle analyses (via Krasnoborski and Martini plates) and the Rogers & Seidule *West Point History of the Civil War* (2014).

Primary value: **visual** — not text. Maps show troop positions at specific times, terrain effects, lines of advance, and key features that shaped command decisions. Facing pages or overlays often include order-of-battle summaries.

## Structure and Coverage

- 128 pages in the filed PDF (map plates + supporting data).
- Organized by campaign / theater: Eastern Theater 1861–65 (emphasis on Army of Northern Virginia vs. Army of the Potomac), Western Theater, Trans-Mississippi where relevant.
- Signature style: clean topographic base, color-coded forces (blue Union / red/gray Confederate in many plates), hour-specific "Situation at..." labels, arrows for movement, unit labels at regimental/brigade scale where space allows.
- Companion to the West Point text series (Doughty/Gruber, later Rogers/Seidule volumes).

## Historiography / Collection Note

The Esposito / Krasnoborski / Martini maps have been the standard at West Point for decades. They prioritize clarity for cadets over exhaustive archaeological-level micro-terrain. Later scholars (e.g. detailed studies by Sears, Gallagher, or battlefield archaeologists) sometimes refine front lines, artillery positions, or timings, but the overall operational picture remains authoritative.

The Bibliotheca Alexandrina now makes the visuals directly usable alongside the already-ingested West Point History volume and the high-resolution battle analyses.
