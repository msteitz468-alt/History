## Volume Synthesis Note — the Cambridge History of China (Chinese compilation), complete

Ingested across **seven cycles** spanning imperial and modern China, the CHC compilation now gives
the wiki a continuous, densely cross-linked Chinese backbone from the first empire to the eve of the
reform era — built on top of, and deepening, the existing dynasty pages.

- **Imperial backbone.** The Qin-Han foundation (with [[wang-mang|Wang Mang's]] interregnum and the
  modernist/reformist policy cycle on [[han-dynasty]]); the Sui-Tang order and its institutional
  signature the **[[chinese-examination-system|examination system]]**, broken at the
  **[[an-lushan-rebellion|An Lushan Rebellion]]**; the steppe **"conquest dynasties"** culminating
  in [[kublai-khan|Kublai's]] **[[yuan-dynasty|Yuan]]**; the [[ming-dynasty|Ming]] and the
  **[[zheng-he-voyages|Zheng He voyages]]**; and the [[qing-dynasty|Qing]].
- **The long crisis and fall.** The Late Qing sequence —
  [[taiping-rebellion|Taiping]] → **[[self-strengthening-movement|Self-Strengthening]]** → its
  failure → the **[[boxer-rebellion|Boxer Rebellion]]** → [[fall-of-the-qing-1911|1911]].
- **Modern China.** The **[[republic-of-china-1912|Republic]]**, the **[[warlord-era|warlord era]]**,
  and the **[[kuomintang]]**; then **[[maoist-china|Mao's PRC]]** with the
  **[[great-leap-forward|Great Leap Forward]]** and the **[[cultural-revolution|Cultural
  Revolution]]**.

**Recurring CHC themes** the wiki now carries: the **dynastic cycle** and [[mandate-of-heaven|
Mandate of Heaven]]; the **conquest-dynasty / sinicization** debate (Liao-Jin-Yuan, and the Qing);
the **examination-gentry-Confucian-state** nexus as the spine of imperial governance; and the
**"why not China?"** comparison (Zheng He vs. European expansion; self-strengthening vs. Meiji).
Two standing caveats are flagged throughout: the **Chinese-translation layer** between the wiki and
the original English scholarship, and the translators' noted **divergences from PRC orthodoxy**
(especially in the modern volumes). It does not cover pre-imperial China
([[cambridge-history-of-ancient-china|see CHAC]]), the Six Dynasties, or the Song as standalone
volumes.
