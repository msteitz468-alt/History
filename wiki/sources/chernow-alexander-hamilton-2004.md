# Alexander Hamilton (Ron Chernow, 2004)

Magisterial biography of Alexander Hamilton by Ron Chernow (following his Washington, Grant, Rockefeller, etc.). 2004. One of the most comprehensive modern lives, restoring Hamilton as the "prophet of the capitalist revolution" and indispensable architect of the early federal government alongside Washington.

Chernow's work rests on the complete modern Papers of Alexander Hamilton (Columbia University Press, Syrett et al., 27 vols + Goebel legal papers) plus fresh archival digging in the Caribbean (Nevis, St. Croix, Danish records) and elsewhere. It reveals the full man: the brilliant, driven West Indian immigrant who rose from poverty and stigma; the wartime aide and battlefield leader; the chief author of the Federalist Papers and architect of the Constitution's implementation; the first Treasury Secretary who created the financial system, funded the debt, established the Bank of the United States, and promoted manufactures; the polarizing partisan; the tragic figure undone by honor and the Burr duel.

The book emphasizes Hamilton's vision of a strong national state with energetic executive, funded credit, central bank, and modern economy — the world we now inhabit — against Jeffersonian agrarianism.

### From Range 01 (288–2000: Prologue + early life)
H01-Eliza Hamilton as Last Link: Eliza (purblind, deaf, 97) devoted half-century to vindicating Hamilton against Jefferson/Adams slanders. “Her Hamilton” quest; enlisted ~30 assistants; destroyed own letters but preserved his; authorized biography fell to son John Church Hamilton.
H01-Birth/Illegitimacy/Orphanhood: Claimed Nevis birth (evidence favors 1755); James Hamilton (failed merchant, laird of Grange’s fourth son) and Rachel Faucette (Lavien marriage, Christiansvaern imprisonment for adultery, flight). Rachel died 1768 of fever beside young Alexander. Court ruled boys “obscene children”; disinherited.
H01-Clerkship + Hurricane Letter + Ambition: Clerk for Beekman & Cruger; exposed to slave trade, currency, commerce. 1769 letter to Ned Stevens: ambition “so prevalent that I contemn the grovelling condition of a clerk... I wish there was a war.” 1772 hurricane letter (published) propelled him to NY via subscription.
H01-Early Exposure to Slavery and Stigma: St. Croix (“twelve blacks for every white”); “source from which every citizen obtains his daily bread”; militia obligations on whites. Illegitimacy and disinheritance shaped drive and antislavery conviction.

### From Range 02 (2001–4000: Revolution early)
H02-Early Pamphlets + Reputation: “A Full Vindication” (Dec 1774) and “The Farmer Refuted” (Feb 1775) against Seabury; lawyerly grounding in natural law/charters. Predicted French/Spanish aid and guerrilla strategy suited to America.
H02-Artillery Captain + Model Discipline + First Combat: Appointed March 1776; company “most beautiful model of discipline.” Under fire at Battery vs. Asia/Phoenix/Rose (cannon burst killed two). Harlem, White Plains, Trenton (left sickbed), Princeton.
H02-Washington Rapport + Burr Coincidence: First sighted Washington June 1775; impressed at Harlem. Burr visited Washington staff June 1776 but relations cool (contrast to Hamilton).
H02-Defense of Myles Cooper: Stood on stairs to delay mob; honor over politics. “Hamilton had shown exemplary courage... fretted about the damage to constituted authority.”

### From Range 03 (4001–6500: War years / marriage / staff)
H03-Washington Aide + Laurens Bond + Angelica: Principal confidential aide 1777; interpreter/drafter. Intimate friendship with John Laurens (abolitionist plans); flirtation with Angelica Schuyler (“ménage à trois” dynamic suspected).
H03-Marriage to Eliza 1780: Instantly smitten at Morristown; “the little saint.” Married Dec 14 1780; Schuyler alliance brought status/wealth. Letters reveal love, doubts about future poverty, requirements for wife.
H03-Laurens Emancipation Plan + Hamilton Draft: Supported black battalions + freedom with muskets. “I have not the least doubt that the negroes will make very excellent soldiers.” Plan failed in SC legislature; Hamilton dejected.
H03-Arnold Treason Witness + André: Eyewitness at Robinson house; “Arnold has betrayed us!” Visited André; argued he wasn’t classic spy; regretted execution (“policy required a sacrifice”). Peggy Arnold performance deceived him.
H03-Despair + Financial Reform Ideas: “I am disgusted with everything... I am not fit for this terrestrial country.” Drafted 1780 letter proposing central bank + foreign loan (precursor to later system).

### From Range 04 (6501–9000: Confederation/Constitution/Federalist)
H04-Annapolis + Convention Call: With Madison, moved beyond commerce to critique Articles; authored address calling for Philadelphia Convention 1787.
H04-Constitutional Convention + Federalist: Minority NY delegate; chief author of Federalist (bulk of essays); NY ratifying fight. “Publius.”
H04-Bank of New York + Early Antislavery: Single-handed constitution 1784. Joined NY Manumission Society 1785; proposed emancipation timetable for members’ slaves; signed petition against slave trade. Contrasted with other founders’ records.
H04-Cincinnati + NY Assembly: Defended merit over hereditary; elected 1786 Assembly to advance national reform. Opposed Clinton; supported federal impost.
H04-Shays + Annapolis: Attributed Shays in part to debt-settlement taxes; saw evidence for stronger union.

### From Range 05 (9001–12000: Treasury I)
H05-Appointment + Vision: Nominated/confirmed Sept 11 1789; “he knows everything.” Complementary to Washington (Hamilton prime-minister-like). Divested conflicts; high ethics.
H05-Report on Public Credit: 40k-word treatise in ~3 months. Funding at par + assumption as “national blessing”; sinking fund; “sin taxes.” No discrimination for original holders (transferable securities principle).
H05-Madison Opposition + Rift: Feb 1790 Madison attacked discrimination failure as betrayal of Revolution. Rift shattered consensus; birth of parties.
H05-Bank + Manufactures Seeds: Bank bill 1791; early industrial interest (NY Manufacturing Society subscriber; Coxe collaboration foreshadowed Report on Manufactures).

### From Range 06 (12001–16000: Treasury II + crises)
H06-Reynolds Affair: Persevering blackmail scheme (Maria + James); Hamilton confessed full adultery in 1797 “Reynolds Pamphlet” to clear financial corruption charges. “If I recollect rightly, I did not immediately accept...”
H06-Industrial Vision + SEUM: Amid affair, envisioned industrial city (Paterson) via SEUM (1791 prospectus, largely by Hamilton/Coxe). Recruited Arkwright defectors (Parkinson, Marshall, etc.); government patents on stolen designs. “Future strength of nation-states... proportionate to their industrial prowess.”
H06-Report on Manufactures (Dec 5 1791): Blueprint for selective promotion (bounties, moderate tariffs, patents, infrastructure). Refuted Physiocrats/Smith on barren manufacturing; endorsed child/women labor as ennobling. Military/strategic rationale (Revolution supply lessons). House shelved; “general welfare” alarm (Madison/Jefferson).
H06-Duer Panic + Market Stabilization 1792: Duer (SEUM governor) cornered bonds/scrip; March failure triggered “bancomania” bust (25 financiers ruined). Hamilton used sinking fund purchases (via Seton, concealed) to steady market and buy cheap; refused to bail Duer personally (“line of separation between honest men and knaves”). Buttonwood Agreement followed.
H06-Jefferson/Madison “Botanizing Tour” 1791: Cover for organizing northern opposition; conferred with Livingston/Burr. Parties cohering.

### From Range 07 (16001–20000: Later politics)
H07-Defence/Camillus (1795–96): 28+ essays defending Jay Treaty; published in enemy paper. “Camillus” (misunderstood virtuous Roman). Pleaded for peace/economy; acknowledged mutual treaty violations (including slave abandonment). Washington approved.
H07-Farewell Address: Revived/updated Madison draft; Washington preferred Hamilton version. Neutrality, no permanent alliances, party warnings. Eliza later testified he wrote/read most to her.
H07-1796 Election: Backed Pinckney (wrong horse); Phocion attacks on Jefferson (Notes on Virginia race theories, possible Hemings allusion); Adams won narrowly (Hamilton influence limited).
H07-Adams Feuds + Cabinet: Adams retained Washington cabinet (High Federalists loyal to Hamilton). Hamilton’s unsolicited policy letter resented (“in a delirium”). Hamilton had three admirers in cabinet (Pickering, Wolcott, McHenry).
H07-Hylton Case + Grange: Argued carriage tax constitutionality before Supreme Court (broad taxing power upheld). Built Grange retreat; family life with Bible readings, singing.
H07-Philip Hamilton Death (1801): Dueled George Eacker after theater taunts; Hamilton advised delope. Philip shot, died after 14 hours. Hamilton/Eliza devastated; Angelica (daughter) suffered mental collapse (sang duets with father, spoke of Philip as alive).

### From Range 08 (20001–23606: Duel, death, Eliza, legacy)
H08-Adams Pamphlet (1800): 54-page “Letter... Concerning the Public Conduct and Character of John Adams” (Oct 24, 1800). Surveyed Adams conduct + personal grievances; endorsed Adams/Pinckney anyway. Widely condemned as hypocritical/indiscreet; damaged Federalists.
H08-1800 Election/Burr: Jefferson-Burr tie. Hamilton indicted Burr as “Catiline”; recalled Burr’s alleged constitution-overthrow conversation. Backed Jefferson over Burr in House (Bayard blank ballot on 36th ballot). “Jefferson probably owed his victory to Hamilton.”
H08-Post-1800 Fade + Legal Fame: Excluded from Adams (then Jefferson) inner circle; pinnacle of NY bar. “This American world was not made for me.”
H08-Duel/Death 1804: “Despicable opinion” letter + political attacks led to challenge. Hamilton instructed delope; Burr shot fatally. Died ~2 p.m. July 12. Massive mourning; Trinity Churchyard.
H08-Eliza’s Half-Century Quest: Outlived Hamilton by 50 years (died 97, 1854). Preserved papers (destroyed own); pushed biography; “Justice shall be done to the memory of my Hamilton.”
H08-Legacy: Financial/administrative sinews (funded debt, Bank, Treasury, customs, coast guard); prophetic industrial/nationalist vision; Federalist Papers; energetic executive/judicial review precedents. Catalyst for parties. Antislavery voice. Personal flaws (combativeness, Reynolds, Adams split, duel) and elitism limited appeal. “Indispensable partner” to Washington.

## Volume Synthesis Note

It restores Hamilton as the “prophet of the capitalist revolution” and the administrative/financial architect who, with Washington, gave institutional life to the Constitution.
