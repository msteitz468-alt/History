# The Incoherence of the Philosophers (al-Ghazālī, c. 1095)

Al-Ghazālī's celebrated refutation of the [[islamic-philosophy|falāsifa]] — chiefly [[ibn-sina|Avicenna]]
and [[al-farabi|al-Fārābī]] — across **twenty Discussions** (sixteen on metaphysics, four on natural
science). Ingested as a **primary-source deepening** of the existing [[tahafut-al-falasifa]] concept page
and the [[al-ghazali]] actor page: agents link those and create pages for the substantive arguments — the
three doctrines al-Ghazālī condemns as unbelief ([[eternity-of-the-world|the world's eternity]],
[[god-knowledge-of-particulars|God's knowledge of particulars]], and bodily resurrection) and his famous
critique of [[occasionalism-islamic|necessary causation]] (Discussion 17).

Deployed-subagent strategy, **4 agents** (sized to the heavy overlap with existing philosophy/theology
pages). Body content: al-Ghazālī's text begins ~line **1,280** of `raw/_incoherence-wrapped.txt`
(after the translator's introduction); the twenty Discussions run to ~line 13,500.
