# F. A. Hayek, *Law, Legislation and Liberty*, Volume 2: *The Mirage of Social Justice* (1976)

Second volume of the trilogy: the argument that "social justice" is a category error — justice attaches to conduct under rules, not to the unintended distributional outcomes of a spontaneous order, which no one wills and no one can be responsible for. Introduces **catallaxy** as the name for the market order and the game of catallaxy ("a game partly of skill and partly of chance").
