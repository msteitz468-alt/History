# History of the World Map by Map (Peter Snow / DK, 2018)

This is a comprehensive cartographic history of the world, presenting key events, empires, battles, explorations, and processes through a series of detailed maps organized chronologically from prehistory to the contemporary era. Foreword by Peter Snow.

Primary value for the wiki: broad visual and spatial context for periods and events across the entire temporal framework. The maps provide clear depictions of movements, borders, battles, and developments at global and regional scales. Strong companion to more specialized atlases and textual sources.

## Structure and Coverage (362 pages)

Organized chronologically with maps for major milestones:

- Prehistory and early civilizations
- Ancient empires and classical world
- Medieval period, crusades, Mongol expansions
- Age of exploration, early modern wars
- Revolutions, industrial era, 19th century conflicts
- World wars, cold war, contemporary developments

Includes battle maps, campaign overviews, empire growth, trade, and cultural shifts.
