# Ronald Reagan: A Life From Beginning to End — Hourly History (2017)

Short popular narrative biography (Hourly History series, Book 40 of Biographies of US Presidents) offering a concise chronological overview of Ronald Reagan’s life from Tampico childhood through Hollywood, political rise, governorship, presidency, post-presidency, and legacy. Emphasizes personal details, key turning points, and the mythic “Great Communicator” image while noting pragmatism alongside conservatism.

## Key Claims & Themes (to be expanded)

- Narrative life details supplementing intellectual analysis: concrete early life anecdotes (lifeguard 77 rescues, dragging drunk father, Eureka student strike success), Hollywood specifics and career interruption by WWII (First Motion Picture Unit), Jane Wyman divorce, Nancy Davis meeting (blacklist confusion via SAG), 52-year marriage with legendary affection (“Ronnie”/“Mommy”, “The Gaze”, love letters).
- Governorship pragmatism: won 53/58 counties 1966; raised taxes for balanced budget despite conservative opposition; signed then regretted Therapeutic Abortion Act; sent National Guard to Berkeley People’s Park (“Bloody Thursday”); 1971 Welfare Reform Act with Democrats; environmental actions (Redwood National Park, wild rivers protection).
- Presidency milestones: 1980 “Are you better off than you were four years ago?”; 1981 assassination survival and humor (“Honey, I forgot to duck!”); PATCO firings strengthening anti-union image; Iran-Contra (covert Iran arms for hostages, diverted to Contras; 1987 public acceptance of responsibility); four summits with Gorbachev leading to INF Treaty and thaw.
- Legacy: Credited with helping end Cold War (“tear down this wall” 1987); shifted GOP and nation rightward like FDR shifted left; “Great Communicator” myth-making from acting background; increased debt/deficits alongside growth; conservative icon despite current party moving further right.
- Personal: First divorced president; devoted second marriage central to legend; Alzheimer’s 1994 letter and graceful decline.
