# World War II Map by Map (DK / Smithsonian, 2019)

This is a comprehensive cartographic atlas of the Second World War (1939–1945), presenting the conflict through a series of detailed maps organized chronologically and thematically by theater. It includes the road to war, the Axis ascendancy 1939–1942, the turning points, and the Allied victory 1943–1945 across Europe, the Mediterranean, the Atlantic, and the Pacific.

The primary value of this source is **visual**. It consists of high-quality, full-color maps for the major campaigns and battles, with accompanying text, keys, timelines, force summaries, and "at a glance" boxes. Ideal for enriching the wiki's WWII battle analyses, the main war hub, and period coverage with clear spatial and operational context.

## Structure and Coverage (288 pages)

Organized into major sections with maps for nearly every significant operation:

- The Slide to War 1918–1939 (origins, dictators, expansion)
- Germany Triumphant 1939–1941 (Poland, Phoney War, Norway, France, Battle of Britain, North Africa, Balkans)
- The Tide Turns 1942 (Eastern Front winter, Midway, El Alamein, Torch)
- Allied Victory 1943–1945 (Italy, D-Day and Normandy, Eastern Front offensives, Pacific island-hopping, final campaigns, surrender)
- Additional thematic spreads on weapons, leaders, home fronts, and key statistics.

Signature style: colorful situation maps with unit symbols, movement arrows, terrain shading, numbered keys, timelines, and concise explanatory text. Page numbers often correspond closely to the depicted events.
