# Emperor of Japan: Meiji and His World (Keene, 2002)

The definitive biography of [[emperor-meiji|Emperor Meiji]] and, through his sixty-year life, the
transformation of Japan from a closed feudal realm into a modern great power. Ingested under the
**Large-Volume Protocol** with **9 agents** mapped chronologically across the 63 chapters. It enriches
[[meiji-period]], [[meiji-restoration-1868]], [[meiji-constitution-1889]], and [[russo-japanese-war-1904]]
and fills the era's many gaps — the Bakumatsu crisis, the Boshin War, the early reforms, the popular-rights
movement, the Constitution, and the imperial wars.
