# Emperor of Japan: Meiji and His World (Keene, 2002)

The definitive biography of [[emperor-meiji|Emperor Meiji]] and, through his sixty-year life, the
transformation of Japan from a closed feudal realm into a modern great power. Ingested under the
**Large-Volume Protocol** with **9 agents** mapped chronologically across the 63 chapters. It enriches
[[meiji-period]], [[meiji-restoration-1868]], [[meiji-constitution-1889]], and [[russo-japanese-war-1904]]
and fills the era's many gaps — the Bakumatsu crisis, the Boshin War, the early reforms, the popular-rights
movement, the Constitution, and the imperial wars.

and index.

## Volume Synthesis Note

The definitive life of [[emperor-meiji|Emperor Meiji]] and, through it, the whole transformation of Japan
from feudal realm to great power. It runs from the [[bakumatsu|Bakumatsu]] crisis and [[sonno-joi|sonnō-jōi]]
agitation through the [[meiji-restoration-1868|Restoration]] and the [[boshin-war-1868|Boshin War]]; the
demolition of the old order ([[abolition-of-han-1871|abolition of the han]], the [[iwakura-mission-1871|
Iwakura Mission]], [[meiji-conscription-law-1873|conscription]]) and the crises it bred ([[seikanron-1873|
the Korea debate]], the samurai revolts culminating in the [[satsuma-rebellion-1877|Satsuma Rebellion]]);
the [[freedom-and-popular-rights-movement|popular-rights movement]] that forced the [[meiji-constitution-1889|
Constitution]] (1889); and Japan's emergence as an imperial power through the [[first-sino-japanese-war-1894-1895|
Sino-Japanese]] and [[russo-japanese-war-1904|Russo-Japanese]] wars and the [[annexation-of-korea-1910|
annexation of Korea]] — closing with the emperor's [[death-of-emperor-meiji-1912|death]] and General Nogi's
[[nogi-junshi-1912|junshi]]. Keene's portrait (a conscientious but largely constitutional monarch reigning
above the [[meiji-oligarchs|oligarchs]]) deeply enriches the wiki's [[meiji-period|Meiji-era]] coverage.
