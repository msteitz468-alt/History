# Japan: A Short Cultural History (Sansom, 1931/1952)

The cultural counterpart to the wiki's [[mason-caiger-history-of-japan|political spine]] for
[[japan|Japan]] — Sansom's classic account of how Japanese civilization absorbed, adapted,
and transformed continental (and later Western) culture across thirteen centuries, organized
by the standard political periods but focused on **religion, the arts, letters, and society**.
