# The Oxford Handbook of Modern German History (Smith, ed., 2011)

Multi-author OUP handbook, 35 chapters, covering German history from the Holy Roman Empire's
last century (c. 1760) to the post-reunification Berlin Republic. Thematic and historiographical
rather than narrative. Editor Helmut Walser Smith frames the volume around the collapse of the
**Sonderweg** ("special path") paradigm and the search for what should replace it — reading German
history as connected (transnational, colonial, European) rather than uniquely deviant, while
keeping the catastrophe of 1933–45 at the analytical centre.
