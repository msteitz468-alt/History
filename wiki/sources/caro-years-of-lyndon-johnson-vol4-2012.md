# The Years of Lyndon Johnson, Volume 4: The Passage of Power (Robert A. Caro)

**Source page for Robert A. Caro's *The Passage of Power* (2012), the fourth volume of his definitive biography of Lyndon Baines Johnson.**

This volume bridges the Senate mastery of Vol. 3 and the full presidency (to be covered in the unfinished Vol. 5). It is the story of five years of failure and humiliation (1960 nomination loss through the vice presidency) followed by the extraordinary seven-week transition after November 22, 1963, in which Johnson broke the legislative logjam on civil rights and the tax cut and began to establish command. The book is simultaneously a character study of powerlessness and its reversal, a history of one of the great personal feuds in American politics (LBJ vs. RFK), and a case study in how a new President seizes or fails to seize the possibilities of the office in a moment of national trauma.
