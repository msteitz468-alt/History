# The New Cambridge Modern History, Vol. XIII: Companion Volume (1979)

The closing volume of the NCMH-Modern series — a **thematic and reference companion** rather
than a narrative, gathering essays on the structures (demographic, economic, social,
intellectual) underlying the period 1493–1945 and reflecting on how its history has been
written. Ingested **light-touch** (it is reference/meta-content).

## What it covers

Thematic essays — population and demography (opening with G. E. Aylmer on European
population), economic and social structures, science and technology, the arts, and the
**historiography** of the modern period — plus reference apparatus (chronological tables,
statistics, genealogies, maps). It is the volume that makes the series' implicit framework
explicit.

## What it contributes

- Enriches **[[world-history-as-field]]** (the reflexive, "how modern history is written"
  dimension of the series).
