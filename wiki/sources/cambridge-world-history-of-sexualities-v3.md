# The Cambridge World History of Sexualities, Vol. 3: Sites of Knowledge and Practice (2024)

The third volume grounds the [[history-of-sexuality|history of sexuality]] in **place** — a
global sequence of **city portraits** showing how sexual life was actually lived, regulated,
and recorded in specific urban settings across three millennia.

## What it contributes

- **[[urban-sexual-cultures]]** — the concept page: the **city** as the privileged site of
  sexual practice, knowledge, regulation, and subculture across world history.
- Enriches **[[history-of-sexuality]]** (the practice/place dimension).

## Approach

By moving city by city — Athens, Rome, Constantinople, Kyoto, Tenochtitlan, Istanbul,
Geneva, Edo, Paris, Philadelphia, Cairo, London, Manila, Lagos, Bombay, Buenos Aires,
Sydney, Toronto, Shanghai, Rio — the volume reveals both the **diversity** of sexual cultures
and a set of **recurring urban themes**: marriage and household formation, commercial sex,
same-sex relations, and the regulation of all three by religious and state authority. The
city emerges as the place where sexual cultures concentrate, mix, are policed, and leave the
records historians can read.
