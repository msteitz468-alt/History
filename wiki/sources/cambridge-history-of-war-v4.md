# The Cambridge History of War, Vol. 4: War and the Modern World (2012)

The modern volume, edited by **Roger Chickering, Dennis Showalter, and Hans van de Ven** — a
social and cultural as much as operational history of war from the mid-nineteenth century to the
early twenty-first, framed around the proposition that the industrialization of warfare made the
period "the most belligerent in human history."
