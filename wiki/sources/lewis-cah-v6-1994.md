# The Cambridge Ancient History, Volume VI: The Fourth Century B.C. (2nd ed., 1994)

The capstone of the second-edition Greek CAH sequence. Where [[lewis-cah-v5-1992|CAH V]]
covered the fifth century to the fall of Athens (404), this volume carries the Greek
world from the Spartan victory through the Corinthian War, the King's Peace, the brief
Theban hegemony, the rise of Macedon under Philip II, and the conquests of Alexander,
ending with Alexander's death in **323 BCE**. It pairs the political narrative with a
rich set of **regional surveys** (Persian lands, the West, the North) and thematic
chapters on society, economy, the polis, culture, science, art, agriculture, and warfare.

Editors: **D. M. Lewis, John Boardman, Simon Hornblower, M. Ostwald.** Processed under
the Large-Volume Protocol. The senior editor (Lewis) is shared with Vol. V, hence the
`lewis-` source slug.
