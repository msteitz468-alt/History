# The Cambridge World History of Slavery, Vol. 1: The Ancient Mediterranean World (2011)

The opening volume of the series, edited by **Keith Bradley** and **Paul Cartledge**, surveying
slavery in the ancient Mediterranean — chiefly classical **Athens** and **Rome**, the two
ancient members of the canonical five "genuine slave societies," set against the ancient Near
East, the Jews, the Hellenistic world, and the late-antique transition.
