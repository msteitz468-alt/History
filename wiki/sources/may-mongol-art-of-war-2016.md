# Timothy May, *The Mongol Art of War* (2016)

The wiki's dedicated operational study of the **[[mongol-army|Mongol army]]** — complementing
[[may-mongol-conquests-2012|*Mongol Conquests in World History*]] (world-historical synthesis)
and [[morgan-the-mongols|Morgan]] (institutional survey). May's argument: Mongol success was not
innovation but **refinement** of steppe tactics welded to iron discipline, decimal organization,
systematic intelligence, and pragmatic adoption of siege engineering and sedentary auxiliaries.
