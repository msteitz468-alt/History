# F. A. Hayek, *Law, Legislation and Liberty*, Volume 3: *The Political Order of a Free People* (1979)

Final volume: the diagnosis that contemporary democracy has degenerated into a bargaining process among organized interests because a single assembly holds both law-making and governmental powers, and the remedy — a **model constitution** with two assemblies of distinct functions (a Legislative Assembly restricted to rules of just conduct, a Governmental Assembly for administration), which Hayek names **demarchy**. Closes with the Epilogue "The Three Sources of Human Values" (instinct, evolved tradition, reason).
