# Griffin (ed.), *Fascism* (Oxford Readers, 1995)

Oxford Readers anthology providing primary and contemporary secondary excerpts illustrating the full spectrum of fascist ideology and practice. Griffin's general introduction and section prefaces frame the selections around the ideal-typical core of **[[palingenetic-ultranationalism|palingenetic populist ultranationalism]]** (defined and defended in his 1991 monograph, already ingested). The volume deliberately samples both the interwar "classic" cases and the post-1945 permutations, including abortive European and non-European movements and the discourses of neo-fascism, Eurofascism, the New Right, and historical revisionism.

## Volume Synthesis Note

This Oxford Readers anthology supplies the documentary backbone for the "new consensus" (Griffin 1991 + 2007) definition of generic fascism as a palingenetic form of populist ultranationalism. Its value lies in the breadth of primary voices — Italian (Papini, Marinetti, Mussolini, Gentile, Farinacci, Rocco, Bottai), German precursors (Wagner, Lagarde, Moeller van den Bruck, Jünger, Spengler), abortive (Mosley/BUF, José Antonio/Falange, Codreanu/Iron Guard, Salgado/Integralism, Nakano/Tōhōkai, Degrelle/Rex, Quisling, etc.), and post-war (veterans Evola/Bardèche/Mosley, denial/revisionism Jordan/Christophersen/Irving/Nolte, New Right de Benoist, militant/electoral expressions).

**Subagent extraction (R1–R3, 3 Sonnet agents, background):** ~60 grounded claims. Key contributions:
- Early Italian: war as renewal (Papini, Revolutionary Syndicalists, Marinetti, Mussolini "Audacia!", "Trincerocrazia"); San Sepolcro declarations; Fiume/Carnaro Charter as harbinger; squadrismo/rural incorporation; nation as myth (Naples 1922); Gentile total conception; demographic power.
- German precursors/rise: völkisch palingenesis (Wagner, Lagarde, Langbehn, George); Moeller "Third Reich"; Jünger worker/front; Spengler Prussian/Caesar; Hitler mission/anti-Marx; Goebbels Christ-Socialism; Darré Blood and Soil; Rosenberg rebirth; Schmitt Führertum.
- Abortive + transition: Mosley philosophy/Europe; José Antonio "total feeling" and national syndicalism; Preto ersatz critique; Degrelle "revolution of souls"; Codreanu resurrection of race + Mota sacrifice; Salgado "fourth humanity"; Nakano totalitarian Japan; early reactions (Pareto on nationalist religion).

**Main-thread (R4–R5 caches + synthesis, atrocity-discourse triage applied):** veteran verdicts, theories (Nolte resistance-to-transcendence, Gregor, Mosse, Payne, Sternhell, Eatwell), denial/revisionist discourses (Christophersen "Auschwitz Lie", Irving "no extermination centres" + Madagascar framing, John Day review), Eurofascism/New Right/Third Position, Levi epilogue. All attributed; denial material treated as object of study, not fact.
