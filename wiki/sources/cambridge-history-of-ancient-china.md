# The Cambridge History of Ancient China (Loewe & Shaughnessy, 1999) — TOC-only placeholder

> **⚠ Not yet ingested.** The collection holds only an OCR-test stub of this volume (front matter +

> future deep ingest. **No wiki content has been written from it.** When a full text is supplied,

The standard reference for **pre-imperial China** — from the origins of civilization to the Qin
unification of **221 BCE** — drawing heavily on archaeology and palaeography (oracle bones, bronze
inscriptions, excavated manuscripts) alongside the transmitted texts. It is the pre-imperial
companion to the [[cambridge-history-of-china|*Cambridge History of China*]] (which begins with the
Qin and Han).

## What it would contribute (on a real ingest)

Pre-imperial China is currently covered in the wiki only at summary level ([[shang-dynasty]],
[[zhou-dynasty]], [[warring-states-period-475-bce]], [[confucius]], etc.). A full ingest of this
volume would add the **archaeological and palaeographic backbone** the wiki lacks — Erlitou/Erligang/
Anyang, the oracle-bone and bronze-inscription evidence, the Western Zhou ritual reform, the social
history of the Spring and Autumn transition, and the pre-imperial roots of the early masters and of
the imperial state.
