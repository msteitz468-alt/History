# The Atlas of the Crusades (Jonathan Riley-Smith, 1991)

This is a dedicated cartographic atlas of the Crusades (1095–1291 and related expeditions), edited by the leading historian Jonathan Riley-Smith. It covers the origins, the main crusades to the Holy Land, the Latin East states, the Reconquista, the Baltic and other fronts, with maps of routes, battles, territories, and logistics.

Primary value for the wiki: high-quality maps for the crusading movement and the Frankish states in the Levant. The maps provide excellent visual context for campaigns, the geography of Outremer, and the interaction with Byzantine and Muslim powers.

## Structure and Coverage (198 pages)

Organized thematically and chronologically:

- Background and First Crusade
- The Latin Kingdom and principalities
- Second and Third Crusades
- Later crusades and the decline of the Latin East
- Other fronts (Spain, Baltic, etc.)
- Thematic maps on military orders, logistics, etc.

Strong on the Eastern Mediterranean theater.
