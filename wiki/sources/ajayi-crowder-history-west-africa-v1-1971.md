# History of West Africa, Volume One (Ajayi & Crowder, eds., 1971)

Volume One of the two-volume *History of West Africa*, the standard nationalist-era
academic reference produced by the Ibadan school. Its organising theme is **state
formation** — how centralised polities arose (and how some societies remained
deliberately stateless) across the West African savannah, forest, and Atlantic
coast from prehistory to c. 1800. The regional approach treats each zone and major
polity-cluster in turn, after three thematic introductory chapters.

This volume largely **enriches** the West-African pages already created during the
UNESCO *General History of Africa* (GHA) ingest (Gap 2), giving them a second
authoritative source and adding pre-1800 depth, while creating a handful of new
West-Africa-specific pages (stateless societies, the Niger Delta city-states, the
Mossi states, the 1591 Moroccan invasion of Songhay).
