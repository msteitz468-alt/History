# The Cambridge History of Africa, Vol. 2: From c. 500 BC to AD 1050 (1978)

The second CHA volume — the **classical-to-early-medieval** African millennium: the Iron
Age and Bantu expansion, the Nilotic and Ethiopian states, Roman and then Islamic North
Africa, and the birth of the trans-Saharan trade. Complements the UNESCO
[[gha-vol2-ancient-civilizations|GHA]] vols 2–3.
