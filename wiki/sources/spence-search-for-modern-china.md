# Jonathan Spence, *The Search for Modern China* (1990; 4th ed. 2024)

The classic narrative of **modern China**, the spine for the wiki's modern-China gap. Its task
here is to build out the transformation from the late Qing to the present — a period for which
the wiki had almost no coverage (no Opium Wars, Taiping, 1911 Revolution, Mao, or People's
Republic pages).

## Spence's theme

The book's title states its thesis: modern Chinese history is a long, often agonized **"search
for modern China"** — for a form of state and national identity that could restore China's
strength, dignity, and unity after the nineteenth-century collapse before Western and Japanese
power. Spence traces this search across the late imperial crisis, the republican experiments,
and the Communist revolution, attentive to social and intellectual life as well as politics.
