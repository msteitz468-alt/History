# The Cambridge History of Africa, Vol. 1: From the Earliest Times to c. 500 BC (1982)

The opening volume of the **Cambridge History of Africa**, ingested to deepen the wiki's
African deep-prehistory coverage and to complement the UNESCO [[gha-vol2-ancient-civilizations|GHA]]
series. Its enduring value is on **Africa as the cradle of humanity** and the **independent
African invention of agriculture**.
