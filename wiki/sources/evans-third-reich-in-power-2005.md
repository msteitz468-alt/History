# Evans, *The Third Reich in Power, 1933–1939* (Penguin, 2005)

Volume II of Richard J. Evans's Third Reich trilogy (between *The Coming of the
Third Reich*, 2003, and *The Third Reich at War*, 2008 — neither yet ingested).
Covers the consolidated dictatorship from the completion of the seizure of power
to the invasion of Poland: terror and the police state, propaganda and culture,
the church struggle, the economy of rearmament, social policy and the
*Volksgemeinschaft*, racial policy, and the road to war. Evans's organizing
argument: the Third Reich in peacetime was a society mobilized for war and
racial reordering — its apparent normality was preparation, not stabilization —
and it was steered from above by Hitler, Göring, and Goebbels rather than driven
purely by chaotic polycratic competition.

## Volume Synthesis Note

Evans's second volume argues that the peacetime Third Reich was a society
being remade for war and racial reordering: "a vast experiment in human
engineering," modern rather than reactionary, whose apparent stabilization
after 1934 was preparation, not normalization. Against the strong
structuralist model, Evans insists the regime was "driven in the first place
from above, by Hitler and his key henchmen" — the tempo of antisemitic
policy, the war timetable, and the great purges were all steered from the
top, even as party-base pressure (1935, 1938) supplied energy from below.
Three cross-cutting deflations run through the book: the "economic miracle"
was substantially statistical and rearmament-driven; the Volksgemeinschaft
changed spirits, not social structure; and propaganda's successes coexisted
with a population that remained war-anxious and only transactionally loyal.

For the wiki, the volume closes the biggest gap in the pre-war Nazi domestic
record: terror and justice (People's Court, camps, Gestapo scale and
denunciation studies), culture (Goebbels's chambers, Degenerate Art),
religion and education (church struggle, Hitler Youth, universities), the
economy (Mefo bills, Four-Year Plan quantities, Aryanization typology),
society (Labour Front/KdF, Winter Aid, aristocracy, peasantry), racial
policy (sterilization at full statistical resolution, Sinti/Roma, Black
Germans, homosexuals), and the road to war. Tensions with existing content
are minimal: Evans and Kershaw (already ingested via *Hubris*/*Nemesis*
ranges) agree closely; Evans's higher Kristallnacht figures (1,000+
synagogues, deaths in the hundreds-to-2,000 range vs. the official 91) and
his caution against the strong "self-policing society" reading of the
Gestapo are recorded as attributed refinements, not contradictions. Volumes
I (*The Coming of the Third Reich*) and III (*The Third Reich at War*) are
in `raw/` and remain to be ingested; the prologue's Weimar-collapse material
was extracted but mostly folded into existing pages pending Volume I.
