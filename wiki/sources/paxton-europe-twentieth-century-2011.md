# Europe in the Twentieth Century (5th ed., 2011)

Paxton and Hessler's survey of Europe from 1914 to the 2000s. Fifth edition
(Cengage, © 2011) substantially rewritten: 1914–1945 compressed, post-1945
expanded (Chs. 16–22 largely new), with heavy new emphasis on consumer society,
popular culture, gender, religious decline, and immigration in Western Europe,
and on the social/economic texture of communism in Eastern Europe.

Relation to already-ingested sources: overlaps heavily with the Cambridge
Cold War volumes, [[paxton-anatomy-of-fascism-2004|The Anatomy of Fascism]],
and [[paxton-vichy-france-1972|Vichy France]]; its interwar and WWII chapters
mostly reinforce existing coverage, while the postwar social-cultural chapters
fill genuine gaps.
