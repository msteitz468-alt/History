# Oxford Handbook of Early Modern European History, Vol. I: Peoples and Place (2015)

The thematic, structural half of the Handbook — demography, environment, society, economy, and
religion across Europe 1350–1750 — edited by Hamish Scott, whose Introduction makes the case for
"**early modernity**" as a period defined by salient *characteristics* rather than event-bookends.
