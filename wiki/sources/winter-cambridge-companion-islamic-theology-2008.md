# The Cambridge Companion to Classical Islamic Theology (Winter, ed., 2008)

A multi-author reference on **classical Islamic theology (kalam)** — its history and its systematic
themes. Ingested as an **enrichment pass** after the Islamic-intellectual-history build (Venture of
Islam ×3 + Adamson): it links the theology pages already created ([[kalam]], [[mutazilism]],
[[asharism]], [[occasionalism-islamic]], [[al-ghazali]], [[al-juwayni]], [[fakhr-al-din-al-razi]]) and
fills the gaps — the founder-figures [[al-ashari]] and [[al-maturidi]], the [[maturidism|Maturidi]] school,
and the systematic theological themes (divine attributes, creation, ethics, revelation, God's existence,
eschatology).

Deployed-subagent strategy, 6 thematic agents. Body content: lines ~**600–12,500**; later lines are
endnotes/bibliography/index.
