# CAH Vol. X — The Augustan Empire, 43 B.C.–A.D. 69 (Bowman, Champlin & Lintott eds., 2nd ed. 1996)

The direct sequel to [[crook-cah-v9-1992|CAH IX]], narrating the **transformation of
Rome from Republic to Principate**: the rise of Octavian/**Augustus** out of the
triumviral wars, the new monarchical system he constructed, its consolidation under
the **Julio-Claudians** (Tiberius, Gaius, Claudius, Nero), and the crisis of 68–69
that ended the dynasty. Picks up exactly where CAH IX left off — the
[[second-triumvirate|Second Triumvirate]] and the proscriptions (43) — and carries the
story to the eve of the Flavians.
