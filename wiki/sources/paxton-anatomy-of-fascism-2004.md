## Volume Synthesis Note

The book completes the arc begun with [[paxton-vichy-france-1972]]: the
1972 ideal type has matured into the five-stage processual model and the
one-sentence behavioral definition now recorded on [[generic-fascism]].
For this wiki it does four things. First, it supplies the **definitional
apparatus** for the whole fascism batch — every subsequent ingest (Payne,
Griffin, Sternhell, Flynn) can now be positioned against a mapped field of
rival definitions, since Paxton's ch. 8 and bibliographical essay survey
them all, with his verdicts. Second, it is **Position 1's fullest
statement** in [[fascism-and-the-political-left]], while honestly
documenting the syndicalist pipeline, the 1919 "national socialism," the
"neither Right nor Left" self-description, and the KKK-as-first-functional-
fascism claim that Position 2 will cite — the controversy page now carries
both uses. Third, its ch. 6 gives the wiki's most compact scholarly
synthesis of **how the Holocaust happened** (cumulative radicalization,
dress rehearsals, the dual state) — added to [[the-holocaust]] at full
fidelity per the atrocity-dense triage rule (that range was read on the
main thread; the chs. 7–8 subagent was content-filter-blocked and likewise
recovered on the main thread with nothing omitted). Fourth, its boundary
work (fascism vs. authoritarianism: Franco, Salazar, Vichy, Perón, imperial
Japan) is the standing reference for classifying borderline regimes across
the wiki.

Cross-source tensions to track: Paxton vs. **Griffin** (behavioral vs.
palingenetic-ideological definition — Paxton explicitly disparages the
one-sentence approach; Griffin's *Nature of Fascism* is in raw/) and vs.
**Sternhell** (France as fascism's cradle; Paxton sides with the critics
who find Sternhell's category "far too loose"). Against **Hayek**
([[hayek-road-to-serfdom-1944]], [[road-to-serfdom-thesis]]): Paxton's
radical right is a distinct formation allied with conservatives against
the Left, not a socialist offshoot — but note the two agree on more than
the polemics suggest (both deny inevitability, both stress elite choices,
and Paxton's "capacious family" history of the pre-Marxist Left overlaps
with Hayek's ch. 12 material). When Payne and Griffin are ingested, check
their definitions against ch. 8's survey rather than re-deriving the field.
