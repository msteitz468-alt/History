# Cox, *Woodrow Wilson: The Light Withdrawn* (2024)

Christopher Cox's 2024 reappraisal of [[wilson-woodrow|Woodrow Wilson]] (1856–1924), framed by Whittier's elegy "Ichabod" ("So fallen! so lost! the light withdrawn…"). The book argues that Wilson's reputation as progressive democrat and international idealist cannot be severed from his sustained opposition to women's national suffrage and his practice of white supremacy in federal administration. It places the Susan B. Anthony Amendment's fifty-year congressional fight — and the women and men who won it — at the center of the Wilson story, against a historiographical tradition that until recently treated suffrage and race as peripheral.

## Volume Synthesis Note

Cox’s book is the wiki’s first dedicated Wilson biography and a deliberate corrective to the Link-school heroic-internationalist tradition. Its load-bearing contribution is **integration**: federal resegregation, *Birth of a Nation*, long Anthony Amendment obstruction (including the Gay-Ewing Jim Crow rewrite Wilson endorsed), Silent Sentinels carceral violence, and Paris double standards on race/sex are one civil-rights structure, not side scandals. New Freedom economic legislation and Wilsonian internationalism remain real; Cox refuses to let them cancel the civil-rights ledger.
