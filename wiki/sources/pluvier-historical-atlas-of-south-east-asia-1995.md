# Historical Atlas of South-East Asia (Jan M. Pluvier, 1995)

This atlas provides a cartographic history of South-East Asia, illustrating the development of states, trade networks (e.g. maritime Silk Road influences), Indianization, Islamization, European colonialism, independence struggles, and modern borders.

Primary value: visual context for a region where the collection has known gaps (per CLAUDE.md). Maps help anchor events and processes in specific geographies.
