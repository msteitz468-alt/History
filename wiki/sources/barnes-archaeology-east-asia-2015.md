# Archaeology of East Asia: The Rise of Civilization in China, Korea and Japan

Gina L. Barnes, *Archaeology of East Asia: The Rise of Civilization in China, Korea and
Japan* (Oxford & Philadelphia: Oxbow Books, 2015). Revised third edition of *China, Korea
and Japan: The Rise of Civilization in East Asia* (Thames & Hudson, 1993; repr. as *The
Rise of Civilization in East Asia*, 1999). xx + 493 pp.

This is the single most important English-language synthesis filling the wiki's East Asian
prehistory gap. It is the only work that treats the archaeology of China, Korea and Japan
as one comparative system from the Palaeolithic to the formation of literate states (3rd–7th
century AD), rather than as three national silos. Its analytical payoff for the wiki is
greatest for **Korean and Japanese prehistory and protohistory** — regions the collection
otherwise covers thinly — and for the **comparative mechanics of state formation** across
the region.
