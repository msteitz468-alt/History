# A History of the Pacific Islands (Steven R. Fischer, 2002)

A single-volume narrative survey of Oceania from first settlement to the turn of the
twenty-first century, in the Palgrave Essential Histories series. Written by Steven
Roger Fischer (a linguist and historian based in Auckland), it runs from the
peopling of Sunda and Sahul through the Austronesian/Lapita expansion, the European
"trespass," the colonial "second colonization," the twentieth-century world wars and
decolonisation, to the contemporary "New Pacific" of diaspora, nuclear legacies, and
regional consolidation.

The wiki's primary Oceania backbone is the multi-author
[[denoon-cambridge-pacific-islanders-1997|Cambridge History of the Pacific
Islanders]] (1997); this Fischer survey is ingested as a **complementary** source —
mainly enriching existing pages and adding his distinctive deep-prehistory framing
(the [[near-and-remote-oceania|Near/Remote Oceania]] distinction) and his "Pacific
Islands as a single region/identity" thesis.
