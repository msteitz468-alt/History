# Atlas of American Military History (Stuart Murray, 2005)

This is a comprehensive illustrated atlas and reference covering the military history of the United States from the colonial period through the early 21st century. It features maps, timelines, battle diagrams, and visual essays on key wars, campaigns, and the evolution of weapons and tactics ("Instruments of War" sections).

Primary value for the wiki: cartographic and visual enrichment. The maps provide clear depictions of theaters, movements, battles, and strategic context, with supporting illustrations of equipment and period scenes. Text is limited to captions, short introductions, and timeline entries.

## Structure and Coverage (260 pages)

Organized chronologically in parts with dedicated "Instruments of War" sections tracking technology:

- Introduction and Timeline of American Military History
- Part One: Wars of the Colonial Period (fur trade, Seven Years' War in America, Revolutionary War)
- Part Two: Wars of the Young Republic (War of 1812, Mexican-American War, frontier conflicts)
- Part Three: The War Between the States (Civil War coverage)
- Part Four: An Emerging World Power (1870–1900, Spanish-American War)
- Part Five: The War to End All Wars (WWI)
- Part Six: Global War (WWII)
- Part Seven: The Hot Wars of the Cold War (Korea, Vietnam)
- Part Eight: A Lone Superpower (Gulf War, Afghanistan, Iraq)
- Bibliography and Index

The atlas balances narrative maps with technical detail on armies, navies, and weapons development. Strong visual emphasis on North American theaters but extends to overseas operations.

## Historiography / Collection Note

A popular/reference-style atlas with solid cartography for teaching and quick reference. The involvement of West Point consulting editors adds credibility for operational details. Maps draw on period sources and modern scholarship; they are generally reliable for geography and sequence while prioritizing clarity over hyper-detailed debate on micro-positions. Excellent companion for enriching the wiki's American military content at multiple scales.
