# Mystical Dimensions of Islam (Schimmel, 1975)

The standard single-volume survey of **Sufism**. Ingested as a DEEP enrichment pass that deepens the
wiki's existing Sufism layer ([[sufism]], [[philosophical-sufism]], [[tariqa-orders]], [[early-sufism]],
[[dhikr]], [[rumi]], [[ibn-arabi]]) and fills its major gaps — the founding figures ([[al-hallaj]],
[[junayd]], [[rabia-al-adawiyya]]), the experiential vocabulary of the mystical path (stations and states,
[[fana-and-baqa|annihilation and subsistence]]), the doctrine of the [[al-insan-al-kamil|Perfect Man]] and
[[wahdat-al-wujud]], the major orders, and the Persian, Turkish, and Indo-Muslim mystical poetry.

Deployed-subagent strategy, **6 agents** mapped to the book's chapters, weighted toward the dense
conceptual chapters (The Path; Sufi psychology; Theosophical Sufism). Body content: lines ~**840–15,500**
of `raw/_schimmel-wrapped.txt`; later lines are endnotes, bibliography, and index.
