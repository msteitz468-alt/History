# Sima Qian, *Records of the Grand Historian: Han Dynasty II* (the *Shi ji*)

The *Shi ji* on the **reign of Emperor Wu of Han** — the great expansionist emperor under whom
[[sima-qian|Sima Qian]] himself served and suffered. The primary source for the high Han.
Ingested over 2 cycles (Phase D, completing the Sima Qian primaries).

## What the volume covers

Watson's *Han Dynasty II* gathers the *Shi ji* chapters most concerned with **Emperor Wu's
reign**: three **Treatises** (the Feng-and-Shan imperial sacrifices and Wu's quest for
immortality; the Yellow River and water control; and the **"Balanced Standard"** — the state's
fiscal and economic interventions); and a long series of **biographies and "Accounts"** — Wu's
victorious cavalry generals (**Wei Qing**, **Huo Qubing**) and the tragic **Li Guang**; the
**Account of the Xiongnu** (Shi ji 110), the foundational ethnography of the steppe enemy; the
**Account of Dayuan** (Ferghana), recording the missions that opened the Silk Road; and the
pointed collective biographies of the **Confucian scholars, the reasonable officials, the harsh
officials, the wandering knights, and the money-makers**.
