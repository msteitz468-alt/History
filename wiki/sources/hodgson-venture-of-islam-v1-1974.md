# The Venture of Islam, Vol. 1 (Hodgson, 1974)

The first volume of Marshall Hodgson's monumental, deeply interpretive history of Islamic
civilization — "The Classical Age of Islam," from the world before Islam through Muhammad and the
early caliphate to the classical civilization of the High Caliphate and its dissipation (c. 945).
Its enduring value is **conceptual and intellectual-historical**: Hodgson's distinctive vocabulary
(**Islamicate** vs. Islamic, the **Irano-Semitic** tradition, the **agrarianate-citied society**, the
**Piety-Minded** opposition) reframed the whole field, and the volume is the wiki's anchor for Islamic
religious and intellectual history — the Quran, Shari'ah, Hadith, kalam, falsafah, Sufism, adab, and
the sectarian movements — which the political/dynastic backbone left thin (Gap 4b).
