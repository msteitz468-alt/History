# The Oxford Handbook of Modern Irish History (Jackson, ed., 2014)

Multi-author OUP handbook, 37 chapters, on modern Irish history. **Part I (Chs. 1–14)** is thematic
and historiographical — Ireland and empire, patriotism, unionism, the colony debate, landscape, land,
migration/diaspora, business, religion, gender, literature, art, material culture, film. **Part II
(Chs. 15–37)** is chronological — from the Ulster plantation (1580s) and the Cromwellian conquest,
through the eighteenth-century Ascendancy and 1798, the Union and the Great Famine, Home Rule, the
Revolution and partition, the two states, to the Troubles ("the Long War") and its end. Editor Alvin
Jackson centres the volume on the **revisionism debate** and the contested question of whether Ireland
was colony or metropole.
