# The Cambridge History of Japan, Vol. 1: Ancient Japan (Brown ed., 1993)

The wiki's first **Cambridge reference volume on Japan** — the deep, interdisciplinary
authority on the ancient period that underpins the Mason political spine and the Sansom
cultural body. Its task here is to **deepen and, where needed, correct** the existing
ancient-Japan pages ([[early-agriculture-japan]], [[yamato-japan]], [[taika-reform-645]],
[[nara-period]], [[shinto]], [[japanese-buddhism]], [[kojiki-yasumaro]]) with current
scholarship, and to add discrete-topic pages.
