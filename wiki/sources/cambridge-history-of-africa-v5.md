# The Cambridge History of Africa, Vol. 5: From c. 1790 to c. 1870 (1976)

The fifth CHA volume — the **pre-colonial nineteenth century**: Islamic revolution in the
western Sudan, the end of the Atlantic slave trade and the rise of "legitimate commerce,"
and the upheavals of southern Africa. Complements the UNESCO [[gha-vol6-19th-century|GHA]]
vol. 6.
