# Colonial Latin America (Burkholder & Johnson, 10th ed., 2019)

The standard textbook on the **colonial period** (c. 1492–1825) of Spanish America and Brazil — the
Americas gap's systematic account of the three centuries between conquest and independence. It is the
connective tissue between the wiki's pre-Columbian pages and the modern era: how a conquered hemisphere
became a set of European colonial societies, with their distinctive institutions (encomienda, the
*mita*, the hacienda), their silver-driven economy, their racial *sociedad de castas*, the Church, and
the Bourbon/Pombaline reforms that set the stage for independence.
