# 1491: New Revelations of the Americas Before Columbus (Charles C. Mann, 2005)

The wiki's foundational narrative source for the **pre-Columbian Western Hemisphere**, opening the
Americas leg of the collection's biggest remaining gap. Mann frames the book around the overturning of
what he calls **"Holmberg's Mistake"** — the long assumption that Native Americans were timeless,
unchanging primitives living lightly on a pristine continent — and the three revisionist findings that
replaced it: the Americas held **more people**, for **longer**, who **shaped their landscapes** far more
profoundly than was believed. The post-Columbian "wilderness" the European settlers found was in large
part an **artefact of catastrophe** — the depopulation of a managed continent by introduced epidemics.
