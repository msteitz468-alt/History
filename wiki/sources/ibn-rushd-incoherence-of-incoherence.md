# The Incoherence of the Incoherence (Ibn Rushd, c. 1180)

Ibn Rushd's point-by-point reply to [[ghazali-incoherence-of-philosophers|al-Ghazālī's Incoherence]],
defending the [[islamic-philosophy|falāsifa]] (chiefly Aristotle) while also correcting
[[ibn-sina|Avicenna]] and faulting al-Ghazālī's dialectics. Ingested as the second half of the
[[philosophy-versus-theology-in-islam|al-Ghazālī ↔ Ibn Rushd controversy]]: agents link the
just-created al-Ghazālī pages ([[eternity-of-the-world]], [[ghazali-on-causality]],
[[god-knowledge-of-particulars]], [[the-three-condemnations]]) and create the corresponding Averroist
replies.

Deployed-subagent strategy, **5 agents** mapped to the Discussions (16 on metaphysics, 4 on physics),
weighted toward the metaphysics and the key causality reply. Body content: lines ~**1,133–15,200** of
`raw/_averroes-wrapped.txt`; later lines are notes/index.
