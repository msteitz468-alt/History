# The Cambridge Modern History, Vol. IX: Napoleon (1907)

The Acton-era *Cambridge Modern History* volume on **Napoleon** — ingested to give the wiki
the Napoleonic era that links the [[french-revolution-1789|French Revolution]] to the
nineteenth century. (The newer NCMH-Modern Vol. IX is absent from the collection; this older
1907 volume fills the gap, with its age flagged.)
