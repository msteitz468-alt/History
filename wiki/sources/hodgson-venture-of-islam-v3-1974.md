# The Venture of Islam, Vol. 3 (Hodgson, 1974)

The final volume — the **gunpowder empires** and the modern world. **Book Five** treats the three
great early-modern Muslim empires as a "second flowering": the Shi'i [[safavid-empire|Safavid]] Iran
(1503–1722), the Indian Timuri ([[mughal-empire|Mughal]]) empire of Hindu-Muslim coexistence
(1526–1707), and the [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman]] Shari'ah-military alliance (1517–1718). **Book Six**
treats the modern transformation: the impact of the **Great Western Transmutation** (Hodgson's term for
the technicalistic/industrial revolution) from the Generation of 1789, European world hegemony in the
nineteenth century, and the rise of Islamic modernism. The **Epilogue** reflects on the Islamic
heritage and the modern conscience.
