# The West Point History of the American Revolution (2017)

The fourth and final volume of the **West Point History of Warfare** series, the U.S.
Military Academy's cadet textbook on the **military history of the American Revolution**.
It is the flagship secondary source in the wiki for the *conduct* of the Revolutionary
War — strategy, operations, logistics, command, and the battlefield — and the anchor for
the dedicated **[[hubs/warfare/age-of-expansion/american-revolutionary-war/american-revolutionary-war|warfare-hub campaign analysis]]**.

It complements, rather than duplicates, the existing **[[american-revolution|American Revolution]]**
event page (drawn from *NCMH* VIII), which carries the political, ideological, and
diplomatic story. This source supplies the military backbone: the creation of the
[[continental-army|Continental Army]], the generalship of [[washington-george|Washington]]
and [[greene-nathanael|Greene]], the failed British strategies under
[[howe-william|Howe]], [[clinton-henry|Clinton]], and [[cornwallis-charles|Cornwallis]],
and the campaigns from Boston to [[siege-of-yorktown-1781|Yorktown]].

## Reliability and Use

- **Authoritative for**: operational and tactical military history, command analysis,
  logistics, the institutional birth of the U.S. Army.
- **Frame to watch**: written to inspire cadets ("bring the smell of gunpowder into the
  text"); celebratory toward Washington and the founding. Treat its admiration as emphasis,
  not as distortion of fact; cross-check interpretive claims against the political account
  in [[american-revolution]] and [[atlantic-revolutions]].
- **Conflicts**: none material with existing wiki content; this volume *adds* the military
  layer the [[american-revolution]] page lacked.
