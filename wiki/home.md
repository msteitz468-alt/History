# World History Wiki — Home

> The front door. This wiki is a network of **~1,700 interlinked pages** spanning
> prehistory to the present. The pages are densely cross-linked at the micro level;
> these **hub pages** are the macro layer that ties them together. Start here, then
> follow the spine, the themes, the civilizations, or the regions.

### Navigation conventions
