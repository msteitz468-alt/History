# Synchronic Snapshot — c. 1200 CE

> A cut across the Eurasian high-medieval world on the eve of the storm. From the Atlantic to the
> Pacific, settled civilizations are at a commercial and cultural peak — and almost none of them sense
> the force gathering on the Mongolian steppe that will, within two generations, overrun half of them.

## The settled civilizations at their peak

- **Latin Europe** — the [[high-middle-ages|High Middle Ages]] at flood tide: [[frederick-i-barbarossa|Barbarossa]]'s
  empire and the Capetian and Angevin monarchies; the [[crusader-states|Crusader states]] reeling after
  [[saladin|Saladin]]'s victories; commercial and urban revolution across the continent.
- **Byzantium & Islam** — the [[byzantine-empire|Byzantine Empire]] is about to be shattered by the
  Fourth Crusade (1204); the [[almohads|Almohads]] rule the western Islamic world; the new
  [[delhi-sultanate|Delhi Sultanate]] is being founded in India.
- **Song China** — the [[song-dynasty|Song]] preside over the most advanced economy on Earth — paper
  money, gunpowder, mass printing — though confined to the south by the Jin.
- **Japan** — the [[kamakura-period|Kamakura]] warrior government under [[minamoto-no-yoritomo|Minamoto no Yoritomo]]
  has just inaugurated rule by the [[samurai|samurai]].
- **Southeast Asia** — the [[khmer-empire|Khmer Empire]] under [[jayavarman-vii|Jayavarman VII]] builds
  its greatest monuments at [[angkor|Angkor]].

## The Americas and Africa

- **North America** — [[cahokia|Cahokia]], the largest city north of Mexico, is at its height on the Mississippi.
- **West Africa** — the [[ghana-empire|Ghana]] empire has given way to the rise of Mali on the gold-and-salt trade.
- **Andes & Mesoamerica** — regional states fill the gap before the Inca and Aztec imperial systems
  (still two centuries off).

## The gathering storm

- **The steppe** — [[genghis-khan|Temüjin]] is uniting the Mongol tribes; proclaimed Genghis Khan in
  1206, he will launch the [[mongol-empire|Mongol Empire]] — the force that, within fifty years, will
  destroy or subjugate Song China, the Islamic heartland, and the Rus principalities.

## The pattern

This is a world of **mature, prosperous, mutually unaware civilizations** — and a textbook case of
how a single mobile, peripheral power ([[hubs/regions/steppe|the steppe]]) can overturn the settled
order. The high-medieval peak and the Mongol shock are causally linked only in hindsight; in 1200 the
courts of Paris, Constantinople, Hangzhou, and Angkor had no idea they shared a future.
