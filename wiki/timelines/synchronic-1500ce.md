# Synchronic Snapshot — c. 1500 CE

> A cut across the world at the hinge of modernity — the generation when the hemispheres, separate for
> 12,000 years, were permanently joined. Great empires flourish on every continent, most still unaware
> of each other; within decades the [[columbian-exchange-1492|Columbian Exchange]] and the sea routes
> to Asia will bind them into a single, violently unequal system. This page reads *horizontally*; the
> [[master-timeline|Master Timeline]] reads *vertically*.

## The Old World's great powers

- **Europe** — the [[italian-renaissance-state-system|Italian Renaissance state system]] at its height;
  the [[fall-of-granada-1492|fall of Granada (1492)]] completes the Reconquista; Iberia launches the
  [[age-of-discovery|Age of Discovery]]. The Reformation is a generation away (1517).
- **The Islamic world** — the [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman Empire]] is the Mediterranean's dominant power
  (Constantinople fell in 1453); Safavid Iran is about to form; [[babur|Babur]] will found the
  [[mughal-empire|Mughal Empire]] in India in 1526.
- **China** — the [[ming-dynasty|Ming]] rule the world's largest economy; the great
  [[zheng-he-voyages|Zheng He voyages]] are a closed chapter, and China has turned inward as Europe
  turns outward.

## The Americas at their imperial peak

- **Mesoamerica** — the [[aztec-empire|Aztec Empire]] dominates central Mexico from Tenochtitlan.
- **The Andes** — the [[inca-empire|Inca Empire]], the largest state in the pre-Columbian Americas,
  spans the length of the Andes.
- Both are at their height — and both will be destroyed within a generation by tiny Spanish forces
  wielding steel, horses, and, above all, [[virgin-soil-epidemics|epidemic disease]].

## Africa

- **West Africa** — the [[songhay-empire|Songhay Empire]] is the last and largest of the great Sahelian
  gold empires.
- The Atlantic coast is just being drawn into the Portuguese trading and, soon, slaving network.

## The pattern

For the last time in history, the world's major civilizations are still operating as **largely separate
systems**. What makes 1500 the true hinge is not that any one of them is uniquely strong, but that the
**ocean** is about to become a highway: the [[columbian-exchange-1492|Columbian Exchange]] joins the
hemispheres biologically, the [[hubs/regions/atlantic-world|Atlantic]] and [[hubs/regions/indian-ocean|Indian Ocean]]
systems fuse into one, and the comfortable mutual ignorance of [[synchronic-1200ce|three centuries earlier]]
ends forever.
