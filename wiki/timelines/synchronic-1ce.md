# Synchronic Snapshot — c. 1 CE

> A cut across the world at the turn of the eras. The Old World's western and eastern ends are each
> dominated by a vast, durable empire — **Rome** and **Han China** — with **Parthia** holding the
> middle and a chain of trade tying them together along the first **Silk Roads**. This page reads
> *horizontally* across space; the [[master-timeline|Master Timeline]] reads *vertically* through time.

## The age of empires (the connected core)

- **Rome** — [[augustus|Augustus]] has refounded the state as the [[principate|Principate]]; the
  [[roman-empire|Roman Empire]] rings the Mediterranean and is digesting the last [[hellenistic-world|Hellenistic]]
  kingdoms. Its frontier is checked in Germany ([[arminius|Arminius]] destroys three legions in 9 CE).
- **Parthia** — the [[parthian-empire|Parthian Empire]] holds Iran and Mesopotamia, the buffer and
  rival between Rome and the East.
- **Han China** — the [[han-dynasty|Han]] empire, comparable to Rome in scale and sophistication, is
  at its first peak; within a decade [[wang-mang|Wang Mang]] will usurp the throne (9 CE).
- **India** — the Mauryan order is long gone; the [[satavahana-empire|Satavahanas]] dominate the
  Deccan, and the [[kushan-empire|Kushans]] are about to rise in the northwest, linking India to the
  Silk Roads.

## Independent and emerging worlds

- **Mesoamerica** — [[teotihuacan|Teotihuacan]] is growing into one of the largest cities on Earth,
  on a wholly separate hemispheric clock.
- **Sub-Saharan Africa** — the [[nok-culture|Nok culture]] works iron in West Africa; [[aksum|Aksum]]
  is rising as a Red Sea trading power.
- **The steppe** — the [[xiongnu|Xiongnu]] confederacy presses on Han China's northern frontier.
- **Temperate Europe** — the [[germanic-peoples|Germanic peoples]] beyond the Rhine and Danube remain
  stateless and non-literate, but increasingly entangled with Rome.

## The pattern

Two things define this moment. First, **scale**: more of humanity lives under large, bureaucratic
empires than ever before, and the Rome–Parthia–Kushan–Han chain means goods (and disease) can travel
the length of Eurasia. Second, **fragility through connection** — the same density that enables the
[[antonine-plague-165|later pandemics]] is being built now. Yet the Americas and sub-Saharan Africa
remain on entirely separate trajectories: simultaneity is not connection
(`concurrent_with` is not `caused_by`).
