# Fomenko New Chronology (radical revision of traditional historical chronology)

## Dispute Overview

The "New Chronology" developed by Anatoly T. Fomenko and collaborators proposes that the conventional chronology of world history before the late medieval period (the system associated with Scaliger and Petavius and refined since) is fundamentally incorrect. Most "ancient" and early medieval history is argued to be fabricated or duplicated, with events actually occurring centuries later (primarily 11th–17th centuries AD). This creates a direct conflict with the periodization, dating, and source interpretation used across ancient, classical, medieval, and early modern history.

## Positions

**Fomenko / New Chronology position (steel-manned from the source):**

The Scaliger-Petavius chronology was constructed in the 16th–17th centuries from incomplete and contradictory sources and contains systematic errors and duplications. Quantitative methods (statistical analysis of texts and king-lists, astronomical retrocalculations of eclipses and zodiacs, detection of dynastic parallels) demonstrate that traditional dates for Egyptian, Greek, Roman, Biblical, and other "ancient" material are off by hundreds or thousands of years. A coherent reconstruction places the bulk of these events in the medieval period, with significant roles for a Russian-centered "Horde-Ataman" imperial structure. Specific claims include re-dating Jesus to the 11th century, re-dating the Bible largely to the Middle Ages, and re-identifying numerous "ancient" polities and figures. The theory is presented as the result of applying rigorous mathematical and natural-science methods to the historical record.

**Mainstream scholarly position (steel-manned summary):**

The traditional chronology, while refined over centuries, rests on a convergent web of evidence: contemporary inscriptions and documents, stratified archaeological sequences, calibrated radiocarbon and other physical dating methods, dendrochronology, astronomical records independently verifiable, genetic and linguistic data, and cross-cultural synchronisms. Claims of wholesale fabrication or massive shifts are incompatible with this evidence. Specific Fomenko identifications and re-datings have been examined and rejected in the relevant specialist literatures (Egyptology, classics, biblical studies, medieval history, astronomy). Statistical parallels are seen as methodologically flawed (cherry-picking, ignoring context and independent evidence). The New Chronology is classified as pseudohistory.

## Shared Factual Ground (framed per side)

Both sides agree that historical chronology has been debated since antiquity and that Scaliger and Petavius produced influential (but not infallible) syntheses in the early modern period. Both accept that some ancient and medieval sources contain errors, biases, or legendary material. Both recognize the value of quantitative and scientific methods in historical research. They differ sharply on the scale of error in the received framework and on the reliability of the statistical/parallel methods versus the convergent material and textual evidence.

## Resolution Status

- Open as public and popular discourse (the books have circulated widely and continue to attract attention).
- Resolved-by-consensus within academic history, archaeology, and related sciences: the New Chronology is not accepted and is treated as a fringe theory.
