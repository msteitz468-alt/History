# The Population of Pre-Columbian Americas

A major scholarly controversy concerning the size of indigenous populations in the Western Hemisphere on the eve of European contact (c. 1492) and the scale and causes of subsequent demographic collapse. Directly affects interpretations of the Columbian Exchange, the nature of pre-contact societies, and the moral/political framing of conquest and settlement.

The debate turns on carrying-capacity estimates, early colonial censuses and tribute records, archaeological settlement data, genetic and paleodemographic proxies, and accounts by participants/observers with strong incentives to minimize or exaggerate numbers.
