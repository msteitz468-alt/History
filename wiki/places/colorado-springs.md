# Colorado Springs

**Colorado Springs**, Colorado, was the site of [[actors/tesla-nikola|Nikola Tesla]]'s experimental high-altitude laboratory in **1899**, where the dry mountain air and free electrical power (supplied by the local power company) let him work at extreme voltages. Here he built an enormous resonant transformer ("magnifying transmitter"), generated artificial lightning many meters long, lit lamps without wires at a distance, studied terrestrial **stationary waves**, and made the claims — that the earth could be made to resonate electrically, and that he had received possibly extraterrestrial signals — that would shape his wireless-power program.

The Colorado Springs work was the experimental basis for the [[places/wardenclyffe|Wardenclyffe]] venture and for [[concepts/wireless-power-transmission|Tesla's "World System"]]. His detailed *Colorado Springs Notes* are a key primary source for this period.
