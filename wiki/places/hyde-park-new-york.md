# Hyde Park, New York

Hudson River estate of the Roosevelt family and lifelong home of Franklin D. Roosevelt. Site of Springwood (the family home), the Roosevelt Library and Museum, and numerous working visits and political meetings during FDR's presidency. FDR's burial place. Central to the personal and symbolic geography of the New Deal and wartime presidency.
