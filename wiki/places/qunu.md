# Qunu

**Qunu** is a rural settlement in Thembuland, Eastern Cape, where Nelson Mandela spent his boyhood after his father’s dismissal as headman. It consisted of beehive-shaped huts in a narrow valley of undulating hills, clear streams and lush pastures. Mandela later described it as the place where he felt his “real roots lay.” He returned there in retirement, building a house on family land. Featured in Meredith’s account of Mandela’s formation and final years.
