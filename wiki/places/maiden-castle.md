# Maiden Castle

**Maiden Castle**, near Dorchester in Dorset, England, is one of the largest and most
elaborate Iron Age hillforts in Britain, its multiple massive concentric ramparts enclosing
around 19 hectares. Occupied and progressively fortified from the Early Iron Age, it
served as a major settlement and probable tribal center for the Durotriges people. The
site was extensively excavated by Sir Mortimer Wheeler in the 1930s, whose discovery of a
war cemetery with skeletons bearing traumatic injuries (including one with a Roman
ballista bolt embedded in the spine) was long read as direct evidence of a violent Roman
assault during the conquest of Britain (c. 43–44 CE); later re-analysis has questioned
whether the burials reflect a single dramatic storming or a longer period of conflict and
disturbance, making the site a frequently cited case study in the interpretation of
archaeological violence.
