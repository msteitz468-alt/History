Kim Liên village, Nam Đàn district, Nghệ An province (central Vietnam panhandle). Birthplace of Ho Chi Minh (Nguyen Sinh Cung, 1890) in a modest Confucian scholar family. Father Nguyen Sinh Sac; local tradition of resistance ("buffalos of Nghệ An"). Site of early childhood and family influences; later a place of pilgrimage and museum.
