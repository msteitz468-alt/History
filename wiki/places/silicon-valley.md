# Silicon Valley

The southern San Francisco Bay Area region (centered on Santa Clara County, including Cupertino, Palo Alto, Mountain View, Sunnyvale) that became the global center of the semiconductor, personal computer, software, internet, and consumer electronics industries from the mid-twentieth century onward. Formative environment for Steve Jobs (raised in Los Altos/Mountain View), Steve Wozniak, and Apple’s garage origins. Home to Hewlett-Packard, Xerox PARC (nearby), Intel, and later the venture capital ecosystem that funded the personal computing revolution. In Isaacson’s narrative, the specific cultural mix of 1970s California engineering, counterculture, and entrepreneurship shaped Jobs’s synthesis of technology and liberal arts.
