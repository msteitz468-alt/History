Hanoi (Hà Nội), capital of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam (1945–) and later Socialist Republic, located in the Red River delta. Long the political and cultural center of northern Vietnam (Thang Long / Đông Kinh under earlier dynasties). Site of the 2 September 1945 independence declaration by Ho Chi Minh at Ba Dinh Square; August Revolution seizures of power; early DRV government seat. Chinese Nationalist occupation forces arrived north of 16th parallel after 1945. Strategic center throughout the wars; location of Ho's stilt house and later mausoleum.

Geographic and strategic importance derived from Red River access, proximity to China border, and historic role as seat of Vietnamese courts and administration. Significance evolved from colonial administrative hub to revolutionary capital to modern political center.
