# Wessex

**Wessex** (southern Britain, especially Wiltshire and adjacent counties) is the key region for the "Wessex culture" phase of Early to Middle Bronze Age chiefly societies, marked by rich single-grave burials under round barrows containing prestige metalwork, gold, amber, and other exotica. It exemplifies the shift to "individualizing" chiefdoms and the operation of the European Bronze Age prestige economy.

## Geographic and Archaeological Significance

Wessex is the classic area for the rich early Bronze Age graves that parallel developments in Brittany and central Europe. "A similar phenomenon may be seen in Brittany... These are dated to c. 2300-2000 BC and they are succeeded by a similar but much less rich group of barrow burials, dating to c. 2000-1500 BC; this situation also parallels southern Britain, where the rich burials continue until c. 1500 BC but the later burials are less rich than the earlier group."

The phase is treated as "that between 'group-oriented' and 'individualizing' chiefdoms."

## Key Evidence: Elite Burials and Prestige Goods

The Bush Barrow grave (near Stonehenge area) is iconic: grave goods include a "Lozenge-shaped plate of sheet gold with incised ornament", copper/bronze daggers, rivets, bone mounts/ferrules, polished stone mace-head, "Belt-hook of hammered gold with incised ornament", flanged axe, etc. (detailed in Fig. 7.9).

These reflect consumption of prestige goods obtained by exchange (gold, amber) and local craft (metalworking, bone).

## Role in Social Differentiation Process

Wessex illustrates the western European trajectory in which "rich early Bronze Age graves appear as a result of the changed ideology and ritual (and consequently changed nature) of societies whose organization was in some sense already regionally centralized" (from prior monument-building phases). This contrasts with central Europe where hierarchies developed during the early Bronze Age.

The area participated in interregional prestige networks: amber from Baltic sources reached Wessex in quantity and was crafted into high-status items (spacer beads, cups).
