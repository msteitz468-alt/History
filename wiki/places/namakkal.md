# Namakkal

Namakkal, the South Indian town whose temple housed the shrine of the goddess Namagiri, Ramanujan’s family deity. In late 1913, before the final decision to travel to England, Ramanujan and Narayana Iyer stayed on the temple grounds for three nights. According to family tradition and Ramanujan’s own statements, he received divine sanction in a dream. The visit crystallized his resolve to go despite the ritual and family obstacles. Namakkal and Namagiri remain central to the devotional framing of his mathematical life.
