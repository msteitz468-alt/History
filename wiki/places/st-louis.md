# St. Louis

Founded 1764 by Pierre Laclède and Auguste Chouteau as a French fur-trading post on the west bank of the Mississippi just below the Missouri confluence. Briefly under Spanish control after 1763, then part of the U.S. Louisiana Purchase (1803).

In 1804 it was the jumping-off point and administrative hub for the Lewis and Clark Expedition (Corps of Discovery). The captains recruited men, acquired supplies, dealt with Spanish and American officials, and launched the first Native delegations to Washington from here. Clark maintained his base and later long career as Superintendent of Indian Affairs in St. Louis.

The city embodied the transition from French/Spanish colonial river world to American continental ambition. Its Creole, French, Spanish, American, and Native populations made it a critical node for information, trade, and diplomacy.
