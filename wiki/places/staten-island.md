# Staten Island

Island in New York Harbor, birthplace and early base of Cornelius Vanderbilt's ferry and steamboat empire.

## Description and Historical Role

In the early 19th century Staten Island was a rural, Dutch-influenced community across the harbor from Manhattan. It was connected to the city by regular ferry service, which Vanderbilt began operating as a teenager.

## Significance

Vanderbilt was born on Staten Island in 1794. He began his career running a small ferry between Staten Island and Manhattan, eventually building a dominant position in local water transport. The island remained a symbolic and practical base even as his operations expanded to steamships, Central America, and railroads. His early mastery of the competitive, low-margin ferry business taught the ruthless cost-cutting and customer focus that defined his later career.
