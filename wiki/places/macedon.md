# Macedon

Ancient region and kingdom in the northern Greek peninsula / southern Balkans. Home of the Argead dynasty (Philip II, Alexander the Great) that unified Greece and conquered the Achaemenid Empire. Later Antigonid kingdom; became Roman province after 146 BCE. Core of the Hellenistic world.

## Geographic Description

Mountainous, with fertile plains (especially around the Axios and Strymon rivers). Borders Illyrians, Thracians, and Greek poleis to the south. Strategic position between Greece and the Danube/Black Sea regions. Rich in timber, minerals, and horses.

## History of Control and Settlement

- Early: Tribal kingdom, Hellenizing under Argeads from the 7th-6th c. BCE.
- Classical peak: Philip II (r. 359-336) modernized army (phalanx + cavalry), conquered Greek poleis, unified under League of Corinth.
- Alexander the Great: Conquered Persian Empire 334-323 BCE; founded Hellenistic kingdoms.
- Hellenistic: Antigonid dynasty after the Wars of the Diadochi. Constant wars with Greek leagues and Rome.
- Roman: Defeated at Pydna (168 BCE); province of Macedonia from 146 BCE.

Why it mattered: Transformed Greek warfare and politics; spread Greek culture across Near East and Central Asia; bridge between Classical Greece and Roman Empire.
