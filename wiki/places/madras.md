# Madras

Madras (now Chennai) was the colonial administrative and educational capital of South India and the place where Ramanujan spent his most difficult patron-seeking years (1909–1914) and where he died in 1920. The Madras Port Trust, the University of Madras (which eventually gave him a research scholarship), the Connemara Library, and the houses in Triplicane and later Chetput are all part of the story. It was in Madras that the letter to Hardy was posted and where the hero’s welcome and funeral took place.
