# Kumbakonam

Kumbakonam, a temple town in the Tanjore (Thanjavur) district of the Cauvery delta, Tamil Nadu. Ramanujan’s childhood home on Sarangapani Sannidhi Street lay a short distance from the Sarangapani Temple. The slow measured thwap of wet clothes being beaten on the river rocks was the constant sound of his early years. The town combined intense traditional Brahmin learning and ritual with the modest colonial administrative and educational infrastructure (Town High School, Government College) that gave the young Ramanujan his first formal exposure to Western mathematics while leaving him largely self-taught. The contrast between the narrow, devout, river-and-temple world of Kumbakonam and the world of Trinity College is one of the central themes of his biography.
