# Fremont Factory (Tesla)

Former NUMMI (GM-Toyota) plant in Fremont, California, acquired by Tesla. Site of “Fremont Factory Hell” production ramp for Model S/3/Y; vertical integration experiments, tent manufacturing, and the intense 2018 production push. Central to Musk’s manufacturing philosophy.
