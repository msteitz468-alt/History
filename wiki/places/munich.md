# Munich

## Geographic Description

Capital of the Kingdom of Bavaria (until 1918); major southern German city on the Isar, Catholic and particularist in character, with strong beerhall political culture. In 1914 a city of ~600,000; center of Bavarian regional identity within the German Reich.

## History of Control and Settlement

Wittelsbach capital for centuries. Part of the German Empire 1871–1918. 1918–19: site of early revolution (Eisner government, Räterepublik) and violent counter-revolution. 1920s–30s: stronghold of the early Nazi movement; site of the 1923 Beer Hall Putsch attempt.

## Why This Place Mattered

For [[actors/adolf-hitler]]: the city of his 1913 arrival ("childhood secret love" for Germany), the famous 2 August 1914 Odeonsplatz crowd photo during the Augusterlebnis, WWI return in 1918, the 1918–19 revolutionary trauma, and the September 1919 DAP meeting in the Sterneckerbräu where he was recruited by Anton Drexler. Munich was the platform on which the "discovery of a talent" occurred. The army's political section operated here; the tiny DAP was a local völkisch group that Hitler rapidly dominated. Later the site of the Bürgerbräukeller putsch attempt and the movement's refoundation.

`preceded_by:` Vienna failure and 1913 move. `followed_by:` DAP/NSDAP growth, 1923 putsch, national power after 1933.

Broader: beerhall politics, paramilitary (Freikorps/SA) culture, and the conservative/reactionary Bavarian milieu that first sheltered and then clashed with the Nazis.

## Historiography

Kershaw (Range 01) stresses Munich as the accidental but decisive site of Hitler's political birth. The city was "scarcely recognized" on his 1918 return; barracks under councils, Eisner (Jewish) in power. The revolution and repression "deeply scarred" Hitler. The DAP meeting was one of many small gatherings; Hitler's oratory made it historic. Police/army surveillance records, the membership card, and the Odeonsplatz photo are key material traces. Hitler's later minimization of his 1918–19 role is noted as suspicious.
