# Wah-sut (South Abydos)

A planned town of the late Twelfth Dynasty built ~4 km from the old town of Abydos to
serve the Osiris-linked mortuary complex of **Senusret III** (whose huge "replica tomb"
and temple stand nearby). Its full name, **Wahsut-Khakaura**, means "Enduring are the
places of Khakaura" (the king's prenomen). With [[places/kahun|Kahun]] it is one of the
two best-known exemplars of the Middle Kingdom's planned [[concepts/provider-state|provider-state]]
settlement.

## Why It Mattered

In [[sources/kemp-ancient-egypt-anatomy-2018|Kemp's *Anatomy of a Civilization*]] (Ch. 5,
"Model communities"), Wah-sut closely parallels Kahun: at least **16 large houses** plus
one even larger **mayor's residence** (comparable to a Kahun mansion), with several
generations of the mayoral office identified in the documentary record. Whatever small-house
zone may have existed is lost beneath modern fields. The town is part of the Middle
Kingdom's deliberate programme of orthogonal, state-founded settlement on fresh ground —
[[actors/middle-kingdom-egypt|the "bureaucratic Utopia"]] made physical.
