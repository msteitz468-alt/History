# South Africa

Southern African country; birthplace of Elon Musk (Pretoria, 1971). Setting of childhood trauma (bullying, veldskool survival camp, family violence, Errol Musk’s regime). Apartheid-era violence and transition referenced in family stories. Musk left permanently in 1989.
