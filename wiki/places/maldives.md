# Maldives

Chain of atolls in the Indian Ocean south-west of India and Sri Lanka, an important node in medieval maritime trade between the Arabian Sea, Malabar, and further east. Ibn Battuta visited and served as chief qadi there (c. 1343–44) under a female ruler.

## 14th-century Description (Ibn Battuta primary)

The islands were already Muslim. Local economy relied on cowrie shells (used as currency across the Indian Ocean and West Africa), coconut products, and inter-island trade. Ibn Battuta describes the queen's court, the vizier, the use of palm-leaf writing, the judge's revenue, marriage customs, and the rapid Islamization narrative current on the islands. He himself married locally and exercised judicial authority.

## Significance

One of the most detailed contemporary external accounts of the Maldives before the Portuguese era. Demonstrates how small Indian Ocean polities participated in the larger *dar al-Islam* through trade, scholarship mobility, and shared legal traditions (Shafi'i in this case).
