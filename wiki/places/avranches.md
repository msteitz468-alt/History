# Avranches

Town in Normandy critical during the 1944 breakout from the bocage. US First Army under Bradley captured it in late July 1944, enabling Patton's Third Army to exploit the gap south into Brittany and east toward the Seine. Site of German counterattacks (Operation Lüttich) that failed, leading to the Falaise encirclement.
