# Môn (Anglesey)

Island off north Wales; classical sources (esp. Tacitus) describe it as a major druidic sanctuary destroyed by Suetonius Paulinus (60/61 CE). Later core territory of Gwynedd; rich in medieval sites and traditions (Llyn Cerrig Bach votive deposit).
