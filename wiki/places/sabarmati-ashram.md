# Sabarmati Ashram

The ashram founded by Gandhi on the banks of the Sabarmati river near Ahmedabad (1917 onward; earlier at Kochrab). It served as the institutional and spiritual base for his early Indian satyagraha experiments, the 1930 Salt March (from which Gandhi and the 78 companions departed), and the development of the Constructive Programme. Gandhi left the ashram in 1930 vowing not to return until swaraj was achieved; it became a major site of national memory.

Guha details its daily routines, the role of Kasturba and resident satyagrahis, and its significance as both model community and organizational headquarters.
