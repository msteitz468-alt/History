# Trieste

Multilingual Adriatic port city (then Austro-Hungarian, later Italian) where Joyce and Nora lived for most of the decade 1904–1915. Joyce taught English at the Berlitz school and privately; Stanislaus joined them and provided crucial financial and emotional support. Here Joyce wrote or completed most of *Dubliners*, drafted *A Portrait*, and conceived the plan for *Ulysses*. The city's irredentist politics, linguistic mixture, and marginal European status resonated with Joyce's own position.
