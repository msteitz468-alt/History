# Bien Hoa

City and major military/air base area northeast of Saigon. Site of the July 1959 attack that killed the first two Americans in the Second Indochina War (Ovnand and Buis).

## Significance

In 1959 Bien Hoa was a relatively lightly defended MAAG advisory facility. The attack on the mess hall there marked the beginning of systematic Viet Cong targeting of U.S. personnel.
