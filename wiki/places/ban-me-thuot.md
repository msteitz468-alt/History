# Ban Me Thuot (Buon Ma Thuot)

**Ban Me Thuot** is a city in Vietnam's Central Highlands, capital of Dak Lak province,
strategically positioned astride roads linking the highlands to the coast and to Saigon.
In March 1975 it was the target of a surprise North Vietnamese offensive (the
[[events/ban-me-thuot-1975|Battle of Ban Me Thuot]]) that overran the lightly defended
garrison and exposed the weakness of South Vietnamese dispositions in the highlands. The
city's fall — and President [[nguyen-van-thieu|Nguyen Van Thieu's]] subsequent order to
abandon the highlands entirely — triggered the "convoy of tears," a catastrophic
withdrawal that effectively destroyed ARVN's II Corps and set off the chain of collapses
culminating in the [[events/fall-of-saigon-1975|fall of Saigon]] six weeks later.
