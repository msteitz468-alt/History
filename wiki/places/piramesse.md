# Piramesse

**Piramesse** ("House of Ramesses"; biblical *Raamses*) was the great eastern-Delta residence
city built by [[actors/ramesses-ii|Ramesses II]] as the capital of Ramesside Egypt. It has
"now been established beyond reasonable doubt" to be the remains at **Qantir** and
**[[places/avaris|Tell el-Dabʿa]]** — the same ground as the old Hyksos capital Avaris, whose
god Seth the Ramesside family from Avaris honoured as their royal ancestor.

## Egypt's window on Asia

Strategically placed near the road to the Sile border fortress and along the **Pelusiac branch
of the Nile**, Piramesse became "the most important international trade centre and military
base in the country." It housed many **foreign deities** and foreign officials (some, like the
"royal butlers," in senior positions), and after the
[[events/egyptian-hittite-treaty-1259bce|year-21 peace treaty]] **Hittite craftsmen** worked
in its armoury, teaching the manufacture of Hittite-style shields. Many of Ramesses II's high
officials lived at Piramesse but were buried in the Memphis necropolis.

## Decline

Piramesse's decline was environmental: the **harbour silted up** as the Pelusiac branch of the
Nile shifted eastward — one of the interlocking factors (with the loss of the Levantine
provinces and the rise of the [[amun-priesthood|Amun priesthood]]) behind the
[[new-kingdom-egypt|New Kingdom's]] 20th-Dynasty decline. Its monuments were later quarried for
the nearby city of Tanis.
