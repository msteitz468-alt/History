# Braunau am Inn

## Geographic Description

Small provincial town on the Inn River, on the border between Austria (Upper Austria) and Bavaria (Germany). Customs post location; modest size in 1889.

## History of Control and Settlement

Habsburg territory; border customs significance shaped local economy and official careers (Alois Hitler's posting).

## Why This Place Mattered

Site of Adolf Hitler's birth, 20 April 1889, 6:30 p.m., in the Gasthof zum Pommer (Vorstadt Nr. 219). "In the very first sentences of *Mein Kampf*, Adolf was to emphasize – what became a Nazi stock-in-trade – how providential it was that he had been born in Braunau am Inn, on the border of the two countries he saw it as his life’s task to unite."

The family left when Hitler was under three (1892 promotion of Alois). No lasting personal connection beyond the birth and the later mythologizing.

## Historiography

Kershaw (Range 01, Ch. 1) treats the birth as entirely ordinary provincial event; the "providential border" rhetoric is retrospective Nazi stock-in-trade. Family records and local Gasthof documentation confirm the date and place. The 1876 name change of Alois (Schicklgruber → Hitler) was the earlier "stroke of good fortune" that spared the future leader the rustic surname.
