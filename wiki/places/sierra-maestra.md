# Sierra Maestra

A rugged mountain range along Cuba's southeastern coast, running through modern Granma and Santiago de Cuba provinces. Densely forested and poorly roaded, it offered natural refuge for the small band of survivors from the December 1956 *Granma* landing (Fidel and Raúl Castro, Che Guevara, Camilo Cienfuegos, and roughly a dozen others) after the disastrous ambush at Alegría de Pío.

From this base the 26th of July Movement rebuilt itself into a guerrilla army over 1957–58, exploiting the terrain to evade Batista's forces, cultivate peasant support, and establish a rear-area administration (schools, a radio station, a rudimentary judiciary) that prefigured the movement's later governing style. The Sierra Maestra campaign became the founding myth of the Cuban Revolution, eclipsing in retrospective memory the movement's urban wing and the near-annihilation of the landing party.
