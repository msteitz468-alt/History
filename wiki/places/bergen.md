# Bergen

The principal town and effective capital of high-medieval [[norway|Norway]] and
its leading export port, founded c. 1070. Bergen was the entrepôt for the North
Atlantic **stockfish** (dried cod) trade from northern Norway and the island
communities, which drew the [[hanseatic-league|Hanseatic League]] to establish one
of its four great foreign Kontors at Bryggen, with a large resident German
merchant community. The town exemplifies [[scandinavian-urbanisation|Scandinavian
urbanisation]] and the Hanseatic penetration of the Nordic economy.
