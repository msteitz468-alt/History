# Western Front

Primary theater of operations in World War I (1914-1918) stretching from the North Sea to Switzerland, characterized by static trench warfare, massive artillery, and high casualties (Somme, Verdun, Passchendaele). In WWII, site of Normandy landings (1944), Battle of the Bulge, and advance into Germany.
