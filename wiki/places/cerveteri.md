# Cerveteri (Caere)

One of the twelve leading Etruscan city-states of the League of Twelve Cities, located roughly 50 km northwest of Rome and among the wealthiest and most powerful Etruscan centers during the Archaic period. Caere allied with Carthage against Greek Phocaean colonists at the naval Battle of Alalia (Battle of the Sardinian Sea, c. 540 BCE). The site is best known today for its extensive Banditaccia necropolis — a UNESCO World Heritage necropolis of monumental tumulus tombs — including the Tomb of the Reliefs (late 4th century BCE, with stucco-decorated interior imitating a house) and the celebrated Sarcophagus of the Spouses (c. 525 BCE), a masterwork of Etruscan funerary sculpture depicting a reclining married couple. Caere gradually came under Roman influence, receiving *civitas sine suffragio* (citizenship without voting rights) after conflicts with Rome, and was fully absorbed into the Roman world as Etruria was Latinized in the following centuries.
