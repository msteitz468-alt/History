# Los Alamos, New Mexico

Remote mesa site in northern New Mexico chosen in 1942 as the secret laboratory for the design and assembly of the first atomic bombs under the Manhattan Project. Directed by J. Robert Oppenheimer with military oversight by General Leslie Groves. Formerly a boys' school (Los Alamos Ranch School), it was transformed into a closed scientific city of several thousand people. Key achievements: development of the plutonium implosion design, Trinity test (16 July 1945), and the bombs dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki.

The laboratory became the model for large-scale, state-directed scientific enterprise in the United States. After the war it continued as Los Alamos National Laboratory, central to the U.S. nuclear weapons program.
