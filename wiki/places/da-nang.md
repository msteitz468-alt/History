# Da Nang

**Da Nang** is a major port city on the central Vietnamese coast, capital of South
Vietnam's I Corps military region and, from 1965, host to one of the largest US air and
naval bases of the Vietnam War, through which large numbers of American ground forces
first landed. In late March 1975, amid the collapse of I Corps following contradictory
withdrawal orders and the fall of [[places/hue|Hue]], Da Nang disintegrated in mass panic
as roughly two million refugees converged on the city; a chaotic sea evacuation on 29
March 1975 ended organized resistance in the north, a pivotal moment in the final
collapse of South Vietnam (see [[events/fall-of-saigon-1975]]).
