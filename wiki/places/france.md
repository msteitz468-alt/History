# France

Core western European polity whose history spans Celtic Gaul, Roman province, Frankish kingdom, medieval feudal monarchy, absolute monarchy under Louis XIV, revolutionary republic and empire, and modern republican state. Central to European power politics, culture (Enlightenment, language, art), military affairs, and colonial expansion.

## Geographic Description
Western European continental power with Atlantic, Channel, and Mediterranean coasts, Pyrenees, Alps, Massif Central, and major rivers (Seine, Loire, Rhone). Fertile plains supported dense population and agriculture.

## Why It Mattered
- Successive unifications and centralizations produced the model absolutist state and then the revolutionary template exported across Europe.
- Military and cultural dominance in multiple eras (Louis XIV, Napoleon, 19th-20th c. colonialism and republicanism).
- Repeated battleground and shaper of the European state system (Westphalia, Vienna, Versailles).
