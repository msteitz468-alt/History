# Starbase (Boca Chica)

SpaceX’s South Texas launch and manufacturing site for Starship/Super Heavy. Formerly Boca Chica. Site of high-bay assembly, rapid iteration, and the first integrated flight test (IFT-1, April 2023). Described by Musk as the future site of a Mars-prototype factory city.
