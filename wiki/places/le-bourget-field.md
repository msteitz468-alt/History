# Le Bourget Field

## Overview
Le Bourget Field is an airfield located northeast of Paris, France. It is historically famous as the landing site of Charles Lindbergh's historic nonstop transatlantic flight on May 21, 1927. Upon his landing in the *Spirit of St. Louis*, a crowd of approximately 150,000 people rushed the airfield, greeting the aviator and capturing the world's attention.

## History and Significance
Established in 1919, Le Bourget was Paris's primary commercial airport until the construction of Orly. During the 1927 flight, the field became the physical focus of global celebration. In World War II, it was occupied by the Luftwaffe and heavily bombed by Allied forces before being restored to commercial and military use in the postwar era. It now hosts the Paris Air Show and a museum of aviation.
