# Cleveland

Cleveland, Ohio, on Lake Erie, was the cradle of [[actors/standard-oil|Standard Oil]] and the city where the young [[actors/rockefeller-john-d|John D. Rockefeller]] grew up commercially, took his first bookkeeping job, founded his produce-commission firm, and entered oil refining in the 1860s. Its position at the junction of lake shipping and competing railroads (giving refiners rate leverage) made it one of the great refining centers of the early American petroleum industry — the setting for the [[events/cleveland-massacre-1872|Cleveland Massacre]] of 1872, in which Standard absorbed most of its local rivals.
