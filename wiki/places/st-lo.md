# St. Lô

A market town in Normandy, France, sited on high ground overlooking the Vire river at the junction of several roads, making it a key communications hub in the Norman bocage country. In the weeks following the June 1944 D-Day landings, American First Army fought a grinding battle to capture it (roughly July 7–19, 1944), advancing through dense hedgerow country ("bocage") that favored the German defense and made supporting armor nearly useless; the town was almost completely destroyed by artillery and bombing, later dubbed the "Capital of Ruins" by Samuel Beckett.

Its capture set the stage for [[hubs/warfare/world-wars-era/second-world-war/operation-cobra-1944|Operation Cobra]] (late July 1944), the concentrated air-and-armor breakout west of St. Lô that shattered the German line in Normandy and opened the way to Avranches and the liberation of Brittany and northern France.
