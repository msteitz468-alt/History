# Venice

Maritime city-state and commercial empire based in the Venetian lagoon. One of the longest-lived and wealthiest republics of the medieval and early modern Mediterranean, dominating trade between Europe and the Levant, patron of Renaissance art and architecture, and a major political player through its navy and diplomacy.
