Huế (Hué), former imperial capital of the Nguyen Dynasty in central Vietnam (Annam). Site of Quoc Hoc academy attended by Ho Chi Minh (Nguyen Tat Thanh) ~1907–08. Location of Emperor Bao Dai's abdication 30 August 1945 during the August Revolution, symbolizing end of monarchy. Historic and cultural center with citadel and royal tombs; strategic during wars.
