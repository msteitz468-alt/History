# Zurich

Neutral Swiss city where the Joyces lived during World War I (1915–1919) and to which they returned in 1940. In Zurich Joyce wrote much of *Ulysses*, founded the English Players with Claud Sykes, and experienced the relative security that allowed sustained work despite poverty and eye disease. He died there on 13 January 1941 and is buried in Fluntern Cemetery.
