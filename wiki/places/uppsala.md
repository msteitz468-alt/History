# Uppsala

The ancient cult and royal centre of the Svear in the Mälaren basin. **Old
Uppsala** (Gamla Uppsala), with its great burial mounds, was the site of the
pagan temple and sacrifices described by Adam of Bremen, and a stronghold of
paganism into the late 11th century. After [[christianization-of-scandinavia|conversion]]
it became the seat of the Swedish archbishopric (1164), making Uppsala the
ecclesiastical capital of [[sweden|Sweden]].
