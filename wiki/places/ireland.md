# Ireland

The westernmost island of the [[british-isles]], whose modern history was shaped by its long and
contested relationship with England and Britain. From the sixteenth-century Tudor conquest and the
[[plantation-of-ireland-1556-1660|Ulster plantation]] through the [[cromwellian-conquest-of-ireland|Cromwellian conquest]],
the Protestant Ascendancy, the [[act-of-union-1801|Union of 1801]], the catastrophe of the

**Atlantic façade Celtic communities (Iron Age/La Tène):** Part of the broader Celtic world with shared warrior culture and La Tène art but regional traits. "it is doubtful if the war chariot was ever seen in Ireland" (despite later vernacular literature). Distinctive shields: "The shield found in a bog at Clonoura, Co. Tipperary, in Ireland. It was composed of a thin sheet of wood covered back and front with leather". Bone trial pieces at Lough Crew, Co. Meath for working out metal designs. Part of Atlantic façade with Britain/Gaul (sling use, torcs, etc. paralleled). See [[actors/celts]], [[concepts/celtic-migrations]], [[sources/cunliffe-ancient-celts-2018]]. (Grounded verbatim in Cunliffe 2018 range.)
[[great-famine-ireland-1845|Great Famine]], the [[home-rule-movement|Home Rule]] struggle, and the
[[easter-rising-1916|1916 Rising]] and revolution, Ireland was partitioned in 1921 into the
[[irish-free-state|Irish Free State]] (later the [[republic-of-ireland|Republic]]) and
[[northern-ireland|Northern Ireland]].

Whether Ireland is best understood as a **British colony** or as an integral part of the metropolitan
United Kingdom is the central interpretive question of the field — see [[irish-historiography-revisionism]].
