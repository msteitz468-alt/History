# Gotland

A large Baltic island whose farmer-traders were the foremost Scandinavian
long-distance merchants of the Viking and early medieval period, reflected in the
island's extraordinary concentration of silver hoards. Its town of **Visby**
became a leading [[hanseatic-league|Hanseatic]] entrepôt linking the Baltic to
the North Sea. Gotland's wealth and strategic position made it a recurrent prize
in late-medieval Scandinavian and Hanseatic conflict.
