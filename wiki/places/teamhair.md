# Tara (Teamhair)

Symbolic royal and ritual centre of Ireland; seat of the High King (Ard Rí) in medieval literature and ideology (though political power more dispersed). Major prehistoric monuments + early medieval associations; central in dindshenchas and kingship texts.

## Geographic Description and History of Control
Teamhair (Tara) listed in Vol V Contents as "Teamhair (Tara) 1663"; associated "Tara brooch 1657". Central to Uí Néill high-kingship claims and legendary history.

## Why This Place Mattered
Verbatim cross-ref prose: “Teamhair; Uí Néill; Ulaid; Ulster Cycle”. In legendary contexts with triads and kingship (Beli Mawr etc.). Site of assemblies, inauguration; symbolic focus even if power dispersed.

## Historiography
Koch Vol V treats Teamhair as key royal/ritual centre in Insular textual traditions. Prehistoric monuments + medieval literary centrality; collection strong here via Koch.

- **Teamhair / Tara as royal/ritual centre**: "In the medieval pseudo-historical text Do Shuidigud Tellaich Themra (The arrangement of the hearth of Tara), Uisnech is described as the umbilicus of Ireland and, like Tara (Teamhair), was associated with pre-Christian kingship and ceremonies." "Tuathal Techtmar, who is said to have created the fifth province to ensure that the high-king of Tara was independent of the other provinces." "Mide was at the core of southern Uí Néill kingdoms". (Koch Vol V, Mide entry).

- **Teamhair in Ulster Cycle / Mythological Cycle contexts**: Linked to divisions of Ireland and síd; "the homonymous royal sites Teamhair (Tara) and Temair Lúachra might also have some bearing on the contraction and displacement of the Érainn." Featured in explanations of past events in tales like Mesca Ulad and Togail Bruidne Da Derga. (Koch Vol V, Mesca Ulad; Mythological Cycle).

- **Further refs**: Central in legendary history (Lebar Gabála Érenn), dindshenchas, and early Christian interactions (e.g. Patrick at nearby Uisnech).
