# New Orleans

Major port city at the mouth of the Mississippi River, acquired by the US in the Louisiana Purchase (1803). Site of the Battle of New Orleans (1815) in the War of 1812. Key economic and cultural center (jazz birthplace), with strategic importance in 19th-century trade and Civil War.
