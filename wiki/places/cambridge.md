# Cambridge

Cambridge, England — the university town whose colleges (especially Trinity) formed the institutional and social setting for Ramanujan’s collaboration with G. H. Hardy and J. E. Littlewood during the First World War. In 1914 the medieval architecture, High Table rituals, and cold climate were radically alien to the man from the Cauvery delta. The war emptied the colleges, turned cloisters into hospitals, and made travel and communication difficult. Yet it was here that Ramanujan produced the work that secured his permanent place in mathematics.
