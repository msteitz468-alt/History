# Sicily

Large island in the central Mediterranean, historically a strategic crossroads between Europe, North Africa, and the eastern Mediterranean. In 1943 it was the target of Operation Husky, the first major Western Allied invasion of Axis-held Europe in WWII.

**1943 Campaign significance (D'Este):** Gateway to Italy. The German defense under Hube turned a lost position into a successful delaying action and model evacuation across the Strait of Messina. The campaign demonstrated both Allied material superiority and persistent problems of coalition command and exploitation.

Geographic features relevant to 1943 operations: Mount Etna (anchoring the German defensive line), Plain of Catania (Eighth Army sector), the Strait of Messina (evacuation route), interior road net used by Patton's westward drive, southern and southeastern beaches used for landings (Gela, Licata, Scoglitti, Syracuse area).
