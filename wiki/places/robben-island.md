# Robben Island

**Robben Island** (Afrikaans: Robbeneiland) is an island in Table Bay, 6.9 km west of Cape Town. Used as a prison, leper colony, and military base for centuries; most famous as the site of the maximum-security prison where Nelson Mandela and other Rivonia Trialists were held for the majority of their sentences (1964–1982 for Mandela). Symbol of the apartheid regime’s repression and of the resilience of the liberation movement. Prisoners turned it into an informal “university.” Now a museum and UNESCO World Heritage site. Central to Meredith’s account of Mandela’s 27 years of imprisonment.
