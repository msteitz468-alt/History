# Medora (Little Missouri Badlands)

Dakota Territory (now North Dakota) cow town and center of TR's ranching life 1884–1886. Site of his ranches (Maltese Cross, Elkhorn). Harsh winters of 1886–87 ended the open-range era and TR's cattle venture. The experience forged his Western persona.
