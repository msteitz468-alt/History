# Trinity College, Cambridge

The largest and most intellectually prestigious of the Cambridge colleges. Home to Newton, and in Ramanujan’s time to Hardy, Littlewood, Russell, and many others. Ramanujan was given rooms in Whewell’s Court and later Bishop’s Hostel. He never ate in Hall because of his strict vegetarianism. The college (and the university) provided the library, the stipend, the audience, and the institutional shelter that allowed his work to be recognized and published. Hardy’s rooms in New Court and the Wren Library were central sites of the collaboration.
