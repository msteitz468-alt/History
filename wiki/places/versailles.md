# Versailles

Versailles, the palace and seat of the French court under Louis XVI, was the formal center of Bourbon monarchy visited by Jefferson during his ministry.

## Description

The seat of the French government and court rituals until the Revolution forced the royal family's removal to Paris in October 1789. Jefferson attended court functions, presented credentials, and observed the pomp and the gathering political storm.

## Significance

For Jefferson, Versailles symbolized both the grandeur and the obsolescence of hereditary absolutism. His dispatches and later recollections contrast the artificiality of court life with the emerging forces of public opinion and reform. The 1787 Assembly of Notables and the calling of the Estates-General were watched from the diplomatic community around the palace.
