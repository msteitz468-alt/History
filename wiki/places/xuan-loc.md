# Xuân Lộc

A provincial capital roughly 60 km east of Saigon on Route 1, sited to guard the principal overland approach to the South Vietnamese capital from the northeast. In April 1975, during the final North Vietnamese offensive, the ARVN 18th Infantry Division mounted an unexpectedly tenacious defense of Xuân Lộc (April 9–21, 1975), inflicting heavy losses on attacking PAVN divisions before being outflanked and overwhelmed.

The battle was the last significant stand of organized South Vietnamese resistance before the [[events/fall-of-saigon-1975|fall of Saigon]] on April 30, and its fall opened the final approach corridor for the North Vietnamese advance on the capital.
