# Warm Springs, Georgia

Site of the Georgia Warm Springs Foundation polio treatment center established and repeatedly visited by Franklin D. Roosevelt after 1924. Direct, repeated exposure to rural Southern poverty during rehabilitation stays shaped FDR's understanding of economic deprivation and informed key New Deal programs (rural electrification, agricultural relief, Social Security). FDR died here on 12 April 1945.
