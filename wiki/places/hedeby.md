# Hedeby (Haithabu)

The largest Viking-Age emporium in Scandinavia, at the base of the Jutland
peninsula near the head of the Schlei, controlling the portage between the Baltic
and North Sea trade systems. A nodal market and minting town of the Danish
kingdom from the 8th–11th centuries, paired with the Danevirke frontier rampart,
Hedeby exemplifies the rise of the first Scandinavian towns and long-distance
trade before its decline and replacement by nearby Schleswig in the 11th century.

See [[helle-cambridge-history-scandinavia-v1-2003]]; compare [[birka]], the
contemporary Mälaren emporium.
