# Cupertino, California

City in Santa Clara County, California, headquarters of Apple Inc. since the early years. Site of Apple’s campus (1 Infinite Loop and later Apple Park “Spaceship”). In Isaacson, the physical center of Jobs’s second tenure, design reviews, and the company’s transformation into a consumer electronics and services powerhouse.
