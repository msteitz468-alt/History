# Sevagram (Wardha)

Gandhi's primary ashram from the mid-1930s, located in the village of Sevagram (originally Segaon) near Wardha in central India. Chosen for its location in the "real India" of villages, it became the center for his later experiments in self-sufficient rural living, basic education, and the Constructive Programme. Many key meetings and fasts of the 1940s were conducted from or near here.

Guha uses it as a recurring site for the personal and political rhythm of Gandhi's final decades.
