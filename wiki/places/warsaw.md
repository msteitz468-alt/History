# Warsaw

Capital of Poland, historic center of Polish state and culture. Partitioned in 18th century, site of major resistance in WWII (Ghetto Uprising 1943, Warsaw Uprising 1944 with massive destruction). Rebuilt post-war under Communist rule, symbol of Polish resilience and later Solidarity movement.
