# Stockholm

The town founded in the mid-13th century (traditionally by [[folkung-dynasty|Birger
Jarl]]) commanding the outlet of Lake Mälaren to the Baltic, controlling access to
the rich Svealand interior and the iron/copper of Bergslagen. Stockholm grew into
the largest town and effective political centre of late-medieval [[sweden|Sweden]],
with a strong [[hanseatic-league|Hanseatic]]-linked German burgher element. It was
the site of the **Stockholm Bloodbath** (1520), the massacre that triggered the
revolt breaking the [[kalmar-union-1397|Kalmar Union]].
