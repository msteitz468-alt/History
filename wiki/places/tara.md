# Tara

A prehistoric ceremonial complex in modern Co. Meath, in the fertile plain of **Brega**,
that became the symbolic seat of the supreme kingship of [[places/ireland|Ireland]]. The
"**kingship of Tara**" signified "an exceptional kingship which proclaimed a king more
powerful than all others in Ireland."

Seized from the **Laigin** (Leinster) by the **[[actors/ui-neill|Uí Néill]]**, who
monopolised the title from the late seventh century and used it to claim the kingship of all
Ireland, Tara was increasingly superseded by the title *rí Érenn* ("king of Ireland") from
the tenth century. Holding it never guaranteed real power: Flann Sinna could be reckoned
king of Ireland only "between 905 and 908." **[[actors/brian-boru|Brian Bóruma]]** failed to
enter the *ferann ríg* (royal demesne) of Tara in 1000 — a symbolic limit even at the height
of his power. In legend, Tara is the seat of the goddess-queen **Medb**, whose serial
marriages "symbolized the transfer of kingship through the metaphor of marriage" (see
[[concepts/queenship-early-medieval|queenship]]).
