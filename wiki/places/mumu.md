# Mumu (Munster)

One of the five traditional provinces of Ireland; historically powerful under Eóganachta and later Dál gCais (Brian Bóruma). Rich in early Irish ecclesiastical, literary, and annalistic traditions. Central to many king-lists and saga settings.
