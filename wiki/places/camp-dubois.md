# Camp River Dubois (Camp Dubois)

Winter 1803–1804 encampment of the Corps of Discovery on the Illinois (east) side of the Mississippi near the mouth of the Wood River / Dubois, opposite St. Louis. Training, recruitment, and final preparations occurred here before the formal departure up the Missouri on 14 May 1804.

Lewis was often in St. Louis on business; Clark commanded the camp. The site marked the transition from "civilized" river settlements to the expedition proper.
