# Newport, Rhode Island

Coastal city on Aquidneck Island that became the premier summer colony of Gilded Age New York wealth. Site of the "cottages" — monumental mansions including [[places/the-breakers-newport|The Breakers]] — and of the social season in which families such as the Vanderbilts performed status. Also the waters of Harold Stirling Vanderbilt's America's Cup defenses (1930, 1934).

## Historical significance

By the 1880s–1890s Newport was the summer counterpart to Fifth Avenue. Cooper & Howe use it for Alva's Marble House, Cornelius II's Breakers, yachting spectacle against Depression-era strikes, and the long afterlife of houses as museums once private fortunes could no longer maintain them.
