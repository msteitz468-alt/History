# Victor Verster Prison

**Victor Verster Prison** (near Paarl, Western Cape) was the final place of Nelson Mandela’s imprisonment (transferred there December 1988). Conditions were markedly better than Robben Island or Pollsmoor (spacious house on prison farm, keys left in doors, garden, privacy). It became the site of intensified secret negotiations with the government. Mandela walked out through its gates on 11 February 1990 hand-in-hand with Winnie. Central to Meredith’s narrative of the transition from prisoner to president.
