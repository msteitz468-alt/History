# Ystrad Marchell (Strata Marcella)

Abbey of the Cistercian order founded 1170 by Owain Cyfeiliog, prince of Powys (buried there 1197). Colonized from Whitland. Ambitious building plan; 62 m long church with early 13th-c. stone carving of great beauty. Declined 15th–early 16th c.; buildings in ruins by 1528. Abbot John ap Rhys (1527–36) produced earliest known printed documents relating to Wales (Richard Pynson work).

Had oversight of small Cistercian nunnery at Llanllugan. Colonized Valle Crucis (Glyn-y-groes) in 1200.
