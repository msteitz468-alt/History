# Genoa

Major maritime republic and commercial power of the medieval and early modern Mediterranean, rival to Venice. Key in trade, banking (including financing Spanish empire), and establishment of colonies and trading posts (Black Sea, Crimea, North Africa, Levant). Birthplace of Christopher Columbus; instrumental in the shift of economic gravity toward the Atlantic after 1492.
