# Wall Street

The financial district in lower Manhattan, New York City, centered on the New York Stock Exchange and the headquarters of major commercial and investment banks. In 1929 it was the epicenter of the American (and to a significant degree global) securities market and credit system.

## Significance in 1929

Home to the New York Stock Exchange, National City Bank, J.P. Morgan & Co., Chase, and other institutions whose decisions and failures defined the crash and its aftermath. The physical and cultural concentration of power made events there national and international news.
