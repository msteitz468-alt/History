# Potsdam

**Potsdam**, on the Havel river just southwest of Berlin, was the principal royal
residence of the Prussian Hohenzollern kings from the 17th century, and grew into a
garrison town closely associated with the Prussian military tradition. [[frederick-the-great|
Frederick the Great]] built his summer palace **Sans Souci** at Potsdam (1745–47), which
became his preferred residence and the site of much of his private and intellectual life
away from Berlin's court. In the twentieth century the city's Cecilienhof Palace hosted
the July–August 1945 Potsdam Conference, at which the victorious Allied leaders (Truman,
Churchill/Attlee, and Stalin) determined the postwar administration and occupation zones
of defeated Germany.
