# Strait of Messina

Narrow body of water separating Sicily from the Italian mainland (Calabria). In August 1943 it was the scene of one of the most successful German evacuations of the war (Operation Lehrgang / "the Great Escape").

Under the direction of Fregattenkapitän Baron Gustav von Liebenstein and overall command of Hube, the Germans ran a scheduled ferry service that moved three divisions plus elements of a fourth (and substantial equipment) to the mainland despite Allied air and naval superiority. The operation was compared by participants to Dunkirk in morale terms but was far more successful in preserving a combat-effective force.

Allied failure to interdict the ferries or cut Route 114 in time was a central element of the "bitter victory" critique in D'Este.
