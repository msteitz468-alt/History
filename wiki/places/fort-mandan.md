# Fort Mandan

The Corps of Discovery's winter fort (1804–1805) among the Mandan and Hidatsa villages on the upper Missouri. Here the permanent party was finalized, Sacagawea and Charbonneau joined, extensive ethnographic and geographic information was gathered from traders and villagers, and the expedition prepared for the push to the Rockies in spring 1805.

A major node of pre-existing Native trade and diplomacy; the site where the "vast enterprise" most clearly intersected with established Indigenous worlds.
