# Villers-Bocage

Village in Normandy where on 13 June 1944, elements of the British 7th Armoured Division were ambushed by a single Tiger tank company under Michael Wittmann, resulting in heavy British tank losses. Iconic example of bocage (hedgerow) fighting and the effectiveness of German heavy tanks in close terrain during the Normandy campaign.
