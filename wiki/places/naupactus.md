# Naupactus

**Naupactus**, on the northern shore of the Corinthian Gulf (modern Nafpaktos), was a
strategically valuable harbor town controlling the gulf's western entrance. Following the
[[events/third-messenian-war-464bce|Third Messenian War]] (c. 464–455 BCE), Athens settled
the defeated Messenian helots who had been allowed to leave the Peloponnese under truce at
Naupactus, creating a loyal Athenian outpost and naval base at a critical strategic
chokepoint. The Messenians of Naupactus subsequently fought as valued Athenian allies
during the [[events/peloponnesian-war|Peloponnesian War]], and the city served as a
forward Athenian naval station for operations in the Corinthian Gulf and against Sparta's
western allies.
