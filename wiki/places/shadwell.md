# Shadwell

Shadwell was the Albemarle County plantation on the Rivanna River where Thomas Jefferson was born on April 13, 1743. Named by his father Peter Jefferson after the London parish of his own childhood. Peter Jefferson had patented land in the area in the 1730s–40s; Shadwell became the family seat after the move from Tuckahoe. Thomas Jefferson spent much of his boyhood here until sent to school with the Maury family and then to the College of William and Mary.

The original house burned in 1770 (Jefferson was then living in Williamsburg). He inherited the property and continued to farm it, though Monticello became his primary residence after 1772. Shadwell remained part of the Jefferson landholdings and was associated with his early formation as a Piedmont planter.
