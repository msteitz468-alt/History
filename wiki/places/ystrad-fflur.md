# Ystrad-fflur (Strata Florida)

Cistercian abbey in Ceredigion, Wales, founded 1164 by Robert Fitz Stephen and given new impetus by the Lord Rhys of Deheubarth. Became the mausoleum of the princely Deheubarth dynasty. Significant custodian of native Welsh cultural tradition; site of the lost Latin work that formed the basis of Brut y Tywysogyon.

Damaged by fire in 1286 and during Edward I occupation in 1294–5. Rebuilding interrupted; became a strong point for Henry IV forces in 1407 during Owain Glyndŵr rebellion. Suppressed 1539.
