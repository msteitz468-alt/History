# Susa

Ancient city in southwestern Iran (modern Shush), one of the capitals of the Achaemenid Empire and a major center of Elamite civilization for centuries before. Important administrative and ceremonial center under Darius I and successors (apadana palace, royal inscriptions); also significant in Elamite, Seleucid, Parthian, and Sasanian periods. Biblical Shushan.

## Why It Mattered
Strategic location at the edge of the Mesopotamian lowlands and Iranian plateau; key node in long-distance trade and imperial control; rich archaeological record (including the Code of Hammurabi stele taken there by Elamites).
