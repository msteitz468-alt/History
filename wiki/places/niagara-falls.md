# Niagara Falls

**Niagara Falls**, on the border between New York State and Ontario, became in the 1890s the proving ground of large-scale hydroelectric power. The Niagara Falls Power Company (with the Cataract Construction Company, backed by an international financial syndicate including [[actors/morgan-john-pierpont|J. P. Morgan]] interests) chose, after extended deliberation, [[actors/tesla-nikola|Tesla]]'s and [[actors/westinghouse-george|Westinghouse]]'s **[[concepts/alternating-current|polyphase AC]]** system over direct current and compressed air.

The [[events/niagara-falls-power-project-1895|Niagara Falls power project]] (first power delivered 1895; transmission to Buffalo 1896) was the decisive demonstration that AC could harness a great natural energy source and distribute it over distance, confirming the outcome of the [[processes/war-of-the-currents|War of the Currents]] and inaugurating the modern era of [[processes/electrification|electrification]].
