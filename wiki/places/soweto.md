# Soweto

**Soweto** (South Western Townships) is a large township southwest of Johannesburg created under apartheid to house Black residents of Johannesburg and the Witwatersrand. Site of the 1976 student uprising that began with protests against Afrikaans-medium instruction and became a nationwide revolt. Symbol of Black urban resistance and the human cost of Group Areas and Bantu Education policies. Featured prominently in Meredith’s account of the resurgence of internal resistance in the 1970s and the “Free Mandela” campaign.
