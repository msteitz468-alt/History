# Tournai

A city in Roman Belgica Secunda (modern Belgium) that served as an early
capital and royal center of the [[merovingian-franks|Merovingian dynasty]]
before the Franks' expansion into Gaul proper.

## Geographic and Historical Description

Tournai was the seat of the Salian Frankish king Childeric I in the later
fifth century, and it was from Tournai that his son Clovis I began the
campaigns that unified the Franks and conquered most of Gaul, founding the
Merovingian kingdom. Childeric's richly furnished tomb, discovered at
Tournai in 1653, remains one of the most important archaeological finds for
understanding early Merovingian kingship, containing weapons, jewelry, and
a signet ring bearing his name.

## Significance

As the point of origin for Clovis's unification of the Franks, Tournai
holds a foundational place in Merovingian and, by extension, early French
and Belgian history, even though the kingdom's political center of gravity
shifted decisively southward (to Paris, Soissons, and beyond) once Clovis's
conquests were complete.
