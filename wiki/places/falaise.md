# Falaise

Site in Normandy, France, of the Falaise Pocket (August 1944), where Allied forces (primarily Canadian, Polish, US, British) encircled and destroyed much of the German Seventh Army and Fifth Panzer Army during the Normandy breakout. One of the largest encirclements of the war in the West, leading to the liberation of Paris and pursuit across France.
