# Pixar

Originally the Lucasfilm Computer Division, acquired by Steve Jobs in 1986 after being spun out. Renamed Pixar. Under Jobs’s ownership and with Ed Catmull and John Lasseter, it became the leader in computer-generated animation, producing *Toy Story* (1995) and a string of hits. The 2006 acquisition by Disney made Jobs Disney’s largest shareholder and provided capital and credibility during Apple’s resurgence. In Isaacson, Pixar is presented as the redemptive “technology meets art” counterpoint to NeXT and the first Apple era.
