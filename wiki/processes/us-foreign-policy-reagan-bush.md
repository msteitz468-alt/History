# US Foreign Policy under Reagan and Bush

The evolution of American strategy from the "second Cold War" buildup through the end of the Cold War, including SDI, arms control, support for anti-communist movements, and management of the Soviet collapse and German unification.

Detailed claims populated from Byrne, *Ronald Reagan: An Intellectual Biography* (2018) + CHCW Vol. III.

## Intellectual and Moral Drivers (Byrne 2018)
Reagan rejected containment’s tacit acceptance of communism in its sphere and détente’s accommodation (“what a turkey and its farmer have, until Thanksgiving”). Applied 1930s appeasement lessons directly (Rhineland/Austria/Munich → Poland; “evil cannot be negotiated with”; curbing military instigates war). Conferences (Yalta, Vienna, Glassboro, 1979 SALT) followed by aggression (Hungary, Berlin Wall, Czechoslovakia, Afghanistan) proved useless. Détente produced US military deconstruction (real defense spending -42% Nixon/Ford) while Soviets reached parity (Brezhnev: achieved more with détente than “mailed fist”).

Cold War framed as moral holy war/good vs. evil (collectivist tyranny/atheism vs. individual freedom/rights; same battle as under Hitlerism/Kaisers; humanitarian not merely economic). “Freedom is never more than one generation away from extinction... This is the last stand on Earth.” Beilenson: “The Soviet Union retreats when faced with strength.” Marx/Lenin: “One must conquer.” Soviet material weakness (US GNP nearly twice Russia’s; ag productivity fractions; food imports) recognized as lever. SDI “product of Reagan’s imagination”; psychological + economic strain (“they can’t keep up”); Gorbachev “hysteria,” “obsession”; Malia “principle factor” triggering perestroika; Andropov on defense burden.

“Peace through strength”: “Armaments do not cause war... Peace loving nations must match their weaponry.” “America’s industrial and economic strength, translated into military potential, [is] the single guarantee of peace.” Hedgehog knowledge of one big thing: communism “a form of insanity—a temporary aberration... contrary to human nature” that “will one day disappear.” 1979 Afghanistan vindicated view.

Universal freedom (all people want it; 1989 “wildfire” across Eastern Europe + Tiananmen “Goddess of democracy”; not Western imperialism) replaced containment/rollback. Ideas contributed to post-1989 policy (Clinton “Iraq Liberation Act”; Bush framing freedom as “God’s gift”). 

Tensions: defense spending grew gov % of income (27.9% Carter end → 28.7% Reagan 1988 Q1 per Mises); welfare per capita higher constant $; deficits ballooned; yet “guns and butter” justified as necessary for liberty (“National defense is not a threat to peace; it is the guarantee of peace with freedom”).
