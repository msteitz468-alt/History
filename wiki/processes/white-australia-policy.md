# White Australia Policy

The set of laws, administrative practices and cultural assumptions by which the
[[commonwealth-of-australia|Commonwealth of Australia]] sought to maintain a
racially homogeneous British settler society from 1901 until its formal
dismantling in 1973. One of the founding pillars of the new nation alongside the
[[australian-settlement]].

## Definition and Scope

Enacted in the first months of Federation through the Immigration Restriction
Act 1901 (the dictation test), the Pacific Island Labourers Act 1901 (deporting
Melanesian "Kanaka" labourers from the Queensland sugar industry), and
associated naturalisation and franchise exclusions. It expressed a "race
nationalism" that linked national living standards, labour protection and racial
purity. It is **not** identical to Indigenous policy, though both flowed from the
same racial order: Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people were
simultaneously denied the federal vote and welfare benefits.

## Causal Drivers

- Labour-movement fear of cheap non-white labour undercutting the
  [[australian-settlement|arbitrated wage]].
- Late-nineteenth-century anti-Chinese agitation on the goldfields (continuity
  from [[australian-gold-rushes-1851]]).
- British-derived racial science and imperial anxiety about Asian proximity.

## Major Phases

1. **Foundation (1901–1914):** dictation test, Pacific Islander deportation.
2. **Interwar consolidation:** tightened administration; assisted British migration.
3. **Post-WW2 strain (1945–1966):** mass European immigration under Arthur
   Calwell broadened "acceptable" whiteness to southern Europeans while holding
   the colour line; Cold War and Asian decolonisation made the policy a
   diplomatic liability in the [[australian-pacific-relations|Asia-Pacific]].
4. **Dismantling (1966–1973):** Holt-era relaxation of non-European entry;
   the Whitlam government's 1973 abolition of race as an immigration criterion.

## Geographic Spread

National; principal friction at the northern frontier (Queensland sugar belt,
Torres Strait) and in relations with China, Japan, India and the decolonising
Asia-Pacific.

## Interaction

Underpinned the [[australian-settlement]] (protected labour for a white
citizenry); contradicted Australia's wartime and Cold War alliances in Asia;
its collapse enabled [[multiculturalism-australia]].

## End Conditions

Formally ended 1973; succeeded by official multiculturalism. Its legacy remains
contested in the "[[history-wars-australia|history wars]]."
