# California Archaic

## Definition and Scope

Holocene forager trajectory of aboriginal **California**: societies that remained “technically hunter-gatherers” at extraordinarily high population densities because of environmental richness and the inappropriateness of available domesticates. Part of [[paleoindian-and-archaic-north-america]]. Concurrent with [[pre-columbian-american-population]] debates (Snow’s density estimates).

## Causal Drivers

- Mosaic of microenvironments and small territories
- Acorn storage, shellfish, marine mammals, fishing
- Formal trade/alliance mechanisms redistributing local specialties
- Language-family layering (Hokan, Uto-Aztecan, Penutian, etc.)

## Major Phases

### Demography at contact

1492 density **probably over one person per square kilometer** → total **over 400,000** (older conservative estimates still 300,000–350,000). About **500** culturally diverse independent communities speaking nearly **50** languages in **at least six families**. Kroeber’s “fish trap theory” is insufficient; Snow stresses microenvironments, territorial husbandry, storage, and regulated trade without far-ranging congregating or informal Eastern-style sharing.

### Sequence (Snow)

- Discount Texas Street, Calico, Santa Rosa, Laguna Beach/Los Angeles skulls as pre-Paleo.
- Accept **Borax Lake** and other Clovis/Paleo sites; **San Dieguito** (late Paleo/Early Archaic) to ~**6000 B.C.**
- Archaic clear by **5500 B.C.**: **Encinitas** (manos/metates, shellfish, fewer points/mammals/fish) to A.D. 1000+ → Cuyamaca → historic Yuman (Hokan).
- **Luis Rey I–II** intrusive → Shoshonean (Uto-Aztecan).
- **Campbell** tradition displaces Encinitas on Santa Barbara coast ~**3000 B.C.** → Canalino/**Chumash** maritime culture.
- Central CA: **Windmiller–Cosumnes–Hotchkiss** → historic Penutian (Yokuts, Miwok, Wintu) with acorn focus and charmstones.

### Exchange

Despite “tribelet” stereotype, formal social/political mechanisms maintained and transcended ethnic boundaries. Archaic California developed **large-scale trade and exchange similar to Adena–Hopewell** in the Eastern Woodlands (specifics and antiquity still unclear). `analogous_to: [[adena-culture]]` · `analogous_to: [[hopewell-culture]]`

## Geographic Spread

California culture area from southern deserts through Central Valley to north coast; coastal maritime specializations (Chumash) vs interior acorn economies.

## Interaction

`preceded_by: [[clovis-culture]]` · `part_of: [[paleoindian-and-archaic-north-america]]` · `contrasts_with: [[northwest-coast-archaic]]` (different path to complexity) · concurrent with high pre-Columbian population estimates

## End Conditions

Archaic (forager) adaptations persist to contact; colonial disruption is abrupt and demographic (beyond Snow’s ch. 3 scope).
