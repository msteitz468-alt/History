# Nazi Racial Hygiene Program (1933–1939)

The peacetime application of eugenic "racial hygiene" to German society:
compulsory sterilization, pronatalist engineering of marriage and
reproduction, and the persecution of Sinti and Roma, Black Germans, and
homosexual men. Distinct from, but interlocking with, the
[[persecution-of-german-jews-1933-1939|persecution of the Jews]]. Based on
Evans, *The Third Reich in Power* (2005), ch. 6 (read on the main thread).

## Compulsory sterilization

The **Law for the Prevention of Hereditarily Diseased Offspring** (14 July
1933) prescribed compulsory sterilization for "congenital feeble-mindedness,"
schizophrenia, manic-depression, hereditary epilepsy, Huntington's chorea,
hereditary deafness/blindness/deformity, and severe alcoholism, administered
through 181 Hereditary Health Courts (a lawyer and two doctors). The scale was
enormous: 84,500 applications in 1934 alone with >90% approved and appeals
nearly always dismissed (3,559 of ~4,000 failed in 1934); over 50,000
sterilizations in each of the first four years; **over 360,000 total by 1945,
almost all before September 1939**; ~2,000 deaths from the operations (0.5%,
overwhelmingly women). Three-quarters of orders cited "congenital
feeble-mindedness" — an elastic category that swept in prostitution as "moral
feeble-mindedness." Two-thirds of the sterilized were asylum inmates, and the
more curable a patient, the more likely sterilization (enabling cost-cutting
discharge). Evans's core argument: the program targeted **social deviance and
the underclass, not demonstrable heredity** — an instrument to "crush those
areas of society that did not conform to the Nazi ideal." Prisoners were
over-represented (~5,400 by December 1939); the physically handicapped were
largely spared (<1% of the sterilized) for economic and war-veteran-visibility
reasons, though banned from secondary education from March 1935.

International context Evans insists on: 28 US states had sterilization laws
(~30,000 sterilized by 1939; eugenicist Harry Laughlin got an honorary
Heidelberg doctorate in 1936), Sweden sterilized ~63,000 (1935–75), Norway
40,000, Denmark 6,000. Pre-war German sterilization was not primarily racial —
its victims were overwhelmingly "Aryan" Germans. **The decisive divergence
came with the war, when the regime moved from sterilizing social deviants to
murdering them.**

## Reproduction engineered

Pronatalism as racial policy: the feminist movement dissolved (1933);
Scholtz-Klink's National Socialist Womanhood; Mother's Honour Crosses (bronze
for four children, gold for eight; three million invested May 1939);
marriage loans conditional on hereditary-political vetting; the Marriage
Health Law (18 October 1935) banning marriage of the "hereditarily ill"; the
1938 Marriage Law adding divorce for "premature infertility" or refusal to
procreate; Himmler's *Lebensborn* homes (from 1936 — only ~8,000 births, and
his 115,690 married SS men averaged 1.1 children each). Sanctioned abortions
were cut from ~35,000 to under 2,000 a year — but eugenic abortion was allowed
from 1935 and a Lüneburg court legalized abortion for Jewish women in November
1938: the same act read through opposite racial signs. The birth-rate rose
from 14.7/1,000 (1933) to 20.4 (1939), but Evans attributes this mainly to
economic recovery and abortion enforcement; the secular decline in family size
continued unabated.

## Sinti and Roma

~26,000 "Gypsies" in Germany; policy escalated from inherited police
harassment (Bavaria's 1926 law and 20,000-file index) through municipal camps
(Marzahn, July 1936: 600 people, two latrines, 40% scabies by 1939) to
Himmler's decree of **8 December 1938** — compulsory registration and
"racial-biological examination" by Robert Ritter's institute, racially coded
identity cards, marriage denial — a decree that already used the phrase "the
final solution of the Gypsy question." Ritter inverted antisemitic dogma:
"pure" Gypsies harmless, the mixed-race majority a criminal danger.

## Black Germans

The so-called "Rhineland bastards" — in fact ~500–600 children mostly of
consensual unions with French colonial soldiers, not the mass rapes of
nationalist propaganda — were sterilized in 1937 **on Hitler's authority
alone**, without legal basis, through a secret Gestapo commission organized by
Rüdin, Lenz, and Gross; girls as young as twelve underwent tubal ligation.

## Homosexual men

Paragraph 175 was expanded on 28 June 1935 ("unnatural sexual act," no proof
of penetration required, applied retroactively); the Reich Central Office for
the Combating of Homosexuality and Abortion followed (October 1936).
Convictions leapt from ~4,000 (1933–35) to over 22,000 (1936–38); ~50,000
arrested across the Third Reich. Evans notes prosecution rates comparable to
1950s West Germany or Britain — the murderous difference was what followed:
5,000–15,000 sent to the camps (pink triangle), with a death rate around 50%
(2,500–7,500 dead), plus coerced "voluntary" castrations (>2,000 in the
camps). Himmler told SS leaders in 1937 that homosexuals found in the SS would
be sent to a camp and "shot while trying to escape." Lesbians were not
criminalized or systematically persecuted, though vulnerable as "asocials."

## The 1933 Opening Assault on Sexual Reform and "Deviance" (Evans, 2003)

Evans's Vol. I ([[evans-coming-of-third-reich-2003]]) documents the program's
1933 launch conditions. On 6 May 1933 the NS Students' League — arriving with
a brass band — ransacked Magnus Hirschfeld's Institute for Sexual Science in
Berlin (12,000–20,000 books); on 10 May stormtroopers burned some 10,000 of
its volumes on the Opera Square with Hirschfeld's bust thrown on the pyre
("Then hopefully he'll snuff it without us; then we won't need to string him
up or beat him to death" — the 65-year-old, abroad and ill, died in French
exile in 1935). The whole Weimar sex-reform world was dismantled within
months: Wilhelm Reich and Helene Stöcker exiled, clinics closed; Hamburg
police arrested hundreds of prostitutes under the Reichstag fire decree
("for the protection of people and state" — Evans: an illustration of "how
the decree could be used as legitimation for almost any kind of repressive
action"); the 26 May 1933 amendment of the 1927 VD law recriminalized
prostitution and re-banned abortion publicity.

Simultaneously, poverty itself was criminalized: compulsory labour schemes
expanded (Farm Aid took 145,000 off the register by August 1933); Hamburg's
Police Shelter deterred the homeless (overnight stays down a quarter,
1930–33); a Prussian decree of 1 June 1933 suppressed public begging;
officials urged camps for vagrants and the "work-shy." Berlin's police
smashed the "ring association" crime networks (May–June 1933) and raided the
heavily Jewish Scheunenviertel on 9 June — the Jewish-crime association
"existed almost wholly in the minds of the Nazis themselves" (Evans). For the
Weimar-era intellectual and institutional preconditions — Binding & Hoche,
Viernstein's criminal-biology index, the blocked sterilization drafts — see
[[racial-hygiene-eugenics-1890-1933]]; Evans's continuity verdict: the Third
Reich applied the accumulated apparatus "with a draconian severity that few
under Weimar had even dreamed of."

## Interaction and trajectory

The program supplied the machinery, personnel, and moral desensitization —
"state-sponsored violence against the body on a systematic scale" — that the
regime would later turn on the Jews and, in the wartime "euthanasia" murders,
on the sterilized categories themselves. See [[the-holocaust]],
[[nazi-concentration-camps]], [[persecution-of-german-jews-1933-1939]],
[[volksgemeinschaft]] (the community these exclusions defined).

## End Conditions

Radicalized, not ended, by the war: sterilization gave way to the T4 murder
program (1939–), and the Ritter registration fed the wartime deportation and
genocide of the Sinti and Roma.

## Historiography

Evans synthesizes Bock (sterilization), Burleigh/Wippermann (*The Racial
State*), Zimmermann (Roma), and the comparative eugenics literature; the
"continuity vs. rupture" question (how directly 1930s eugenics led to
genocide) remains a live debate he handles by stressing the wartime threshold.
