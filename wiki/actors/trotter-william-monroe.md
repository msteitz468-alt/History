# William Monroe Trotter (1872–1934)

Harvard-educated editor of the *Boston Guardian*; militant civil-rights journalist who had supported Wilson in 1912 and then confronted him over federal segregation. Cox uses Trotter's White House interviews (and Wilson's angry reaction) as a set-piece of the administration's racial policy and of Black disillusionment with Wilsonian democracy rhetoric — the same "light withdrawn" pattern Whittier's epigraph frames for the broader book.
