# Gunther E. Rothenberg

Gunther E. Rothenberg (Gunther Erich Rothenberg, 1923–) is a military historian best known for his institutional and doctrinal studies of the armies of the French Revolutionary and Napoleonic Wars. His 1978 book *The Art of Warfare in the Age of Napoleon* provides a systematic survey of the transformation from *ancien régime* limited professional warfare to mass national armies, with detailed treatment of French organization under Napoleon, the "soldier's trade," tactical innovations, and the structure and weaknesses of coalition opponents. He also wrote on the Habsburg Military Border.

Rothenberg taught courses in the history of land warfare at the University of New Mexico, Southern Illinois University, and Purdue University. He acknowledges the influence of the Department of War Studies at the Royal Military Academy, Sandhurst (particularly David Chandler, Christopher Duffy, and Antony Brett-James) and Professor Peter Paret.
