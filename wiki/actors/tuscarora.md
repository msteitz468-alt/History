# Tuscarora

Iroquoian people of North Carolina who, after the Tuscarora War, largely migrated north and were incorporated as the **sixth nation** of the [[haudenosaunee-confederacy]] (1722).

## Overview

Once-prosperous North Carolina Tuscaroras faced settler occupation of allies’ lands, Virginia trader tensions, and Iroquois/Conestoga retaliatory raids for Bacon-era Susquehannock treatment. 1710 delegates to Pennsylvania made peace with Five Nations and Conestogas as Swiss/German immigrants arrived. 1711 Lower Tuscaroras rose to drive out newcomers; defeated by South Carolina, Virginia, Yamasees, and Cherokees intervening for North Carolina. By 1713, **1,500–2,000 Lower Tuscaroras** moved north among the Iroquois; incorporated as sixth nation 1722—thereafter “Six Nations.” Upper Tuscaroras remaining became Virginia/NC tributaries; most later moved to mountains or joined Six Nations kin. Militantly traditionalist Upper Tuscarora incorporation reinforced anti-European tendencies especially missionary rejection. Southern remnants and Upper groups suffered Catawba raids and settler crowding. Revolution: many Tuscaroras with [[oneida]] resistance to full British commitment; Confederacy badly divided.
