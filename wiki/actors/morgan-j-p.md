# J. P. Morgan

Financier (1837–1913). Head of J.P. Morgan & Co. Organized the 1901 syndicate that purchased Carnegie Steel and formed United States Steel, the first billion-dollar corporation. Central figure in Gilded Age railroad finance and the 1907 banking crisis. Carnegie's sale to Morgan marked the transition from individual industrial empire to modern corporate finance capital.
