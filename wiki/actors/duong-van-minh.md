# Duong Van Minh ("Big Minh")

Senior South Vietnamese general and leader of the November 1963 coup that overthrew and killed President Ngo Dinh Diem and his brother Nhu. Later served briefly as head of state and in other roles during the chaotic post-Diem period.

## Role in the Coup

Coordinated the plot with fellow generals Tran Van Don and Le Van Kim. Maintained contact with the U.S. through CIA operative Lucien Conein. After the successful seizure of power, Minh's junta proved unable to stabilize the country; he was himself overthrown by Nguyen Khanh in January 1964. Later testimony implicated him in ordering the murder of the captured Diem and Nhu.
