# Hermann Hoth

German Wehrmacht general, commander of the 4th Panzer Army on the Eastern Front, whose forces drove alongside [[actors/paulus-friedrich|Friedrich Paulus's]] 6th Army toward Stalingrad and the Don-Volga crossings in the summer and autumn of 1942, contributing to the encirclement of the city before being reassigned south to cover the Caucasus flank. After the Soviet counteroffensive (Operation Uranus) trapped the 6th Army at Stalingrad in November 1942, Hoth's 4th Panzer Army spearheaded the December 1942 relief attempt, Operation Wintergewitter (Winter Storm), which came within roughly 30 miles of the encircled army before being halted and driven back by Soviet forces, sealing the 6th Army's fate. Hoth was later convicted at the "High Command Trial" in the postwar Nuremberg proceedings for war crimes connected to the treatment of Soviet prisoners and civilians under his command, and served several years of a fifteen-year sentence.
