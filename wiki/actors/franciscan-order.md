# Franciscan Order (Order of Friars Minor)

## Overview

The **Franciscan Order**, founded by [[francis-of-assisi|Francis of Assisi]] and approved in outline by [[innocent-iii|Innocent III]] c.1209, was a **mendicant** brotherhood dedicated to poverty, itinerant preaching, and urban ministry. Together with the [[dominican-order|Dominicans]] it transformed high-medieval pastoral care, university theology, and the church's response to dissent.
