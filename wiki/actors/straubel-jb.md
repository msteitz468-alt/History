# JB Straubel

Co-founder and longtime Chief Technology Officer of Tesla. Key technical figure in battery pack architecture, powertrain, and early Roadster development. Remained at Tesla through the 2008 crisis and production ramp before later roles in energy storage and Redwood Materials.
