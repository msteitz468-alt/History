# Sweyn Forkbeard (Sven Tjugeskæg)

King of [[denmark|Denmark]] (986–1014), son of [[harald-bluetooth|Harald
Bluetooth]], whom he overthrew. He intensified Danish raiding and conquest in
England, exacting Danegeld, and after the St Brice's Day massacre (1002) and
sustained campaigning was recognised as king of England in late 1013, dying weeks
later (February 1014). His success laid the foundation for his son
[[cnut-the-great|Cnut the Great]]'s [[north-sea-empire|North Sea empire]].
