# Kingdom of East Anglia

**East Anglia** — the kingdom of the East Angles (the North Folk and South Folk of
Norfolk and Suffolk) — reached its zenith under [[raedwald-of-east-anglia|Rædwald]]
(d. c. 624), whom Bede counts among the holders of [[bretwalda|overlordship]] and whose
court is most plausibly connected with the great ship-burial at Sutton Hoo. Rædwald's
defeat of [[æthelfrith-of-bernicia|Æthelfrith]] at the River Idle (c. 616) installed
[[edwin-of-northumbria|Eadwine]] in Northumbria.

Thereafter East Anglia was repeatedly overshadowed by [[mercia|Mercia]] — several of its
kings fell fighting [[penda-of-mercia|Penda]] — and it lay within the Mercian and then the
West Saxon hegemonies until the Viking *micel here* conquered it and killed King Edmund in
869.
