# Desiderius, King of the Lombards (c. 720 – after 774)

**Desiderius** was the last king of the Lombards (r. 756–774), deposed by
[[charlemagne|Charles the Great]] in the [[carolingian-conquest-of-lombard-italy-774|conquest
of Lombard Italy]]. His daughters married into the leading dynasties of the West — one to
Charles, one to Duke [[tassilo-iii|Tassilo III]] of Bavaria — making him briefly the
father-in-law of the two greatest princes north of the Alps.

## The Breach and the Fall

Per [[sources/nelson-king-and-emperor-2019|Janet Nelson]]:

- The alliance, brokered by Charles's mother Bertrada in 770, collapsed when Charles
  **repudiated Desiderius's daughter** in 771–2 — "the most blatant of insults to her and
  her family's honour."
- When Charles's nephews (sons of his dead brother Carloman) fled to Desiderius's court,
  Charles offered **14,000 solidi** for them. Nelson's distinctive reading: this was not a
  payment for papal territories but "a pair of wergilds — the price of two princes, 7,000
  solidi apiece" — an attempt to buy custody of the rival princes. Desiderius refused:
  "What father does deals with the man who has repudiated his daughter?"
- That refusal forced the war. **Pavia fell in 774 not by assault but to epidemic** —
  Cathwulf says Charles took it "without any shedding of blood." Charles assumed the title
  *rex Langobardorum*, leaving Lombard law and institutions largely intact.
- Desiderius and his queen Ansa were exiled to the monastery of **Corbie**, where he last
  appears "occupied in prayers and fasts and good works until the day of his death." His
  daughter was "terminally expunged from memory and from the historical record."
