# Laurene Powell Jobs

**Laurene Powell Jobs** (born 6 November 1963) is an American billionaire businesswoman, philanthropist, and widow of Steve Jobs. Married 1991; mother of Reed, Erin, and Eve Jobs. Described in Isaacson as the “sensible anchor” in Jobs’s life. Provided stability during the NeXT/Pixar and return years; present during final illness and reflections. Post-2011 leads Emerson Collective and manages significant philanthropic and investment activities.
