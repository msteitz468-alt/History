# The Asante Empire (Ashanti)

The **Asante (Ashanti) Empire** was the most powerful **Akan** state of the West African forest — a
gold-rich confederacy centred on **Kumasi** that dominated what is now Ghana for two centuries. Documented
for the wiki through the **UNESCO [[gha-vol5-16th-18th-century|General History of Africa, Vol. V]]**
(ch. 14, Boahen), which treats it as a leading case of the militarized states that rose with the early-modern
gold and Atlantic economy.

## Foundation: the Golden Stool

Asante was forged around **1701** when **Osei Tutu**, with the counsel of the priest **Okomfo Anokye**,
united the Akan chiefdoms and threw off the overlordship of **Denkyira**. Its symbol and constitutional
core was the **Golden Stool (Sika Dwa Kofi)** — said to have descended from the sky to embody the soul of
the Asante nation — uniting the previously independent chiefdoms under the **Asantehene** at Kumasi while
preserving their internal autonomy: a sophisticated federal kingship.

## Power: gold, kola, and the Atlantic

Asante's wealth rested on the **gold** of the Akan forest (the source that had fed the medieval
[[trans-saharan-trade|trans-Saharan trade]]) and on **kola nuts**, traded north to the Sudan, and on the
**Atlantic trade** to the south — exchanging gold and **enslaved people** (war captives) for European
firearms, which in turn powered further conquest. Under **Opoku Ware** (r. 1720–50) and his successors the
empire expanded across the region, developing an elaborate bureaucracy, road network, and the famous
**kente** cloth and goldweight arts.

## Decline and end

In the nineteenth century Asante collided with **British** power on the coast (allied with the Fante). A
series of **Anglo-Asante wars** culminated in the British sack of Kumasi and the **annexation of Asante in
1901**, ending its independence — though the Asantehene and the Golden Stool remain potent symbols in
modern Ghana.

## Significance

Asante is the great example of a **West African forest empire** built on gold and the Atlantic trade, with
a distinctive **federal constitution** (the Golden Stool) and a rich material culture — a sophisticated
indigenous state that long resisted European encroachment.
