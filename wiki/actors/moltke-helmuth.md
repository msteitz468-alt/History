# Helmuth von Moltke the Elder (1800–1891)

Chief of the Prussian General Staff (1857–1888) and architect of the **Prussian/German military
system** that closed Doughty & Gruber's volume. Moltke fused the **railway, the telegraph, the
breech-loading rifle, and a professional General Staff** into a method of warfare built on
**mobilization speed, decentralized execution under a common intent ("Auftragstaktik"), and
concentric convergence on the battlefield** — "march divided, strike united." His system delivered
the swift, decisive victories of the **[[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/wars-of-german-unification/wars-of-german-unification|Wars of German Unification]]**: **[[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/wars-of-german-unification/battle-of-koniggratz|Königgrätz]]** (1866) over Austria and **[[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/wars-of-german-unification/battle-of-sedan|Sedan]]** (1870) over France, the political
work of his partner [[otto-von-bismarck|Bismarck]].
