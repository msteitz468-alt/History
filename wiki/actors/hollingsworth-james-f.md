# James F. Hollingsworth

U.S. Army major general, senior American military adviser to the South Vietnamese Third Corps region during the 1972 North Vietnamese Easter Offensive. When North Vietnamese forces besieged the provincial capital of An Loc, Hollingsworth directed the American advisory effort and coordinated the massive B-52 and tactical airstrikes that broke up North Vietnamese armor and infantry assaults on the town, a role widely credited by historians of the battle as decisive to the South Vietnamese garrison's survival through weeks of siege in the absence of significant American ground forces (Vietnamization having already withdrawn most U.S. combat units). An Loc's successful defense, driven substantially by Hollingsworth's coordination of firepower, is regarded as a major test — and partial vindication — of Vietnamization's reliance on South Vietnamese ground forces backed by American air power.
