# Joseph Stalin

Soviet leader (1924–1953). Consolidated power through purges and terror; industrialized via Five-Year Plans at enormous human cost; signed pact with Hitler then led USSR through the Great Patriotic War to victory; imposed Soviet control over Eastern Europe after 1945, initiating the Cold War. One of the most consequential and brutal figures of the 20th century.

See: [[world-war-ii-1939-1945]], [[operation-barbarossa-1941]], [[battle-of-stalingrad-1942-1943]], [[cold-war]].
