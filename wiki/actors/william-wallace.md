# William Wallace

Scottish knight and leader of resistance against English occupation in the late 13th/early 14th c.; victor at Stirling Bridge; Guardian of Scotland; captured and executed 1305. Became legendary symbol of Scottish independence.

## Historiography
Koch Vol V entry positions Wallace in context of Celtic countries' political figures (alongside Tone, Glyndŵr etc.). Contributed to long-term Scottish identity; cross to Union with Scotland (1707).
