# Oneida

One of the original Five Nations of the [[haudenosaunee-confederacy]]. Salisbury’s load-bearing colonial note is their Revolutionary alignment opposite most Mohawks.

## Overview

### Seventeenth century

1615 French and [[huron-wendat]] sealed alliance with an attack on an Oneida or Onondaga village. 1660 Oneida attacks on Piscataways challenged Susquehannock protectorship, drawing Maryland military aid—part of mid-century Iroquois–Susquehannock struggle. Shared Confederacy experience of Beaver Wars, Covenant Chain, Grand Settlement neutrality, and southern mourning wars.

### Revolution

While most Iroquois initially sought neutrality, **Oneidas under Congregationalist missionary Samuel Kirkland generally favored the Patriots**, while most [[mohawk]] followed Joseph Brant for the British. Oneida/Tuscarora resistance was the main brake on full Confederacy commitment to Britain (fall 1776–winter 1777). Green: Oneidas and Tuscaroras allied with the U.S.; at war’s end Confederacy towns devastated regardless of alignment. Oneida communities persist in New York and Wisconsin.
