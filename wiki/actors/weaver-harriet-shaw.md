# Harriet Shaw Weaver

English editor, publisher, and patron (1876–1961). Editor of *The Egoist*. Joyce's most important financial supporter from 1914 onward and his literary executor. Her steady, often anonymous subsidies made the completion of *Ulysses* and *Finnegans Wake* possible. She handled his affairs after his death and preserved the archive that enabled Ellmann's biography.
