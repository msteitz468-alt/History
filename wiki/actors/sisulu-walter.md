# Walter Sisulu

**Walter Max Ulyate Sisulu** (1912–2003) was a South African anti-apartheid activist and leader, co-founder of the ANC Youth League, mentor and close political partner of Nelson Mandela, and a central figure in the move to armed struggle. Imprisoned on Robben Island with Mandela after the Rivonia Trial. After release he served in the post-1994 government and remained a revered elder of the movement. 

**Key role in Mandela’s life**: intellectual and organizational anchor, father-figure, co-strategist from the 1940s through the transition. See [[actors/mandela-nelson]] and Meredith *Nelson Mandela: A Biography* for extended treatment.
