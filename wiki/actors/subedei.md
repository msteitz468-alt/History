# Sübedei

**Sübedei** (also Subutai, Subedei) was among the greatest operational commanders of the
[[mongol-conquests-13th-century|Mongol conquests]] — one of Chinggis Khan's **dörben noqas**
("four hounds") and the architect, with **[[jebe|Jebe]]**, of the 1220–23 pursuit of Sultan
Muhammad II and the reconnaissance campaign that ended in the **[[battle-of-the-kalka-river-1223|Battle
of the Kalka River]]**. [[may-mongol-art-of-war-2016|May]] treats him as emblematic of Mongol
meritocratic command: battlefield promotion, keshik training, and independent execution of
timetabled strategic objectives.
