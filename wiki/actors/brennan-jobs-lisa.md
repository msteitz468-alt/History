# Lisa Brennan-Jobs

**Lisa Nicole Brennan-Jobs** (born 1978) is an American writer. Daughter of Steve Jobs and Chrisann Brennan. Jobs initially denied paternity and provided minimal support; later reconciled. Lisa lived with the Jobs family (Laurene, Reed, Erin, Eve) during high school. Her memoir *Small Fry* (2018) provides additional primary texture on the family dynamics described in Isaacson.
