# Georges Bidault

French foreign minister and later premier (MRP). He was a key figure in the hard-line French position at the Geneva Conference in 1954 before being replaced by Pierre Mendès France, who accepted the necessity of a rapid settlement.

## Role

Bidault represented the French government that still hoped to extract better terms at Geneva. He resisted rapid concessions on partition and elections. His replacement by Mendès France on 18 June 1954 with a promise to achieve peace by 20 July marked the final French decision to cut losses.
