# J. P. Morgan

**John Pierpont Morgan** (1837–1913) was the dominant American financier of the Gilded Age, organizer of U.S. Steel and General Electric and the central figure in turn-of-the-century Wall Street consolidation. In Seifer's *Wizard*, Morgan is [[actors/tesla-nikola|Tesla]]'s decisive patron and nemesis: in 1901 he financed Tesla's [[places/wardenclyffe|Wardenclyffe]] "World Telegraphy Center" for $150,000 in exchange for a controlling interest in Tesla's patents, then declined to provide further funds as costs ballooned and [[actors/marconi-guglielmo|Marconi]]'s cheaper wireless telegraphy advanced — leaving the project to collapse.

Not to be confused with [[actors/morgan-daniel|Daniel Morgan]]. His son **J. P. Morgan Jr.** (1867–1943) later gave Tesla limited additional support.
