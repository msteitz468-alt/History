# Kikkawa Hiroie

**Kikkawa Hiroie** (1561–1625) was a Mori clan retainer who commanded Mori forces
positioned on Nangu Hill during the [[events/battle-of-sekigahara-1600|Battle of
Sekigahara]] (1600), nominally aligned with the Western Army. Before the battle he had
secretly agreed with [[tokugawa-ieyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]] not to engage, and during the
fighting he physically blocked the rest of the Mori contingent (commanded by Mori
Hidemoto) from descending the hill to join the battle — a betrayal that removed a large
Western Army force from the field and contributed materially to the Eastern Army's
victory. In reward, Ieyasu preserved a reduced Mori domain (Choshu) after the battle rather
than destroying the clan outright, though the Mori never forgave the arrangement — a
grievance later cited as a distant root of Choshu's role in the 19th-century overthrow of
the Tokugawa shogunate.
