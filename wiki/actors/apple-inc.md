# Apple Inc.

**Apple Inc.** (originally Apple Computer, Inc.) is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, it pioneered the personal computer with the Apple II and Macintosh, then transformed consumer electronics with the iPod, iPhone, iPad, and associated services under Jobs’s second tenure (1997–2011). Known for end-to-end integration of hardware, software, and content, premium design, and closed ecosystems. As of the early twenty-first century, it became one of the world’s most valuable companies by market capitalization. Its history is central to the biography of Steve Jobs.
