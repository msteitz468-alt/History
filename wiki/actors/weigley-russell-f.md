# Russell F. Weigley

Russell Frank Weigley (1930–2004) was a leading American military historian and author of the seminal *The American Way of War: A History of United States Military Strategy and Policy* (1973). He taught at Temple University and wrote extensively on U.S. Army history, including *History of the United States Army*. His work defined the distinctive American preference for strategies seeking the complete destruction of enemy forces (annihilation) over prolonged attrition or limited political goals, tracing this from George Washington through Ulysses S. Grant, the world wars, and into the nuclear age perplexities of Korea and Vietnam.

Weigley’s analysis remains central to understanding U.S. strategic culture and the conduct of the Civil War and WWII.
