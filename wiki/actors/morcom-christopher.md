# Christopher Morcom

Christopher Morcom (d. 13 February 1930) was a brilliant young mathematician and school friend of Alan Turing at Sherborne School. His early death from tuberculosis (after operations) was a profound formative trauma for Turing.

Turing maintained a close intellectual and emotional friendship with Morcom; after his death Turing wrote to Morcom’s mother and continued to think about the questions of mind, matter, and survival that the loss raised. Biographers (especially Hodges) treat the Morcom relationship as central to understanding Turing’s later combination of emotional intensity, scientific ambition, and sense of outsider status.
