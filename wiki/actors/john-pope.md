# John Pope

**John Pope** (1822–1892) was a Union major general given command of the newly formed
Army of Virginia in mid-1862 after earlier successes in the Western theater. His tenure in
the East was brief and disastrous: [[actors/robert-e-lee|Robert E. Lee]] and
Stonewall Jackson outmaneuvered him at the
[[events/battle-of-second-bull-run-1862|Second Battle of Bull Run]] (August 1862),
inflicting a heavy defeat that forced the Union back on Washington and ended Pope's field
command in the East — he was reassigned to the Department of the Northwest, where he
oversaw the suppression of the Dakota War of 1862. His combative, boastful pre-battle
proclamations to his troops were widely mocked and contributed to his unpopularity among
subordinate officers.
