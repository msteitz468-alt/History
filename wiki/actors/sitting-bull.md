# Sitting Bull (Tȟatȟáŋka Íyotake)

Hunkpapa Lakota leader; co-led the 1876 coalition that defeated Custer; later Canadian exile and Standing Rock politics; killed 15 December 1890 during attempted arrest amid Ghost Dance crisis. Scaffold from CHNPA I.2 (Fowler/Hoxie); expand from claims.
