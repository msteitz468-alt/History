# Geta

Roman emperor, younger son of [[actors/septimius-severus|Septimius Severus]] and brother of [[actors/caracalla|Caracalla]], with whom he was elevated to joint Augustus in 209 CE, creating an unworkable co-emperorship the two brothers loathed on sight — sources describe them dividing the imperial palace into hostile camps. On his deathbed at York in 211, Severus reportedly urged them to rule jointly and stay unified against the army and Senate; the arrangement collapsed within months. In December 211, Caracalla had Geta murdered in their mother Julia Domna's arms, then launched a *damnatio memoriae* against him — erasing his name and image from inscriptions and monuments across the empire — and had thousands of Geta's real and suspected supporters executed in the purge that followed.
