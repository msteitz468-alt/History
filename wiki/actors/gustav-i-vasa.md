# Gustav I Vasa (1496–1560)

King of Sweden 1523–1560 and founder of the Vasa dynasty. Leader of the revolt
that ended the [[kalmar-union-1397|Kalmar Union]] after the
[[christian-ii-of-denmark|Christian II]]-ordered Stockholm Bloodbath (1520), he
was elected king in 1523. Vasa broke with Rome and carried through the
[[reformation-in-scandinavia|Swedish Reformation]], confiscating church wealth to
underwrite the crown and converting Sweden into a hereditary monarchy (1544). His
reign laid the fiscal and administrative foundations of the Swedish state that
would become a [[swedish-empire|great power]] in the seventeenth century.
