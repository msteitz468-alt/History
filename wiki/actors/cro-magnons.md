## Overview

"Cro-Magnon" (after the rock shelter "Magnon's Shelter," SW France) is the name for
the **first anatomically and behaviorally modern *Homo sapiens* in Europe**,
arriving ~40,000 BP — most likely a later wave out of Africa via the east. In
[[tattersall-world-from-beginnings-2008|Tattersall's]] account they provide the
most dramatic archaeological evidence for the arrival of [[symbolic-cognition]],
thrown into sharp relief by contrast with the essentially symbol-free record of the
contemporary [[neanderthals]] whom they replaced within a few millennia.

## The Symbolic Explosion

Cro-Magnon material culture displays "the full panoply of the modern mind":
- **Cave painting** — Chauvet (>32 kya), the world's earliest known paintings;
  later Lascaux-grade Magdalenian art.
- **Figurative and abstract art** — the Vogelherd ivory horse and the
  Hohlenstein-Stadel **lion-man** (~34 kya); Gravettian "Venus" figures.
- **Notation, music, ornament** — incised bone notations; bone/vulture-wing flutes;
  pierced-tooth and bead jewelry; fired-clay figurines and eyed bone needles
  (Dolní Věstonice, ~27 kya) implying tailored clothing.
- **Stratified burial** — Sungir (Russia, ~28 kya): an old man and two children with
  thousands of mammoth-ivory beads and straightened tusks, implying social ranking
  and an economic surplus.
- **Advanced hunting** — fish/bird exploitation, spear-throwers, and ultimately the
  bow and arrow; storage technology (Mezhirich mammoth-bone huts, ~15 kya).

The Upper Paleolithic unfolds through successive, regionally diversifying traditions
— **Aurignacian → Gravettian → Solutrean → Magdalenian** — an unprecedented tempo
of cultural change. See [[paleolithic-art-symbolic-culture]].

## Significance (Counterfactual)

The Cro-Magnons mark the point at which the modern human actor — symbolic,
linguistic, cumulatively cultural — is unambiguously present. Tattersall stresses
that the underlying *capacity* (and the African evidence, e.g. Blombos ~75 kya)
predates them; the Cro-Magnon record is its most vivid expression, not its origin.
Their replacement of the Neanderthals — by economic out-competition and probably
also conflict, with at most marginal interbreeding in this source's reading — is the
European instance of a global pattern of archaic-hominid disappearance as *Homo
sapiens* spread.

## Cro-Magnon / Neanderthal Interaction

The brief, transitional **Châtelperronian** industry (~36–32 kya, France/Spain;
makers found are Neanderthals) blends Mousterian and Aurignacian elements and
includes body ornaments — raising the unresolved question of imitation, trade, or
independent invention. Tattersall reads the overall pattern as a fairly abrupt
replacement, not fusion.

## Historiography

How much, if any, Neanderthal–Cro-Magnon interbreeding occurred is the live issue:
Tattersall (writing pre-2010) minimizes it, whereas later nuclear ancient DNA shows
~1–2% Neanderthal ancestry in non-Africans (see [[modern-human-origins-debate]]).
The makership and meaning of the Châtelperronian remain debated. The European Upper
Paleolithic is exceptionally well documented; the African precursors of modern
behavior are more sparsely (but increasingly) recorded.
