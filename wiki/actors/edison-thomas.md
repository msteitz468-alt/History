# Thomas Edison

**Thomas Alva Edison** (1847–1931) was an American inventor and businessman, the "Wizard of Menlo Park," whose laboratory produced the practical incandescent light bulb, the phonograph, and a complete **direct-current (DC)** electrical generation and distribution system. In Seifer's *Wizard*, Edison is the first American patron and antagonist of [[actors/tesla-nikola|Nikola Tesla]], who emigrated in 1884 to work for him and left after a dispute over promised pay.

Edison's commitment to DC put him on the losing side of the **[[processes/war-of-the-currents|War of the Currents]]** against the Tesla–[[actors/westinghouse-george|Westinghouse]] alternating-current system, a struggle Edison waged partly through a propaganda campaign emphasizing the dangers of high-voltage AC (including its association with the electric chair).

## Relationship to Tesla

- Continental Edison employment in Paris; emigration and brief work at the Edison Machine Works in New York (1884–85); the disputed bonus and Tesla's resignation.
- DC vs. AC commercial and public-relations conflict.
