# Willibrord (c. 658–739)

## Overview

**Willibrord**, a Northumbrian monk trained in Ireland, led the late-seventh/early-eighth-century **mission to Frisia** and became the first archbishop of the Frisians. Consecrated at Rome and backed by the Pippinid mayors, he founded **Echternach** and established a durable bridge between the English missionary impulse, the [[papacy]], and Frankish power — the template [[boniface]] would expand deeper into Germany.

## Role and Significance

Willibrord stands at the start of the English mission to the Continent that Logan treats as part of the "set" completed by Boniface's death (754): papal commission + Frankish protection + Anglo-Saxon monastic energy.
