# German Empire (Kaiserreich, 1871–1918)

The unified German nation-state proclaimed in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles in January 1871,
welding 25 states under Prussian leadership and the Hohenzollern Kaiser. A federal but
Prussian-dominated, authoritarian-constitutional monarchy: a powerful executive and military
answerable to the Kaiser, alongside a male-suffrage Reichstag with limited power.

Under [[otto-von-bismarck|Bismarck]] the new Reich pursued the [[kulturkampf]] against political
Catholicism and anti-socialist laws against the rising SPD, while pioneering the world's first
welfare-state insurance. After Bismarck's dismissal in 1890, **[[wilhelm-ii|Wilhelm II]]** steered a
more assertive *Weltpolitik* — colonial expansion, a naval race with Britain, and the alliance
politics that culminated in the [[first-world-war-1914|First World War]]. Defeat brought the
[[november-revolution-1918|November Revolution]] of 1918, the Kaiser's abdication, and the
[[weimar-republic|Weimar Republic]]. The Kaiserreich is the central object of the
[[german-sonderweg|Sonderweg debate]] over whether Germany followed a uniquely illiberal path to modernity.
