# Weldon Honeycutt

U.S. Army lieutenant colonel who commanded the 3rd Battalion, 187th Infantry Regiment ("Rakkasans") of the 101st Airborne Division during the May 1969 assault on Hill 937 (Dong Ap Bia) in the A Shau Valley — the battle that became known as "Hamburger Hill." Honeycutt ordered repeated frontal assaults up the heavily fortified, jungle-covered slope over ten days despite mounting American casualties, a decision that drew sharp controversy at the time and afterward: the hill was taken on 20 May 1969 but abandoned by U.S. forces days later as militarily insignificant, fueling domestic criticism of the war's conduct and becoming a symbol, alongside other high-casualty, low-strategic-value engagements, of the war's attritional character in its final years of major American ground involvement.
