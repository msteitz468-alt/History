# Leslie R. Groves

U.S. Army officer (later lieutenant general) who as head of the Manhattan Project selected J. Robert Oppenheimer to direct the Los Alamos laboratory and managed the overall engineering and industrial effort that produced the atomic bombs. Groves and Oppenheimer formed a highly effective, if unlikely, partnership. Groves valued Oppenheimer's scientific judgment and leadership; Oppenheimer respected Groves's drive and ability to cut through bureaucracy.

After the war Groves was passed over for higher command in part because of the atomic project; he retired and wrote *Now It Can Be Told*.
