# Edward Teller

Hungarian-born physicist, key figure in the Manhattan Project and the primary advocate for a crash program to develop the hydrogen bomb after World War II. His technical contributions (with Stanislaw Ulam) helped solve the design problem for a practical thermonuclear weapon. He testified against Oppenheimer at the 1954 security hearing, stating he would feel "more secure" if public matters rested in other hands. Later directed the Lawrence Livermore laboratory and became a leading proponent of strategic missile defense.
