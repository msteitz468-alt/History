# Kasturba Gandhi

Kasturba Gandhi (née Kapadia, c. 1869–1944) was the wife of Mohandas K. Gandhi. Married in childhood (traditional arranged marriage), she accompanied him to South Africa and became an active participant in the satyagraha campaigns there, including going to jail in 1913.

In the Guha volume she appears as a strong, often long-suffering partner who endured repeated separations, the rigours of settlement life, diet experiments, and political imprisonment. Gandhi credited women's courage (including Kasturba's and that of the Tamil volunteers) with raising the moral temperature of the 1913 campaign.

She continued to play a public role in India (imprisoned multiple times) until her death in detention in 1944 (Aga Khan Palace).
