# William "Bull" Halsey

U.S. Navy admiral, aggressive carrier task force commander. Led early raids, Guadalcanal support, Central Pacific drive. Famous for "hit hard, hit fast, hit often." Controversial decisions (e.g., chasing Japanese carriers at Leyte Gulf leaving landing beaches exposed; sailing into Typhoon Cobra). Key in turning the Pacific naval war.

See: [[world-war-ii-1939-1945]], [[battle-of-leyte-gulf-1944]], [[nimitz-chester]], [[macarthur-douglas]].

**From Murray & Millett (range 3):** "Halsey ordered the Third Fleet north, leaving no task force to cover San Bernardino strait. He did, however, order Rear Admiral Willis Lee... to prepare to form Task Force 34... Kinkaid thought the message also meant that the act had been done, but it had not." "Halsey’s aviators 'turned on Ozawa’s sacrificial fleet with gusto and sank all four carriers and three destroyers.'" (p. 367-368) `[[halsey-william]]` `produced:` [[events/world-war-ii-1939-1945]]

**From Murray & Millett *A War to Be Won* (range 4 extraction):** Nimitz/Halsey/Spruance theater command (Iwo/Okinawa): "Pressed by MacArthur and Nimitz, Halsey’s carrier battle groups of TF 38 returned to the war on 10 December for more raids on Japanese air bases, but on 19 December the task forces ran into a typhoon whose course Halsey had misjudged. Task Force 38 lost 3 destroyers and 18 other warships damaged." Halsey’s airmen claimed >150 kills but lost 86 aircraft (mostly accidents); kamikazes penetrated. "Admiral Nimitz, as commander of the Pacific Ocean Areas Theater..." Nimitz (with subordinates) questioned Formosa; unanimously favored Iwo Jima/Okinawa for siege bases. "Recovering the Third Fleet from Halsey, Spruance—trusted by all except the carrier admirals—would command the joint task forces built from the Fifth Fleet in both operations." MacArthur approved Nimitz plan (to sink Formosa option). On Okinawa: "Only Spruance and Nimitz had the authority to order Buckner to change his plan, and once again the admirals shrank from conflict with the army." Spruance lost flagship; Mitscher shifted flags multiple times under kamikazes. Fifth Fleet heavy losses (64 ships sunk/heavily damaged; carriers Bunker Hill, Franklin, etc.) but maintained sea control. Nimitz signed surrender for US on Missouri (Halsey’s flagship); MacArthur presided.
