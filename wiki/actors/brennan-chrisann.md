# Chrisann Brennan

**Chrisann Brennan** is an American painter and writer. Early girlfriend of Steve Jobs at Homestead High; mother of his daughter Lisa Brennan-Jobs (born 1978). Relationship detailed in Isaacson as part of Jobs’s early personal life and later family reconciliation. Brennan’s account supplements the primary biography.
