# Kobayakawa Hideaki

**Kobayakawa Hideaki** (1577–1602), an adopted nephew of Toyotomi Hideyoshi, held a
strategically vital position on Mount Matsuo at the [[events/battle-of-sekigahara-1600|
Battle of Sekigahara]] (1600), nominally as part of the Western Army. His defection to
[[tokugawa-ieyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu's]] Eastern Army partway through the battle — turning
his forces against the Western Army units below, including those of
[[otani-yoshitsugu|Otani Yoshitsugu]] — is generally regarded as the single decisive
act of the battle, collapsing the Western Army's position and precipitating a general
rout. Accounts differ on how long Kobayakawa had wavered before committing to Ieyasu; some
traditions hold that Ieyasu ordered warning shots fired at his position to force his hand.
He was rewarded with an enlarged domain but died without an heir only two years later,
and his line was extinguished.
