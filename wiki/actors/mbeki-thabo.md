# Thabo Mbeki

**Thabo Mvuyelwa Mbeki** (b. 1942) is a South African politician who served as Deputy President (1994–1999) and President of South Africa (1999–2008). Son of Rivonia Trialist Govan Mbeki. Key ANC intellectual and diplomat in exile; succeeded Mandela as ANC president in 1997 and state president in 1999. His presidency was marked by strong emphasis on African Renaissance and Black economic empowerment alongside controversial positions on HIV/AIDS and Zimbabwe. Meredith covers the tensions between Mandela’s reconciliation style and Mbeki’s more assertive transformation agenda, as well as Mbeki’s insecurity in the shadow of Mandela’s global stature.
