# William McKinley

**25th President of the United States (1897–1901).** Last Civil War veteran to hold the office. Presided over the Spanish-American War and the acquisition of empire (Philippines, Puerto Rico, Guam). Assassinated by anarchist Leon Czolgosz in Buffalo, 6 September 1901; died 14 September. Theodore Roosevelt succeeded him.
