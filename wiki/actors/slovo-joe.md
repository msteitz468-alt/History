# Joe Slovo

**Joe Slovo** (1926–1995) was a South African lawyer, communist, and anti-apartheid leader. Long-time general secretary of the South African Communist Party, he was a key strategist of Umkhonto we Sizwe and a senior figure in the ANC in exile. White South African of Lithuanian Jewish origin; his marriage to Ruth First made him a symbol of non-racialism. Returned after 1990; served as Minister of Housing in the first Mandela cabinet until his death from cancer. Close comrade of Mandela and the Rivonia group.
