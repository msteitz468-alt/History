# Gellius Egnatius

Samnite general and the principal architect, per Livy's account, of the great anti-Roman coalition of the Third Samnite War. Around 298–296 BCE he organized and led a Samnite army on a long march north through central Italy to unite with Etruscan, Umbrian, and Senone Gallic forces, assembling the multi-ethnic coalition that met Rome's consular armies at the Battle of Sentinum (295 BCE). In the battle he commanded the Samnite contingent on the coalition's right wing; Livy reports he was killed there after the coalition's defeat, a battle in which the Roman consul Publius Decius Mus is said to have sacrificed himself (*devotio*) to secure Roman victory. Sentinum broke the coalition and effectively decided the Third Samnite War in Rome's favor.
