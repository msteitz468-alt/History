# Irish Free State

The self-governing dominion established in 26 of Ireland's 32 counties by the Anglo-Irish Treaty of
1921, which ended the [[irish-war-of-independence-1919|War of Independence]] but split the nationalist
movement and triggered the [[irish-civil-war-1922|Civil War]] of 1922–23. Governed first by the
pro-Treaty Cumann na nGaedheal and from 1932 by [[eamon-de-valera|de Valera's]] Fianna Fáil, it
progressively dismantled the Treaty's constraints, culminating in the 1937 Constitution (renaming the
state Éire) and the declaration of the [[republic-of-ireland|Republic]] in 1949. Its creation
entrenched the [[northern-ireland|partition]] of the island.
