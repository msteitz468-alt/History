# Arthur Wellesley, 1st Duke of Wellington (1769–1852)

The British general who mastered the defensive answer to the **[[napoleonic-warfare|Napoleonic
system]]** and, at **[[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/napoleonic-wars/battle-of-waterloo|Waterloo]]**
(1815), ended [[napoleon|Napoleon]]'s career. In the **Peninsular War** (1808–1814) Wellington
perfected a method of **reverse-slope defense, disciplined line-versus-column firepower, and
careful logistics** that repeatedly defeated French marshals, while the Spanish *guerrilla* bled the
occupiers — the "Spanish ulcer" of Doughty & Gruber's Ch 8. His steady allied defense at Waterloo,
until the Prussians under [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/napoleonic-wars/battle-of-waterloo|Blücher]]
arrived, closed the [[napoleonic-wars|Napoleonic Wars]].
