# John D. Rockefeller, Jr.

John Davison Rockefeller, Jr. (1874–1960), "the Dauphin," was the only son of [[actors/rockefeller-john-d|John D. Rockefeller, Sr.]] and the sole heir to active management of the family fortune and philanthropies. Chernow's *Titan* treats him as the dutiful, conscience-driven steward who transferred the family's energies fully from accumulation to giving and to dynasty-building. His handling of the [[events/ludlow-massacre-1914|Ludlow Massacre]] (1914) — and the public-relations reckoning that followed — was a formative crisis. He was the father of [[actors/rockefeller-nelson|Nelson Rockefeller]].
