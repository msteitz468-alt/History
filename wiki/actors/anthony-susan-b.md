# Susan B. Anthony (1820–1906)

Principal organizer of the nineteenth-century American woman suffrage movement; co-architect with [[stanton-elizabeth-cady|Elizabeth Cady Stanton]] of the federal amendment strategy. The proposed amendment that became the [[nineteenth-amendment-1920|Nineteenth Amendment]] was known throughout the Wilson era as the **Susan B. Anthony Amendment**.

## Significance for the Wilson story

Anthony died in 1906, before Wilson's presidency, but Cox's *Light Withdrawn* treats her name-amendment as the through-line of the book: Wilson's long opposition, the 1919 attempt (with southern allies) to rewrite the amendment to protect Jim Crow, and final ratification as deferred justice to Anthony's generation. Linked from the first chapters to abolition/"kindred causes."
