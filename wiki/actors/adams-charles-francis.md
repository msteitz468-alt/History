# Charles Francis Adams

U.S. Minister to the United Kingdom (1861–1868). Son and grandson of presidents, Adams was the key Union diplomat in London during the Civil War. He worked to prevent British recognition of the Confederacy, protested the building of Confederate commerce raiders (Alabama, Florida), and managed the Trent Affair crisis. His dispatches and presence were central to the success of the anti-Confederate diplomatic effort.

## Role in Bonner's Account

Adams is the steady professional counterpoint to Sumner's public oratory. He pressed the legal and practical case against Confederate shipbuilding and belligerent rights in daily diplomacy with Lord Russell and the British government.
