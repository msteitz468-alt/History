# Giraldus Cambrensis (Gerald of Wales)

Cambro-Norman cleric, historian, and ethnographer (c. 1146–1223). Author of influential works on Wales (*Itinerarium Cambriae*, *Descriptio Cambriae*) and Ireland, providing key medieval descriptions of Celtic societies.
