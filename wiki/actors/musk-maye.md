# Maye Musk

Mother of Elon, Kimbal, and Tosca Musk (b. 1948, née Haldeman), a Canadian-South African model and registered dietitian who continued a decades-long modeling career into her seventies. Raised her children largely as a single parent in South Africa after divorcing Errol Musk, working multiple jobs (dietetics practice and modeling) to support the family amid financial instability, and later relocated to Canada and the United States.

Isaacson's biography of Elon Musk describes Maye as a stabilizing, resilient presence contrasted with Errol's volatility, though the household environment during Elon's South African childhood was strained by Errol's authoritarian and often abusive behavior toward the children. Maye later became a public figure in her own right, appearing in magazine campaigns and publishing a memoir, and has spoken publicly about resilience, aging, and family.

## Significance

Maye Musk's resilience amid a difficult marriage and single motherhood is presented in biographical accounts of Elon Musk as a formative counterweight to his father's volatility, shaping the family dynamics behind his early life.
