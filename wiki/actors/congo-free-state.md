# Congo Free State

The **Congo Free State** (*État indépendant du Congo*, 1885–1908) was the vast central-African territory
held by **King Leopold II of Belgium** as his **personal possession** — not a Belgian colony but a
sovereign domain of a single monarch — and run as an extraction regime for ivory and, above all, wild
rubber. It is documented for the wiki principally through **[[hochschild-king-leopolds-ghost|Hochschild's
*King Leopold's Ghost*]]** and stands as the most notorious case of atrocity in high
[[imperialism|imperialism]].

## Formation

Denied an overseas colony by Belgium's parliament, Leopold acquired the Congo basin through private
diplomacy — funding H. M. Stanley's treaty-making expeditions and securing recognition of his
International Association of the Congo at the **Berlin Conference (1884–85)** that regularized the
[[imperialism|Scramble for Africa]]. The resulting state, roughly **seventy-six times the size of
Belgium**, answered to Leopold alone.

## The Rubber Regime

Demand from the bicycle and automobile booms made wild Congo rubber hugely profitable. The state and its
concession companies imposed **rubber quotas** enforced by the **Force Publique**, an African conscript
army under European officers. Non-compliance brought hostage-taking (often of women), village burning,
flogging with the *chicotte*, and the mutilation that became the regime's emblem — **severed hands**
collected as proof that cartridges had been spent on killing. The combination of murder, forced labour,
famine, flight, disease and a collapsed birth rate produced a demographic catastrophe that Hochschild,
following the historian Jan Vansina, estimates at a population fall of roughly **ten million** between
c. 1880 and 1920 (an order-of-magnitude figure, debated in detail — see [[genocide]]).

## The Reform Campaign and Annexation

The Congo provoked the first great modern **human-rights movement**: **E. D. Morel**, who inferred forced
labour from the asymmetry of the trade statistics, built the **Congo Reform Association**; the British
consul **Roger Casement** documented the abuses on the ground; missionaries (and the African-American
George Washington Williams and William Sheppard) bore witness. International pressure forced Leopold to
cede the territory to the Belgian state in **1908**, when it became the **Belgian Congo** (independent as
the Republic of the Congo / Zaire in 1960).

## Significance

The Congo Free State is the wiki's central case for the **violence of colonial extraction** and the
**colonial production of demographic collapse**, a companion to the famine analysis of
[[davis-late-victorian-holocausts|Davis]]; it is a recurring borderline case in the [[genocide]]
literature; and its reform campaign marks the **birth of modern transnational humanitarianism**.
