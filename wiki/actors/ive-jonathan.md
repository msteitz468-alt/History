# Jonathan Ive

**Sir Jonathan Paul Ive** (born 27 February 1967) is a British-American industrial designer. Chief Design Officer at Apple (1997–2019) under Steve Jobs and successor leadership. His partnership with Jobs from the iMac onward produced the signature minimalist aesthetic, material choices (anodized aluminum, glass), and obsessive attention to unboxing and user experience that defined Apple’s resurgence. Central to the “design studio of Jobs and Ive” chapter in Isaacson.
