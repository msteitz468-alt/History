# Frank Jack Fletcher

U.S. Navy rear admiral who commanded American carrier task forces in the first year of the Pacific War. He led Task Force 17 (carrier *Yorktown*) at the Battle of the Coral Sea (May 1942), the first carrier-versus-carrier engagement in history, and again at the Battle of Midway (June 1942), where as senior officer afloat he had overall tactical command until *Yorktown* was crippled, after which [[actors/spruance-raymond|Raymond Spruance]] and Task Force 16 carried the main strikes. Fletcher's caution — conserving carriers he could not afford to lose — drew criticism from some subordinates and later historians, but his forces sank one Japanese carrier at Coral Sea (checking the invasion of Port Moresby) and helped destroy four at Midway.

After Midway, Fletcher commanded carrier forces during the opening of the Guadalcanal campaign (August 1942), withdrawing his carriers from the invasion area after covering the landings — a decision that also drew controversy — before being reassigned away from front-line carrier command. He spent the rest of the war commanding North Pacific naval forces.
