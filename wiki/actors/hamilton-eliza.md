# Elizabeth Schuyler Hamilton (1757–1854)

Daughter of Revolutionary general and U.S. Senator Philip Schuyler; wife of **[[hamilton-alexander|Alexander Hamilton]]** (married 1780). Known as Eliza, she managed the household through Hamilton's long absences, raised their eight children (one died young), and after his 1804 death devoted the remaining fifty years of her life to vindicating his reputation.

Eliza outlived her husband by half a century (died at 97). She destroyed her own letters but preserved and organized Hamilton's papers, enlisted assistants, and pushed for the publication of his writings and an authorized biography (eventually completed by their son John Church Hamilton). She was a central figure in early national social and charitable circles in New York.
