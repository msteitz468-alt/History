# Owain Glyndŵr

Welsh leader who led the major revolt against English rule 1400–c.1415; declared Prince of Wales; held parliaments; sought French alliance. Last major native Welsh bid for political independence in the medieval period; became iconic in later Welsh national memory. During the rebellion, the monastery of Ystrad-fflur (Strata Florida) became a strong point for the forces of Henry IV in 1407.
