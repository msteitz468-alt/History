# Justine Musk

First wife of Elon Musk (married 2000, divorced 2008). Author. Mother of five of Musk’s surviving children (twins + triplets, one child died in infancy). Provided detailed accounts to Isaacson of the early years, PayPal period, and the psychological impact of Errol Musk’s parenting on Elon.
