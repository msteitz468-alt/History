# J.E.B. Stuart

Confederate major general, [[actors/robert-e-lee|Robert E. Lee's]] chief cavalry commander in the Army of Northern Virginia, celebrated for aggressive reconnaissance rides around Union armies (including two circuits of the entire Army of the Potomac) that made him one of the Confederacy's most flamboyant early war heroes. During the Gettysburg campaign of 1863, Stuart took much of his cavalry on an extended raid around the advancing Union army rather than screening Lee's own march, leaving Lee largely without cavalry reconnaissance in unfamiliar Pennsylvania territory in the days before the battle began; Stuart did not rejoin the army until the second day of the battle (2 July), and his absence has been widely debated by historians as a contributing factor to Lee's difficulty locating and gauging Union strength before Gettysburg, though its precise causal weight against other factors remains disputed.

Stuart was mortally wounded at the Battle of Yellow Tavern in May 1864 during Grant's Overland Campaign, dying the following day; his death deprived Lee of his most experienced cavalry commander in the war's final year.
