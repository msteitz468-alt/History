# Éamon de Valera

The dominant figure of independent Ireland for half a century. A commander in the [[easter-rising-1916|1916 Rising]]
(spared execution), he led Sinn Féin and then rejected the 1921 Anglo-Irish Treaty, taking the
anti-Treaty side in the [[irish-civil-war-1922|Civil War]]. Returning to constitutional politics, he
founded **Fianna Fáil** (1926), governed from 1932, and reshaped the state — dismantling the Treaty,
promulgating the 1937 Constitution (which renamed the state Éire and claimed the whole island), and
keeping Ireland neutral in the [[second-world-war-1939|Second World War]].

His vision of a Gaelic, Catholic, rural, self-sufficient Ireland defined the [[irish-free-state|Free State]]
and early [[republic-of-ireland|Republic]] — and became the foil against which the modernizing
[[sean-lemass|Lemass]] era later defined itself.
