# Dál Riata (Dalriada)

Gaelic-speaking kingdom in Argyll (Scotland) and parts of Ireland (6th–9th c.). Hosted St Columba (Colum Cille) and Iona; key in emergence of Scottish identity through union with Picts under Cinaed mac Ailpín.
