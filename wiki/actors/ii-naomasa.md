# Ii Naomasa

Japanese daimyo and one of the "Four Guardians" (Shitenno) of [[actors/tokugawa-ieyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]], commander of the elite "Red Devils" (Akazonae) unit distinguished by its red-lacquered armor. At the Battle of Sekigahara (1600) he commanded a wing of Ieyasu's forces and, according to most accounts, fired the opening shots of the battle; he was wounded during the fighting by gunfire from Shimazu forces while pursuing the retreating Shimazu contingent from the field. His conduct and standing at Sekigahara secured him substantial reward under the new Tokugawa order, and he was granted the domain of Sawayama (later Hikone), founding a line that remained among the most powerful and trusted vassal houses of the Tokugawa shogunate for its entire history.
