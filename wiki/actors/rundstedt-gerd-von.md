# Gerd von Rundstedt (1875–1953)

German Field Marshal. Commander-in-Chief West (OB West) during the Normandy campaign.

## Role in Normandy

Nominal overall commander in the West. Advocated a more traditional doctrine of holding mobile panzer reserves in the rear for a decisive counterattack rather than Rommel's forward "defeat them on the beaches" approach. Hitler’s interference in reserve release severely hampered the German response. Later commanded Army Group B briefly and was involved in the Battle of the Bulge planning.

## Historiography

Seen as a professional soldier caught between Rommel, the OKW, and Hitler. D'Este and other Normandy studies examine the command disagreements on the German side and the effects of centralized control from Hitler.
