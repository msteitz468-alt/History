# Olaf Tryggvason

King of [[norway|Norway]] (995–1000), a former Viking who used force and patronage
to push [[christianization-of-scandinavia|Christianization]] in Norway and,
according to tradition, in the North Atlantic island communities including
[[iceland|Iceland]]. He fell at the sea battle of Svolder (1000) against a
coalition of Danish, Swedish, and rival Norwegian forces, after which Norway was
divided among the victors. His missionary reputation was magnified by later saga.

See [[helle-cambridge-history-scandinavia-v1-2003]].
