# Frederick T. Gates

Frederick Taylor Gates (1853–1929), a Baptist minister turned philanthropic strategist, was the principal architect of Rockefeller's giving. Chernow's *Titan* presents Gates as the indispensable partner of Rockefeller's second career: he devised the doctrine of "wholesale," [[concepts/scientific-philanthropy|scientific philanthropy]] — endowing institutions and attacking the root causes of problems rather than dispensing retail charity — and drove the creation of the [[actors/rockefeller-john-d|University of Chicago]] funding, the Rockefeller Institute for Medical Research, the General Education Board, and the Rockefeller Foundation.
