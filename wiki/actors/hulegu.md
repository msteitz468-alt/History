# Hülegü

**Hülegü** (Hulagu), son of Tolui and grandson of **[[genghis-khan|Chinggis Khan]]**, founded the
**[[ilkhanate|Ilkhanate]]** through Möngke's Middle Eastern campaign (1255–65): destruction of the
Ismaili fortresses, the **[[hubs/warfare/high-middle-ages/mongol-conquests/siege-of-baghdad-1258|sack
of Baghdad (1258)]]**, and the brief Mongol occupation of Syria before his withdrawal on Möngke's
death left **[[kitbuqa|Kitbuqa]]** overextended at Ain Jalut. [[may-mongol-art-of-war-2016|May]]
stresses his leisurely advance with pasture management and the engineering-heavy siege of Baghdad.
