# Henry J. Kaiser

American industrialist and shipbuilder. Key figure in U.S. wartime mass production, particularly Liberty Ships and other vessels. Exemplified American "economics of superabundance" and rationalized assembly-line techniques that dramatically reduced construction times.

See: [[processes/war-economies-wwii]], [[events/world-war-ii-1939-1945]], [[actors/roosevelt-franklin-d]].

**From West Point History Vol. 1 Range 5 (Overy):**
- Kaiser Shipyard rationalization: At start of program, each ship took 1.4 million man-hours and 355 days; by 1943, 500,000 man-hours and 41 days. Robert E. Peary built in 4 days. Prefabricated sections on assembly lines.
- Symbol of U.S. production surge and efficiency in the "arsenal of democracy."
