# Jubal A. Early

Confederate lieutenant general whose July 1864 raid through Maryland threatened [[places/washington-dc|Washington, D.C.]] and forced diversion of Union forces from Grant's Virginia front. Ellsworth (*Midnight on the Potomac*, 2025) treats Early's approach and the delaying action at [[events/battle-of-monocacy-1864|Monocacy]] as a genuine existential scare for the capital — "Darkness" over the Potomac — not a sideshow.

## Overview

Virginia lawyer and Mexican War veteran; Confederate brigade/division then corps commander under Lee. In summer 1864 Lee detached Early to the Valley and Maryland to relieve pressure on Richmond and threaten Washington. After Monocacy (9 July 1864) Early reached the capital's northern defenses before withdrawing. Later defeated in the Valley by Sheridan; postwar became a leading Lost Cause polemicist.


## Ellsworth Detail (*Midnight on the Potomac*, 2025)

Commands the June–July 1864 Valley–Maryland expedition (~10,000): Lexington (Jackson's grave), Staunton, Winchester, Potomac crossing, Monocacy, approaches within five miles of the White House, withdrawal after Fort Stevens probes. Ellsworth's judgment that Early "blinked" is interpretive. Postwar: rode into Mexico exile after final collapse; later Lost Cause publicist (pair with Afterword on memory).
