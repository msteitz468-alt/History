# Kuroda Nagamasa

**Kuroda Nagamasa** (1568–1623) was a daimyo who fought for [[tokugawa-ieyasu|Tokugawa
Ieyasu's]] Eastern Army at the [[events/battle-of-sekigahara-1600|Battle of Sekigahara]]
(1600). His unit fought effectively against Western Army forces on the field, and — in
some accounts more consequentially — he is credited with the political work of
negotiating the pre-battle defections and hesitations of several Western-aligned commanders,
including helping arrange [[kobayakawa-hideaki|Kobayakawa Hideaki's]] switch of sides.
Ieyasu personally commended Kuroda after the battle as among those most responsible for
the victory, and he was rewarded with the large and wealthy Fukuoka domain in Kyushu,
which his descendants ruled through the rest of the Edo period.
