# Sergius of Radonezh
## Biography / Description
**Saint Sergius of Radonezh** (c. 1314 – 1392 CE) was the spiritual leader of medieval Russia and the founder of the Holy Trinity-St. Sergius Monastery. Beginning as a simple hermit in the dense forest wilderness north of Moscow, Sergius became the recognized moral head of the Russian Orthodox Church and the political counselor to the grand princes of Moscow.

He is widely regarded as one of the most influential figures in Russian history, credited with inspiring both a religious revival and the national struggle for independence from Mongol rule.

## Key Decisions and Achievements
* **Founding of the Holy Trinity Monastery (c. 1340s CE):** Sergius established a small wooden chapel dedicated to the Holy Trinity in the Radonezh forests, which rapidly grew into Russia's most important monastic, cultural, and spiritual center.
* **Blessing of Kulikovo (1380 CE):** Sergius famously blessed Grand Prince Dmitry Donskoy and the Russian army before they set out for the Battle of Kulikovo. According to tradition, his blessing and moral support gave the Russians the spiritual strength to challenge the Mongol Horde.
* **Mediation of Princely Feuds:** Sergius repeatedly intervened in the internecine conflicts of the appanage princes, using his moral authority to reconcile quarreling Rurikid rulers and promote cooperation.
* **Promotion of the Monastic Community:** Sergius emphasized physical labor, humility, charity, and learning within his monastery. He refused to become the Metropolitan of Kiev and All Russia, choosing instead to remain a simple abbot.

## Historical Significance
St. Sergius played a pivotal role in the **monastic colonization** of northern Russia. His disciples and followers spread out into the northeastern wilderness, founding more than thirty monasteries that served as outposts of settlement, agriculture, and culture (l. 1719, 1775).

Spiritually, he shifted the focus of Russian Christianity toward the contemplative, mystical tradition of Eastern monasticism, emphasizing humility, love, and community service. His moral legacy was a direct inspiration for the art of Andrei Rublev. St. Sergius remains the patron saint of Russia.
