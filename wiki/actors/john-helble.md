# John Helble

U.S. Foreign Service officer, consul in Hue 1961–1963. Fluent Vietnamese speaker who investigated the May 8, 1963 massacre of Buddhists at the Hue radio station. His on-the-ground reporting and unpublished memoir provided key primary material for later histories of the Buddhist crisis origins.
