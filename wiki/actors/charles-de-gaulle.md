# Charles de Gaulle

Leader of Free France (1940–1944) and head of the Provisional Government. After the liberation of France he insisted that Indochina must be recovered for the French Empire and selected Leclerc to lead the Expeditionary Corps. His early postwar policy set the course for the French return and the subsequent war.

## Role in Indochina

De Gaulle viewed the preservation of the French Empire (including Indochina) as essential to France’s great-power status after the humiliation of 1940. He rejected Roosevelt’s trusteeship ideas and pushed for the rapid reassertion of French sovereignty after Japan’s surrender.
