# Conrad Habicht

Swiss mathematician and physicist, one of the three founding members (with [[actors/einstein-albert|Albert Einstein]] and Maurice Solovine) of the informal "Olympia Academy" discussion group that met in Bern between roughly 1902 and 1904, reading and debating philosophy of science (Hume, Mach, Poincaré, Spinoza) alongside the mathematics and physics that fed Einstein's 1905 "miracle year" papers. Habicht worked at the same period on his own (unsuccessful) invention of a device to measure small electrical charges; Einstein corresponded with him about it and teasingly referenced it while announcing the special theory of relativity in a 1905 letter. Habicht later became a schoolteacher in Switzerland.
