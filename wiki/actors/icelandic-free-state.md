# Icelandic Free State (Commonwealth)

The stateless polity of [[iceland|Iceland]] from the establishment of the
[[thing-assembly|Althing]] (c. 930) to submission to the Norwegian crown
(1262–64). It had law and courts but no king, no central executive, and no
standing coercive power: authority lay with chieftains (*goðar*) holding personal
followings (*þingmenn*), the regional things, and the annual Althing, where the
Lawspeaker recited the law. Iceland adopted Christianity by collective decision at
the Althing c. 1000. In the 13th-century Sturlung Age the concentration of
chieftaincies and feud broke the system, and Iceland accepted Norwegian
overlordship. The commonwealth is a classic case in comparative political and
legal history of order without a state, and the matrix of the
[[saga-literature|sagas]].
