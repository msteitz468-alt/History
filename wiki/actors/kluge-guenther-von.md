# Günther von Kluge

German field marshal. Commanded 4th Army in Poland/France/Barbarossa; later Army Group Center (Moscow, Stalingrad relief attempts). Replaced Rundstedt in Normandy 1944. Involved in or aware of 20 July plot circles; committed suicide (or was forced) after dismissal, en route to Berlin.
