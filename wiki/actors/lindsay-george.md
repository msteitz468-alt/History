# George Lindsay

Key advocate and organiser within the British Expeditionary Force for the proper tactical and organisational use of the machine gun. Instrumental in the creation of the Machine Gun Corps; involved in the establishment and direction of the Grantham machine-gun school.

Griffith draws heavily on Lindsay’s papers and career to illustrate how specialist technical arms were integrated into the broader tactical evolution of the BEF, the struggle to control automatic fire, and the professionalisation of training.

Central figure in Part Three discussions of heavier weapons and automatic weapons in *Battle Tactics of the Western Front*.
