# Jeannette Rankin (1880–1973)

First woman elected to the U.S. Congress (Montana, House, 1916); seated as Wilson prepared for war. Voted against U.S. entry into [[first-world-war-1914|World War I]] (and later against World War II). Cox treats her as a living proof of women's political capacity and a congressional actor in the Anthony Amendment endgame.
