# Tomoyuki Yamashita

Japanese general known as the "Tiger of Malaya." Led the lightning conquest of Malaya and the surrender of Singapore (1942), one of the most humiliating British defeats. Later commanded Japanese forces in the Philippines (1944-45); conducted a skillful but ultimately hopeless defense against MacArthur's return. Tried and executed after the war for war crimes committed by his troops (command responsibility precedent).

See: [[world-war-ii-1939-1945]], [[fall-of-saigon-1975]] (contextual parallels in command).

**From Murray & Millett *A War to Be Won* (range 4 extraction - final campaigns/assessment):**
- Yamashita masterful attrition defense (Luzon 1945): "General Yamashita deployed his forces in order to prolong the battle for Luzon and inflict as many American casualties as possible. His conduct of the defense was one of the most brilliant of the Pacific War and one for which MacArthur eventually hanged the Japanese general." Correctly predicted Lingayen landing/central valley drive; prepared armored counterattack force. Divided into four groups (Shobu ~150k Caraballo Mountains for flank/delay; Shimbu ~80k east Manila; Kembu Zambales; naval Iwabushi for Manila destruction/port denial). "Yamashita had no intention of making Manila an 'open city'... but he also did not intend to turn it... into a second Stalingrad." "Thanks to Yamashita’s skill, the Luzon campaign continued, to the embarrassment of the Sixth Army except at the tactical level." "Even in defeat, the Tiger of Malaya set a standard for generalship seldom matched by the American commanders he faced in the Philippines, since he focused only on ways to spend his soldiers’ lives wisely." Surrendered ~100k Shobu Group Sep 1945 (refused suicide). (lines 53–56, 84–87, 216–217, 275–278)
