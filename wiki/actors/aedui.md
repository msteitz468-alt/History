# Aedui / Haedui

One of the most powerful Celtic polities (civitas) of central Gaul in the Late Iron Age, with capital at Bibracte. Frequently mentioned in classical sources (Caesar) as allies and rivals in the politics leading to the Gallic Wars. Key player in elite networks, oppida economy, and the transition to Roman provincial structure.

## Role and Significance

- Major actor in the 'developed Celtic world' of oppida and complex polities.
- Interactions with Rome, Arverni, and other Gallic groups.
- Site of Bibracte (Mont Beuvray) — major oppidum and later Augustodunum (Autun).
