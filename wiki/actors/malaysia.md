# Malaysia

The federation grown from **[[british-malaya|British Malaya]]** — the Straits Settlements and the
protected Malay states — whose tin and rubber economy drew massive Chinese and Indian immigration,
producing the classic colonial **[[plural-society]]** of Malays, Chinese, and Indians that has defined
its politics ever since. British rule was interrupted by the [[japanese-occupation-southeast-asia|Japanese occupation]]
and contested after 1948 by the communist insurgency of the Malayan Emergency.

Independence came in 1957 (Malaya) and the wider federation of Malaysia in 1963; Singapore separated in
1965. Communal balance among its ethnic communities remains the central theme of the Malaysian state.
It is a founding member of [[asean-and-regionalism|ASEAN]].
