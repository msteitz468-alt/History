# Ben Jonson

Playwright, poet, actor, and classical scholar (c. 1572–1637). Shakespeare's younger contemporary and friend/rival. Jonson wrote for multiple companies, became the leading writer of court masques under James and Charles, and in 1616 published his *Works* in folio — a bold claim for the literary status of plays. He famously contributed the prefatory poem "To the memory of my beloved, The Author Mr. William Shakespeare" to the 1623 Folio, praising him as "not of an age, but for all time."

Jonson was more explicitly neoclassical and satirical than Shakespeare; their temperaments and methods differed, yet they worked in the same ecosystem and respected each other's craft. Anecdotes (drinking bouts, the "second-best bed" story) are late. Jonson's *Timber* contains the most important contemporary critical remarks on Shakespeare.
