# Jebe

**Jebe** ("The Arrow") was one of Chinggis Khan's **dörben noqas** — the vanguard commander
at **[[hubs/warfare/high-middle-ages/mongol-conquests/battle-of-chakirmaut-1204|Chakirma'ut (1204)]]**
and, with **[[subedei|Sübedei]]**, leader of the legendary pursuit of Muhammad Khwarazmshah II
and the western reconnaissance that destroyed the Rus'–Kipchak coalition at the
**[[battle-of-the-kalka-river-1223|Kalka River (1223)]]**. [[may-mongol-art-of-war-2016|May]]
cites the campaign as proof of Mongol **feigned-retreat** doctrine and leader-pursuit SOP.
