# Edwin Catmull

**Edwin Earl Catmull** (born 31 March 1945) is an American computer scientist and former president of Pixar and Walt Disney Animation Studios. Co-founder of Pixar; key figure in the technical and creative development of computer-generated imagery. In Isaacson, Catmull represents the successful fusion of technology and art at Pixar that paralleled and influenced Jobs’s thinking. Remained a trusted colleague after Disney acquisition.
