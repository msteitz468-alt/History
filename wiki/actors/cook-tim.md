# Tim Cook

**Timothy Donald Cook** (born 1 November 1961) is an American business executive. Joined Apple in 1998 as head of operations under Steve Jobs; became COO and later succeeded Jobs as CEO in 2011. Praised in Isaacson for operational excellence, calm execution, and supply-chain mastery that supported Jobs’s product vision.
