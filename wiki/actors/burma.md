# Burma (Myanmar)

The westernmost Southeast Asian state, conquered piecemeal by Britain in three [[british-burma|Anglo-Burmese Wars]]
(1824–1885) and governed as a province of British India until 1937. Colonial rule dismantled the
monarchy, imported Indian labour and capital (producing acute plural-society tensions), and provoked
both Buddhist-led and Thakin nationalist resistance.

[[aung-san|Aung San]]'s generation won independence in 1948 after the [[japanese-occupation-southeast-asia|Japanese occupation]],
but the new state fractured along ethnic lines and fell to military rule in 1962, beginning the long
isolation that shaped modern Myanmar.
