# Oleg of Kiev
## Biography / Description
**Oleg** (often called Oleg the Prophet or Oleg the Wise) was the first historical ruler of the Kievan state, reigning from approximately 882 CE until his death in 912 or 913 CE. According to the *Primary Chronicle* (Laurentian Text, ll. 3000–6000), Oleg was a Varangian commander and a relative of Rurik, who assumed the regency for Rurik’s young son, Igor. Oleg consolidated Scandinavian and Slavic control over the Dnieper River basin, laying the political foundations for [[kievan-rus|Kievan Rus′]]. "Oleg set himself up as prince in Kiev, and declared that it should be the mother of Russian cities." He led the 907 expedition and concluded the 911/912 treaty with Byzantium; died from serpent bite per prophecy from his horse.

## Key Decisions and Achievements
* **Succession and march south (c. 879–882):** Rurik on deathbed "bequeathed his realm to Oleg, who belonged to his kin, and entrusted to Oleg’s hands his son Igor’, for he was very young." Oleg leads Varangians/Chuds/Slavs/Merians/Krivichians; captures Smolensk (garrison), Lyubech (garrison). At Kiev: hides warriors in boats; tricks Askold/Dir ("representing himself as a stranger on his way to Greece on an errand for Oleg and for Igor’, the prince’s son"). "You are not princes nor even of princely stock, but I am of princely birth." Igor brought forward "the son of Rurik." Kills and buries them on hill (later Hungarian/Ol'ma castle site; Dir behind St. Irene’s). "Oleg set himself up as prince in Kiev, and declared that it should be the mother of Russian cities. The Varangians, Slavs, and others who accompanied him, were called Russes." Builds stockaded towns; tribute on Slavs/Krivichians/Merians; Novgorod 300 grivny/year to Varangians "until the death of Yaroslav" (ll. 3308–3342).
* **Tribal Subjugation and Tribute (883–885):** "Oleg began military operations against the Derevlians, and after conquering them he imposed upon them the tribute of a black marten-skin apiece." Attacks Severians, light tribute, "forbade their further payment of tribute to the Khazars, on the ground that there was no reason for them to pay it as long as the Khazars were his enemies." Radimichians switch from Khazars to shilling each. "Thus Oleg established his authority over the Polyanians, the Derevlians, the Severians, and the Radimichians, but he waged war with the Ulichians and the Tivercians" (ll. 3343–3356).
* **Campaign against Constantinople (907 CE) and treaty (911/912):** With 2000 ships (all listed tribes, "Great Scythia" per Greeks), disembarks, wheels on ships + favorable wind to city. Greeks fear; poison attempt foiled; "This is not Oleg, but St. Demetrius". Halt for tribute (12 grivny per man, 40 per ship). Detailed terms (grain, 6-mo supplies, baths for Russes; Russes not enter unarmed >50 at time via one gate; city lists incl. Kiev/Chernigov etc.). Russes swear "by their weapons and by their god Perun, as well as by Volos, the god of cattle". Sails brocade (Russes)/silk (Slavs); wind tears silk; "The Russes hung their shields upon the gates as a sign of victory". Full 6420 (911/912) treaty text: peace/amity; murder (death or kin share); assault (5 lbs silver); theft (3x or resist-kill no penalty); shipwreck aid/pilotage; prisoner ransom (20 bezants); inheritance; voluntary service; etc. Envoys honored with gold/palls/robes/relics (crown/nails/purple robe/saints' bones) (ll. 3478–3717).
* **Death (912):** Magicians foretell death "from the steed which you love". Never mounts; years pass. Horse dead; laughs "Soothsayers tell untruths... This horse is dead, but I am still alive." Stamps skull: "So I was supposed to receive my death from this skull?" Serpent bites; sickens/dies. Buried Shchekovitsa hill ("His tomb stands there to this day, and it is called the Tomb of Oleg"). 33 years reign. Digression on Apollonius of Tyana magic (ll. 3723–3814).

## Historical Significance
Oleg's reign marks the transition of the Rus' from scattered, raiding bands into a organized, territorial state. By unifying Kiev and Novgorod, he secured the entire length of the vital trade route **"from the Varangians to the Greeks"** (l. 757). 

His treaty of 911 CE represented the first formal international recognition of the Kievan state and established highly developed commercial and legal norms. He was succeeded by Rurik's son, Igor.
