# Narragansett

Southern New England Algonquian nation of Narragansett Bay; major wampum producers and, after the [[pequot-war-1637]], primary wampum source for [[mohawk]] / Five Nations during the [[beaver-wars-17th-century]] (Salisbury).

## Overview

Dutch found commercial value in Narragansett Bay/Long Island Sound shell beads by mid-1620s; specialized winter wampum production. After 1616–19 epidemics, Narragansetts were among groups pressing survivors. Co-attacked Pequots at Mystic 1637; thereafter supervised some Pequot survivors and outbid Mohawk gifts to kill Pequot fugitives. For >two decades supplied wampum to Mohawks for Beaver Wars; received furs for English goods—client pattern of tense interdependence: Massachusetts/Connecticut limited Narragansett land/influence but depended on them for trade and Mohawk ties. Archaeology: large cemeteries reflecting contact mortality, burials with European goods and wampum. Entered violent opposition in [[king-philips-war-1675]]; defeated with other southern New England Algonquians. Descendants endure in Rhode Island.
