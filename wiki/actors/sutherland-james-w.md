## Role and Background

Lieutenant General James W. Sutherland commanded the US Army's XXIV Corps, which provided artillery, aviation, engineer, and logistical support from South Vietnamese territory for the February–March 1971 [[hubs/warfare/cold-war/vietnam-war/lam-son-719|Lam Son 719]] incursion into Laos — an operation conducted entirely by South Vietnamese (ARVN) ground troops under the Vietnamization policy, with no US ground forces permitted across the border.

## Lam Son 719 (1971)

Sutherland's XXIV Corps ran parallel to, rather than under, the ARVN I Corps command of Lieutenant General Hoang Xuan Lam, a split the operation's own after-action assessments identified as a critical unity-of-command failure: US and ARVN forces operated under separate chains with no single combined commander, limiting the coordination of American fire support with ARVN maneuver as the operation ran into determined NVA resistance around Tchepone.
