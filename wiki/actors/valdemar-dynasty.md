# Valdemar Dynasty of Denmark

The dynasty that brought Denmark to its high-medieval peak. **Valdemar I the
Great** (r. 1157–82), ending civil war, and his sons Knud VI and **Valdemar II
the Victorious** (r. 1202–41) extended Danish power around the Baltic and codified
provincial law (the Law of Jutland, 1241). After a 13th–14th-century crisis and
near-dissolution, **Valdemar IV Atterdag** (r. 1340–75) reconstituted the kingdom;
his daughter [[margaret-i-of-denmark|Margaret I]] carried the line into the
[[kalmar-union-1397|Kalmar Union]].
