# George G. Meade

Union general, commander of the Army of the Potomac from 1863. Best known for his victory at Gettysburg (July 1863), where he defeated Robert E. Lee's invasion of Pennsylvania but was criticized for not aggressively pursuing the retreating Confederates. Later served under Grant in the Overland Campaign and the siege of Petersburg. Competent, cautious, and often overshadowed by more flamboyant figures.

## Role
Exemplifies the professional, engineering-trained Union generals who eventually wore down the Confederacy through superior resources and persistent pressure, despite political and command frictions.
