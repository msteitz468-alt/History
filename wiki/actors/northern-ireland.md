# Northern Ireland

The six-county region of north-east Ireland retained within the United Kingdom when the island was
partitioned in 1921, created to secure a Protestant-unionist majority ([[ulster-unionism]]). Governed
from Stormont by the Ulster Unionist Party, its structural discrimination against the Catholic-nationalist
minority produced the civil-rights crisis and the thirty-year conflict known as [[the-troubles]]
(c. 1968–1998). Direct rule from London (1972) followed the collapse of Stormont; the
[[good-friday-agreement-1998|Good Friday Agreement of 1998]] established power-sharing and largely
ended the violence.
