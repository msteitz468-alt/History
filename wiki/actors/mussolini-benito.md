# Benito Mussolini

Italian dictator, founder of Fascism. Rose to power 1922 (March on Rome); established one-party state; pursued aggressive foreign policy (Ethiopia, intervention in Spain, alliance with Hitler). Overthrown 1943, rescued by Germans, executed by partisans 1945. Model for other authoritarian movements; his regime's military performance was weak relative to rhetoric.
