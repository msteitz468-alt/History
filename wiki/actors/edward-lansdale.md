# Edward Lansdale

U.S. Air Force officer and CIA operative who played a prominent role in the American advisory and psychological warfare effort in Vietnam, especially in support of Ngo Dinh Diem in the mid-1950s. He is often (though not exclusively) identified as a model for Graham Greene’s Alden Pyle in *The Quiet American*.

## Role

Lansdale advocated civic action, psychological operations, and the building of a non-Communist Vietnamese alternative to both the French and the Viet Minh. He was deeply involved in the consolidation of Diem’s power after Geneva and in early counterinsurgency thinking.
