# Orestes

**Orestes**, son of Agamemnon and Clytemnestra in Greek myth, avenged his father's murder
by killing his mother and her lover Aegisthus — the central subject of Aeschylus's
*Oresteia* and a major figure across Greek tragedy. Beyond his literary significance,
Orestes played a distinctive role in real historical politics: according to Herodotus
(1.67–68), the Spartans, on the advice of the Delphic oracle, located and retrieved bones
identified as those of Orestes from Tegea in the mid-6th century BCE. Possession of the
relic was presented as securing divine sanction for Spartan hegemony over the
Peloponnese, illustrating how mythological genealogy and claimed relics were mobilized as
tools of interstate legitimacy in archaic Greece — a theme relevant to the formation of
the [[peloponnesian-league|Peloponnesian League]].
