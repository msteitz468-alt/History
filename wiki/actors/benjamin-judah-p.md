# Judah P. Benjamin

Confederate Secretary of State (and earlier War/Attorney General). The most capable Confederate diplomat; Jewish, Louisiana, British-educated. Handled the difficult European correspondence, including the 1863 dispatch on the slave trade that was seized upon by Union advocates. After the war lived in Britain and became a leading barrister (QC).

## In Bonner's Account

Benjamin is the professional face of Confederate foreign policy; his pragmatic dispatches contrast with the fire-eater rhetoric that damaged the cause. His slave-trade memo became evidence in the pariah case.
