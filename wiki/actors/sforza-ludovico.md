# Ludovico Sforza

Ludovico Sforza (1452–1508), known as “il Moro,” was de facto ruler of Milan from 1481 and Duke from 1494 until the French conquest of 1499. He was Leonardo da Vinci’s most important early patron, employing him as court artist, festival designer, military consultant, and engineer. Leonardo’s long Milan residence (1482–1499) and many of his major projects (including the *Last Supper* and the Sforza Horse) were made possible by Sforza support.
