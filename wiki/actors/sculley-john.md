# John Sculley

**John Sculley** (born 6 April 1939) is an American businessman. Former PepsiCo president hired by Steve Jobs as Apple CEO in 1983. Oversaw the Macintosh launch and later the power struggle that resulted in Jobs’s ouster in 1985. His tenure is portrayed in Isaacson as a clash between consumer-marketing professionalism and Jobs’s product-obsessed, artist-CEO style. Later ventures in technology and venture capital.
