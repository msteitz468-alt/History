# Roger Hilsman

Assistant Secretary of State for Far Eastern Affairs under Kennedy. Author of the pivotal August 1963 "green light" cable that signaled to the Saigon embassy and the Vietnamese generals that the U.S. would not oppose (and would support) a coup against Diem if the generals could succeed.

## Role in the Thesis of the Source

Cheevers presents Hilsman (with Michael Forrestal) as the driving bureaucratic force behind active U.S. encouragement of the plot, operating with less than full presidential control. JFK reportedly recognized the cable as a mistake but did not override it. Hilsman's activism is contrasted with the more cautious Nolting/Harkins line.
