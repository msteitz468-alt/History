# Harald Fairhair (Harald Hárfagri)

By later saga tradition (notably the *Heimskringla*) the first king to unify
[[norway|Norway]], said to have subdued the petty kings after the sea battle of
Hafrsfjord (conventionally dated c. 872–900) and to have prompted emigration to
[[iceland|Iceland]] by his consolidation of power. Modern scholarship treats much
of the tradition as later construction projecting a 13th-century ideal of unified
monarchy back onto a still-fragmented 9th–10th-century reality; his real authority
was probably confined to western and southern Norway.

## Counterfactual Significance

Whether or not the unification narrative is historically accurate, the *Harald
Fairhair tradition* became the legitimising charter of the medieval Norwegian
monarchy — the dynastic origin myth later kings invoked. See
[[helle-cambridge-history-scandinavia-v1-2003]].
