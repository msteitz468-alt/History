# Joseph Goebbels

Reich Minister for Popular Enlightenment and Propaganda (1933–1945), Gauleiter
of Berlin, and the Third Reich's chief manager of opinion, culture, and media.
A failed novelist with a doctorate in German literature, Goebbels built the
propaganda apparatus that coordinated press, radio, film, and the arts through
the Reich Culture Chamber, staged the regime's mass rituals, and cultivated the
"Hitler myth." He was a principal instigator of the November 1938 pogrom
([[kristallnacht-1938]]) and, with Hitler and [[goring-hermann|Göring]], one of
the small circle Evans identifies as actually driving the regime from above.

## The propaganda empire (Evans, 2005)

Appointed to the new Reich Ministry of Popular Enlightenment and Propaganda
13 March 1933, defining its task as the "spiritual mobilization" of the
people; his doctrine: "the secret of propaganda [is] to permeate the person it
aims to grasp, without his even noticing that he is being permeated." The
**Reich Culture Chamber** (22 September 1933, seven sub-chambers; membership
compulsory to work in any cultural field) was his key instrument of
[[gleichschaltung|coordination]] and the exclusion of Jews and dissidents from
cultural life, and his decisive victory over Rosenberg. Film (Reich Cinema Law
1934; Film Credit Bank financing ~75% of features by 1936 — political films
*fell* from 24% to 10% of output by 1938 as entertainment carried the
message); radio (the subsidized *Volksempfänger* put a set in over 70% of
households by 1939, the world's highest rate; "Don't become boring!"); the
press (Editors' Law, 4 October 1933; ~1,300 Jewish and left journalists barred
within two years; German newspapers fell from 4,700 in 1932 to 977 by 1944;
Amann controlled two-thirds of the press by 1939); art criticism banned
outright (27 November 1936). He organized both the
[[degenerate-art-exhibition-1937|Degenerate Art Exhibition]] (cynically — he
privately did not share Hitler's hatred of modernism) and its "Great German
Art" counterpart, and forced the resignations of Richard Strauss (1935, over
the Zweig letter) and Furtwängler (1934, over Hindemith). His maxim: "politics
is actually the greatest and noblest of the arts... to convert an amorphous
mass into a living people."

## Antisemitic escalation

Recorded Hitler's intent in his diary (29 November 1937): "The Jews must get
out of Germany, indeed out of Europe altogether... The Leader is firmly
resolved on it." Ran the summer 1938 Berlin action ("drive the Jews out of
Berlin... without any sentimentality"); on 9 November 1938 received Hitler's
instructions and orchestrated [[kristallnacht-1938|Kristallnacht]], staging it
as spontaneous popular rage — his diary records the glee ("Bravo! Bravo!") and
his reply to news of the first death that "thousands of Jews were going to cop
it in the coming days" — then managed the mendacious press aftermath and
co-drove the 12 November expropriation conference. In 1939 his ministry
manufactured the anti-Czech and anti-Polish atrocity campaigns that dressed
the road to war.

## Wealth

Declared income of 619 RM in 1932; within years drew 300,000 RM/year from
Amann for weekly articles and acquired a Schwanenwerder villa forcibly sold by
its Jewish owner — yet counted in popular opinion among the *less* corrupt
Nazi leaders.

## The Campaign Years — Evans, *The Coming of the Third Reich* (2003)

Directed the 1930 and 1932 campaigns from Munich with fresh-slogan directives;
manufactured the Horst Wessel martyr cult ("Through sacrifice to redemption";
the Wessel song made party anthem — see [[sturmabteilung-sa]]). Diary
punctuation of the endgame: "Fantastic... an unbelievable advance" (Sept
1930); on the SPD's passivity in the Prussian coup, "You only have to bare
your teeth at the reds and they knuckle under... The reds have missed their
big chance. It's never going to come again" (20 July 1932); after July 1932,
"We won't get to an absolute majority this way... Now deeds!"; after November
1932, "We have suffered a set-back." In 1933: the 17 April diary entry laying
the May Day trap for the unions ("On 2 May the trade union offices will be
occupied... then they will belong to us" — see
[[destruction-of-german-trade-unions-1933]]), "We are the masters of Germany"
(3 May), and "The road to the total state. Our revolution has an uncanny
dynamism" (28 June).

## Historiography

Longerich's biography (2010) and the surviving diaries (ed. Fröhlich) are the
foundations; the diaries are indispensable but self-dramatizing and require
constant source criticism.

## From Evans, *The Third Reich at War* (2008)

The bombing war made him the regime's de facto domestic crisis manager: chair of the Inter-Ministerial Bomb Damage Committee (January 1943) and of Hitler's Reich Inspectorate for Civil Air War Measures (December 1943, after the Kassel firestorm exposed Gauleiter Weinrich), though Göring and Himmler blocked total control. His diary registers the helplessness ("We find ourselves in a situation of helpless inferiority . . . and have to receive the blows of the English and Americans with dogged fury") and his frustration at Hitler's withdrawal ("Everybody does and leaves undone what he pleases . . . because there's no strong authority anywhere"; "Everything goes through Bormann"). The 18 February 1943 Sportpalast "total war" speech — staged before 14,000 hand-picked loyalists, "a feat of mass hypnosis" — brought him nearer the center of economic management than ever before (see [[total-war]]); his deliberate slip of the same day's month — "exter…[correcting himself] exclusion of Jewry" — enlisted the audience in the open secret (see [[the-holocaust]]). His propaganda framed Allied bombers as gangsters and aristocrats serving "Jewish conspirators," and from 1944 incited the lynching of downed airmen. Source: [[evans-third-reich-at-war-2008]]

## Total war plenipotentiary and death — Evans (2008), Part 7

Appointed Reich Plenipotentiary for the Total War Effort (18 July 1944), he combed out ~450,000 men for the front by gutting culture, consumer trades, and administration, and judged the failed July Plot to have "had a cleansing effect." Named Reich Chancellor in Hitler's testament, he refused the order to leave Berlin ("a life which for me personally has no further value if it cannot be used in the service of the Leader"). On the evening of 30 April–1 May 1945 an SS doctor sedated the six Goebbels children with morphine and Stumpfegger crushed prussic-acid capsules in their mouths — Magda Goebbels had written, "The world that will come after the Leader and National Socialism will not be worth living in, and therefore I have taken my children away" — before the parents' own suicides in the Chancellery garden. Source: [[evans-third-reich-at-war-2008]]
