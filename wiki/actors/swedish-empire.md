# Swedish Empire (stormaktstiden, c. 1611–1721)

Sweden's "Age of Greatness" — the period when the Swedish realm (including
Finland and Baltic provinces) was a European great power and the dominant force
in the Baltic (*dominium maris baltici*). Built on the military reforms of
[[gustavus-adolphus|Gustavus Adolphus]], the fiscal-military apparatus directed
by Axel Oxenstierna, and victories in the [[thirty-years-war-1618|Thirty Years'
War]], the empire reached its height under Karl X Gustav and Karl XI before
collapsing in the [[great-northern-war-1700|Great Northern War]] (1700–21), which
ended Sweden's great-power status. Domestically the era saw the turn to
[[scandinavian-absolutism|royal absolutism]] under Karl XI (1680).
