# John Churchill, 1st Duke of Marlborough (1650–1722)

The pre-eminent allied commander of the **[[war-of-the-spanish-succession-1701|War of the Spanish
Succession]]** (1701–1714) and, for Doughty & Gruber, the soldier who showed how **decisive battle
could still be sought within the age of [[limited-war|limited war]]**. Against the prevailing
caution of siege-and-maneuver warfare, Marlborough married strategic mobility (the famous march to
the Danube), close coalition cooperation with **Prince Eugene of Savoy**, and aggressive
combined-arms tactics to win a string of victories — above all **[[hubs/warfare/age-of-expansion/war-of-the-spanish-succession/battle-of-blenheim|Blenheim]]** (1704), which broke Franco-Bavarian power
on the Danube and ended the myth of French invincibility under Louis XIV.
