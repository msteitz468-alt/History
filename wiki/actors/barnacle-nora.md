# Nora Barnacle (Joyce)

Life partner (from 1904) and later wife (1931) of James Joyce (1884–1951). Born in Galway, worked as a chambermaid in Dublin when she met Joyce. Left Ireland with him and shared the entire exile life in Pola, Trieste, Zurich, and Paris. Bore two children (Giorgio and Lucia). Provided emotional and domestic stability that made Joyce's sustained work possible. Her speech, personality, and relationship with Joyce are woven throughout his fiction (especially Molly Bloom in *Ulysses*).

Ellmann and later Brenda Maddox (*Nora*, 1988) established her central importance; earlier accounts often diminished her.
