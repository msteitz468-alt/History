# Anne Hathaway

Wife of William Shakespeare (c. 1555/6–1623), from Shottery near Stratford. Married at 26/27 to the 18-year-old Shakespeare in late 1582 (special licence, suggesting haste or pregnancy); Susanna born May 1583, twins in 1585. She outlived her husband by seven years. The only direct documentary trace of the marriage is the bond and the later will (the notorious "second-best bed" bequest). Almost nothing is known of her personality or daily life. Later traditions and biographical speculation (the "older woman" narrative, the nature of the marriage) are largely unsupported by evidence.
