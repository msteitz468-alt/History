# Joseph P. Tumulty (1879–1954)

Wilson's private secretary (and earlier NJ political aide); Catholic Democrat who managed access, press, and much congressional liaison. Cox uses Tumulty's papers and correspondence as a primary window on day-to-day suffrage politics inside the White House — often more flexible than Wilson, often overruled.
