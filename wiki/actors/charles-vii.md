# Charles VII of France

Charles VII (r. 1422–1461), known as "the Victorious" (*le Victorieux*) or "the Well-Served" (*le Bien-Servi*), was the Valois king whose reign saw the salvation of the French monarchy, the final expulsion of the English from France (except Calais), and the restoration of royal sovereignty.
