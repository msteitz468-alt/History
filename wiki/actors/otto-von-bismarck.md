# Otto von Bismarck

The Prussian statesman who unified Germany and dominated European diplomacy for a generation. As
Minister-President of [[prussia|Prussia]] from 1862, he defied parliament to build the army and
practiced *Realpolitik* — "blood and iron" — engineering three wars (against Denmark 1864, Austria in
the [[austro-prussian-war-1866]], and France in the [[franco-prussian-war-1870]]) that produced the
[[german-empire|German Empire]] under Prussian leadership in 1871.

As imperial Chancellor (1871–90) he waged the [[kulturkampf]] against political Catholicism, outlawed
the Socialists while co-opting workers with the first modern welfare insurance, and built an intricate
alliance system to keep France isolated and preserve the European balance. His dismissal by the young
**[[wilhelm-ii|Wilhelm II]]** in 1890 removed the restraining hand from German foreign policy.
Bismarck's authoritarian-yet-modernizing settlement is the pivot of the [[german-sonderweg|Sonderweg
debate]]: did he found a modern nation-state, or entrench an illiberal order that led toward 1933?
