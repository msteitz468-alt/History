# George C. Marshall

**George C. Marshall** (1880–1959) was US Army Chief of Staff throughout the Second World
War, overseeing the wartime expansion and global deployment of American forces, and later
served [[truman-harry-s|President Truman]] as Secretary of State (1947–1949) and Secretary
of Defense (1950–1951).

## Role

As Secretary of State, Marshall gave his name to the [[events/marshall-plan-1948|Marshall
Plan]] (European Recovery Program), the large-scale US aid program for the reconstruction
of Western Europe, conceived as both a humanitarian response to postwar devastation and a
strategic bulwark against Communist political gains — a foundational act of the
containment-era international order. He was the only professional soldier to win the
Nobel Peace Prize (1953), awarded explicitly for the Plan. Winston Churchill called him
the "organizer of victory" for his wartime role.
