# Elon Musk

**Summary actor page.** See detailed analytic biography: [[hubs/biographies/contemporary/musk-elon|Elon Musk — Detailed Biography]].

Elon Reeve Musk (b. 28 June 1971, Pretoria, South Africa) is an engineer, entrepreneur, and founder or co-founder of Zip2, X.com (later PayPal), SpaceX, Tesla Inc., SolarCity (merged into Tesla Energy), The Boring Company, Neuralink, and xAI. He acquired and transformed Twitter into X (2022). His companies pursue vertical integration, first-principles engineering, and missions at civilizational scale: sustainable energy/transport, making humanity multi-planetary, brain–machine interfaces, and maximum truth-seeking AI.

## Formation
Born to Errol (engineer, fantasist) and Maye (model and dietitian) Musk. Childhood marked by severe bullying, a paramilitary veldskool, a near-fatal dog attack, and intense psychological abuse from his father. Emigrated to Canada (1989), then the United States. Attended Queen’s University and the University of Pennsylvania; briefly enrolled at Stanford before dropping out to found Zip2.

## Companies and Turning Points
- Zip2 (1995–1999): sold for ~$307M; Musk’s first fortune.
- X.com / PayPal (1999–2000): co-founded; ousted in the “coup” but the merged entity became PayPal.
- SpaceX (2002–): founded to make life multi-planetary. Early Falcon 1 failures nearly bankrupted the company; fourth launch succeeded (2008). Developed Falcon 9, Dragon, Starlink constellation, and Starship.
- Tesla (2004–): joined as chairman/investor, became CEO. Roadster (2008), Model S, Fremont factory “hell,” Shanghai Gigafactory, Cybertruck, Autopilot/FSD, Optimus robot.
- Twitter / X (2022–): hostile acquisition for $44B; rebranded, implemented “hardcore” mode, mass layoffs, content moderation reversals, Community Notes emphasis.
- Other: SolarCity/Tesla Energy, The Boring Company, Neuralink, xAI (2023).

## Character and Method
Isaacson documents Musk’s “adversity shaped me” psychology: high pain threshold, siege mentality, drama-seeking, batch processing, “demon mode,” and simultaneous management of multiple high-stakes companies. Signature methods include first-principles reasoning, the “idiot index,” extreme deletion of parts/processes, and vertical integration of product + factory.

## Scale and Legacy
Scale: global (EV transition acceleration, reusable orbital rockets, global low-latency internet via Starlink, platform governance debates). As of 2023 he was simultaneously running six companies with ambitions spanning energy, space, AI, infrastructure, and neurotechnology.
