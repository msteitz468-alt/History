# John Lasseter

**John Alan Lasseter** (born 12 January 1957) is an American animator, director, and former chief creative officer of Pixar and Walt Disney Animation Studios. Key creative force behind *Toy Story* and Pixar’s feature films. In Isaacson, Lasseter embodies the artistic perfectionism that resonated with Jobs; their partnership at Pixar influenced Jobs’s later design leadership at Apple.
