# Russian Empire

The **Russian Empire** was a historical state that existed from 1721 to 1917, ruling vast territories across Europe, Asia, and North America.

## Biography / Description
The Russian Empire was proclaimed by Peter the Great on November 2, 1721, following the Treaty of Nystad which concluded the Great Northern War. Peter was declared "Emperor" by the Senate, succeeding the Tsardom of Russia. 

The empire was governed as an absolute monarchy by the Romanov dynasty. It grew to be the third-largest empire in history, stretching from the Baltic Sea to the Pacific Ocean. It was dissolved during the February Revolution of 1917, when Nicholas II abdicated.

## Governing Institutions
* **Governing Senate (1711):** Established by Peter I as the supreme administrative and judicial organ of the empire, overseen by an Ober-Procurator ("the sovereign's eye").
* **Collegia / Colleges (1717):** Board-based ministerial departments that replaced the old Muscovite *prikazy*.
* **Holy Synod (1721):** A state-controlled commission that replaced the Patriarchate, placing the Russian Orthodox Church under direct secular oversight.
* **Provinces:** The empire was subdivided into provinces (gubernias), reorganised by Catherine the Great in 1775 into 50 provinces with class-based courts and Marshals of the Nobility.

## Demographics & Finance
* **Population Growth:** The empire grew from 13 million subjects in 1725 to 19 million in 1762, and 36 million in 1796 (spurred by the partitions of Poland and conquest of Crimean territories). By 1917, the population exceeded 170 million.
* **Fiscal Structure:** Driven primarily by the introduction of the poll (head) tax (1718–1722) which merged slaves and serfs into a single tax bracket. A massive portion of imperial spending (often 46%) was dedicated to maintaining the army and navy.
