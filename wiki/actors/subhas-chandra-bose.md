# Subhas Chandra Bose

**Subhas Chandra Bose** (1897–1945), "Netaji", was the radical nationalist who broke with Gandhi and the Congress high command over the pace of confrontation with Britain, formed the Indian National Army (INA) with Japanese support during World War II, and became the iconic alternative to Gandhian non-violence for many younger nationalists and soldiers.

Guha's volume covers Bose's early Congress career, the 1939 Tripuri crisis and split, his dramatic escape, the INA, and Gandhi's complex attitude (public condemnation of collaboration with the Axis combined with private recognition of Bose's sacrifice and the boost the INA trials gave to post-war British exit).
