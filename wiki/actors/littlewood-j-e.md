# J. E. Littlewood

John Edensor Littlewood (1885–1977), English mathematician, Hardy’s long-time collaborator and the other central figure in the Hardy–Littlewood school. When Ramanujan’s letter arrived, Hardy immediately consulted Littlewood; together they began the work of understanding and editing the results. Littlewood later contributed important analytic work on the circle method that helped prove some of Ramanujan’s partition conjectures. He was part of the Cambridge circle that received and supported Ramanujan.
