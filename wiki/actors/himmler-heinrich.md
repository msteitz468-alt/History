# Heinrich Himmler

Reichsführer-SS from 1929; from 1936 Chief of German Police, uniting the
[[schutzstaffel-ss|SS]], [[gestapo|Gestapo]], criminal police, and concentration
camp system into a single ideological police empire outside normal state and
judicial control. With his deputy [[heydrich-reinhard|Reinhard Heydrich]] he
built the instrument of terror in peacetime that became the executive organ of
genocide in war ([[the-holocaust]]).

## Building the police empire (Evans, 2005)

Headed the political police of every German state except Prussia by early
1934; Göring handed him the Prussian Gestapo on 20 April 1934 — completing the
centralization on the eve of the [[night-of-the-long-knives-1934|Röhm purge]],
whose fabricated "putsch" evidence he manufactured with
[[heydrich-reinhard|Heydrich]]. As Chief of German Police from 1936 he turned
the mission from suppressing the crushed left to "purging the German race":
the 1937–38 "asocial" and "habitual criminal" round-ups that refilled the
[[nazi-concentration-camps|camps]], the 8 December 1938 Gypsy decree (already
speaking of "the final solution of the Gypsy question"), and the persecution
of homosexual men (his February 1937 SS speech: offenders to a camp, "shot
while trying to escape"). His midnight meeting with Hitler on 9 November 1938
produced the Gestapo telex ordering 20–30,000 arrests during
[[kristallnacht-1938|Kristallnacht]].

## Ideology

An ex-Catholic converted to racial-agrarian mysticism under
[[darre-richard-walther|Darré]]'s influence, he modelled the SS as a
quasi-religious order (left the Church 1936; SS Christmas replaced by
Midsummer; >25% of the SS registered *gottgläubig* by 1938) and founded the
*Lebensborn* homes (1936) — a breeding project that failed on its own terms
(his 115,690 married SS men averaged 1.1 children). Recruited the nobility
aggressively: by 1938 nearly a fifth of senior SS ranks were titled.

## Historiography

Longerich's *Heinrich Himmler* (2008/2012) is standard; earlier treatments
(Höhne, *The Order of the Death's Head*) remain useful for the SS apparatus.

## From Evans, *The Third Reich at War* (2008)

The volume's index of his war: the Einsatzgruppen escalation orders and inspection tours of 1941 ("all Jewish men must be shot. Drive Jewish women into the marshes"); the commissioning of Bełżec (13 October 1941) and the drive behind [[operation-reinhard-1942|Operation Reinhard]] and the ghetto liquidations; the Posen speech (4 October 1943) calling the extermination "a laudable page in our history that will never be written"; the camp-evacuation and gas-chamber-demolition orders of late 1944 and the [[death-marches-1944|death marches]]; Reich Interior Minister from August 1943 and Replacement Army commander after the July Plot; the final-weeks terror order that "where a white flag appears, all the male persons of the house concerned are to be shot." His Bernadotte negotiations (releasing 4,000 Scandinavian prisoners, then offering surrender to the West) were Hitler's "most shameful betrayal in human history"; stripped of all offices in the testament, he fled in disguise, was caught at a British checkpoint, and bit a cyanide capsule at Lüneburg on 23 May 1945, aged 44.
