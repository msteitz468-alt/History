# Raoul Salan

French general who succeeded de Lattre de Tassigny as commander in Indochina (1952–53). Known as “Le Chinois” or “Le Mandarin” for his long service in the region and inscrutable style. He conducted the Northwest Campaign period and oversaw the successful defense at Na San.

## Role

Salan was a cautious, experienced colonial soldier. He presided over the period of French tactical recovery after de Lattre’s death and the apparent vindication of the hedgehog/base aéro-terrestre concept at Na San. He was later involved in the planning that led to Dien Bien Phu under Navarre.
