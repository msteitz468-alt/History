# Jean Sainteny

French colonial official and Commissioner in Hanoi (1945–46). He developed the closest working relationship any senior French figure achieved with Ho Chi Minh during the critical 1945–46 period and was the main French negotiator of the March 6, 1946 Preliminary Convention.

## Role

Sainteny was the primary French interlocutor with Ho in the months after the Japanese surrender. He negotiated the March 6, 1946 agreement that recognized the “Republic of Vietnam” as a “free state” within the Indochinese Federation and French Union in exchange for the return of French troops to the north. He later helped negotiate the September 1946 Modus Vivendi.

## Assessment

Sainteny was more willing than many of his colleagues (especially High Commissioner d’Argenlieu) to explore genuine political compromise. His efforts were repeatedly undermined by hard-liners in Saigon and Paris. After the outbreak of full war he remained a voice (often ignored) for negotiated solutions.
