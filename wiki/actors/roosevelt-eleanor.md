# Eleanor Roosevelt

**First Lady of the United States (1933–1945)** and leading American political figure in her own right. Transformed the role of First Lady into an active platform for social reform, civil rights, and international human rights. Key political partner to Franklin D. Roosevelt; independent voice on labor, race, refugees, and women's issues. Later U.S. delegate to the United Nations and chair of the committee that drafted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948).
