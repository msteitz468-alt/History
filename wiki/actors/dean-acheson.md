# Dean Acheson

U.S. Secretary of State (1949–1953) under Truman. He was central to the decisions to recognize the Bao Dai government (February 1950) and to begin large-scale U.S. military and economic aid to France in Indochina. He framed the conflict in stark Cold War terms (“outright Commie” regarding Ho).

## Role

Acheson was the key architect of the early U.S. commitment that turned the French colonial war into a front in the global containment struggle. He accepted the French argument that the loss of Indochina would have domino effects far beyond Southeast Asia.
