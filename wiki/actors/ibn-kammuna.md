# Ibn Kammuna

Jewish philosopher and physician active in Baghdad under the Ilkhanate, one of the most significant figures in the transmission and development of Suhrawardi's Illuminationist (*Ishraqi*) philosophy after the founder's death, writing an important commentary on Suhrawardi's *Talwihat*. He is best known for *Tanqih al-Abhath li-l-Milal al-Thalath* ("Examination of the Inquiries into the Three Faiths"), a rare medieval work of comparative religion analyzing and critiquing the truth-claims of Judaism, Christianity, and Islam side by side with notable even-handedness for its time; the work's frank treatment of Islam reportedly provoked a riot against him in Baghdad in 1284, forcing him to flee the city shortly before his death. His writings are a significant source for understanding Illuminationist metaphysics and epistemology (theory of knowledge, divine attributes, causality) as they developed after Suhrawardi.
