# Kingdom of Kerma (Classic Kush)

The **Kingdom of Kerma** — called the **Kingdom of Kush** in Egyptian sources — was the
powerful Nubian state centred on the city of **Kerma**, south of the Third Cataract, which
flourished as Egypt's great southern rival across the third and second millennia BCE. Its
**Classic Kerma** phase corresponds to the Egyptian
[[processes/second-intermediate-period|Second Intermediate Period]], when "the King of Kush"
was at the height of his power. It is the early, **pre-Meroitic** Kushite state — distinct
from the later [[kush-meroe|Napatan–Meroitic Kush]] that would one day rule Egypt.

## A literate Egypt's illiterate rival

"The Kerma people kept no written records," so the kingdom is known archaeologically and
through Egyptian references. At its centre stood an enormous round ceremonial hut within a
stockade, alongside monumental mud-brick sacred and administrative buildings (the *deffufa*);
"an extensive building and rebuilding programme during the Classic Kerma phase testifies to
the immense resources in materials and manpower at the king's disposal." Royal **tumulus
tombs** south of the Third Cataract contained sacrificed retainers and lavish provisions,
many imported from Upper Egypt — material proof of wealth drawn from the **south–north trade**
(gold, ivory, ebony, cattle).

## Egypt's rival in the Second Intermediate Period

With Egypt divided, Kerma pressed north: the abandonment of dedications at
[[places/elephantine|Elephantine]] coincides with the period of maximum Kushite power, and
statues looted from Egyptian sites (e.g. a nomarch of Asyut) ended up in a Kerma royal tomb.
The King of Kush allied with the [[hyksos|Hyksos]] against Thebes — the alliance
[[kamose|Kamose]] tried to sever by intercepting Apepi's letter. **Kerma Nubians** also
served (voluntarily or by levy) in the Theban armies of Kamose and [[ahmose-i|Ahmose]]; "it
seems likely that the Kerma Nubians were a federation of tribes, not all of whom necessarily
accepted the authority of the King of Kerma."

## End

Kerma was finally conquered only under the early **18th Dynasty**, after Ahmose and "at
least three more long campaigns," and thoroughly destroyed — folding Nubia into the
[[new-kingdom-egypt|New Kingdom]] empire under a viceroy of Kush.
