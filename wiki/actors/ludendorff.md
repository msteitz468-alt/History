# Erich Ludendorff

German general and de facto military dictator of Germany in the later years of World War I (with Hindenburg). Mastermind of the 1918 Spring Offensives; advocate of unrestricted submarine warfare and total economic mobilization. After defeat, a leading proponent of the "stab-in-the-back" legend, participant in the Kapp Putsch, and early associate (then rival) of Hitler.

## Significance
Emblematic of the "silent dictatorship" of the German high command, the radicalization of warfare in 1914–1918, and the toxic militarist politics of the Weimar era that helped undermine the republic.
