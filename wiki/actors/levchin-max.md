# Max Levchin

Ukrainian-American software engineer and entrepreneur (b. 1975), co-founder and former chief technology officer of Confinity, the payments/security startup that merged with Elon Musk's X.com in 2000 to become PayPal. Levchin and Musk clashed over technical direction (Unix versus Windows infrastructure) during the run-up to the merger, one of several friction points between the two companies' founding teams.

In September 2000, while Musk was on his honeymoon, Levchin joined with Peter Thiel, Reid Hoffman, and David Sacks in a boardroom move that removed Musk as CEO of the merged company (retaining the PayPal name and product direction Levchin's team had built). Levchin went on to co-found the "PayPal Mafia" network of subsequent technology ventures, including the fraud-detection company Slide and later the consumer-lending company Affirm.

## Significance

Levchin's role in the 2000 "PayPal coup" against Musk is a formative episode in Musk's early career, illustrating the sharp internal conflicts of the dot-com-era startup and the tight-knit "PayPal Mafia" network it produced.
