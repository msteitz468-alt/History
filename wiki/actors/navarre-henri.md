# Henri Navarre

French general (1898–1983) who commanded French Union forces in Indochina from May 1953 until the defeat at Dien Bien Phu. Architect of the "Navarre Plan" — an attempt to regain the initiative through a combination of mobile warfare in the south and a fortified position in the northwest intended to lure the Viet Minh into a decisive battle.

## Role

Appointed after the failure of previous commanders to achieve a military solution. Navarre sought to stabilize the situation long enough for political negotiations or for the Associated States (especially the State of Vietnam under Bao Dai) to build viable national armies. The plan called for concentration of elite units in the Red River delta while using paratroops and air mobility elsewhere; the Dien Bien Phu airhead was the northern anchor meant to block infiltration into Laos and force Giap to fight on French terms.

## Outcome and Assessment

The plan collapsed when Giap accepted the challenge, massed artillery and manpower, and isolated the garrison. Navarre has been criticized for underestimating Viet Minh logistics and artillery capabilities and for political over-optimism about the viability of the "Navarre Plan" under Eisenhower-era U.S. support. He was recalled after the fall.
