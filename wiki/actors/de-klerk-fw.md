# F.W. de Klerk

**Frederik Willem de Klerk** (1936–2021) was the last State President of apartheid-era South Africa (1989–1994) and Deputy President in the Government of National Unity (1994–1996). In February 1990 he unbanned the ANC, PAC and SACP and ordered the release of Nelson Mandela, initiating the negotiations that ended apartheid. Shared the 1993 Nobel Peace Prize with Mandela. His decisions opened the door to the 1994 election; his party participated in the GNU. Meredith presents him as a pragmatic Afrikaner nationalist who recognized the unsustainability of the old order and chose a controlled transition over continued confrontation.
