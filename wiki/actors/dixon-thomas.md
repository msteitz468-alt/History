# Thomas Dixon Jr. (1864–1946)

North Carolina-born novelist and playwright; author of the Reconstruction trilogy (*The Leopard's Spots*, *The Clansman*, *The Traitor*) whose *Clansman* became D. W. Griffith's *The Birth of a Nation*. Johns Hopkins acquaintance of [[wilson-woodrow|Woodrow Wilson]]; Cox traces Dixon's access to Wilson and the 1915 White House screening ([[birth-of-a-nation-white-house-1915]]) as emblematic of the administration's racial culture — not a casual entertainment, but a political signal.
