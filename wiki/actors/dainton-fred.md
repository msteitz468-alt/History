# Fred Dainton

British physical chemist and later prominent science administrator (Chairman of University Grants Committee, etc.). As a young Cambridge researcher supervised and supported Rosalind Franklin; recognized the triviality of the project assigned her by Norrish and backed her in conflicts. Key figure in UK polymer and physical chemistry; later knighted and influential in national research policy.
