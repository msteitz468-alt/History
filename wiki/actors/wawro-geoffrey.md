# Geoffrey Wawro

**Geoffrey Wawro** (b. 1956) is an American military historian and Distinguished Professor of Military History at the University of North Texas. He is the author of numerous works on 19th- and 20th-century warfare, including studies of the Austro-Prussian War, the Franco-Prussian War, World War I, and a major one-volume military history of the Vietnam War (Basic Books, 2024).

## Major Contribution to the Wiki

Wawro's *The Vietnam War: A Military History* (2024) supplies a comprehensive operational narrative of the American involvement in Vietnam, with particular value for the warfare hub on key campaigns and battles (Ia Drang 1965, the Tet Offensive and Khe Sanh 1968, Hamburger Hill 1969, Cambodia/Lam Son context, and the 1975 final offensives). The work emphasizes the political constraints and "war of choice" dynamics that shaped U.S. strategy alongside detailed treatment of ground and air operations, airmobility, ARVN performance under Vietnamization, and the persistent gap between battlefield results and strategic outcome.
