# Piahito (Arikara leader)

Arikara leader from the town of Wahoera. Joined one of the captains' delegations to Washington after the expedition's return journey. Featured in Part IV chapters in Fehrman (2026) illustrating the diplomatic dimension and the limited leverage of the Americans on the middle Missouri.
