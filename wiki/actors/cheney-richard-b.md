# Richard B. "Dick" Cheney

**Dick Cheney** (b. 1941) served as White House Chief of Staff under Gerald Ford, US
Secretary of Defense during the Gulf War (1989–1993), CEO of Halliburton, and Vice
President of the United States (2001–2009) under [[bush-george-w|George W. Bush]] — widely
regarded as the most influential vice president in modern US history.

## Role

Cheney was a central architect of the administration's response to the September 11
attacks and the case for the [[invasion-of-iraq-2003|2003 invasion of Iraq]], arguing
forcefully (alongside claims about weapons of mass destruction and links to al-Qaeda) for
regime change in Baghdad. He was also a leading advocate of expanded executive power in
counterterrorism policy (surveillance, detention, interrogation practices). His influence
waned somewhat in Bush's second term as other advisers gained ground, but he remained a
polarizing figure, defended by supporters as a clear-eyed strategist and criticized by
opponents as the driving force behind a costly and, in retrospect, poorly justified war.
