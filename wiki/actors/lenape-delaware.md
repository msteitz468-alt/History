# Lenape (Delaware)

Algonquian peoples of the Delaware Valley and related Munsee groups of the lower Hudson (colonial “Delawares”). Salisbury uses both Munsee and Delaware strands within [[eastern-woodlands-colonial-encounter-1600-1783]].

## Overview

### New Netherland Munsees

After Manhattan “purchase”/New Amsterdam 1626, initial trade harmony; 1630s fur depletion, Dutch farming, immigrant surge, taxes, and Mahican/Mohawk wampum-tribute demands squeezed Munsees. 1643–45 Munsees and Long Island allies fought Mahicans and Dutch; 1650s “Peach” and Esopus wars ended in defeat. By English conquest 1664 only few lower New Netherland enclaves—most Munsees among northern Mahicans or western Delawares.

### Mid-Atlantic and Walking Purchase

Delawares aided Susquehannocks repelling Iroquois 1663. Welcomed New Sweden 1638. 1729–34 PA proprietors bought >50,000 acres from Delaware leaders; **Nutimus** resisted further cessions; Penn brothers and James Logan with Six Nations cooperation used forged deed and myth of prior Iroquois Delaware conquest for the notorious **Walking Purchase**—Delawares removed; first English-colony settlers crossed Appalachians. Many forced west to Ohio with [[shawnee]], claiming ownership independent of French, English, and Iroquois; joined by Mingos.

### Seven Years’ War and after

Braddock alienated still-willing Delawares 1755; Delawares attacked PA frontier. **Teedyuscung** sought Delaware unity, Wyoming homeland, Walking Purchase investigation, independence from Iroquois. Easton 1758 accepted Iroquois suzerainty as anti-French means. 1762 Connecticut settlers invaded Wyoming, expelled Delawares, burned Teedyuscung alive. Neolin (“Delaware Prophet”) and Fort Pitt smallpox blankets to Delaware delegation shaped [[pontiacs-war-1763]]. Fort Stanwix 1768 ceded Delaware hunting lands via Iroquois. Revolution: mixed Ohio Delaware alignments; pro-American chief White Eyes murdered by American soldiers Sept 1778; Brodhead burned Delaware villages 1779.
