# Waldensians (Poor of Lyons)

## Overview

The **Waldensians** originated with **Waldès (Valdes)** of Lyons, who c.1173 renounced wealth and began a life of apostolic preaching. Unlike the dualist [[catharism|Cathars]], Waldensians were largely orthodox in creed but **rejected the monopoly of licensed clergy on preaching** and embraced radical poverty. Condemned with other groups at the Council of Verona (1184), they survived as a dissenting network into the Reformation era and beyond.
