# North Sea Empire

The composite, personally held realm of [[cnut-the-great|Cnut the Great]] uniting
England, [[denmark|Denmark]], and [[norway|Norway]] (with claims over parts of
Sweden and the Norse isles) c. 1016–1035 — the apogee of Scandinavian power in
the [[viking-age|Viking Age]]. It was a dynastic union of crowns rather than an
integrated state, dependent on Cnut's person and on dual governance through native
elites. It fragmented within a decade of his death (Norway lost by 1035, England
by 1042), illustrating the fragility of early medieval personal empires.
