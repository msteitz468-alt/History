# The Safavid Empire

The dynasty that unified Iran and made **Twelver [[shia-islam|Shi'ism]] the state religion**, giving the
Iranian plateau the distinct religious identity it retains. Emerging from a militant [[sufism|Sufi]]
order backed by the Turkmen **Qizilbash**, Shah **Ismail I** conquered Tabriz in 1501 and imposed Shi'ism
by force, setting Iran permanently apart from its Sunni [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman]] and [[mughal-empire|Mughal]]
neighbours — one of Hodgson's three "gunpowder empires."

Its zenith came under **Shah Abbas I** (r. 1588–1629), who reorganized the army with gunpowder infantry,
made Isfahan a magnificent capital ("Isfahan, half the world"), and presided over a flowering of
Persianate art and architecture. The empire declined through the seventeenth century and collapsed when
Afghan invaders sacked Isfahan in 1722.
