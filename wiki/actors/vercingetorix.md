# Vercingetorix

Arverni chieftain who united Gallic tribes against Julius Caesar in 52 BCE; won at Gergovia but defeated at Alesia; paraded in Caesar's triumph and executed. Archetype of Gallic/Celtic resistance in later historiography and national memory.
