# Grimes (Claire Boucher)

Canadian musician and artist (Claire Elise Boucher). Partner of Elon Musk (2018–2022, on-and-off); mother of three of his children (X Æ A-Xii, Exa Dark Sideræl, Techno Mechanicus). Provided perspective to Isaacson on Musk’s inability to “savor success,” conditioning that “life is pain,” and the tension between his missions and personal relationships.
