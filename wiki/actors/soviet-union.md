# Soviet Union

The **Union of Soviet Socialist Republics (USSR)** was a Marxist-Leninist state that existed from 1922 to 1991, spanning northern Eurasia.

## Biography / Description
The Soviet Union was established on December 30, 1922, following the Bolshevik victory in the Russian Civil War. It congealed the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic (RSFSR) with Ukrainian, Belarusian, and Transcaucasian republics into a highly centralized single-party state directed by the Communist Party. 

At its peak, the USSR encompassed fifteen constituent republics and was the largest country in the world by land area. It dissolved on December 25-26, 1991, during the presidency of Mikhail Gorbachev.

## Key Decisions and Leaders
* **[[lenin-vladimir|Vladimir Lenin]] (1922–1924):** Founded the state security apparatus ([[cheka|The Cheka]]), established the centralized administration under [[democratic-centralism|Democratic Centralism]], and guided the transition from [[war-communism|War Communism]] to the mixed-economy [[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy (NEP)]].
* **[[stalin-joseph|Joseph Stalin]] (1924–1953):** Abolished the NEP in favor of rapid [[soviet-industrialization|Soviet Industrialization]] and coercive [[soviet-collectivization|Soviet Collectivization]]. He created a personal dictatorship enforced by the [[nkvd|NKVD]] during the [[great-purge|Great Purge]], led the country through World War II, and initiated the post-war ideological purge ([[zhdanovshchina|Zhdanovshchina]]) at the start of the Cold War.
* **[[khrushchev-nikita|Nikita Khrushchev]] (1953–1964):** Launched [[de-stalinization|De-Stalinization]] with his 1956 "Secret Speech," initiated a cultural thaw, and presided over the [[cuban-missile-crisis-1962|Cuban Missile Crisis]].
* **[[brezhnev-leonid|Leonid Brezhnev]] (1964–1982):** Stabilized the party hierarchy but led the nation into a period of deep economic and social immobility known as [[brezhnev-stagnation|Brezhnev Stagnation]], while executing military interventions in Czechoslovakia ([[prague-spring-1968|Prague Spring]]) and Afghanistan.
* **[[gorbachev-mikhail|Mikhail Gorbachev]] (1985–1991):** Tried to reform the system through [[perestroika|Perestroika]] (economic restructuring) and [[glasnost|Glasnost]] (openness), which inadvertently triggered nationalist movements and the [[dissolution-of-the-soviet-union|Dissolution of the Soviet Union]].

## Historical Significance
The Soviet Union was a global superpower that represented the first large-scale historical attempt to build a socialist society based on state ownership and central planning. It played a decisive role in the defeat of Nazi Germany in World War II and was one of the two main antagonists of the global Cold War. However, its centralized political monopoly, suppression of civil liberties, and economic inefficiencies eventually led to its collapse.
