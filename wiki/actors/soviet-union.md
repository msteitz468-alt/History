# Soviet Union

The **Union of Soviet Socialist Republics (USSR)** was a Marxist-Leninist state that existed from 1922 to 1991, spanning northern Eurasia. Its foundational "compound" (one-party dictatorship, ideological monopoly, state economic dominance, legal nihilism, hyper-centralism) was assembled by Lenin 1917–1921 and remodelled by Stalin.

## 1917–1928 Foundations (Service lines 774–1450)
Proclaimed RSFSR 1918 after Oct Revolution; USSR federation ratified 31 Dec 1922 (Lenin defeated Stalin’s plan to absorb republics into RSFSR). Core policies: Decrees on Peace/Land (1917); Cheka (1917); Brest-Litovsk (1918); Red Terror and Food Dictatorship (1918); NEP (1921: tax-in-kind, private trade re-legalized); korenizatsiya and federal concessions during Civil War. By 1928 NEP had restored output roughly to 1913 levels but "scissors crisis" and procurement shortfalls triggered Stalin’s coercive turn. The compound combined official hierarchy with unofficial clientelism and low effort as "lubricating oil." Lenin’s leadership and Stalin’s rise shaped its durable form.

## Biography / Description
The Soviet Union was established on December 30, 1922, following the Bolshevik victory in the Russian Civil War. It congealed the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic (RSFSR) with Ukrainian, Belarusian, and Transcaucasian republics into a highly centralized single-party state directed by the Communist Party. 

At its peak, the USSR encompassed fifteen constituent republics and was the largest country in the world by land area. It dissolved on December 25-26, 1991, during the presidency of Mikhail Gorbachev.

The compound of the Soviet order... one-party dictatorship, ideological monopoly, state economic dominance, legal nihilism, and administrative hyper-centralism first assembled under Lenin and drastically remodelled under Stalin (Service source summary). And yet both agriculture and industry were altogether too chaotic to be described without reservation as being integrated within ‘a planned economy’ (line 1458). The Soviet order therefore continued to need a constant dosage of excitation in order to keep functioning... The central authorities aimed at the total penetration of society... The totalitarian order was therefore full of contradictions. Perfect central control eluded Stalin. The Soviet compound was a unity of extremely orderly features and extremely chaotic ones (lines 1840, 1844, 1886).

## Key Decisions and Leaders
* **[[lenin-vladimir|Vladimir Lenin]] (1922–1924):** Founded the state security apparatus ([[cheka|The Cheka]]), established the centralized administration under [[democratic-centralism|Democratic Centralism]], and guided the transition from [[war-communism|War Communism]] to the mixed-economy [[new-economic-policy|New Economic Policy (NEP)]].
* **[[stalin-joseph|Joseph Stalin]] (1924–1953):** Abolished the NEP in favor of rapid [[soviet-industrialization|Soviet Industrialization]] and coercive [[soviet-collectivization|Soviet Collectivization]]. He created a personal dictatorship enforced by the [[nkvd|NKVD]] during the [[great-purge|Great Purge]], led the country through World War II, and initiated the post-war ideological purge ([[zhdanovshchina|Zhdanovshchina]]) at the start of the Cold War. In November 1936, when introducing a new Constitution... Stalin proclaimed: ‘Socialism, which is the first phase of communism, has basically been realized in our country.’... the USSR was defined as ‘a socialist state of the workers and peasants’ (lines 1812, 1816). On 30 June [1941] it was decided to form a State Committee of Defence, bringing together leading Politburo members Stalin, Molotov, Beria, Malenkov and Voroshilov... On 10 July he was also appointed Supreme Commander... chairman of the High Command (Stavka) on 8 August (line 1945).
* **[[khrushchev-nikita|Nikita Khrushchev]] (1953–1964):** Launched [[de-stalinization|De-Stalinization]] with his 1956 "Secret Speech," initiated a cultural thaw, and presided over the [[cuban-missile-crisis-1962|Cuban Missile Crisis]].
* **[[brezhnev-leonid|Leonid Brezhnev]] (1964–1982):** Stabilized the party hierarchy but led the nation into a period of deep economic and social immobility known as [[brezhnev-stagnation|Brezhnev Stagnation]], while executing military interventions in Czechoslovakia ([[prague-spring-1968|Prague Spring]]) and Afghanistan.
* **[[gorbachev-mikhail|Mikhail Gorbachev]] (1985–1991):** Tried to reform the system through [[perestroika|Perestroika]] (economic restructuring) and [[glasnost|Glasnost]] (openness), which inadvertently triggered nationalist movements and the [[dissolution-of-the-soviet-union|Dissolution of the Soviet Union]].

## Historical Significance
The Soviet Union was a global superpower that represented the first large-scale historical attempt to build a socialist society based on state ownership and central planning. It played a decisive role in the defeat of Nazi Germany in World War II and was one of the two main antagonists of the global Cold War. However, its centralized political monopoly, suppression of civil liberties, and economic inefficiencies eventually led to its collapse.
