# Hans-Valentin Hube (1890–1944)

German general (later Colonel General). Commander of XIV Panzer Corps in Sicily. Mastermind of the German defense and the successful evacuation of Axis forces from the island.

Hube took effective control of the defense as Italian resistance collapsed. He organized a series of stubborn delaying actions (Primosole, Troina, Etna line) that bought time for the evacuation plan. The evacuation itself (Operation Lehrgang) was a model of staff work and discipline: ferries ran on schedule under cover of darkness and flak, preserving three German divisions plus supporting units and much of their heavy equipment.

Hube received high Italian decorations for his leadership. He was killed in a plane crash in 1944 while commanding 1st Panzer Army on the Eastern Front.

## Historiography

German accounts and D'Este treat Hube as one of the outstanding defensive commanders of the war. His handling of the "hopeless" position in Sicily turned a potential disaster into a "glorious retreat" that allowed the Wehrmacht to fight again in Italy with combat-effective formations.
