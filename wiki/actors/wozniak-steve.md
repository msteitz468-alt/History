# Steve Wozniak

**Stephen Gary Wozniak** (born 11 August 1950), known as “Woz,” is an American electronics engineer, programmer, and technology entrepreneur. He co-founded Apple Computer with Steve Jobs in 1976 and designed the Apple I and Apple II computers, the latter becoming the first highly successful mass-produced personal computer. Wozniak’s engineering brilliance provided the hardware foundation for Apple’s early success; Jobs supplied the vision, marketing, and product integration focus. After leaving day-to-day Apple operations in the 1980s, Wozniak has remained a public figure in technology education and philanthropy. He appears in Isaacson’s biography as the complementary “odd couple” partner whose openness contrasted with Jobs’s closed-system instincts.
