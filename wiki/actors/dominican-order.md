# Dominican Order (Order of Preachers)

## Overview

The **Dominican Order** (Order of Preachers), founded by [[dominic-de-guzman|Dominic de Guzmán]] and confirmed by the papacy in 1216, was a **mendicant** order of learned preachers formed to combat heresy through doctrine and example. Dominicans quickly dominated chairs of theology at the [[medieval-university|universities]] and staffed much of the later medieval machinery of orthodoxy.
