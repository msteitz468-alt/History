# The Philippines

The Southeast Asian archipelago shaped by the region's longest and most thoroughly transforming
colonial experience: over three centuries of **Spanish** rule (from 1565) that made it Asia's largest
Catholic society, followed by half a century of **American** rule after 1898. The first modern Asian
nationalist revolution — the Propaganda Movement of [[jose-rizal|José Rizal]] and the Katipunan rising
of 1896 — was redirected by US conquest in the Philippine–American War.

After the [[japanese-occupation-southeast-asia|Japanese occupation]], the Philippines gained
independence in 1946, later passing through the Marcos dictatorship and the 1986 People Power
revolution. It is a founding member of [[asean-and-regionalism|ASEAN]].
