# Sergei Kirov

Sergei Mironovich Kirov (born Kostrikov) was a prominent Bolshevik, Leningrad party leader, and full Politburo member. His assassination on 1 December 1934 in the Smolny Institute (by Leonid Nikolaev) was used by Stalin as the immediate pretext for the escalation of political terror, the Kirov stream of the Great Purge, show trials, and mass repression.

Kirov was seen as a popular, relatively moderate figure within the Stalinist leadership. His death removed a potential rival or independent voice and provided the catalyst for the "enemies hunting enemies" dynamic and quota-driven terror of 1936–38.
