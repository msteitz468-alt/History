# John Bell Hood

Confederate general. Aggressive division and corps commander under Lee and Longstreet; later commander of the Army of Tennessee. Launched costly offensives in 1864 (Atlanta, Franklin, Nashville) that shattered his army. Known for personal bravery (multiple wounds) and "fight" mentality that contributed to Confederate defeat in the West.

## Significance
Exemplifies the "offensive a outrance" school of Confederate generalship and the high human cost of aggressive tactics against superior Union resources and firepower in the later war.
