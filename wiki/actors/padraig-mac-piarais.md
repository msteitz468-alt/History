# Pádraig Mac Piarais (Patrick Pearse)

Irish educator, writer, language activist and revolutionary; key figure in Conradh na Gaeilge (Gaelic League) and leader in 1916 Easter Rising. Symbol of modern Irish republicanism and cultural nationalism.
