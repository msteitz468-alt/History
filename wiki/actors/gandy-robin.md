# Robin Gandy

British mathematician and logician, the only doctoral student of [[actors/turing-alan|Alan Turing]] (supervised at Cambridge in the late 1940s–early 1950s) and one of his closest personal friends. After Turing's death in 1954, Gandy inherited and helped preserve his papers and notebooks, later contributing to their scholarly assessment. Gandy went on to an academic career in mathematical logic at Leicester, Manchester, and Oxford, working on recursion theory and generalizing Turing's model of computation (the "Gandy machine," an attempt to formalize the physical limits of what any discrete deterministic mechanism can compute). He is a minor but significant figure in the historiography of Turing's life and the transmission of his intellectual legacy.
