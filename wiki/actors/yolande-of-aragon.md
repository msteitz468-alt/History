# Yolande of Aragon

Yolande of Aragon (1381 – 1442), Queen of Aragon and Naples, Duchess of Anjou, was one of the most powerful and influential political figures in fifteenth-century France. As the mother-in-law and key advisor of Charles VII, she played a crucial role in securing the Valois throne, financing the relief of Orléans, and promoting the mission of Joan of Arc.
