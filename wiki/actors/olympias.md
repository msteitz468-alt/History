# Olympias

Molossian princess from Epirus, wife of Philip II of Macedon, and mother of Alexander the Great. Dominant and controversial figure at the Macedonian court. Allegedly involved in the murder of Philip and in the elimination of rivals after Alexander's death. Executed in 316 BCE during the wars of the Successors. Source of enduring legends about her ambition, occult interests, and influence over her son.

## Significance
Archetype of the powerful Hellenistic royal woman; central to debates about the nature of Argead kingship, the role of women in Macedonian politics, and the sources (especially Plutarch and the Alexander Romance) for Alexander's life.
