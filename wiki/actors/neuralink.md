# Neuralink

Neurotechnology company co-founded by Elon Musk in 2016 to develop implantable brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), initially aimed at restoring function for patients with paralysis and severe neurological conditions, with Musk's longer-term stated ambition of enabling direct human-AI symbiosis to keep pace with advancing artificial intelligence. The company develops surgical robots for precise implantation of flexible electrode arrays ("threads") into brain tissue, a technical approach distinct from older, more invasive BCI methods.

Neuralink received FDA approval for human clinical trials in 2023 and conducted its first human implant in early 2024, part of a broader wave of BCI research competing with academic and other commercial efforts. Like Musk's other ventures, Neuralink reflects his pattern of using existential or civilizational framing (in this case, human cognitive augmentation as insurance against being outpaced by artificial intelligence) to justify high-risk, capital-intensive technical ventures.

## Significance

Neuralink represents Musk's extension of his engineering and venture strategy into brain-computer interface technology, framed within his broader narrative of pursuing existential-risk-mitigating "civilizational" technologies.
