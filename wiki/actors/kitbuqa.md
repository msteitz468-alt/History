# Kitbuqa

**Kitbuqa** (Ket-Buqa, Ked-buqa) was **[[hulegu|Hülegü]]**'s general in Syria when the Ilkhanid
advance was checked at **[[battle-of-ain-jalut-1260|Ain Jalut (1260)]]**. [[may-mongol-art-of-war-2016|May]]
notes he commanded a **reduced detachment** after Hülegü withdrew east on news of Möngke's death;
Syrian auxiliaries may have deserted at the decisive moment. His defeat ended ephemeral Mongol
control of Syria and fixed the Euphrates frontier for decades.
