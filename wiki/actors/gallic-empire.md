# Gallic Empire

Breakaway Roman state in the western provinces (Gaul, Britain, Spain) during the Crisis of the Third Century, founded by the usurper Postumus (260) and lasting until reconquered by Aurelian (274). It maintained Roman institutions, coinage, and defense against Germanic invaders while independent of the central empire under Gallienus and later Aurelian.

## Significance
Classic example of the fragmentation of the Roman Empire in the 3rd century and the limits of central control; its "restoration" by Aurelian was a key step in the military and administrative reforms that allowed the empire to survive in the East.
