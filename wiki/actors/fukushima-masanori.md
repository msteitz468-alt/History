# Fukushima Masanori

Japanese daimyo, one of the "Seven Spears of Shizugatake" who rose to prominence under [[actors/toyotomi-hideyoshi|Toyotomi Hideyoshi]] and became one of his most senior generals, rewarded with the large domain of Aki-Hiroshima. Despite his deep personal loyalty to the Toyotomi house, longstanding rivalry with Hideyoshi's civil administrator Ishida Mitsunari led Fukushima to side with [[actors/tokugawa-ieyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]] in the succession struggle following Hideyoshi's death, and at the Battle of Sekigahara (1600) he commanded the vanguard of the Eastern Army's right wing, engaging Ishida's forces directly in some of the battle's heaviest fighting. His defection was strategically significant: as one of Hideyoshi's most prestigious former generals, his choice to back Ieyasu helped legitimize the Eastern Army's cause among other wavering Toyotomi loyalists. He was rewarded after the battle but later fell out of Tokugawa favor and had his domain confiscated in 1619 for unauthorized castle repairs.
