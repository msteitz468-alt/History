# Anthony Eden

British Foreign Secretary (1951–55) and later Prime Minister. Key Western participant at the 1954 Geneva Conference on Indochina and Korea.

## Role in Indochina / Geneva

Eden was the chief Western mediator at Geneva. He worked to find a compromise between the French desire to salvage something and the Viet Minh/Chinese/Soviet desire for a settlement that recognized the military reality on the ground. He resisted Dulles' more confrontational line and helped broker the partition formula and the "consultations" provisions for future elections (which were never held). Eden's diplomacy preserved some British influence and prevented a wider rupture in the Western alliance over Indochina.

## Broader Context

Eden was a traditional balance-of-power statesman who distrusted both American moralism and French colonial rigidity. His later clash with the U.S. over Suez (1956) illustrated the limits of British power and the new realities of the post-1945 order.
