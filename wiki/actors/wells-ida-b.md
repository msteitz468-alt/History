# Ida B. Wells (1862–1931)

Journalist, anti-lynching crusader, and suffrage activist who insisted that women's enfranchisement not be purchased by excluding Black women. Cox documents her presence in the 1913 [[woman-suffrage-procession-1913|Woman Suffrage Procession]] and the racial fractures inside the predominantly white suffrage movement that Wilson's Jim Crow politics amplified.
