# Edward M. ("Colonel") House (1858–1938)

Texas political operator and intimate adviser to [[wilson-woodrow|Woodrow Wilson]] until their break after Paris. Cox cites House extensively as a diary witness to Wilson's private views on suffrage, party strategy, and personality — including counsel (echoed in Hartz's earlier reading) that Anglo-American politics required compromise Wilson often refused.
