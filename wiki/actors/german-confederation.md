# German Confederation

The loose association of 39 sovereign German states created by the Congress of Vienna in 1815 to
replace the defunct Holy Roman Empire, presided over by Habsburg Austria through the Federal Diet at
Frankfurt. Designed by Metternich as a bulwark of conservative order, it suppressed liberal and
national movements (the Carlsbad Decrees) even as economic integration advanced through the
Prussian-led [[zollverein|Zollverein]].

The Confederation was the arena of the **dualism** between Austria and [[prussia|Prussia]] for
leadership of the German lands. It nearly gave way to a unified nation-state in [[revolutions-of-1848|1848]]
(the Frankfurt Parliament), survived that crisis, and was finally destroyed when Prussia defeated
Austria in the [[austro-prussian-war-1866]] — clearing the path to the Prussian-dominated North German
Confederation and, in 1871, the [[german-empire|German Empire]].
