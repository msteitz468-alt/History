# Margaret I of Denmark

Daughter of Valdemar IV of Denmark and effective ruler of all three Scandinavian
kingdoms by the 1380s–90s. Through inheritance, her son Olaf, and the defeat and
capture of Albert of Mecklenburg (1389), she united [[denmark|Denmark]],
[[norway|Norway]], and [[sweden|Sweden]] and had her great-nephew Erik of Pomerania
crowned king of all three at the [[kalmar-union-1397|Kalmar Union]] (1397), which
she dominated until her death. One of the most capable rulers of late-medieval
Europe, she built the dynastic framework of Nordic union that lasted, in varying
form, into the 16th century.
