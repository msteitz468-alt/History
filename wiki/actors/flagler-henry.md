# Henry M. Flagler

Henry Morrison Flagler (1830–1913) was Rockefeller's most important early partner and the co-architect of [[actors/standard-oil|Standard Oil]]. Chernow credits Flagler with negotiating the crucial secret railroad rebate contracts and with drafting much of the company's tight legal and contractual framework in the formative Cleveland years; Rockefeller regarded the partnership as the most consequential of his business life. Flagler later built the Florida East Coast Railway and resort empire.
