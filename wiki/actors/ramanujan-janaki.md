# Janaki Ramanujan

Janaki (c. 1899–1994), wife of Srinivasa Ramanujan. Married in 1909 when she was about nine (standard arranged child marriage of the period). She lived with Ramanujan’s family in Kumbakonam and later Madras during his years of struggle. She accompanied or joined him in England for part of his stay. After his death she lived as a widow, largely cut off from his family, supporting herself as a seamstress for many years. In later life she was interviewed extensively by biographers and became an important source for the human story of Ramanujan’s final years and domestic life. She outlived him by more than seventy years.
