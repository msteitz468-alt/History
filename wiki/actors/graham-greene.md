# Graham Greene

British novelist and journalist who spent extended time in Vietnam in 1951–52 and again later. His novel *The Quiet American* (1955) remains one of the most influential literary portrayals of the early American involvement and the “Third Force” idea.

## Role in the History

Greene’s reporting and fiction captured the atmosphere of Saigon in the early 1950s, French-American tensions, the opium scene, the bombing campaigns, and the emerging U.S. search for a non-Communist, non-French alternative. His character Alden Pyle is a composite that has been widely (though not exclusively) linked to Edward Lansdale and the Third Force concept.
