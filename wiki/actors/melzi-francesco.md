# Francesco Melzi

Francesco Melzi (c. 1493–c. 1568), from a noble Milanese family, joined Leonardo da Vinci’s household around 1507 and became his closest companion, surrogate son, and literary heir. After Leonardo’s death Melzi preserved and organized the notebooks and drawings, taking them back to Italy. He is primarily responsible for the survival and transmission of Leonardo’s written legacy.
