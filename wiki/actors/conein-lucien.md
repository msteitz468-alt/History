# Lucien Conein

CIA officer and primary liaison between the U.S. government and the South Vietnamese generals plotting the November 1963 coup against Ngo Dinh Diem. OSS veteran of WWII operations in France and Indochina; colorful, hard-drinking operative who had long-standing relationships in Vietnamese military circles.

## Role

Conein served as the cut-out and messenger, delivering assurances, money, and weapons to the plotters while reporting to Ambassador Lodge and Washington. His presence at the generals' headquarters on the day of the coup made the depth of U.S. involvement unmistakable to participants.
