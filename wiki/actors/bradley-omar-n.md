# Omar N. Bradley (1893–1981)

US Army general. Commander US First Army during the Normandy landings and breakout; later 12th Army Group commander.

## Role in Normandy (D'Este)

Bradley commanded the American sector of the lodgment. He planned and executed Operation Cobra, the decisive breakout. He was frequently frustrated by the slow progress on the British flank and by Montgomery's command style and public statements. Deeply involved in the decisions around the Falaise gap (the controversial halt at Argentan).

His postwar writings (A Soldier's Story, A General's Life) contain sharp critiques of Montgomery and of the handling of the pursuit.

## Historiography

Bradley is generally viewed as a competent, steady commander who delivered results in Normandy. D'Este and others examine his role in the command controversies and his relationship with Patton and Eisenhower. His accounts are important primary sources but must be read with awareness of postwar rivalries.
