# Nagumo Chuichi

**Vice Admiral Nagumo Chuichi** (1887–1944) commanded Japan's First Air Fleet (Kido
Butai), the carrier strike force that attacked Pearl Harbor in December 1941 and, in June
1942, engaged US carrier forces at the [[events/battle-of-midway-1942|Battle of Midway]].
A surface-warfare officer by background rather than an aviator, Nagumo has been
criticized by historians for cautious and reactive decision-making at Midway — notably
the fateful rearming of his strike aircraft between anti-ship and land-attack ordnance
just as US carrier planes were located — which contributed to the loss of all four
Japanese fleet carriers in the battle, a defeat from which the Imperial Japanese Navy
never recovered its offensive carrier superiority. Nagumo was killed during the American
invasion of Saipan in 1944, reportedly by suicide as the position became untenable.
