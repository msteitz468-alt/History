# Harry S. Truman

President of the United States (1945–1953). After Roosevelt’s death, the Truman administration moved away from FDR’s strong anticolonial trusteeship policy toward accepting a French return to Indochina (while seeking assurances of greater self-government). His administration provided the first major U.S. military and economic aid to France in Indochina.

## Role

Truman’s government made the key early Cold War decisions that “internationalized” the French war. By 1950 the United States was supplying the bulk of the matériel for the French effort. Truman also approved the initial NSC documents that framed Indochina as vital to containing Soviet/Chinese expansion.
