# Innocent III (Pope)

One of the most powerful medieval popes (r. 1198–1216). Asserted papal supremacy over secular rulers ("vicar of Christ"), greatly expanded the administrative and legal reach of the papal monarchy, launched the Fourth Crusade and the Albigensian Crusade, presided over the Fourth Lateran Council (1215), and intervened extensively in European politics (England, Sicily, Germany, France).

## Role and Significance
Embodied the high-medieval ideal of a universal papal authority that could depose kings, authorize crusades, and regulate doctrine and discipline across Christendom. His reign marks a high point of papal political power before the crises of the later 13th–14th centuries.
