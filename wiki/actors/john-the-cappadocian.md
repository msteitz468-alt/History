# John the Cappadocian

**John the Cappadocian** was Praetorian Prefect of the East under Emperor
[[justinian|Justinian I]], serving as the emperor's chief fiscal and administrative
officer through much of the 530s–540s. A capable but ruthlessly efficient tax
administrator, his aggressive fiscal extractions — needed to fund Justinian's wars of
reconquest and building program — alienated both the aristocracy and the urban poor of
Constantinople and are counted among the grievances that fed the explosive
[[events/nika-riots-532|Nika riots]] of 532, during which the mob demanded his dismissal
alongside that of the quaestor Tribonian. Justinian briefly removed both men
to quell the unrest, though John was later reinstated. He was eventually disgraced and
exiled in 541 after a plot involving Empress Theodora, who resented his influence and
suspected him of conspiring against Justinian.
