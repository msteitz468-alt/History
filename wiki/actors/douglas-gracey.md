# Douglas Gracey

British Major-General Douglas Gracey commanded the 20th Indian Division, the first Allied force to arrive in southern Indochina after Japan’s surrender (September 1945). His actions in rearming French troops and facilitating their return to power in Saigon/Cochin China were instrumental in the outbreak of full-scale fighting.

## Role

Gracey’s orders were to disarm Japanese forces and maintain order. In practice he refused to deal with the Viet Minh Committee of the South, declared martial law, and on 23 September 1945 effectively enabled a French coup that restored French control of key installations in Saigon. This triggered the massacres of French and Eurasian civilians on 25 September and the beginning of sustained Franco-Vietnamese combat in the south.
