# James Monroe

Fifth President of the United States (1817-1825). Key figure in the Louisiana Purchase negotiations, War of 1812, and author of the Monroe Doctrine (1823), warning European powers against new colonization in the Americas. Oversaw the "Era of Good Feelings," Missouri Compromise, and acquisition of Florida. Last president from the Revolutionary generation.
