# Reinhard Heydrich

Chief of the SS Security Service (SD) and from 1936 head of the Security Police
(Gestapo and criminal police); [[himmler-heinrich|Himmler]]'s deputy and the
chief architect of the surveillance-and-terror apparatus of the peacetime Third
Reich. A cashiered naval officer, Heydrich combined bureaucratic system-building
with radical ideological policing ("preventive detention", enemy-category
targeting) that prefigured his wartime role directing the Einsatzgruppen and
chairing the Wannsee Conference. Assassinated in Prague, 1942.

## Evans (2005) — the peacetime apparatus

Co-organized the [[night-of-the-long-knives-1934|Röhm purge]] (presented the
fabricated putsch evidence to SS leaders 24 June 1934; directed regional
executions by phone; ordered Erich Klausener's murder). Classified the Jews
and the Catholic Church as Nazism's two principal enemies (1936). Directed
the 1938 "asocial" mass raids into the camps; preserved the Fritsch file
Hitler had ordered destroyed and resubmitted it to trigger the
[[blomberg-fritsch-affair-1938|Blomberg–Fritsch affair]]. In annexed Austria
his SD's Vienna model — Eichmann's Central Agency for Jewish Emigration
(August 1938), combining terror, coerced Jewish administration, and asset
plunder — "became a model for the SS Security Service in its subsequent
dealings with the Jews" (Evans). Issued the 01:20 telex steering
[[kristallnacht-1938|Kristallnacht]], then controlled the aftermath: release
of camp prisoners conditional on emigration (January 1939, return meaning
"camp for life"), the Reich Centre for Jewish Emigration (24 January 1939).
Organized the Gleiwitz false-flag attack (31 August 1939) using murdered
camp inmates ("canned goods") to pretext the invasion of Poland.

## Evans (2008) — war, genocide, and assassination

Gave the [[einsatzgruppen|Einsatzgruppen]] their killing orders in 1941 and toured the eastern killing fields with Himmler, urging the pace on. Received Göring's 31 July 1941 commission "to make all necessary preparations . . . for a total solution of the Jewish question in the German sphere of influence in Europe" and convened the [[wannsee-conference-1942|Wannsee Conference]] (20 January 1942) that coordinated the Europe-wide machinery and confirmed his authority. As Reich Protector of Bohemia and Moravia from September 1941, "the Butcher of Prague" broke the Czech resistance (404 executions in two months; the show trial of Prime Minister Eliáš) while courting Czech workers with rations, shoes, and spa holidays — a carrot-and-stick success alarming enough that the London government-in-exile ordered his death partly to provoke reprisals that would revive resistance. Ambushed on 27 May 1942 by British-trained Czech agents Gabčík and Kubiš while riding without escort in an open car (a habit Hitler privately called "stupid and idiotic"); died of wound infection on 4 June 1942. The reprisals killed some 5,000 Czechs, including the total destruction of **Lidice** (10 June 1942: men shot, women to Ravensbrück, 81 children murdered after racial screening) — restrained from worse only by the Reich's need for Bohemian arms output. [[operation-reinhard-1942|Operation Reinhard]] carried his name. Evans's character sketch stresses the combination of cold administrative ambition with total, unironic assimilation of Nazi ideology — against the older "unideological technician of power" reading — and records the contemporaries' sense of a divided man (Burckhardt: "Two people are looking at me simultaneously").

## Shirer (1960) additions
