# Margaret of Scotland

Great-niece of **Edward the Confessor** and granddaughter of Edmund Ironside, Margaret was
an English royal refugee after the [[norman-conquest-of-england-1066|Norman Conquest]] who
married **Máel Coluim III** of [[actors/kingdom-of-alba|Alba]] c.1067 and brought English
and Continental norms to the Scottish court. Pauline Stafford's chapter on
[[concepts/queenship-early-medieval|queenship]] in
[[sources/stafford-companion-early-middle-ages-britain-ireland-2009|the *Companion*]] treats
her as a key transmitter of royal blood and reforming culture.

A Life written by her chaplain Turgot portrays her as a **church reformer** and exemplary
household mistress. Through her the West-Saxon dynastic claim passed into the Scottish royal
line: her sons became kings of Scots, her daughter **Edith/Mathilda** married **Henry I** of
England (1100), and **Mary** married Eustace III of Boulogne (1102) — weaving Scotland into
the dense web of post-Conquest dynastic ties and opening the Anglo-Norman era in Scottish
history. She was later canonised.
