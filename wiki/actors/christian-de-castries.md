# Christian de Castries

French colonel (later brigadier general) who commanded the garrison at Dien Bien Phu during the 1953–54 siege. He was in direct communication with René Cogny in Hanoi during the final days.

## Role

De Castries was the on-site commander of the fortified camp. He conducted the defense under extremely difficult conditions (artillery disadvantage, isolation, deteriorating morale). He followed orders not to raise a white flag and surrendered on 7 May 1954 when the central position fell.
