# Aaron Klug

South African-born British biophysicist and chemist. Worked with Rosalind Franklin at Birkbeck College on the structure of Tobacco Mosaic Virus (TMV) using X-ray crystallography and helical diffraction theory. Shared the 1982 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for development of crystallographic electron microscopy and work on nucleic acid-protein complexes. Key figure in the transfer of structural techniques from DNA/virus work to broader molecular biology.
