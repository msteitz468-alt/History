# Marcel Grossmann

Swiss mathematician, a fellow student and close friend of [[actors/einstein-albert|Albert Einstein]] at the Zurich Polytechnic (ETH Zurich), where Grossmann's meticulous lecture notes reportedly helped Einstein pass courses he attended irregularly, and whose father's recommendation helped Einstein secure his position at the Swiss Patent Office in Bern. Grossmann became a professor of mathematics (specializing in differential geometry) at the Polytechnic, and from 1912 collaborated directly with Einstein on the mathematical formulation of general relativity: their joint 1913 "Entwurf" (outline) paper introduced Einstein to the Riemannian tensor calculus and Ricci curvature methods that became indispensable to the final 1915 field equations. Grossmann's contribution is regarded by historians of science as essential to supplying Einstein the mathematical apparatus general relativity required.
