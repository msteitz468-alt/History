# Albert Gallatin

Swiss-born American statesman, diplomat, and ethnologist; longest-serving United States Secretary of the Treasury (1801–1814) under Presidents Jefferson and Madison. Key architect of early federal fiscal policy, negotiator of the Louisiana Purchase financing and the Treaty of Ghent ending the War of 1812. Later served as minister to France and Britain and founded the American Ethnological Society.
