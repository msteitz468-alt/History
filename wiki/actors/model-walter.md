# Walter Model

German field marshal known as "Hitler's Fireman" for his skill in stabilizing crumbling fronts. Commanded in Russia (Kursk, later Army Group North Ukraine/Center), then Western Front (Normandy after Rommel, Ardennes, Ruhr). Master of improvised defense, but often hamstrung by Hitler's no-retreat orders. Committed suicide in the Ruhr pocket as the end neared.
