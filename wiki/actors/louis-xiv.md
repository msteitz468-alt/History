# Louis XIV

King of France (1643–1715). Embodiment of absolute monarchy. Centralized power, built Versailles as symbol and instrument of control, pursued aggressive foreign policy aimed at French hegemony in Europe, revoked the Edict of Nantes (1685) ending toleration of Huguenots. His wars (Dutch War, War of the Spanish Succession) brought glory but also fiscal exhaustion and the beginnings of relative decline. "L'état, c'est moi" attributed.
