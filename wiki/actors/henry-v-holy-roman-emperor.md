# Henry V, Holy Roman Emperor

Last ruler of the [[actors/salian-dynasty|Salian dynasty]], son of [[actors/henry-iv-holy-roman-emperor|Henry IV]], whom he supplanted: in 1104–1105 he joined a rebellion of German princes and reform-minded churchmen against his father (partly over the unresolved Investiture Controversy), forcing Henry IV's abdication in 1105 and succeeding him as king, then as emperor (crowned 1111). Despite having risen against his father in the name of ending lay investiture, Henry V continued to insist on the emperor's right to invest bishops with ring and staff once in power, and pursued the same conflict with the papacy through his own reign, including a dramatic 1111 expedition to Rome in which he briefly seized Pope Paschal II and forced a settlement later repudiated as extracted under duress.

The Investiture Controversy that had convulsed his father's reign was finally settled under Henry V by the Concordat of Worms in 1122, a compromise brokered with Pope Callixtus II: the emperor renounced investiture with ring and staff (the spiritual symbols of office) while retaining a role in the temporal investiture of imperial bishops (via the scepter) and continued influence over episcopal elections in practice. Henry V died in 1125 without a direct heir, ending the Salian line and opening a contested royal election won by Lothair of Supplinburg.
