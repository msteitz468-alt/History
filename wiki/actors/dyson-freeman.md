# Freeman Dyson

British-American mathematical physicist and writer. Key figure in the postwar renormalization of quantum electrodynamics. While a young visitor at Cornell, he recognized the power of Richard Feynman’s diagrammatic and path-integral methods, translated them into more conventional language, proved their equivalence to Julian Schwinger’s formalism, and thereby made the new tools acceptable and usable by the broader physics community. Long career at the Institute for Advanced Study; prolific writer on science, technology, and the future.
