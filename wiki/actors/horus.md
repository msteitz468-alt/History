# Horus

One of the most ancient and important deities of Egyptian religion, falcon-headed god of the sky, kingship, and protection, worshipped continuously from the earliest dynastic period through the Roman era. In the central Osiris myth cycle, Horus is the son of [[actors/osiris|Osiris]] and Isis, conceived after Osiris's murder by his brother Set; raised in hiding, Horus grows to avenge his father, contending with Set for the throne of Egypt in a long mythological struggle (including the loss and magical restoration of his eye, the *wedjat*, a powerful protective symbol) before ultimately prevailing and becoming rightful king. This myth underpinned Egyptian royal ideology: every living pharaoh was identified with Horus (the "Horus name" was among the pharaoh's official titles), while the deceased pharaoh was identified with Osiris, making the succession of kingship a ritual re-enactment of Horus succeeding his father.
