# George Westinghouse

**George Westinghouse** (1846–1914) was an American entrepreneur and engineer (inventor of the railway air brake) and founder of **Westinghouse Electric**. He licensed [[actors/tesla-nikola|Nikola Tesla]]'s polyphase **alternating-current (AC)** patents in 1888, backing the AC system that defeated [[actors/edison-thomas|Edison]]'s direct current in the **[[processes/war-of-the-currents|War of the Currents]]**. Westinghouse Electric lit the [[events/worlds-columbian-exposition-1893|1893 World's Columbian Exposition]] and built the generators for the [[events/niagara-falls-power-project-1895|Niagara Falls power project]].

In Seifer's account, when financial pressure threatened Westinghouse Electric, Tesla released the company from a royalty contract that would have made him enormously wealthy — a decision central to Tesla's later poverty.

## Relationship to Tesla

- 1888 license of the AC patents; the Pittsburgh collaboration on the induction motor.
- The torn royalty contract (Tesla's renunciation of per-horsepower royalties).
