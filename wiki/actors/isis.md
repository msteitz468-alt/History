# Isis

Major goddess of ancient Egyptian religion, sister-wife of [[actors/osiris|Osiris]] and mother of [[actors/horus|Horus]], associated with magic, motherhood, protection, and the restoration of the dead. In the Osiris myth she reassembles her murdered husband's dismembered body, using magic to conceive Horus posthumously and later protecting and raising him in hiding from his uncle Set until he could claim his father's throne — making her one of the mythological cycle's central active agents rather than a passive figure. Her cult, originally Egyptian, spread widely across the Hellenistic and Roman Mediterranean world from the Ptolemaic period onward, becoming one of the most popular "mystery" cults of the Roman Empire, with temples as far as Rome, Britain, and the Danube frontier; some scholars have traced parallels between her iconography (nursing the infant Horus) and later Christian Marian imagery, though the extent of direct influence is debated.
