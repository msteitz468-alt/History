# Alexander McGillivray

Creek (Muscogee) leader of mixed Scottish-Creek ancestry, fluent in English, Spanish, and Creek. Dominant figure in Creek diplomacy in the 1780s-90s. Owned a substantial plantation and enslaved people. Negotiated the Treaty of New York (1790) with the Washington administration, securing temporary guarantees for Creek lands in exchange for concessions and a US commission. His career illustrates both the possibilities and ultimate failure of accommodationist Native policy against demographic pressure.
