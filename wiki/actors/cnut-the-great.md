# Cnut the Great (Knut den Store)

Son of [[sweyn-forkbeard|Sweyn Forkbeard]]; king of England (1016), Denmark
(1018), and Norway (1028), ruler of the [[north-sea-empire|North Sea empire]],
the high point of Danish power. He governed England as a Christian king in the
West Saxon tradition, made a celebrated pilgrimage to Rome (1027), and drove
[[olaf-haraldsson|St Olaf]] from Norway (whose death at Stiklestad in 1030
nonetheless launched the cult that undid Danish control). His composite realm did
not survive his death in 1035.

## Counterfactual Significance

Cnut's empire showed that a Scandinavian ruler could integrate England, Denmark,
and Norway under Christian kingship; its rapid dissolution showed how dependent
such constructions were on the person of the king. See
[[helle-cambridge-history-scandinavia-v1-2003]].
