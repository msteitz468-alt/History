# John Ordway (d. after 1806)

Sergeant in the Corps of Discovery; often acted as de facto first sergeant. Kept one of the most complete and valuable journals of the expedition. From a Kentucky farming family; wrote home with pride about the patriotic mission and the land rewards promised.

Featured in Fehrman's rotating chapters as a working-class counterpoint to the officers. His journal provides critical day-to-day detail and a non-elite perspective on the journey.
