# Donald Trump (b. 1946)

45th and 47th President of the United States. Real estate developer, reality television personality (*The Apprentice*), and populist political figure. First president without prior elected or military experience. Two non-consecutive terms (2017–2021, 2025–).

## Overview / Role
Born in Queens, New York, to Fred and Mary Trump. Built business empire in New York real estate, Atlantic City casinos, branding. Declared candidacy 2015; won 2016 Republican nomination and general election against Hillary Clinton on nationalist, anti-immigration, anti-trade "America First" platform, carrying Rust Belt states.

First term: tax cuts (2017 TCJA), deregulation, criminal justice reform (First Step Act), Abraham Accords, pressure on NATO allies, trade war with China, border wall efforts, two impeachments (Ukraine, Jan 6). Lost 2020 election; disputed results led to January 6, 2021 Capitol riot and second impeachment. Left office Jan 2021.

Re-elected 2024. Early second term actions (per 2025 sources): aggressive use of executive orders, appointment of loyalists (Vance VP, Rubio, Hegseth, RFK Jr, Musk at DOGE), "war on woke", mass deportation plans, Project 2025 influences, challenges to institutional norms.

## Key Relationships
- Family: Melania Trump, children (Ivanka, Don Jr., Eric, Tiffany, Barron); Roy Cohn (early mentor).
- Allies: Steve Bannon, Michael Flynn, later J.D. Vance, Elon Musk.
- Opponents: Democratic Party, "deep state", Never Trump Republicans, mainstream press.

## Causal Role and Counterfactuals
Accelerated partisan polarization and erosion of norms around elections, media, and executive power. First term shifted GOP toward populism/nationalism; trade and immigration policy realignments. Jan 6 tested constitutional order. Second term early moves suggest further centralization and retribution themes. Without Trump, 2016/2024 outcomes and Republican trajectory likely conventional; his personal style and media mastery were catalytic.

## Historiography
Highly contested. Supporters see him as disruptor exposing elite failures and delivering results for forgotten Americans. Critics view as authoritarian threat, norm-breaker, and con man. Early scholarship split between "fascism" analogies and "populist" or "transactional" framings. Primary material: rally transcripts, tweets/Truths, *Art of the Deal*, Mueller report, Jan 6 committee. Ongoing and polarized as of 2025–26.
