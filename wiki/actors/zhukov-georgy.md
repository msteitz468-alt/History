# Georgy Zhukov

Soviet marshal and strategist. Key commander on the Eastern Front; architect or executor of major Soviet victories (Moscow defense, Stalingrad counteroffensive planning, Kursk, Bagration, Berlin). Master of operational art and deep battle. Post-war fell in and out of favor under Stalin and successors.

See: [[stalin-joseph]], [[world-war-ii-1939-1945]], [[operation-barbarossa-1941]].

**From West Point WWII Vol 1 (range 3 extraction - Barbarossa context):**
- Pre-invasion role: "In January 1941 he was appointed chief of the General Staff, in which role he helped to refine plans and preparations for war with Germany."
- Wartime "fire brigade": "After the Germans invaded, Zhukov became a sort of one-man fire brigade, as Stalin sent him to take over or oversee operations in whatever sector of the front was most threatened. Zhukov organized the defense of Leningrad in September and then took over the Western Front (an army group) in front of Moscow. He stopped the Germans there and then directed a successful counteroffensive in December."
