# Eberhard von Mackensen

German general (1889–1969), son of the celebrated First World War Field Marshal August von Mackensen, and commander of III Panzer Corps under Ewald von Kleist's 1st Panzer Army during the 1942 Caucasus offensive (Operation Edelweiss). His corps spearheaded the rapid advance across the Manych and Kuban steppes in extreme summer heat, reaching the Kuban by early August 1942 and participating in the capture of Maikop.

Mackensen later commanded the 14th Army in Italy (1943–1944), where he directed the initial containment of the Allied landing at Anzio (January 1944) before being relieved by Kesselring after the eventual Allied breakout. After the war he was convicted by a British military court for his role in the Ardeatine massacre reprisal killings in Rome (1944) and imprisoned before an early release in the 1950s.

## Significance

Mackensen's corps exemplified the speed and reach of German panzer operations at their operational peak in 1942, a high point his later, more troubled command in Italy would not match.
