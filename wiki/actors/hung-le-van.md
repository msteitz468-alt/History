# Le Van Hung

**Brigadier General Le Van Hung** (1933–1975) was deputy commander of the ARVN 5th
Division during the [[events/battle-of-an-loc-1972|Battle of An Loc]] (1972), the
prolonged siege in which a heavily outnumbered ARVN garrison, backed by intensive US air
support, held the town against a major North Vietnamese armored offensive — a battle
frequently cited as demonstrating that ARVN forces could hold under extreme pressure when
adequately supported. Hung later commanded the Mekong Delta's IV Corps during the final
collapse of April 1975; rather than surrender, he took his own life on 30 April 1975, the
day Saigon fell, becoming one of several senior ARVN officers who chose suicide over
capitulation.
