# René Cogny

French general in Indochina, commander in Tonkin under Navarre. He was a strong advocate of the delta as the decisive theater and had serious reservations about the Dien Bien Phu position, though he participated in the planning. He was in direct radio contact with de Castries during the final days of the siege.

## Role

Cogny’s relationship with Navarre was strained. He favored a more delta-focused strategy and was skeptical of committing so many elite units to a remote valley. During the battle he was the senior officer in Hanoi communicating with the garrison.
