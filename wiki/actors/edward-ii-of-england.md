# Edward II of England

King of England (1307–1327). Son of Edward I. His reign was dominated by conflict with powerful barons (especially over the royal favorite Piers Gaveston and later the Despensers), military failure against Scotland (Bannockburn 1314), and economic and political crisis. Eventually deposed in 1327 by his wife Isabella and her ally Roger Mortimer; traditionally said to have been murdered in Berkeley Castle.

## Significance
Classic case study of the limits of medieval English kingship, the power of the baronage and emerging parliamentary institutions, and the personal vs. structural causes of political breakdown. Symbol of failed kingship in later English historiography and literature.
