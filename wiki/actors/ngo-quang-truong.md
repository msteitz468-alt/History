# Ngo Quang Truong

**Ngo Quang Truong** (1929–2007) was widely regarded as one of the ablest generals of the
Army of the Republic of Vietnam (ARVN), commanding I Corps (the northernmost military
region) during the final PAVN offensive of early 1975. Despite his reputation for
competent, careful command, I Corps disintegrated under a sequence of contradictory
withdrawal orders from President [[nguyen-van-thieu|Nguyen Van Thieu]] amid mass civilian
panic, and Truong was unable to organize an orderly defense or retreat as
[[places/hue|Hue]] and [[places/da-nang|Da Nang]] fell in rapid succession — a collapse
that historians (e.g., Willbanks) treat as decisive in the final unraveling that led to
the [[events/fall-of-saigon-1975|fall of Saigon]] weeks later. Truong escaped Vietnam and
resettled in the United States, where he worked with the US Army in historical studies of
the war.
