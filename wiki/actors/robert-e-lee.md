# Robert E. Lee

Career US Army officer who resigned to command the Confederate Army of Northern Virginia (1862-1865). Master of offensive defense and maneuver in the Eastern Theater despite resource disadvantages. Victories at Chancellorsville and early Gettysburg campaign; strategic defeat at Gettysburg and eventual surrender to Grant. Post-war president of Washington College (now Washington and Lee University).
