# Robert E. Lee

Career US Army officer who resigned to command the Confederate Army of Northern Virginia (1862-1865). Master of offensive defense and maneuver in the Eastern Theater despite resource disadvantages. Victories at Chancellorsville and early Gettysburg campaign; strategic defeat at Gettysburg and eventual surrender to Grant. Post-war president of Washington College (now Washington and Lee University).

See: [[american-civil-war]], [[battle-of-gettysburg-1863]], [[battle-of-chancellorsville-1863]].


### From Ellsworth, *Midnight on the Potomac* (2025)

Overland defense (Wilderness–Spotsylvania–Petersburg); strategic detachment of [[actors/early-jubal-a|Early]] to threaten Washington when Valley opened; trapped after Petersburg breach and Sailor's Creek; surrendered ANV at McLean House **~1 p.m. 9 Apr 1865**. Ellsworth stresses war continued after Appomattox (>100k Rebels still fielded). Lost Cause memory (Afterword) later sanitized Lee while isolating Booth—historiographical claim of the source.
