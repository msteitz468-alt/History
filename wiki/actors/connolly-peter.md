# Peter Connolly (1935– )

British historian, illustrator, and experimental archaeologist specialising in the arms, armour, and military organisation of ancient Greece and Rome.

## Contribution

Connolly is best known for his detailed, reconstruction-based books that combine archaeological evidence, practical experimentation (reproducing and testing shields, weapons, and equipment), and clear illustration. His work has been widely used for visualising how ancient soldiers were equipped and how formations actually functioned on the battlefield.

Key publication for the wiki: *Greece and Rome at War* (first 1981; new and revised edition 1998, Greenhill/Stackpole). This volume surveys military development from the Greek city-states through the later Roman Empire, with exceptional depth on equipment, the hoplite phalanx, Macedonian army, Republican and Imperial Roman legions, siege warfare, naval warfare, and military costume. It includes authoritative guest sections by Dr Brian Dobson (Imperial army organisation) and Dr Roger Tomlin (later army).
