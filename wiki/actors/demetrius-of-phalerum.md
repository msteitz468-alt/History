# Demetrius of Phalerum

Athenian statesman, orator, and Peripatetic philosopher who governed Athens on behalf of Cassander (317–307 BCE). Imposed a moderate oligarchy, conducted a census, reformed laws and finances, and promoted culture and philosophy. Driven out by Demetrius Poliorcetes; spent later life at the Ptolemaic court in Alexandria, where he reportedly helped organize the Library.

## Significance
Bridge figure between classical Athenian democracy and Hellenistic monarchy; example of philosopher-statesman in practice; key source of information on late 4th-century Athens via his own writings and later historians.
