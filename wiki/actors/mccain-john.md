# John McCain

**John McCain** (1936–2018) was a US Navy aviator and prisoner of war during the Vietnam
War (1967–1973), later a US Senator from Arizona (1987–2018), and the Republican
presidential nominee in 2008, losing to Barack Obama.

## Role

McCain's 2008 campaign was damaged by his association with the unpopular incumbent
[[bush-george-w|George W. Bush]] administration and by the onset of the
[[global-financial-crisis-2008|global financial crisis]] in September 2008, which shifted
the campaign's focus decisively toward the economy — a context widely seen as favoring
his opponent's message of change. Known in the Senate for a reputation as a
"maverick" willing to break with his party (campaign finance reform, occasional
bipartisan deals), he remained a prominent voice on foreign policy and defense issues
until his death from brain cancer in 2018.
