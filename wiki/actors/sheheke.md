# Sheheke (Big White, Mandan leader)

Mandan leader who traveled with the returning Corps and a delegation to Washington and St. Louis in 1806–1807. One of the key diplomatic outcomes of the expedition; his experiences and the mixed results of the visit are covered in Fehrman's later chapters.
