# Frederick Nolting

U.S. Ambassador to South Vietnam 1961–1963. Close personal friend of Diem; pursued a policy of patient support and working within the Diem government. Replaced by Henry Cabot Lodge in mid-1963 as Washington turned against Diem. His ouster and the subsequent policy reversal were bitterly felt by the "pro-Diem" faction in the embassy and MACV.
