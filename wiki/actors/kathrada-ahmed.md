# Ahmed Kathrada

**Ahmed Mohamed Kathrada** (1929–2017), also known as “Kathy”, was a South African anti-apartheid activist, politician, and long-time political prisoner. A leading member of the South African Indian Congress and later the ANC and SACP, he was a Rivonia Trialist sentenced to life imprisonment with Mandela. Served on Robben Island and at Pollsmoor; close confidant and friend of Mandela throughout the prison years and after. Post-release he served in parliament and as a moral voice of the movement. Meredith draws extensively on Kathrada’s recollections.
