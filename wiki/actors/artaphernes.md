# Artaphernes

Persian commander, co-leader with Datis of the 490 BCE expedition against Athens and Eretria.

## Command at Marathon

Artaphernes shared responsibility for the landing at Marathon and the subsequent operations. The Persian force relied on archers (mounted and on foot) and lacked an equivalent to the Greek phalanx's massed forward pressure. When the Athenians attacked from the protected Vrana valley position, the Persian wings were driven in and the army collapsed.

The commanders were unable to prevent heavy losses during the flight to the ships and the fighting on the beach.
