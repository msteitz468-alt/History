# Karl Lueger

Christian-Social populist Mayor of Vienna (1897 to his death), whose
demagogic, opportunistic antisemitism — combined with genuinely effective
municipal reform — made him the model of antisemitism as a mass-political
technique.

## Role

Lueger used antisemitic rhetoric instrumentally to build a mass Catholic
lower-middle-class coalition; criticized for dining with Jews, he replied
"I decide who's a Yid." Evans (2003) pairs him explicitly with
[[schonerer-georg-von|Schönerer]] to distinguish opportunistic from doctrinal
antisemitism: Lueger wielded prejudice for power and governed pragmatically;
Schönerer's racial hatred was "visceral and unyielding." The young
[[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] observed both in Vienna and later praised Lueger's
mastery of mass politics while faulting his non-racial antisemitism.

## Counterfactual Significance

Evans (attributed): Lueger demonstrated that antisemitism could win and hold
mass urban electorates within a parliamentary system — a lesson about
technique, absorbed by movements with far more radical content.
