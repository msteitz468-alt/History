# The Partisan (Lakota leader)

Rival Lakota leader to Black Buffalo. More theatrical and violent in style; preferred direct extraction of gifts and tolls from traders. Featured prominently in the 1803 Cedar Island confrontation with French traders described in Fehrman (2026) prologue.

Encountered the Corps of Discovery during the tense 1804 Teton Sioux councils. Embodies the competitive, high-stakes politics of the middle Missouri that the American expedition entered as newcomers.
