# Gylippus

Spartan general (mothax, of mixed status). Sent in 414 BCE to Syracuse with a small force to organize resistance against the Athenian Sicilian Expedition. His arrival transformed the defense: fortified Epipolae, coordinated Syracusan naval and land efforts, captured key Athenian positions, and ultimately forced the catastrophic Athenian retreat and surrender in 413 BCE. One of the decisive interventions of the Peloponnesian War.
