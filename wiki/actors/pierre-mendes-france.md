# Pierre Mendès France

French premier (June 1954 – February 1955). He took office with a mandate to end the Indochina war and set a firm deadline of 20 July 1954 for a settlement at Geneva. He personally kept the Foreign Ministry and drove the final negotiations that produced the Geneva Accords.

## Role

Mendès France represented the French political class’s recognition that the war was lost and that continued fighting would destroy what remained of France’s position in Europe and North Africa. His “we must go fast” approach and the July 20 deadline were decisive in forcing the final partition agreement.
