# Adrienne Weill

French physicist and refugee in Britain during WWII. Hosted and mentored a group of French exiles including Rosalind Franklin at a Cambridge hostel. Introduced Franklin to French intellectual and social circles, deepening her Francophilia and influencing her decision to work in Paris after the war at the Laboratoire Central des Services Chimiques de l'État under Marcel Mathieu and Jacques Mering.
