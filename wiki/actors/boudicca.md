# Boudicca (Boudica)

Queen of the Iceni who led a major revolt against Roman rule in Britain (60–61 CE). Iconic figure in Insular Celtic and British history; treated in classical sources (Tacitus, Cassius Dio) and later Celtic and English traditions.
