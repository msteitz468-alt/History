# Gopal Krishna Gokhale

Gopal Krishna Gokhale (1866–1915) was the leading moderate (constitutional) nationalist of the pre-Gandhi era in the Indian National Congress. A protégé of Mahadev Govind Ranade, founder of the Servants of India Society, and a master of parliamentary-style advocacy in the Imperial Legislative Council, he represented the Gladstonian liberal strand of Indian nationalism.

In the context of the Guha biography, Gokhale is the key mentor who visited South Africa in 1912, lent prestige and practical advice to Gandhi's satyagraha campaigns, and smoothed Gandhi's return to India in 1915. Gandhi regarded him as a political guru even while diverging from his methods. Gokhale's early death in 1915 removed a potential bridge between moderate and mass politics.
