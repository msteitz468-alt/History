# Bruno Richard Hauptmann

## Overview
Bruno Richard Hauptmann was a German-born carpenter who was convicted and executed for the abduction and murder of Charles Lindbergh's 20-month-old son, Charles Augustus Lindbergh Jr., in 1932. Hauptmann was arrested in September 1934 after passing a gold certificate from the ransom money. His subsequent trial in Flemington, New Jersey, was called the "Trial of the Century," resulting in his execution in April 1936.

## Actions and Decisions
- **The Kidnapping (March 1, 1932):** Abducted the infant from his nursery in the Lindbergh home in Hopewell, New Jersey, leaving a ladder and a series of ransom notes.
- **Ransom Negotiation:** Extorted a $50,000 ransom through negotiations involving Dr. John F. Condon.
- **Trial and Execution (1935–1936):** Stood trial in a highly sensationalized courtroom environment, maintaining his innocence until his execution by electric chair at Trenton State Prison.
