# Georg Ritter von Schönerer

Austrian Pan-German nationalist and doctrinal racial antisemite — a formative
influence on the political vocabulary and style the young
[[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] absorbed in Vienna.

## Role

Author of the Linz Programme (1879), to which he added an antisemitic twelfth
point (1885) demanding removal of "Jewish influence" from public life; his
slogan ran "Religion's all the same, it's race that is to blame." His
followers called him "the Leader" (*Führer*) — per Evans, possibly the term's
first far-right political usage — and he popularized the "Heil!" greeting. He
sought Austria's annexation to the German Reich. After physically assaulting a
newspaper office (1888) over a false report of Kaiser Wilhelm I's death, he
was stripped of his nobility and imprisoned. His Pan-German Association won 21
Austrian parliamentary seats in 1901 before collapsing in factional infighting.

## Counterfactual Significance

Evans (attributed): Schönerer's influence on Nazism was ideological and
symbolic — vocabulary, greeting, leader-cult, Anschluss aim, doctrinal
antisemitism — rather than organizational, since he never held power. Contrast
[[lueger-karl|Karl Lueger]], whose antisemitism was opportunistic but who
actually governed.
