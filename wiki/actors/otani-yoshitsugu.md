# Otani Yoshitsugu

**Otani Yoshitsugu** (1559–1600) was widely regarded as the most capable battlefield
commander on the Western Army side at the [[events/battle-of-sekigahara-1600|Battle of
Sekigahara]] (1600), fighting in support of [[ishida-mitsunari|Ishida Mitsunari]] against
[[tokugawa-ieyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]]. Despite reportedly suspecting in advance that
[[kobayakawa-hideaki|Kobayakawa Hideaki's]] loyalty was unreliable and positioning his
forces accordingly, Otani's small contingent was overwhelmed once Kobayakawa's defection
triggered a cascade of other Western Army units turning or fleeing. Rather than be
captured — he suffered from a severe illness, traditionally identified as leprosy, that
had already left him nearly blind — Otani committed suicide on the field, and by his own
request his head was hidden to deny the enemy the trophy.
