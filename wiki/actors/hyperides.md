# Hyperides

Athenian orator and statesman, one of the ten canonical Attic orators, and a leading figure — alongside [[actors/demosthenes-orator|Demosthenes]] — of the Athenian anti-Macedonian political faction throughout the mid-fourth century BCE. He helped prosecute the case against Demosthenes in the Harpalus affair (324 BCE) but reconciled with him to lead Athens into the Lamian War (323–322 BCE), the Greek revolt against Macedonian rule launched immediately after Alexander the Great's death, delivering the funeral oration for the war's early Athenian dead. When the war ended in Macedonian victory under [[actors/antipater|Antipater]], Athens was forced to accept a garrison and surrender its anti-Macedonian leaders; Hyperides and Demosthenes fled but were captured, and both were put to death in 322 BCE, effectively ending the era of independent Athenian democratic politics.
