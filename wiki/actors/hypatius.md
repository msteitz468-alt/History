# Hypatius

Byzantine nobleman, nephew of the earlier emperor Anastasius I, and a former general who had held commands (including against the Sassanid Persians) under [[actors/justinian|Justinian I]]. During the Nika riots of January 532 — the violent Constantinople uprising that combined Blue and Green circus-faction unrest with aristocratic opposition to Justinian's ministers — rioters seized on Hypatius, a figure with a plausible claim to the throne through his imperial uncle, and proclaimed him emperor against his own reported reluctance, using him as a figurehead to legitimize their revolt while Justinian's court considered flight. On the advice of Empress Theodora and general Belisarius, Justinian instead had loyal troops trap and massacre the rioters in the Hippodrome, reportedly killing tens of thousands; Hypatius was captured and executed on Justinian's orders the following day despite his apparent lack of direct complicity in instigating the uprising.
