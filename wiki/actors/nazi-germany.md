# Nazi Germany (the Third Reich, 1933–1945)

The totalitarian one-party state established when **[[adolf-hitler|Adolf Hitler]]** and the NSDAP
dismantled the [[weimar-republic|Weimar Republic]] in 1933. Through *Gleichschaltung* the regime
absorbed civil society into a racial "people's community" ([[volksgemeinschaft]]), persecuted Jews and
other targeted groups (the Nuremberg Laws, [[kristallnacht-1938|Kristallnacht]]), rearmed, and
pursued territorial expansion.

In 1939 it launched [[second-world-war-1939|the Second World War]], conquering most of Europe before
the tide turned at Stalingrad. Its war of annihilation in the East and the industrialized murder of
six million Jews — [[the-holocaust|the Holocaust]] — make the regime the moral and analytical centre
of modern German history and the reference point against which the whole [[german-sonderweg|Sonderweg
debate]] is conducted. It ended in total defeat, the suicide of Hitler, and unconditional surrender in
May 1945, followed by Allied [[cold-war|occupation]] and division.
