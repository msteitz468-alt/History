# William L. Yancey

Fire-eating Alabama secessionist, "father of secession," and Confederate diplomat. Famous for his 1858 pro-slave-trade tirade ("the slave trade be piracy, the slave must be plunder") and his disastrous 1861 mission to Britain and France as part of the Yancey-Rost-Mann commission. His public extremism and the Cornerstone Speech damage combined to make early Confederate diplomacy nearly impossible.

## Role in Bonner

Yancey supplied some of the most quotable self-condemnations used by the Confederacy's overseas enemies. His mission's failure illustrated how the South's own words preceded it.
