# 1st Cavalry Division (United States)

US Army division that in 1965 became the Army's first air-mobile (airmobile) division, reorganized around organic helicopter lift to move infantry and artillery rapidly across the battlefield. Under General [[westmoreland-william|William Westmoreland]]'s command in Vietnam, its 1st Cavalry (Airmobile) fought the Battle of Ia Drang in November 1965 — the first major clash between US and regular North Vietnamese Army forces — against troops commanded by Chu Huy Man.

## Role

Ia Drang validated the airmobile concept the division had been created to test, allowing rapid insertion, resupply, and extraction of a small force deep in contested territory, at the cost of intense close-quarters fighting once landing zones were surrounded.
