# Ngo Dinh Nhu

Younger brother and closest political adviser to President Ngo Dinh Diem of South Vietnam. Architect of the family's authoritarian control apparatus, head of the secret Can Lao political party, and de facto controller of the ARVN Special Forces and security services. Married to the influential and polarizing Madame Nhu (Tran Le Xuan).

## Role and Style

Nhu was the Machiavellian operator behind the throne: he ran the secret police, organized the Can Lao as a parallel loyalty structure, and pushed the Strategic Hamlets program as the centerpiece of rural pacification and social engineering. Charismatic in private, ruthless in practice, and deeply suspicious of American intentions even while dependent on US aid. He and his wife became the focal point of US frustration in 1963 over the handling of the Buddhist crisis.

## Fate

Murdered alongside Diem on 2 November 1963 during the coup led by General Duong Van Minh ("Big Minh"). The brothers were shot (or bayoneted) inside an American-made M113 armored personnel carrier after surrendering under false assurances of safe exile.
