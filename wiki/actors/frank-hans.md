# Hans Frank (1900–1946)

Nazi Party chief jurist and, from October 1939, **General Governor of occupied Poland** — ruler of the colonial rump state where most of European Jewry was murdered. Per [[evans-third-reich-at-war-2008|Evans (2008)]]: Frank ran the General Government as "a colony, outside the Reich and beyond its law," executing Hitler's brief of "a hard ethnic struggle that will not permit any legal restrictions." He welcomed the persecution openly — "A pleasure finally to be able to tackle the Jewish race physically. The more that die, the better" — and after the December 1941 Berlin conferences told his staff: "We must annihilate the Jews wherever we come upon them . . . We can't shoot these 3.5 million Jews, we can't poison them, but we will be able to take measures that somehow lead to their successful annihilation." His administration ran the AB-Aktion killings of the intelligentsia (May 1940), compulsory labor drafts, and racial food rationing, and cooperated fully with [[operation-reinhard-1942|Operation Reinhard]], while Frank fought a running jurisdictional war with the SS (winning Krüger's removal in 1943) and looted on a spectacular scale — the Potocki estate, a Berghof imitation near Zakopane, warehouses of confiscated goods documented by Himmler's own 1942 corruption investigation, which he survived through Göring's protection.

Tried at the [[nuremberg-trials-1945-1949|Nuremberg trials]]; the only major defendant to voice (partial, wavering) contrition — "a thousand years will pass and still this guilt of Germany will not have been erased" — hanged 16 October 1946.

## Historiography

Frank's forty-volume official diary, seized in 1945, is a central Nuremberg document and a key source for the General Government; his son Niklas Frank's *Der Vater* (1987) opened the German second-generation reckoning literature.
