# Guglielmo Marconi

**Guglielmo Marconi** (1874–1937) was an Italian electrical engineer and entrepreneur who built the first commercially successful wireless telegraph system and, in December 1901, claimed the first transatlantic radio transmission. He shared the 1909 Nobel Prize in Physics. In Seifer's *Wizard*, Marconi is [[actors/tesla-nikola|Tesla]]'s great rival for radio priority: Tesla held foundational patents on tuned wireless transmission predating Marconi's work, and his apparatus and lectures (1893) anticipated key elements of radio.

Marconi's 1901 transatlantic success helped persuade [[actors/morgan-john-pierpont|J. P. Morgan]] that cheaper point-to-point wireless made Tesla's grander [[places/wardenclyffe|Wardenclyffe]] scheme unnecessary. The priority question was litigated for decades; the U.S. Supreme Court upheld key Tesla patents over Marconi's in 1943, months after Tesla's death (see [[events/marconi-radio-priority-dispute-1943]]).
