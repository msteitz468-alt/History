# Robert Gates

**Robert Gates** (b. 1943) was Director of Central Intelligence (1991–1993) and later
United States Secretary of Defense (2006–2011), the only Defense Secretary retained
across a change of presidential administration of opposing parties, serving under both
[[bush-george-w|George W. Bush]] and Barack Obama.

## Role

Gates succeeded Donald Rumsfeld at the Pentagon after the 2006
midterm elections, amid rising casualties and public disillusionment with the Iraq War.
He worked closely with Bush and commanders (notably Gen. David Petraeus) to implement the
2007 Iraq troop "surge," replaced holdover military leadership seen as resistant to the
new strategy, and later oversaw the Afghanistan surge and the drawdown from Iraq under
Obama. He was widely credited with restoring functional civil-military relations and
institutional competence at the Pentagon after a period of friction under Rumsfeld.
