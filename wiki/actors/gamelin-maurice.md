# Maurice Gamelin

French Army general and Allied Commander-in-Chief (1939-40). Architect of the Dyle Plan for advancing into Belgium. His forces were outmaneuvered by German sickle-cut through the Ardennes. Replaced by Weygand after the rapid collapse of France. Symbol of the failures of interwar French military thought and Allied coordination.
