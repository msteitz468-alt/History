# Lewis L. Strauss

Investment banker, naval officer, and chairman of the Atomic Energy Commission (1953–58). Strauss developed a strong personal and policy antagonism toward J. Robert Oppenheimer, particularly over the H-bomb and Oppenheimer's perceived influence. In December 1953 he presented Oppenheimer with the letter of charges that led to the security hearing. He played a central role in the proceedings and the subsequent revocation of clearance.
