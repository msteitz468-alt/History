# Folkung Dynasty (Bjälbo Dynasty)

The dynasty that consolidated the Swedish monarchy from the mid-13th century,
founded in practice by the regent **Birger Jarl** (d. 1266), whose son Valdemar
became king and who is traditionally credited with founding [[stockholm|Stockholm]]
and issuing peace legislation. Under Magnus Ladulås and Magnus Eriksson the dynasty
strengthened royal law and aristocratic privilege and extended Swedish power into
[[finland|Finland]]. Dynastic failure and aristocratic revolt opened the way to
Mecklenburg rule and ultimately to the [[kalmar-union-1397|Kalmar Union]].
