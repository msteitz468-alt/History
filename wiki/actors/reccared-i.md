# Reccared I

Visigothic king of Hispania (r. 586–601). Converted from Arianism to Nicene (Catholic) Christianity at the Third Council of Toledo (589), along with much of the Gothic aristocracy and clergy. This religious unification with the Hispano-Roman population was a major step in the consolidation of the Visigothic kingdom and its integration into the Catholic world of late antiquity and the early Middle Ages.

## Significance
Symbolic and practical turning point in the ethnogenesis of medieval Spain; model for later royal conversions (e.g., Clovis, Ethelbert) in the "barbarian" West.
