# Graça Machel

**Graça Machel** (née Simbine, b. 1945) is a Mozambican politician, humanitarian, and the widow of both Mozambican President Samora Machel and Nelson Mandela. Minister of Education in post-independence Mozambique; widow of Samora Machel (killed 1986). Developed a close relationship with Mandela after 1990; married him on his 80th birthday in 1998 in a private ceremony. Remained an independent public figure focused on child welfare, education, and women’s rights. Meredith portrays the late-life marriage as a source of genuine happiness for Mandela in retirement.
