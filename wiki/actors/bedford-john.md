# John of Lancaster, Duke of Bedford

John of Lancaster, Duke of Bedford (1389 – 1435) was the third son of Henry IV of England and brother of Henry V. During the minority of his nephew Henry VI, Bedford served as Regent of France (1422–1435), where he worked tirelessly to consolidate and defend the Lancastrian dual monarchy.
