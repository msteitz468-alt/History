# John Marshall

Fourth Chief Justice of the United States (1801-1835). Established the principle of judicial review in Marbury v. Madison (1803). Key decisions expanded federal power over states (McCulloch v. Maryland, Gibbons v. Ogden). Former Secretary of State under Adams; veteran of Revolutionary War. Shaped the Supreme Court as a co-equal branch and US constitutional law.
