# Anna Howard Shaw (1847–1919)

Physician, ordained Methodist minister, and president of NAWSA (1904–1915); major orator of the pre-war suffrage movement. Bridged the Anthony generation and [[catt-carrie-chapman|Carrie Chapman Catt]]'s Winning Plan era. Appears in Cox as a principal interlocutor with Wilson-era official Washington before the final ratification push.
