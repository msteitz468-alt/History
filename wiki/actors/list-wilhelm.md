# Wilhelm List

**Wilhelm List** (1880–1971) was a German field marshal who commanded the invasion of
Yugoslavia and Greece in 1941 and, in 1942, Army Group A during the drive into the
Caucasus ([[events/operation-edelweiss-1942|Operation Edelweiss]]). Hitler relieved him of
command in September 1942 amid frustration over the slowing advance toward the Caucasus
oilfields, and he held no further active command for the rest of the war. Postwar, List
was convicted of war crimes (relating to reprisal killings in the Balkans campaign) at the
US Hostages Trial (1948) and sentenced to life imprisonment, though he was released in
1952.
