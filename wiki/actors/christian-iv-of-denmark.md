# Christian IV of Denmark (1577–1648)

King of [[denmark-norway|Denmark–Norway]] 1588–1648, the longest reign in Danish
history. A builder-king (Christiania/Oslo, fortifications, the chartered trading
companies) and patron of culture, his ambitious foreign policy drew Denmark into
the [[thirty-years-war-1618|Thirty Years' War]] (1625–29), where defeat by
Imperial forces and later wars against [[swedish-empire|Sweden]] eroded Danish
primacy in the [[struggle-for-baltic-supremacy|struggle for Baltic supremacy]].
His reign frames the transition from the aristocratic regime toward the fiscal
pressures that produced [[scandinavian-absolutism|Danish absolutism]] in 1660.
