# Neville Chamberlain

British Prime Minister (1937–1940). Architect of the Munich Agreement ("peace for our time") with Hitler. Pursued appeasement to buy time for rearmament while avoiding immediate war. After German invasion of Poland declared war; led early "Phoney War" period. Resigned after Norway debate and failures in France; replaced by Churchill. Died shortly after.
