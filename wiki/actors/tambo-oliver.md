# Oliver Tambo

**Oliver Reginald Tambo** (1917–1993) was a South African lawyer, anti-apartheid activist, and long-time president of the African National Congress in exile (1967–1991). Close partner of Mandela and Walter Sisulu from the Youth League days; went into exile after the 1960 emergency while Mandela remained underground. Provided the external face and diplomatic leadership of the ANC for three decades. Returned to South Africa in 1990; died shortly before the 1994 election. Mandela regarded him as the movement’s conscience and organizational backbone.
