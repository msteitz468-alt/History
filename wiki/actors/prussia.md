# Prussia

The militarized north-German state that, from a scattered Hohenzollern patrimony, became the
dominant German power and the architect of [[german-unification|German unification]] in 1871.
Forged into a great power by **Frederick William I** and **[[frederick-the-great|Frederick the Great]]**
(whose seizure of Silesia in 1740 launched a century of rivalry with Habsburg Austria), Prussia
combined a disciplined army, an efficient bureaucracy, and a Junker landed aristocracy.

Crushed by Napoleon at Jena-Auerstedt (1806), Prussia rebuilt through the Stein–Hardenberg reforms,
emerged from the Congress of Vienna with the industrializing Rhineland, and led the [[zollverein|Zollverein]]
customs union. Under [[otto-von-bismarck|Bismarck]] it defeated Austria ([[austro-prussian-war-1866]])
and France ([[franco-prussian-war-1870]]) to found the [[german-empire|German Empire]], within which
Prussia remained the hegemonic core until 1918. It survived as the largest Weimar state, was
captured by the Nazis in 1932–33, and was formally abolished by the Allies in 1947.
