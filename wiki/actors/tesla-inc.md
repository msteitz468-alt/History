# Tesla Inc.

Electric vehicle and energy company. Co-founded 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning; Elon Musk joined as chairman and lead investor 2004, became CEO 2008. Key products: Roadster (2008), Model S (2012), Model 3/Y mass market, Cybertruck, Semi; energy storage and solar via SolarCity merger. Fremont Factory, Gigafactory Nevada, Shanghai, Berlin, Texas. Mission: accelerate the world’s transition to sustainable energy.
