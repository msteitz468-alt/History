## Role and Background

Witigis was a Gothic officer of undistinguished birth elevated to the Ostrogothic throne by the army in 536 after it deposed the ineffectual [[actors/theodahad|Theodahad]] in the face of [[actors/belisarius|Belisarius's]] rapid advance through Italy at the opening of the [[events/gothic-war-535-554|Gothic War]].

## War Against Belisarius

Witigis gathered the main Gothic field army and besieged Rome for over a year (537–538) after Belisarius occupied the city, cutting its aqueducts and mounting repeated assaults that Belisarius's smaller garrison repulsed. Unable to dislodge the Byzantines from Rome, Witigis withdrew north; Belisarius subsequently marched on the Gothic capital, Ravenna, which fell in 540 after Belisarius induced its surrender by feigning acceptance of a Gothic offer to proclaim him Western Roman Emperor. Witigis was taken prisoner and brought to Constantinople.

## Aftermath

In captivity, Witigis was granted the honorary rank of patrician by Justinian and lived out his remaining years in Constantinople, dying around 542. His capture did not end Ostrogothic resistance, which revived under his successors Ildibad, Eraric, and above all Totila.
