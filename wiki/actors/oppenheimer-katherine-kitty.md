# Katherine "Kitty" Oppenheimer

Biologist; second wife of J. Robert Oppenheimer. Former Communist Party member whose first husband died in the Spanish Civil War. Married Oppenheimer in 1940; accompanied him to Los Alamos. The marriage was intense, passionate, and marked by heavy drinking and volatility. She stood by him during the 1954 hearing. Later life was difficult; she died in 1972.
