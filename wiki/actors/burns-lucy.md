# Lucy Burns (1879–1966)

Vassar-educated American suffrage organizer; closest collaborator of [[paul-alice|Alice Paul]] in the Congressional Union / National Woman's Party. Shared British militant experience; co-organized White House picketing ([[silent-sentinels-1917]]) and endured repeated imprisonment, including Occoquan Workhouse force-feeding. Cox's *Light Withdrawn* uses Burns as a principal witness to administration repression of wartime suffrage protest.

## Role

Operational co-leader of the militant federal campaign: banners, picket logistics, jail solidarity, and publicity. Less the public theorist than Paul; more the field commander of civil-disobedience pressure on [[wilson-woodrow|Wilson]].
