# Komalatammal

Komalatammal (also referred to as Komalatammal Iyengar), mother of Srinivasa Ramanujan. The dominant figure in his early life and the decisive influence on his character and persistence. A devout Iyengar Brahmin woman, she managed the household, arranged her son’s marriage, and provided the emotional and cultural framework in which mathematics was understood as a form of devotion. She supported his mathematical obsession even when it destroyed his prospects for a conventional degree and career. Ramanujan attributed many of his insights to dreams sent by the family goddess; his mother was the human guardian of that world.
