# Dwight D. Eisenhower

President of the United States (1953–1961). He inherited the growing U.S. commitment to the French in Indochina. During the Dien Bien Phu crisis he was deeply involved in the debate over direct U.S. intervention (Operation Vulture) but ultimately decided against it without allied (especially British) support and congressional authorization. After Geneva he supported the policy of building a non-Communist state in southern Vietnam.

## Role

Eisenhower was cautious about committing U.S. ground forces in Asia after Korea but accepted the strategic importance of preventing a Communist takeover of Indochina. His administration dramatically increased aid to the French and then to the Diem regime. He is closely associated with the domino theory in public statements.
