# Alfred E. Smith

Four-time Governor of New York and 1928 Democratic presidential nominee. Smith provided the political protection and the initial institutional opportunities that allowed Robert Moses to build the legal and organizational machinery of public authorities and the state park system. Moses' early career and the foundational statutes for the Long Island State Park Commission and related bodies were made possible by Smith's governorships. Later Moses operated with far greater autonomy, but the Smith-era innovations in bypassing normal legislative and budgetary controls were the template for everything that followed.
