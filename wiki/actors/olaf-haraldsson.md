# Olaf Haraldsson (St Olaf)

King of [[norway|Norway]] (c. 1015–1028) who advanced Christianization and royal
power, was driven out by [[cnut-the-great|Cnut the Great]], and was killed at the
[[battle-of-stiklestad-1030|Battle of Stiklestad]] (1030) attempting to recover
his throne. Rapidly canonised, he became *Rex Perpetuus Norvegiae* — Norway's
eternal king and patron saint — his shrine at Nidaros (Trondheim) the spiritual
centre of the kingdom. His cult, more than his reign, consolidated both Norwegian
[[christianization-of-scandinavia|Christianity]] and the national monarchy.
