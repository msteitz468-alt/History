# The NKVD
## Biography / Description
The **NKVD** (People's Commissariat for Internal Affairs) was the central state security and law enforcement agency of the Soviet Union from 1934 to 1946. It absorbed the OGPU (the successor to the Cheka) and assumed responsibility for regular police work, border guards, state security, and the administration of the Gulag concentration camp system. The NKVD was successively headed by Genrikh Yagoda (1934–1936), Nikolai Ezhov (1936–1938), and Lavrentii Beria (1938–1945). It was renamed the MVD (Ministry of Internal Affairs) in 1946.

## Key Decisions and Achievements
- **Execution of the Great Purge (1936–1938):** Under Nikolai Ezhov, the NKVD executed Stalin's orders to purge the Party, military, and general population. Millions of citizens were arrested, interrogated, sent to labor camps, or executed. The NKVD focused heavily on extracting false confessions to document a massive, imaginary conspiracy against Soviet power (Line 6121).
- **Administration of the Gulag:** The NKVD managed a vast empire of forced-labor camps (the Gulag), utilizing millions of prisoners for large-scale economic projects such as mining, logging, and building canals and railways (e.g., the White Sea Canal).
- **Mass Deportations:** The NKVD conducted mass deportations of "suspect" ethnic minorities from border areas and conquered territories, including the Volga Germans, Chechens, and Crimean Tatars during World War II, as well as class enemies from the Baltic states and eastern Poland.

## Historical Significance
The NKVD was the primary institutional arm of Stalinist terror, responsible for some of the worst human rights abuses of the twentieth century. Riasanovsky notes that "the vast resources of the NKVD were concentrated on one objective—to document the existence of a huge conspiracy to undermine Soviet power" (Line 6121). Ezhov himself and many of his collaborators were eventually executed in the purge, illustrating the "permanent purge" dynamic of the era. Its state security functions were later split off and ultimately reconstituted in the KGB.
