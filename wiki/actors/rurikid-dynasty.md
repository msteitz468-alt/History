# The Rurikid Dynasty
## Biography / Description
The **Rurikid Dynasty** was the founding ruling dynasty of [[kievan-rus|Kievan Rus′]] and the subsequent Russian principalities. According to the *Primary Chronicle*, the dynasty was founded by **Rurik**, a Varangian (Norse/Scandinavian) prince who was invited by Slavic and Finnic tribes in 862 CE to rule over Novgorod and bring order to their lands. 

The dynasty moved its seat to Kiev in 882 CE under Oleg, establishing a centralized state along the Dnieper trade route. Over the centuries, the dynasty branched out extensively, ruling various appanages during the period of political fragmentation, and eventually culminating in the Grand Dukes of Moscow who unified the Great Russian lands and established the Russian Tsardom.

## Key Decisions and Achievements
* **Invitation and early bases (862 CE):** Per Primary Chronicle (ll. 3250–3287): after Varangians driven out and "tribe rose against tribe," Chuds/Slavs/Krivichians/Ves’ invite "the Varangian Russes" ("Our land is great and rich, but there is no order in it. Come to rule and reign over us"). Rurik locates in Novgorod; Sineus Beloozero; Truvor Izborsk. "On account of these Varangians, the district of Novgorod became known as the land of Rus’. The present inhabitants of Novgorod are descended from the Varangian race, but aforetime they were Slavs." Rurik sole rule after brothers' deaths; assigns Polotsk/Rostov/Beloozero. Askold/Dir (non-kin) settle Kiev.
* **Unification of the Dnieper Route (882 CE):** Oleg (Rurik kin, regent for young Igor) marches south; captures Smolensk/Lyubech; tricks and kills Askold and Dir ("You are not princes nor even of princely stock, but I am of princely birth"; Igor displayed as Rurik's son). "Oleg set himself up as prince in Kiev, and declared that it should be the mother of Russian cities." (ll. 3312–3337). Builds stockaded towns; imposes tribute; Novgorod pays 300 grivny/year to Varangians.
* **Oleg's expansion and Byzantine treaty (883–912):** Subjugates Derevlians (black marten tribute), Severians (light tribute, forbid Khazar payment), Radimichians (shilling). 907 raid: 2000 ships vs Greeks (wheels on ships); 911/912 treaty (detailed: 12 grivny/man, supplies, Perun/Volos oath by Russes/cross by Greeks; full provisions on trade/murder/theft/ransom). Oleg dies 912 from serpent (horse prophecy; "So I was supposed to receive my death from this skull?").
* **Igor, Olga, Svyatoslav (913–972):** Igor renews treaties (detailed 945 text with Romanus et al., sworn Perun + St. Elias); killed 945 by Derevlians on extra tribute. Olga regent: multi-phase revenge on Derevlians (ll. 4174–4261: trench burial, bath fire, funeral massacre 5000); reforms tribute (pogosti); baptised Constantinople c.955 as Helena (refuses emperor marriage; "How can you marry me, after yourself baptizing me and calling me your daughter?"). Urges Svyatoslav baptise (refused). Svyatoslav: nomadic campaigns (no wagons/kettles); 965 Khazars (Bela Vezha); 967 Danube (Pereyaslavets base); 968 Pecheneg Kiev siege (youth swims warning; Pretich deception rescue); 971 Greek treaty (Perun/Volos; no Kherson attack); killed 972 by Pechenegs at cataracts (skull cup).
* **Vladimir's rise and conversion (980–988):** 978–980: returns with Varangians; kills Rogvolod/Polotsk sons; takes Rogned; betrays/kills Yaropolk via Blud ("two Varangians stabbed him"). Sets pagan idols (Perun silver head/gold mustache + others); "the land of Rus’ and this hill were defiled with blood"; many wives/concubines (300+). 983 Varangian martyrs refuse idols. 986 missions ("test of the faiths" — Greek scholar's full creation-to-judgment narrative). 987 envoys impressed by Greek rite ("we knew not whether we were in heaven or on earth"); cite Olga. 988: takes Kherson (Anastasius cuts water pipes); demands/marries Anna (baptism precondition); baptised St. Basil church in Kherson (eyes healed: "I have now perceived the one true God"); creed, 7 councils, anti-Latin instruction; returns with relics/Anna (ll. 5796–5999+).
* **Conversion to Orthodoxy (988 CE):** Vladimir I decided to adopt Byzantine Christianity, a choice that permanently determined the cultural, religious, and political alignment of the East Slavic lands. From Primary Chronicle (lines 6006-6062): idols overthrown (Perun dragged/beaten/cast in Dnieper); mass Dnieper baptism; churches founded on idol sites (St. Basil on Perun hill); tithe church built 989 with Greek artisans and Kherson spoils; children sent for book learning.
* **Fratricide and Civil Wars (1015):** After Vladimir's death (July 15, 1015; buried in Church of Virgin he built; eulogized as new Constantine, lines 6393-6478), Svyatopolk murdered brothers Boris (Al'ta: stabbed in tent while singing psalms; finished by Varangians; George Hungarian servant beheaded for necklace, lines 6506-6551) and Gleb (lured, stabbed by cook Torchin; extensive martyrdom panegyric naming them protectors/healers of Rus', lines 6570-6656); also Svyatoslav. Yaroslav defeated Svyatopolk (1016 Lyubech with Novgorod/Varangian force; 1019 decisive Al'ta after invoking brothers' blood; Svyatopolk dies tormented, "new Cain", evil-odored tomb, lines 6680-6827).
* **Codification of Law and Division:** Yaroslav the Wise (r. 1019–1054) promulgated the *Pravda Russkaia* (Russian Justice), the first legal code of the East Slavs. 1026 division with Mstislav (Dnieper boundary; "great calm", lines 6919-6926); Yaroslav sole after 1036. 1054 testament: Kiev to Izyaslav, Chernigov Svyatoslav, Pereyaslavl' Vsevolod etc.; "Love one another... if ye dwell in envy... bring to ruin the land of your ancestors" (lines 7214-7231). Led to fragmentation.
* **Monastic and cultural legacy:** Under Yaroslav and sons, Crypt Monastery founded (Antonius, Theodosius adopts Studion rule, lines 7081-7207); Theodosius death 1074 with detailed hagiography (lines 7818-7961); 1072 translation of Boris/Gleb relics with miracle (lines 7751-7790).
* **Establishment of the Appanage System:** Dynastic practice (such as Yaroslav's testament of 1054) partitioned lands among sons, leading to the fragmentation of Rus' into autonomous principalities.
* **The Gathering of Russian Lands:** Rurikid grand princes of Moscow (starting with Daniel and Ivan I Kalita) successfully consolidated territory, defeated their rivals (notably the Rurikid princes of Tver), and overthrew the Mongol yoke under Ivan III.

## Historical Significance
The Rurikid dynasty provided the essential thread of political and cultural continuity that preserved the concept of the "Russian land" (*Russkaia zemlia*) during centuries of feudal fragmentation (the appanage period) and Mongol domination. 

While the early Rurikids were of Scandinavian origin, they rapidly Slavicized, adopting Slavic names (beginning with Sviatoslav I), the Slavic language, and local customs. The dynasty's rule witnessed the ethnic and linguistic differentiation of the East Slavs into Great Russians, Ukrainians, and Belorussians. The dynasty's senior branch in Moscow ruled Russia until the death of Tsar Feodor I in 1598 CE, leading to the Time of Troubles.
