# Christian II of Denmark

The last [[kalmar-union-1397|union]] king to rule all three Scandinavian kingdoms
(king of Denmark and Norway 1513, of Sweden 1520), and the figure whose actions ended
the union. Per [[helle-cambridge-history-scandinavia-v1-2003|the Cambridge History]],
he governed Norway as viceroy 1506–13, crushed Knut Alvsson's Norwegian rebellion,
and made war on Sten Sture the Younger to enforce union rule over Sweden.

## Reign and Fall

After his conquest and coronation in Sweden he ordered the
[[stockholm-bloodbath-1520|Stockholm Bloodbath]] (1520), which provoked Gustav Vasa's
revolt and the union's breakup. A centralising ruler, he issued a uniform **Landlaw
(1521) and Town Law (1522)** strengthening the crown's judicial role at the expense of
Church and nobility and favouring burghers (especially in København and Malmö) — laws
cancelled by his successor's accession charter. Alienating the Danish council and
nobility, he was **deposed in 1523** in favour of his uncle Frederik I, spent years in
exile attempting to recover his throne, and died a prisoner. See
[[helle-cambridge-history-scandinavia-v1-2003]].
