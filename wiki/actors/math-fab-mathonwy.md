# Math fab Mathonwy

Legendary king and magician of Gwynedd, the title character of the Fourth Branch of the Mabinogi (the "Math fab Mathonwy" branch) in the medieval Welsh prose collection known as the Mabinogion. A quasi-divine, otherworldly figure bound by a taboo requiring that, except during war, his feet must rest in the lap of a virgin footholder — a condition central to the branch's plot involving his nephews Gwydion and Gilfaethwy, the war with Pryderi of Dyfed, and the eventual creation of Blodeuwedd, a woman made of flowers.

As with other figures in the Mabinogion, Math is not a historical person but a legendary/mythological figure preserved in medieval Welsh literary tradition, generally understood by scholars to encode much older, pre-Christian Celtic mythological material reworked within a medieval narrative frame. The Fourth Branch is considered one of the most mythologically dense of the Four Branches, dealing with shapeshifting, magic, and taboo in ways that scholars connect to broader Insular Celtic mythological patterns.

## Significance

Math fab Mathonwy is a central figure for understanding the survival of pre-Christian Welsh/Celtic mythological material within medieval literary form, illustrating how the Mabinogion preserves older mythic structures beneath a medieval narrative surface.
