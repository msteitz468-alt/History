# Pequot

Southern New England Algonquian nation controlling lower Connecticut trade before the [[pequot-war-1637]]. Survivors endured under supervision/enslavement; some later fought as English allies in [[king-philips-war-1675]]. Modern Pequot nations persist in Connecticut.

## Overview

In the 1620s–30s Pequot sachems (with Narragansetts) specialized in wampum production exchanged for European goods; Dutch commercialized the beads. Resented for Connecticut trade dominance by [[narragansett]], Mohegans, and Dutch. Smallpox 1633–34 and English immigration undermined the system. Massachusetts extracted tribute then, with Connecticut, Mohegans, and Narragansetts, destroyed Mystic (several hundred of all ages/sexes killed); survivors supervised by Narragansett/Mohegan or enslaved; fugitives seeking [[mohawk]] aid killed. Autonomy crushed; English secure on Connecticut. Residual Pequots sided with English in 1675–76 (ties to outside Indians severed earlier).
