# Ronald Norrish

British physical chemist, Professor at Cambridge. Nobel laureate (1967, shared) for studies of extremely fast chemical reactions (flash photolysis). During WWII his lab at Cambridge was severely reduced; he supervised Rosalind Franklin early in her career, assigning projects she found uninteresting and clashing with her over research direction. Later recognized for foundational contributions to reaction kinetics.
