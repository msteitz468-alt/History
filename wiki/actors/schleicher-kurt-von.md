# Kurt von Schleicher

Political general of the Reichswehr, master intriguer behind the presidential
cabinets of 1930–32, and the Weimar Republic's last Chancellor (December 1932 –
January 1933). His scheme to split the Nazis via Gregor
[[gregor-strasser|Strasser]] failed; outmaneuvered by
[[papen-franz-von|Papen]], he was murdered with his wife in the
[[night-of-the-long-knives-1934|Night of the Long Knives]].

## Role and Decisions

Per Evans ([[evans-coming-of-third-reich-2003]]): "ambitious, quick-witted,
talkative and rather too fond of political intrigue for his own good," he rose
via the army's new "Ministerial Office" (1929) representing the armed forces
to government — Trotsky called him "a question mark with the epaulettes of a
general"; a journalist, a "sphinx in uniform." His doctrine, autumn 1930: "Not
a brick can be moved any more in the political process in Germany without the
word of the army being thrown decisively onto the scales." He believed an
authoritarian regime could gain mass legitimacy by "harnessing and taming"
the Nazis in the service of rearmament — while combating Nazi sympathies among
junior officers, he shared "the national part" of their program.

He made and unmade chancellors: recommended [[papen-franz-von|Papen]] (June
1932), engineered the July elections and SA-ban lift, then withdrew the
army's support from Papen and took the chancellorship himself (3 December
1932). As Chancellor he negotiated separately with
[[gregor-strasser|Gregor Strasser]] for a cabinet post (triggering Strasser's
resignation of all party offices, 8 December 1932) and prepared a job-creation
program exploiting the departure from gold. Hindenburg, resentful over Papen's
fall, refused him the extra-constitutional powers he had denied to no one
else's disadvantage — and used the refusal to be rid of him. Amid rumors that
Schleicher and army chief Hammerstein would stage a counter-coup (Papen on 30
January: "If a new government is not formed by 11 o'clock, the army will
march"), Hitler was sworn in. Schleicher's own verdict was complacent: "If
Hitler wants to establish a dictatorship in the Reich, then the army will be
the dictatorship within the dictatorship."

He and his wife were shot in their home in the
[[night-of-the-long-knives-1934|Night of the Long Knives]] (30 June 1934).

## Counterfactual Significance

The Schleicher military-regime counterfactual is one Evans himself poses in
his conclusion (see [[evans-coming-of-third-reich-2003]]): a
Schleicher-Hammerstein authoritarian corporate state suppressing both Nazis
and Communists was the live alternative to 30 January 1933 — arguably the
last one.
