# Coboway (Clatsop leader)

Clatsop leader near the mouth of the Columbia. Hosted the Corps during their 1805–1806 winter at Fort Clatsop. Featured in Fehrman's rotating chapters as an example of a local leader for whom the expedition was a minor episode in ongoing trade and intertribal politics. The Corps traded with, observed, and depended on Clatsop knowledge and resources.
