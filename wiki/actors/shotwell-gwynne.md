# Gwynne Shotwell

President and Chief Operating Officer of SpaceX (joined 2002 as employee #7). Responsible for day-to-day operations, customer contracts, and launch cadence while Musk focused on product and long-term vision. Key stabilizing force during 2008 near-bankruptcy period.
