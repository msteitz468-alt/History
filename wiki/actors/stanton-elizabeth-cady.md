# Elizabeth Cady Stanton (1815–1902)

Intellectual leader of the early American women's rights movement; Seneca Falls Declaration of Sentiments (1848); lifelong collaborator and occasional rival of [[anthony-susan-b|Susan B. Anthony]]. Co-editor of *History of Woman Suffrage*. Cox opens *Light Withdrawn* with the abolition–women's-rights "kindred causes" lineage of which Stanton is a central figure.
