# Hugh Dowding

Air Chief Marshal and head of RAF Fighter Command during the Battle of Britain. Architect of the integrated air defense system (radar/Chain Home, sector control, Spitfire/Hurricane deployment). Advocated conservation of fighter strength and resisted diversion of resources to France or premature offensive use. Key to Britain's survival in 1940 despite being sidelined afterward.
