# Lavrentiy Beria

Lavrentiy Pavlovich Beria was a Georgian Bolshevik who rose through the secret police apparatus in the Caucasus. He succeeded Nikolai Yezhov as head of the NKVD in late 1938, consolidating control over the security services. Beria managed the later phases of the Terror, the Gulag economy, deportations, and wartime security/intelligence roles. He was a key member of Stalin’s inner circle until Stalin’s death, after which he was arrested and executed in the power struggle.

Beria appears toward the end of the 1929–1941 period as Yezhov’s successor-designate and a rising figure in the security apparatus.
