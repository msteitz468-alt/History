# Socrates

Athenian philosopher (c. 470–399 BCE). Father of Western ethical and political philosophy. Known through the writings of Plato, Xenophon, and Aristophanes. Developed the elenchus (Socratic method of questioning) to expose ignorance and pursue definitions of virtues (justice, piety, courage, temperance). Served as hoplite; criticized Athenian democracy and sophists. Tried and executed in 399 BCE on charges of impiety and corrupting the youth (refused to escape or compromise). Central figure in the intellectual life of Classical Athens and the transition from Presocratic to systematic philosophy.
