# 101st Airborne Division (United States)

US Army airborne division ("Screaming Eagles") activated in 1942, famed for its parachute assault into Normandy on D-Day and its defense of Bastogne during the Battle of the Bulge. Converted to an air-assault (helicopter-borne) role, it deployed to Vietnam from 1967, where its 3rd Brigade under Weldon Honeycutt conducted the assault on Ap Bia Mountain in May 1969 — the Battle of Hamburger Hill — a costly ten-day frontal assault against entrenched North Vietnamese forces that the division abandoned days after capturing it, fueling domestic controversy over the war's conduct.

## Role

The division's shift from parachute to helicopter-borne air assault operations in the 1960s made it the US Army's premier air-mobility formation, a doctrinal template later formalized across the post-Vietnam force.
