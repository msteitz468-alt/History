# Denmark–Norway (the Oldenburg dual monarchy, c. 1524–1814)

The composite monarchy ruling Denmark, Norway, and the North Atlantic
dependencies (Iceland, the Faroes, Greenland), plus the duchies of Schleswig and
Holstein, under the House of Oldenburg. After the Lutheran
[[reformation-in-scandinavia|Reformation]] (1536) the crown absorbed church lands;
the realm developed a colonial and trading reach (chartered companies, tropical
colonies) under [[christian-iv-of-denmark|Christian IV]] and successors. Defeats
in the [[struggle-for-baltic-supremacy|Baltic supremacy struggle]] against
[[swedish-empire|Sweden]] cost the eastern provinces (Roskilde, 1658) and drove
the introduction of [[scandinavian-absolutism|absolutism]] in 1660. The union was
dissolved when Norway was ceded to Sweden by the
[[treaty-of-kiel-1814|Treaty of Kiel]] (1814).
