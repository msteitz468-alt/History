# Paul Moses

Elder brother of Robert Moses. A highly qualified electrical and utility engineer, Paul Moses received markedly different treatment from his mother Bella's estate and from his brother's influence in New York government.

After Bella Moses' death, her will left Paul only a $100,000 trust (interest only, with Robert as a trustee figure), while the bulk went to Robert and sister Edna. Paul experienced long periods with no income from the trust due to investments and deductions.

Despite recommendations from La Guardia aides (e.g., Windels, Kern), Robert Moses blocked Paul from permanent high-level posts in the city administration (Water Supply, Gas & Electricity, etc.). Paul received only temporary consulting work. He lived in severe poverty (Salvation Army lodgings, inadequate food and clothing) while his brother directed billions in public projects and patronage.

Robert Moses refused to publicly acknowledge having a brother in interviews and controlled biographies; he reacted with rage to questions about Paul.

This family story forms a significant counter-narrative in Caro's account of power, loyalty, and ruthlessness.
