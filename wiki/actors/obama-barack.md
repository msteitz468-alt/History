# Barack Obama (b. 1961)

44th President of the United States (2009–2017), first African American president. Community organizer, Illinois state senator, US Senator (2005–2008). Rose to national prominence with 2004 Democratic National Convention keynote. Won 2008 election amid financial crisis on platform of hope, change, and post-partisan governance.

## Overview / Role
Born in Honolulu to Barack Obama Sr. (Kenyan) and Ann Dunham (Kansas); raised partly in Indonesia and by grandparents in Hawaii. Harvard Law, editor of Law Review, University of Chicago lecturer, worked as civil rights attorney and community organizer in Chicago. Memoir *Dreams from My Father* (1995) explores identity.

Presidency defined by Affordable Care Act (Obamacare) as signature domestic achievement (expanded coverage but political lightning rod); response to Great Recession (stimulus, auto bailout); foreign policy pivot to Asia, Iran nuclear deal, Paris climate accord, drawdown in Iraq/Afghanistan with mixed results (rise of ISIS); authorization of bin Laden raid; domestic battles over immigration, guns, race (post-Ferguson). Re-elected 2012.

## Key Relationships
- Family: Michelle Obama, daughters Malia and Sasha.
- Political: Joe Biden (VP), Hillary Clinton (Secretary of State then 2016 nominee), Rahm Emanuel.
- Opponents: Tea Party movement, Republican congressional opposition (Mitch McConnell), later Donald Trump.

## Causal Role and Counterfactuals
ACA represented major expansion of social safety net; survived legal challenges and repeal attempts. Foreign policy emphasized multilateralism and "leading from behind" but criticized for weakness (red line in Syria, Crimea). Helped normalize discourse on race and inequality while facing backlash. Without Obama, trajectory of US healthcare reform and post-2008 recovery likely different; polarization arguably accelerated regardless.

## Historiography
Early assessments focus on symbolism vs. substantive change, ACA durability, and whether post-racial hopes were realistic. Critics on left fault insufficient structural reform; on right, overreach and cultural shifts. Primary sources include his own writings and post-presidency memoir *A Promised Land*. Collection coverage of Obama era still developing.
