# William Butler Yeats

Poet and playwright, foremost contributor to the Irish Literary Revival. Born in Dublin to painter John Butler Yeats; folk-tales heard in Sligo provided inspiration. Collections include *The Celtic Twilight* (1893) and *The Wind Among the Reeds* (1899). Instrumental in founding the National Literary Society (1892) and the Irish Literary Theatre (later Abbey). Nationalist play *Cathleen Ní Houlihan* (1902). Dramatic output includes Noh-influenced plays and several based on Cú Chulainn and the Ulster Cycle. Esoteric works *Per Amica Silentia Lunae* (1918) and *A Vision* (1925, 1937). Perceived his lineage as that of the Anglo-Irish Ascendancy. Final expression in *Purgatory* (1938).
