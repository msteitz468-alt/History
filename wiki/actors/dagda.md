# Dagda (Dagdae, Dagán)

One of the principal pre-Christian deities of Ireland (Ériu) in the Mythological Cycle. “Good god” (*Dago-dēwos) in the sense of technical competence; father of many, owner of cauldron of plenty and club that kills and revives. Central to síd division and relations with sons of Míl.
