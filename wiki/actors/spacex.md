# SpaceX

Space exploration and satellite company founded 2002 by Elon Musk with the explicit goal of making humanity multi-planetary. Developed Falcon 1 (first privately developed liquid-fuel orbital rocket to reach orbit, 2008), Falcon 9 (reusable first stage), Dragon capsule (ISS cargo and crew), Starlink constellation, and Starship/Super Heavy. Launch sites include Cape Canaveral, Vandenberg, and Starbase (Boca Chica). Gwynne Shotwell President/COO.
