# Ewald von Kleist

**Ewald von Kleist** (1881–1954) was a German field marshal who commanded panzer forces
in the invasions of Poland and France and, on the Eastern Front, led the 1st Panzer Army
during the drive into the Caucasus (Case Blue and [[events/operation-edelweiss-1942|
Operation Edelweiss]], 1942) and at the [[events/second-battle-of-kharkov-1942|Second
Battle of Kharkov]], where his forces helped encircle and destroy a major Soviet
offensive. He later commanded Army Group A. Captured after the war, he was extradited to
the Soviet Union, convicted of war crimes, and died in Soviet captivity in 1954.
