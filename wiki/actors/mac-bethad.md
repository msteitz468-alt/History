# Mac Bethad (Macbeth)

King of Scots (1040–1057), traditionally son of Findláech of Moray; married Gruoch (Gruoch ingen Boite). Historical Macbeth remembered in later legend and in Shakespeare; in contemporary record a legitimate ruler within Gaelic kingship structures of Alba.
