# X Corp (formerly Twitter)

Social media and “everything app” platform. Founded 2006 as Twitter; Elon Musk acquired in 2022 for $44 billion in hostile takeover, rebranded to X, implemented “hardcore” operating mode, reduced headcount dramatically, reversed prior content moderation policies, emphasized Community Notes, and positioned as free-speech maximalist town square / financial platform. Headquarters moved to Austin area.
