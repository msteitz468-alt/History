# Paul Gachet

French physician, amateur artist, and art collector (1828–1909). Homeopathic doctor who treated Vincent van Gogh during his final weeks in Auvers-sur-Oise and became the subject of one of Vincent's most famous portraits.

## Narrative

Gachet had known artists including Pissarro and Cézanne. Theo arranged for Vincent to board in Auvers under Gachet's supervision after the Saint-Rémy asylum. Gachet, eccentric and himself artistically inclined, provided some support but was limited in his ability to help Vincent's deepening isolation. Vincent painted two versions of *Portrait of Dr. Gachet* (one now in private collection, one in Musée d'Orsay). Gachet was present at the death and funeral.

## Causal Analysis

Gachet represented a combination of medical authority and artistic sympathy that Theo hoped would stabilize Vincent while allowing continued work. The relationship was cordial but Vincent's letters express ambivalence about Gachet's effectiveness.

## Consequence Analysis

Gachet's portraits are among Vincent's most reproduced late images. Gachet's son later donated works to French museums. The doctor's role has been debated in accounts of the final period and death.

## Historiography

Naifeh & Smith use Gachet's own accounts and family records alongside letters; earlier biographies vary in their assessment of his competence and influence.
