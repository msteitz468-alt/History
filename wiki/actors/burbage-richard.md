# Richard Burbage

Actor and theatrical entrepreneur (c. 1567–1619), son of James Burbage (builder of The Theatre). The leading tragedian of the Lord Chamberlain's and King's Men and the first performer of most of Shakespeare's great tragic roles (Hamlet, Othello, Lear, Macbeth, Richard III, Brutus, Antony, Coriolanus, etc.). He was also a painter and a sharer in the company and the Globe/Blackfriars.

Shakespeare wrote the central male roles of his mature tragedies with Burbage's voice, physique, and acting style in mind. Contemporary tributes (e.g. the elegy on Burbage's death) single him out as the preeminent actor of the age. His partnership with Shakespeare as writer-sharer and actor-sharer was the creative and commercial core of the company for over two decades.
