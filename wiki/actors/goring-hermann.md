# Hermann Göring

Prominent Nazi leader, WWI flying ace, and head of the Luftwaffe. Designated Hitler's successor; built the air force and oversaw the Four-Year Plan for rearmament and autarky. Personally directed the Battle of Britain (failure damaged his prestige). Deeply involved in the economic exploitation of occupied territories and the early organization of the Holocaust (though rivalry with Himmler and others). Captured at Nuremberg; suicide before execution.

## Evans, *The Third Reich in Power* (2005)

The peacetime record: creator of the Prussian Gestapo (1933) and of SA
auxiliary-police terror; enrolled and then leashed the SA; handed the
Prussian political police to Himmler (20 April 1934) and directed the Berlin
killings of the [[night-of-the-long-knives-1934|Röhm purge]] in person.
Supreme over the [[four-year-plan-1936|Four-Year Plan]] from October 1936 —
"very little understanding of economics" (Evans) but decisive power: the
Hermann Göring Works, labour conscription decrees (22 June 1938), the
Foreign Currency Search Office under Heydrich (August 1936) weaponized
against Jewish firms. Antisemitic policy manager: chaired the 12 November
1938 conference after [[kristallnacht-1938|Kristallnacht]] ("I would have
preferred it if you had beaten 200 Jews to death and hadn't destroyed such
valuable property"), issued the billion-mark fine and expropriation decrees,
and the "privileged mixed marriage" rules (28 December 1938). In foreign
policy both hawk and dove: engineered the Anschluss by telephone ultimatum
(11 March 1938) coveting Austrian iron ore, then brokered Munich behind
Hitler's back; urged Hitler on 29 August 1939 not to "gamble everything"
("In my life, I've always put my whole stake on the table"). Corruption on a
pharaonic scale (Carinhall's 15m+ RM refit; the popular joke defined a "Gör"
as "the quantity of tin that one man can carry on his chest"). Source:
[[evans-third-reich-in-power-2005]]

## From Evans, *The Third Reich at War* (2008)

His Dunkirk boast (the Luftwaffe would finish the trapped armies) helped persuade Hitler to halt the panzers; his air force then lost the [[battle-of-britain-1940|Battle of Britain]] on intelligence that undercounted British fighter losses by an order of magnitude. As economic overlord: the Hermann Göring Reich Works led the seizure of foreign industry; his 7 November 1941 guidelines set the murderous terms for Soviet labor ("Russians to arrange own food (cats, horses, etc.) . . . Range of punishment: from cutting food rations to execution by firing squad; generally nothing in between" — see [[forced-labor-nazi-germany]]); he repeated the Hunger Plan's arithmetic to Ciano (15 November 1941): "This year, 20-30 million people in Russia will starve." His 31 July 1941 commission to [[heydrich-reinhard|Heydrich]] authorized preparation of the "total solution of the Jewish question" (see [[wannsee-conference-1942]]). Losing the Todt succession to [[speer-albert|Speer]] (February 1942) and the Stalingrad airlift fiasco (300 tons/day promised, ~90 delivered; his 30 January 1943 Thermopylae broadcast comparing Sixth Army to Spartans who, Evans notes, were wiped out) completed his decline — by 1943 "weakened by heavy doses of morphine, to which he had now become addicted." Source: [[evans-third-reich-at-war-2008]]
