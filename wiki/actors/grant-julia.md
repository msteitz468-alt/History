# Julia Dent Grant

Wife of Ulysses S. Grant and First Lady of the United States (1869–1877). Born Julia Boggs Dent in St. Louis, Missouri, daughter of a slaveholding planter. Married Grant in 1848 after a long courtship complicated by her family's reservations and his military postings. She accompanied him to remote army posts in the 1850s, endured the "lost years" of his resignation and business failures, and provided emotional steadiness during the Civil War and presidency.

As First Lady she was a popular hostess who enjoyed the social role her husband disliked. She was a staunch defender of her husband against critics and later wrote her own *Personal Memoirs*, published posthumously. Chernow portrays her as the indispensable personal and social partner who humanized Grant and anchored the family through repeated crises, including the final cancer battle.
