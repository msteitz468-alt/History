# Ibn Juzayy (Muhammad ibn Juzayy)

Granadan scholar and litterateur who, at the command of the Marinid sultan **Abu Inan Faris**, received **[[ibn-battuta|Ibn Battuta]]** upon his return and prepared the literary redaction of the *Rihla*. Ibn Juzayy supplied the polished Arabic style, chapter divisions, and some interpolations and comparisons drawn from earlier writers (notably Ibn Jubayr). The surviving text is therefore an *epitome* shaped by a court literary project rather than a raw dictation.

## Role in the Rihla

Ibn Battuta dictated his memories; Ibn Juzayy turned them into a work of *adab* (belles-lettres) suitable for royal presentation. This editorial layer must be borne in mind when using the text for historical detail. The Lee translation (1829) is based on the resulting abridged manuscripts.

## Significance

Without Ibn Juzayy's redaction the *Rihla* would likely not have survived in usable form. He is therefore the indispensable literary collaborator for the single richest primary source on 14th-century Islamic mobility and the connected Afro-Eurasian world.
