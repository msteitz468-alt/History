# Louise Whitfield Carnegie

Wife of Andrew Carnegie (married 1887 after a long courtship; she was 30 years younger). Daughter of a New York merchant. The marriage was happy by most accounts; Louise exerted a moderating and organizing influence on Carnegie's later life and philanthropy. After Carnegie's death she chose Burton J. Hendrick as the authorized biographer and exercised control over the publication and editing of Carnegie's Autobiography. She lived until 1946.
