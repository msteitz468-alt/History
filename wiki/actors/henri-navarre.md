# Henri Navarre

French general who commanded French Union forces in Indochina from May 1953 until the defeat at Dien Bien Phu. He was the author of the “Navarre Plan” (sometimes called the “American Plan” because of strong U.S. influence), which aimed to regain the initiative and create conditions for a negotiated settlement or for the Vietnamese National Army to take over the war.

## Role

Navarre inherited a deteriorating situation. His plan combined mobile operations in the south with the establishment of a major fortified position at Dien Bien Phu in the northwest. The plan collapsed when Giap accepted the challenge and successfully besieged the base.
