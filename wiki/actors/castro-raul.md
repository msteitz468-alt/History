# Raúl Castro

Younger brother of Fidel Castro and key early member of the 26th of July Movement. Participated in the Moncada attack, the Granma landing, and the Sierra Maestra campaign. Longtime military and political figure in revolutionary Cuba; later succeeded Fidel as President (2008–2018). In Anderson's account, the initial contact point between Che and the Castro brothers in Mexico; present at key moments of the revolutionary war.
