# Andrea del Verrocchio

Andrea di Michele di Francesco de' Cioni, called Verrocchio (c. 1435–1488), was a leading Florentine sculptor, goldsmith, and painter. Leonardo da Vinci trained in his workshop from c. 1466 to c. 1477. Verrocchio’s collaborative, technically ambitious studio shaped Leonardo’s mastery of drawing, modeling, and workshop practice.
