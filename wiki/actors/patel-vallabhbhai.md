# Vallabhbhai Patel

**Vallabhbhai Jhaverbhai Patel** (1875–1950), known as Sardar Patel, was the organizational strongman of the Indian National Congress, a master of mass satyagraha (Bardoli 1928), and independent India's first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister. He played a decisive role in the acceptance of Partition and the rapid integration of more than 500 princely states into the Union.

Guha's *Gandhi: The Years That Changed the World* (2018) provides granular detail on Patel's evolution from Gujarat lawyer and local satyagraha leader (Kheda, Bardoli) to all-India Congress manager, his pragmatic partnership and occasional tensions with Gandhi and Nehru, and his critical interventions in 1946-47 when he concluded that Partition was unavoidable to secure a strong central state.
