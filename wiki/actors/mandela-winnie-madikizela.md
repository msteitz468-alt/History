# Winnie Madikizela-Mandela

**Winnie Madikizela-Mandela** (1936–2018), born Nomzamo Winifred Madikizela, was a South African anti-apartheid activist, politician, and second wife of Nelson Mandela. During his long imprisonment she became a powerful symbol of resistance in her own right (“Mother of the Nation”) but was also accused of involvement in human-rights abuses by her Mandela United Football Club bodyguard unit. Convicted in 1991 of kidnapping (sentence later reduced). Separated from Mandela in 1992; divorced 1996. Remained an influential and polarizing ANC figure until her death. Meredith devotes substantial attention to the personal and political strains of the marriage under apartheid and during the transition.
