## Role and Background

Bess Wallace Truman was the wife of President [[actors/truman-harry-s|Harry S. Truman]] and First Lady of the United States from 1945 to 1953. A lifelong resident of Independence, Missouri, she maintained a famously private public role, giving no press conferences and making minimal public appearances, in deliberate contrast to her predecessor Eleanor Roosevelt's highly public activism.

## Significance

Though publicly reticent, Bess Truman was a close and trusted political confidante to her husband throughout his career, and their extensive surviving correspondence — written during his long absences in Washington and, earlier, military service — is a significant primary source for understanding Truman's private thinking on major decisions of his presidency.
