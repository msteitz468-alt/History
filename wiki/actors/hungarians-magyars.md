# The Magyars (Hungarians)

The **Magyars** were a Finno-Ugric-speaking steppe confederation that migrated westward
from the Volga-Ural region and, under pressure from the Pechenegs, entered and settled the
Carpathian Basin around 895 CE, displacing or absorbing earlier populations including
remnants of the [[avars|Avars]]. From this base they launched decades of far-ranging
mounted raids across Western Europe (Germany, Italy, even into Francia and Iberia) until
their decisive defeat by Otto I at the Battle of Lechfeld (955), which ended the raiding
era and pushed Magyar society toward settlement and state formation.

## Transition to Statehood

Under Grand Prince Géza and his son Stephen (Vajk), the Magyars converted to Latin
Christianity and Stephen was crowned the first King of Hungary around 1000 CE with papal
sanction, founding a kingdom that endured as one of medieval Europe's major powers and
the ancestor of the modern Hungarian state and people.
