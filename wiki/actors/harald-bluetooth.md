# Harald Bluetooth (Harald Blátönn Gormsson)

King of [[denmark|Denmark]] (c. 958–986), son of Gorm the Old. The larger
[[jelling|Jelling]] runestone records his claim to have "won all Denmark for
himself, and Norway, and made the Danes Christian" — a foundational monument of
Danish state formation and [[christianization-of-scandinavia|conversion]]. He
built ring-fortresses (Trelleborg type) and extended the Danevirke. He was
overthrown by his son [[sweyn-forkbeard|Sweyn Forkbeard]].

His name was adopted in the 1990s for the Bluetooth wireless standard, whose logo
is his bind-rune. See [[helle-cambridge-history-scandinavia-v1-2003]].
