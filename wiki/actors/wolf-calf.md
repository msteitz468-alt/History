# Wolf Calf (Piikani / Blackfoot teenager)

Young Piikani (Blackfoot Confederacy) master equestrian featured in Fehrman's rotating chapters. Represents the Native perspective on the returning expedition in 1806. His chapter highlights equestrian skill, youth, and the encounter from the viewpoint of a rising warrior society on the northern plains.
