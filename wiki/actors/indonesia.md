# Indonesia

The sprawling archipelagic state that emerged from the **[[dutch-east-indies|Dutch East Indies]]** — the
Netherlands' most valuable colony, knit together by Dutch rule and a shared nationalist project. The
twentieth-century story runs from the rise of organized nationalism, through the [[japanese-occupation-southeast-asia|Japanese occupation]],
to the 1945 declaration of independence under [[sukarno]] and the armed national revolution (1945–49)
that forced Dutch recognition.

As the world's largest Muslim-majority nation and most populous Southeast Asian state, independent
Indonesia moved from Sukarno's "Guided Democracy" through the violent 1965–66 transition to Suharto's
"New Order," and is a founding pillar of [[asean-and-regionalism|ASEAN]].
