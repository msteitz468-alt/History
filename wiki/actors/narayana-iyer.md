# Narayana Iyer

Narayana Iyer, Ramanujan’s immediate superior at the Madras Port Trust and one of his most ardent early champions. A mathematician himself, he recognized the quality of Ramanujan’s work, gave him employment, stayed up late working through the collapsing steps in his proofs, and was instrumental in the efforts to find patrons and to get the 1913 letter sent. One of the key Indian figures who kept Ramanujan’s mathematics alive during the lean years.
