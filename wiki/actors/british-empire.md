# British Empire

The **British Empire** was the global system of colonies, dominions, protectorates, and
trading posts ruled or administered from Britain — at its nineteenth-century height the
largest empire in history. This page is a **network anchor**; its full treatment is
distributed across regional and period pages.

In the context of [[bashford-macintyre-cha-v1-2013|CHA Vol. 1]], the Empire is the frame
within which [[australia|Australia]] was founded as a penal and settler colony, governed,
and ultimately federated. The volume's later chapters read colonial Australia as part of
**"Greater Britain"**, the [[australian-pacific-relations|Asia-Pacific region]], and a
wider maritime and imperial world.
