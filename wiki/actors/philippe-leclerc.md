# Philippe Leclerc

General Philippe Leclerc (born Philippe de Hauteclocque) was chosen by de Gaulle to command the French Expeditionary Corps for the Far East tasked with reasserting French control in Indochina after World War II. He led the southern campaigns in late 1945 that re-established French presence in Cochin China.

## Role

Leclerc was the military face of the French return. His forces, working with the British, rapidly took control of most towns south of the 16th parallel. He later clashed with hard-liners in Paris over the limits of what could be achieved militarily.
