# Hoang Xuan Lam

Army of the Republic of Vietnam (ARVN) lieutenant general (1928–2017), commander of I Corps in northern South Vietnam and nominal overall commander of the South Vietnamese ground forces in Operation Lam Son 719 (February–March 1971) — the invasion of Laos aimed at cutting the Ho Chi Minh Trail, conducted with U.S. air, artillery, and logistical support but no U.S. ground troops under the terms of the Cooper-Church Amendment.

Lam's leadership during Lam Son 719 was widely criticized by American advisers, including General Creighton Abrams, as passive and indecisive; ARVN forces became overextended against a heavier-than-expected North Vietnamese Army response and suffered a costly, disorganized withdrawal under fire. The operation is generally regarded as exposing the limits of "Vietnamization" — the ability of ARVN forces to conduct large independent operations even with substantial U.S. air support.

## Significance

Lam Son 719's difficulties under Lam's command became a widely cited data point in assessments of ARVN's readiness to operate without direct U.S. ground participation, feeding skepticism about the pace of Vietnamization.
