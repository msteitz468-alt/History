# Paul Harkins

U.S. Army general, first commander of MACV (1962–1964). Optimistic "can-do" officer selected despite limited counterinsurgency experience. Famously at odds with the Saigon press corps and with Ambassador Lodge during the 1963 crisis. Embodied the conventional-war mindset critiqued by later analysts (Nagl, Krepinevich).
