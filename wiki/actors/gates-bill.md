# Bill Gates

**William Henry Gates III** (born 28 October 1955) is an American business magnate, software developer, and philanthropist. Co-founder of Microsoft. Long-time rival and occasional collaborator/ally of Steve Jobs. Their orbits intersected in the personal computer era; Jobs criticized Microsoft for lacking humanities in its DNA and for copying without understanding (Windows vs. Mac). Gates appears in Isaacson interviews reflecting on the rivalry and the different paths taken by the two companies.
