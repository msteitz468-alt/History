## Overview

*Homo ergaster* (often called "African *Homo erectus*") is the first hominid built
essentially like modern humans **from the neck down**. It lived in eastern Africa
from ~1.9 mya, and is best known from the near-complete **"Turkana Boy"** skeleton
(KNM-WT 15000, ~1.6 mya) — a tall, long-legged, striding biped, dark-skinned and
largely hairless, adapted to open savanna and heat loss. In
[[tattersall-world-from-beginnings-2008|Tattersall's]] reading it is pivotal: its
modern body plan appeared **abruptly** (no intermediates), plausibly via a
regulatory-gene change, and it was this "walking machine" anatomy — not bigger
brains or better tools — that made the first [[out-of-africa-1-1800kbp|exit from
Africa]] possible.

## Anatomy

- **Modern body, archaic head.** Tall (Turkana Boy ~5'3" at ~8 years, projected
  >6 ft at maturity), long-legged, narrow-hipped — but with a small braincase
  (~880 cc, roughly half a modern human's), a forward-projecting face, and large
  chewing teeth.
- **Heat adaptation.** Linear tropical body proportions; reduced body hair and
  proliferated sweat glands inferred; dark, melanin-rich skin.
- **Speech uncertain.** The narrow vertebral canal once read as precluding the fine
  breath control for speech is more likely pathological in this individual.
- **Rapid development.** Like earlier hominids, matured faster than modern humans
  (the 8-year-old was developmentally ~11).

## Technology and Dispersal

For its first several hundred thousand years *H. ergaster* used the same crude
Oldowan (Mode 1) technology as its predecessors. Only ~1.5 mya — long after the
species appeared and after the diaspora began — did the teardrop-shaped **Acheulean
handaxe** (Mode 2) appear, the first tool made to a mental "shape template." The
first dispersal from Africa (Dmanisi in Georgia ~1.8 mya, with small brains and
crude tools; and into tropical Asia) thus tracks the **new anatomy**, not new tools
or larger brains. See [[out-of-africa-1-1800kbp]] and [[paleolithic-global-dispersal]].

## Significance (Counterfactual)

The arrival of the modern hominid body is one of the two great abrupt transitions
in [[human-evolution]] (the other being [[symbolic-cognition]]). Without the shift
to a striding, endurance-walking body plan, the hominid range would plausibly have
remained confined to African forest fringes; *ergaster*-grade anatomy is the
enabling condition for the genus's hemispheric spread and the later radiations that
produced [[homo-erectus]], [[homo-heidelbergensis]], and the [[neanderthals]].

## Fagan & Durrani (2020) — Terminology Note

Fagan & Durrani (2020) present African *H. erectus*/*H. ergaster* as one
lineage rather than two species — using "*H. erectus* (sometimes known as
*H. ergaster*)" for the African form throughout. On their reckoning this
population appears in Africa ~1.9 Ma and is the first hominin to leave the
continent, with Dmanisi, Georgia (by 1.8 Ma) as the earliest secure evidence
outside Africa, alongside China and Java/Indonesia. This "lumping" usage
should be noted when reconciling this page with [[homo-erectus]]: the wiki
retains the two as distinct pages, following the "splitter" framing, but
Fagan & Durrani's terminology does not draw that line.

## Historiography

"Lumpers" subsume *H. ergaster* within a broad [[homo-erectus|*Homo erectus*]],
treating both as one widespread, variable species; "splitters" like Tattersall keep
them distinct (Asian *erectus* a separate local descendant). The Acheulean's late
appearance relative to *ergaster* is well attested; the makers of the very earliest
(~2.5 mya) stone tools remain unidentified. Dating from K/Ar–Ar/Ar tuffs. African
collection coverage for this period is strong.
