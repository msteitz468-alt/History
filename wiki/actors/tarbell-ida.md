# Ida M. Tarbell

Ida Minerva Tarbell (1857–1944) was the muckraking journalist whose serialized *History of the Standard Oil Company* (*McClure's*, 1902–1904) became the most damaging public indictment of [[actors/rockefeller-john-d|Rockefeller]] and [[actors/standard-oil|Standard Oil]]. The daughter of an Oil Regions producer ruined in part by Standard's methods, she combined documentary rigor with moral force; Chernow's *Titan* treats her as Rockefeller's defining "nemesis," fixing his public image as the era's archetypal predatory monopolist and feeding the political momentum toward the [[events/standard-oil-dissolution-1911|1911 dissolution]].
