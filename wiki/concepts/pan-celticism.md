# Pan-Celticism

Modern ideological and organizational movement promoting unity and mutual support across the Celtic nations, often linked to language revival, eisteddfodau, Celtic Congresses, and anti-imperial or nationalist politics. Overlaps with but distinct from individual national revivals (e.g. Conradh na Gaeilge, Cymru Fydd).
