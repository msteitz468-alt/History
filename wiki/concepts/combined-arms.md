# Combined Arms

The integration of different combat arms (infantry, cavalry/armor, artillery, engineers, air support, etc.) in mutually supporting roles to achieve effects greater than the sum of individual arms. Contrasted with single-arm or massed tactics. Key doctrinal principle in Western and other militaries from antiquity through modern combined-arms warfare.

## Key Applications
- Macedonian: heavy infantry phalanx screened and exploited by companion cavalry and light troops.
- Napoleonic: corps system, artillery-infantry-cavalry coordination.
- 20th century: armor + mechanized infantry + close air support + artillery (Blitzkrieg, US WWII doctrine, modern).
