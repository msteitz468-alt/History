# Welsh Prose Literature

Medieval Welsh prose includes many examples based on Latin Christian apocryphal texts (Ystorya Aªaf, Ystorya Bilatus, Ystorya Dared, Ystorya de Carolo Magno, Ystorya Bown de Hamtwn — the earliest translation of a secular French narrative into Welsh). Ymborth yr Enaid (Sustenance of the Soul) is a Middle Welsh prose treatise on mysticism, the third and sole surviving book of Cysegrlan Fuchedd (Holy Living); an original composition and the only known medieval example of an original mystical treatise in a Celtic language. The Welsh Transitus provides evidence for the strength of the pan-European Mary cult in medieval Welsh vernacular culture. Later prose includes works by Ellis Wynne and modern novelists such as Angharad Tomos.
