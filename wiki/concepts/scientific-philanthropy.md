# Scientific Philanthropy

"Scientific" or "wholesale" philanthropy was the doctrine — central to Rockefeller's second career — that great fortunes should be given away systematically through professionally managed, permanently endowed institutions that attack the *root causes* of social problems (disease, ignorance) rather than dispensing ad hoc "retail" charity to individuals. [[actors/gates-frederick-t|Frederick T. Gates]] was its principal designer for [[actors/rockefeller-john-d|Rockefeller]], producing the University of Chicago, the Rockefeller Institute for Medical Research, the General Education Board, the hookworm sanitary commission, and the Rockefeller Foundation (1913) — the template for the modern grant-making foundation.

**Contested:** admirers saw a rationalization of charity into a force-multiplier for human welfare; critics saw tax-advantaged perpetual power, image-laundering of tainted fortunes, and unaccountable private control over public goods.
