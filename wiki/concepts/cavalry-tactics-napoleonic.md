# Cavalry Tactics in the Napoleonic Era

The organization, training, charge mechanics, mêlée realities, pursuit, and interaction with infantry (especially squares) and artillery of cavalry in the period. Muir emphasizes that cavalry was a weapon of fear and moral shock, that rallies frequently failed, that pursuit (when delivered) was devastating, and that success almost always required the enemy to waver first.
