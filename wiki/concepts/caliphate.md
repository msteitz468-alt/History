# Caliphate

The supreme political-religious leadership of the Muslim umma, claimed by successors of the Prophet Muhammad. The office combined (in theory) spiritual guidance, military command, and governance of the community according to Islamic law. In practice it evolved through the Rashidun, Umayyad, Abbasid, and later regional caliphates (Fatimid, Umayyad of Cordoba, Ottoman, etc.), with major disputes over legitimacy (Sunni vs Shi'i lines, especially the Alid claim).

## Evolution and Significance
- Rashidun (632–661): elective, conquests.
- Umayyad (661–750): dynastic, Arab empire.
- Abbasid (750–1258): universal claim, Persianate administration, golden age of learning; later symbolic under Buyids/Seljuks.
- Later claimants and the Ottoman assumption of the title.

Central to Islamic political thought, law, and identity; flashpoint for sectarian division and debates over authority vs. community.
