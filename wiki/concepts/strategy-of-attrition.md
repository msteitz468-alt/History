# Strategy of Attrition

A strategic approach that seeks victory by wearing down the enemy's material resources, manpower, and will through prolonged operations, rather than by decisive battle or maneuver for position. Distinguished from annihilation or decisive battle strategies. Often associated with WWI Western Front, some Vietnam debates, and Fabian approaches in earlier history.

## Historiography Note
Debated whether "attrition" is a deliberate strategy or emergent from failed maneuver; critics (e.g. in Vietnam literature) contrast it unfavorably with strategies of dislocation or counter-insurgency.
