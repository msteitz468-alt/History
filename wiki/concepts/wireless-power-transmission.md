# Wireless Power Transmission (Tesla's "World System")

**Wireless power transmission** was [[actors/tesla-nikola|Tesla]]'s grandest and most contested project: the transmission of electrical **energy** as well as **information** through the earth and atmosphere without wires, enabling a global "World System" in which power and communication would be available anywhere. It grew out of his high-frequency, high-voltage research with the **[[concepts/alternating-current|resonant transformer]]** (Tesla coil) and his theory that the earth itself could be made to resonate electrically.

Tesla developed the idea at his **[[places/colorado-springs|Colorado Springs]]** experimental station (1899), where he generated millions of volts and claimed to detect stationary waves and even extraterrestrial signals, and tried to realize it commercially at **[[places/wardenclyffe|Wardenclyffe]]** (1901–1906) with [[actors/morgan-john-pierpont|J. P. Morgan]]'s backing.

**Contested status**: Tesla's claim that he could transmit usable power worldwide with low loss has never been validated; mainstream physics holds the global-scale, low-loss scheme he envisioned to be unworkable. Seifer treats the demonstrated near-field effects (lighting lamps without wires, resonant coupling) as real while flagging the planetary "World System" as visionary and unproven. The concept anticipates modern resonant inductive coupling and radiative power transfer at far shorter ranges.
