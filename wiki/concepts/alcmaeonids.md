# Alcmaeonids

Powerful and influential Athenian aristocratic family (genos) claiming descent from Alcmaeon and ultimately Nestor. Exiled after the Cylonian conspiracy; returned under Solon and especially Cleisthenes. Key players in the overthrow of the Pisistratid tyranny (with Spartan help and Delphi oracle influence). Cleisthenes' democratic reforms (508/7 BCE) are closely associated with the family. Later figures include Pericles (through his mother) and Alcibiades (related by marriage). Exemplifies the interplay of aristocratic networks, exile, and the emergence of radical democracy in Athens.
