# Intellectual History of the Late Twentieth Century

Framework for understanding the ideas, ideologies, and cultural shifts that shaped (and were shaped by) the final phase of the Cold War, including the crisis of Marxism-Leninism, the rise of human rights discourse, environmental thought, and the intellectual underpinnings of 1989 and the post-Cold War order.
