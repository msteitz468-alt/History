# Eisteddfod

Traditional Welsh competitive festival for bards and musicians. First recorded 1176 (Cardigan under Lord Rhys); national revival from 19th c. (e.g. National Eisteddfod 1861). Symbol of Welsh cultural identity and language preservation.
