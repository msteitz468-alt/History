# Namagiri (Goddess of Namakkal)

The family goddess whose shrine at Namakkal was the site of Ramanujan’s decisive pre-England consultation. Ramanujan repeatedly stated that his mathematical insights came as thoughts or dreams from Namagiri. The goddess appears throughout the biographical sources as the source of his inspiration and the authority he invoked when deciding major life steps (including the journey to England). The concept embodies the devotional, non-secular framing Ramanujan gave to his own creativity — a framing that Hardy respected even while rejecting its religious premises.
