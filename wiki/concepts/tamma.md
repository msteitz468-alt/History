# Tamma

The **tamma** was the Mongol **frontier occupation force** — "selected chiliarchies from the total Mongolian levy" sent into conquered borderlands "to secure and hold them, and if possible, expand Mongolian power" (Buell, via [[may-mongol-art-of-war-2016|May]]). **Alginci** scouts/vanguards sat closer to cities; the main tamma body held better pasture.

May compares the tamma to a Western **castle**: defensive anchor, base for raids, intimidation of neighbours — but mobile, not stone. The Yuan *Shi* distinguished **Meng-ku chün** (ethnic Mongol army) from **Tan-ma-ch'ih chün** (tammaci army of mixed nomads). Unlike **cerik** sedentary garrisons, tamma units eventually **advanced** as the frontier moved.
