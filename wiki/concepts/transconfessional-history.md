# Transconfessional History

**Transconfessional history** is Tannous’s proposed program for the medieval Middle East: by analogy with modern **transnational** history, write histories that cross confessional boundaries and refuse the unspoken **millet system** in scholarship that treats the region’s medieval past as synonymous with Islamic history and gives Islam — a long-term demographic minority after the conquests — automatic priority in research agendas and hiring.

## Emic vs. etic

- **Emic:** Not a medieval actors’ term.
- **Etic:** Methodological stance: center all inhabitants (Christians, Muslims, Jews, others), simple and learned; study Christian–Christian competition as the precondition of Christian–Muslim relations; track shared daily life (“rubbing shoulders”) as a vector of cultural transmission into Islam.
