# Blitzkrieg

"Lightning war": operational doctrine emphasizing speed, surprise, and concentration of mobile forces (armor + mechanized infantry + close air support) to achieve rapid breakthroughs and deep encirclements (Kesselschlacht), bypassing strongpoints and disrupting command/logistics before the enemy can react. Contrasted with attritional or linear warfare of WWI.

## Key Elements
- Combined arms at operational level.
- Decentralized command (Auftragstaktik).
- Radio and air-ground integration.
- Psychological shock.

Practiced with great effect in Poland (1939), France/Low Countries (1940), and early Barbarossa, but strained by overextension, logistics, and Soviet recovery. Debated whether a fully coherent "doctrine" or emergent success.
