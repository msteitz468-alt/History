# Svayambhu

“Self-born.” The term used by G. H. Hardy and by Kanigel to describe Ramanujan’s intellectual independence. Ramanujan had almost no formal mathematical education beyond Carr’s *Synopsis*; he developed his results in isolation and attributed many of them to dreams and the grace of the goddess Namagiri. Hardy wrote: “I did not invent him. Like other great men he invented himself.” The concept captures both the astonishing autonomy of Ramanujan’s achievement and the question of how much such autonomy depends on (or is destroyed by) the surrounding educational and patronage systems.
