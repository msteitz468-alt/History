# League of Nations

International organization established after WWI (Covenant in Treaty of Versailles) to prevent future wars through collective security, disarmament, and arbitration. Weaknesses: U.S. never joined; no effective enforcement power (no army); major powers (Germany, Japan, Italy, USSR) withdrew or were expelled. Failed to stop Japanese invasion of Manchuria, Italian Ethiopia, or German expansion. Predecessor to the United Nations (1945) with lessons learned on enforcement and universality.
