# Arthurian Literature

**Scope**: The corpus of Arthurian narrative and related traditions in the Celtic languages (and texts in non-Celtic languages produced in Celtic countries), including Irish, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Breton, Cornish, and Latin/English/French works connected to the matter of Britain and Insular heroes.
