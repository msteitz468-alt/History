# Hardcore Mode

Musk’s post-acquisition operating philosophy at X (formerly Twitter): “extremely hardcore” work required; employees must be willing to be “hardcore” or leave. Implemented via all-hands, email ultimatum, mass voluntary departures + layoffs, and cultural reset. Described as necessary for the company to survive financially and execute the “everything app” vision. Controversial; led to significant talent exodus and public debate over work culture.
