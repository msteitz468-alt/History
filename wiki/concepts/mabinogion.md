# Mabinogion / Four Branches of the Mabinogi

Collection of medieval Welsh prose tales, most famously the Four Branches (Pwyll, Branwen, Manawydan, Math). Foundational text of Welsh literature; blend of myth, heroic narrative, otherworldly elements, and political/kinship themes. Central to Arthurian and Celtic literary studies.
