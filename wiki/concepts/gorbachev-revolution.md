# The Gorbachev Revolution

The set of reforms initiated by Mikhail Gorbachev after 1985 — perestroika (economic restructuring), glasnost (openness), and "new thinking" in foreign policy — that transformed the Soviet Union internally and led to the abandonment of the Brezhnev Doctrine, the withdrawal from Afghanistan, arms control agreements, and ultimately the peaceful collapse of the Soviet empire and the USSR itself.

## Key Elements

- Domestic: Democratization, reduced Party control, market elements.
- Foreign: End to ideological confrontation, acceptance of Eastern European autonomy, INF Treaty, etc.
- Outcome: Accelerated the end of the Cold War by removing the Soviet threat and enabling 1989 revolutions.

## Historiography

Vol. III emphasizes Gorbachev's personal role and contingency: his choices made the swift, non-violent end possible. Not inevitable from "containment victory" alone, but from Soviet internal reform.
