# Radioactivity

The spontaneous emission of particles or radiation from unstable atomic nuclei. The Curies' work (1898) on pitchblende established that radioactivity is an atomic property (not dependent on chemical state or external conditions), leading to the identification of polonium and radium and the modern understanding of atomic structure, transmutation, and nuclear physics/medicine.
