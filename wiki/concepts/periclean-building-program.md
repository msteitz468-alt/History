# Periclean Building Program

Major public building campaign on the Acropolis and elsewhere in Athens, funded by league tribute (controversially redirected from defense). Included the Parthenon (with Phidias' Athena Parthenos), Propylaea, Erechtheion, and Temple of Athena Nike. Symbolized Athenian power, piety, and cultural supremacy; employed large numbers of citizens and metics. Criticized by rivals (e.g. Thucydides son of Melesias) as misuse of allied funds for Athenian glory.
