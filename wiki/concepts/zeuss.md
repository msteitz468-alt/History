# Zeuss (Johann Kaspar Zeuss)

## Definition and Scope

Zeuss refers to the pioneering work in Celtic linguistics and philology by Johann Kaspar Zeuss (and related 19th-c. scholars). Listed in the Vol V topic inventory of Koch's Celtic Culture encyclopedia as part of the historiography of the field of Celtic studies.

## Role in Historiography

The encyclopedia's Vol V section on T–Z explicitly includes “Zeuss, Zimmer” among key topics alongside modern figures and the full Bibliography. This reflects the foundational role of Zeuss's comparative grammar in establishing the scientific study of Celtic languages as a family, parallel to other Indo-European branches.

Zimmer (Heinrich Zimmer) appears in further reading citations, e.g. in the wild man legend entry: “Zimmer, King and the Corpse 183–4.”
