# Zeuss, Johann Kaspar

Considered one of the founders of the modern study of the Celtic languages. His main work, the Grammatica Celtica (1853), established in much fuller linguistic detail that the Celtic languages belong to the Indo-European family and is regarded as a major milestone that set Celtic philology on a firm scientific basis.
