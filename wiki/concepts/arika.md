# Arika (heretics / Magi)

## Definition and Scope
Arika is used in an evil sense for heretics or Magi — followers of the proscribed sacrificial worship and magical invocations suppressed by Darius. It is not a general term for "evil-doer." The vocabulary connects it explicitly to the ayadana (places of worship) passage: their predominance in the state paved the way for the Magian revolution (Gaumata's usurpation). Darius counts "not being an arika" among the virtues that secured Ormazd's favour.

## Historiography
The 19th-c. edition (see [[behistun-inscription]]) draws the term from Col. I of the Behistun inscription and links it to the religious restoration narrative after Gaumata. The Median version adopts the Persian word as a proper name in related contexts. The author distinguishes it from general evil and ties it to the Magophonia feast and Zoroastrian reverence for Darius as reformer.
