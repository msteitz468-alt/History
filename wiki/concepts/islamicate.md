# Islamicate

A term coined by **Marshall Hodgson** to distinguish the **religion** of Islam from the broader
**civilization and culture** that arose in the regions where Muslims ruled and set the social tone.
*Islamic* (emic) properly refers to the religion proper — its faith, law, and worship. *Islamicate*
(etic) refers to "the social and cultural complex historically associated with Islam and the Muslims,
both among Muslims themselves and even when found among non-Muslims" — the art, literature, science,
administration, and manners of the society, much of which was produced by, or shared with,
Christians, Jews, and Zoroastrians living within it.

The distinction lets historians speak of, say, "Islamicate art" or "Islamicate literature" produced by
non-Muslims, without the category error of calling them "Islamic" (religious). Hodgson paired it with
two further coinages: **Islamdom** (the society/region, parallel to "Christendom") and the
**[[irano-semitic-tradition|Irano-Semitic]]** lineage of the underlying culture. The terminology has
been widely adopted, though some find it cumbersome.
