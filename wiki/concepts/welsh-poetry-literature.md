# Welsh Poetry (Gogynfeirdd and Cywyddwyr traditions)

Trawsganu Cynan Garwyn mab Brochfael is a Welsh praise poem in 50 two-stress rhyming lines preserved in Llyfr Taliesin. Ymrysonau (bardic contests or debates) are a common feature of early Welsh poetry, referenced in Gogynfeirdd works; by the Cywyddwyr period used mainly as a framework for debate on the craft and purpose of poetry. Gododdin represents early Welsh heroic elegies. The Hendregadredd Manuscript is a key source for Gogynfeirdd poetry.
