# Ulysses (novel)

James Joyce's 1922 novel, set in Dublin on 16 June 1904. Follows Leopold Bloom, Stephen Dedalus, and Molly Bloom through one day using stream-of-consciousness, mythic parallels (Homer's *Odyssey*), encyclopedic detail, and radical formal experimentation across 18 episodes in varying styles. Published in Paris by Sylvia Beach after rejections and censorship battles in Britain and the US. Widely regarded as the central work of high modernism in English prose and one of the most influential novels of the 20th century.
