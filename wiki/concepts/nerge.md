# Nerge (Great Hunt)

The **nerge** was the Mongol **great hunt** — a vast ring of riders driving game for weeks, none permitted to let an animal escape before the khan loosed the first arrow. [[may-mongol-art-of-war-2016|May]] and [[morgan-the-mongols|Morgan]] treat it as **military training** for encirclement, discipline, and coordinated maneuver.

Operationally, nerge became **double envelopment at campaign scale**: after Vladimir (1237) the Mongols proceeded "tümän by tümän in järge formation" encircling regions until escape routes narrowed "just as they would in a battle." At **[[siege-of-caizhou-1234|Caizhou (1234)]]** Tachar approached "in the nerge fashion," herding population inside to exhaust food stocks.
