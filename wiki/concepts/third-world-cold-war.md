# The Cold War in the Third World (1963–1975)

The extension and intensification of superpower rivalry into the decolonizing and developing world, where local actors often drove events more than Moscow or Washington.

From Vol. II: Superpowers competed for influence in Africa, Latin America, Asia, and the Middle East through aid, proxies, ideology (modernization vs. revolution), and interventions. Vietnam was the most costly example. Détente did not end this competition.
