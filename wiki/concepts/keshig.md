# Keshig

The **keshik** was the Mongol imperial **bodyguard** — from 80 night guards and 70 day guards to **10,000 men** at the 1206 quriltai — functioning as officer training school, political hostage system, and the Khan's security apparatus.

[[may-mongol-art-of-war-2016|May]] details three elements: **kebte'ül** (night guards who prepared the Khan's food and guarded the royal household in battle), **qorcin** (day quiver-bearers — the only armed men near the Khan), and **turqa'ud** (8,000 day guards in eight *minqan*). Shifts rotated every three days; dereliction meant rod blows (3, 7, 37) and banishment. Rank in the keshik equalled **one step up** in the field army — arban-u noyan in the keshik = jaghun-u noyan outside.

Sons of commanders entered with companions (up to ten for *minqan* commanders' sons); Chinggis took youngest sons as **hostages** (Thomas Allsen) while indoctrinating future governors. Generals of conquest — **[[subedei|Sübedei]]**, **[[jebe|Jebe]]** — rose from the **dörben noqas** and keshik pipeline.
