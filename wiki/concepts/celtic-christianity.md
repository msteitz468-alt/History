# Celtic Christianity

Distinctive forms of Christianity that developed in the Celtic-speaking regions (Ireland, Britain, Brittany), characterized by particular monastic traditions, saints' cults, liturgical practices, and interactions with pre-Christian beliefs. The term is modern and somewhat contested.

## Key Features from Sources

- Regional variations: Ireland, Scotland (pre- and post-1100), Isle of Man, Wales, Brittany, Cornwall.
- Prominent saints and foundations: Colum Cille (Iona), Columbanus, Germanus, etc.
- Monastic and peregrinatio traditions.
- Interaction with secular powers and law (e.g. Cáin Adomnáin).
