# Ogam inscriptions and Primitive Irish

Ogam (or Ogham) is an early alphabetic script used in Ireland and parts of Britain for monumental inscriptions, mostly personal names. Represents the earliest written attestations of Irish (Primitive Irish); crucial for Celtic historical linguistics and the transition from oral to literate culture in Ireland.
