# Artillery in Napoleonic Battles

The employment, effects, and limitations of field (and horse) artillery as an integral element of combined-arms combat 1792–1815. Key themes from Muir: grand batteries from 1809, realistic rates of fire and ammunition consumption, primarily psychological wearing effect even at long range, tendency of gunners to withdraw rather than stand, and the horror of passive exposure for infantry under cannonade.
