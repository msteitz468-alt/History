# Einstein's God (Cosmic Religion)

**Einstein's personal religious stance: "I believe in Spinoza’s God, who reveals himself in the lawful harmony of all that exists, but not in a God who concerns himself with the fate and the doings of mankind." Veneration for "this force beyond anything that we can comprehend" as the fundamental emotion of science and art.**

## Definition and Scope

Not a personal deity who intervenes or judges. "The most beautiful emotion we can experience is the mysterious... To sense that behind anything that can be experienced there is something that our minds cannot grasp... this is religiousness. In this sense, and in this sense only, I am a devoutly religious man."

Determinism central: "I am a determinist. I do not believe in free will." "He does not play dice." Science and religion complementary: "Science without religion is lame, religion without science is blind."

## Emic vs Etic

Emic (Einstein's own): cosmic awe + strict causality as faith that "God Himself could not have arranged these connections any other way."  
Etic (critics): agnosticism or atheism disguised; "childish analogies" rejected.
