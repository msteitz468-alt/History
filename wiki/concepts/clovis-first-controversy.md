# Clovis-First Controversy

## Definition (emic/etic)

**Etic historiographical concept** naming the mid-to-late twentieth-century orthodoxy that the [[clovis-culture|Clovis]] fluted-point complex (~13,500–12,800 BP / Snow: 10,000–9000 B.C.) records the **first human occupation** of the Americas, versus competing **pre-Clovis** claims for earlier entry. Not an emic Native category. Full multi-source dispute record: [[clovis-first-vs-pre-clovis]].

## Core claims of the Clovis-first model

1. Clovis is the oldest *secure, continent-wide* material culture south of Pleistocene ice.
2. Entry via Beringia and (when open) the ice-free corridor after the LGM.
3. Pre-Clovis site claims fail on stratigraphy, dating contamination, non-diagnostic “artifacts,” or isolation inconsistent with hunter-gatherer social networks.
4. High burden of proof is justified because first arrivals were few and much of their landscape is now submerged.

## Critiques and limitations

- **Monte Verde** (Chile) and other accepted pre-Clovis sites break strict Clovis-first timing after the 1997 specialist panel.
- Coastal-route models explain early southern sites and the scarcity of shoreline evidence under post-glacial inundation.
- Nenana and other Alaskan complexes lack clear Clovis affinity — Clovis fluting may be an **in-continent** development.
- Snow (CHNPA I.1 ch. 3) himself documents how once-accepted early claims (e.g. Old Crow) collapsed under re-dating — showing both why skepticism was rational and why the orthodoxy overreached.

## CHNPA I.1 — Snow’s methodological package

Snow contributes the **detection-problem triad** (low numbers; inundation; social-network threshold), **pre-8000 B.C. radiocarbon caveats**, a **discarded-sites list** (Old Crow, Lewisville, Calico, Laguna Beach, Pikimachay, Sandia Cave, Tule Springs, Koch, Taber…), and a residual-candidate assessment (Meadowcroft, Wilson Butte; Latin American cluster “problematic”). His working threshold: convincing evidence younger than ~14,000 years (12,000 B.C.). Bibliographic pointers: Shutler, Bryan, Owen, Martin & Klein, Fagan, Greenberg (controversial). Full detail: [[clovis-first-vs-pre-clovis]] § CHNPA I.1.
