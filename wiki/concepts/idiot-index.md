# Idiot Index

Musk metric: for any part or process, calculate how much more it costs than the raw materials or a “dumb” baseline. High idiot index = target for radical cost reduction or redesign. Applied aggressively to Raptor engine components and vehicle manufacturing. Part of the broader “delete any part” and first-principles culture.
