# First Principles Thinking

Musk’s stated problem-solving method: break a problem down to its most fundamental truths (“first principles”) rather than reasoning by analogy or analogy to what already exists, then rebuild from there. Used at SpaceX to question every rocket part cost and at Tesla for battery and manufacturing design. Isaacson documents it as core to “delete any part or process you can” and the idiot index.
