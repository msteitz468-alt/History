# Cynghanedd

The defining ornament of Welsh strict-metre (cywydd) poetry: harmony through alliteration, rhyme, and consonance. Developed from earlier Cynfeirdd traditions; rules codified in bardic grammars (Gramadegau’r Penceirddiaid).
