# Mythological Cycle

Category of early Irish narrative literature focused on the gods and pre-Christian supernatural history of Ireland (Tuath Dé, battles of Mag Tuired, invasions, etc.). Provides the primary literary source for Irish pagan pantheon and cosmogony, heavily euhemerized in Christian manuscripts.
