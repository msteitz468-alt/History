# Vitruvian Man

The *Vitruvian Man* (c. 1490) is Leonardo da Vinci’s famous drawing of a male figure inscribed in both a circle and a square, illustrating the proportions described by the Roman architect Vitruvius. It has become the iconic image of the Renaissance synthesis of art, mathematics, and the study of the human body as a microcosm of the universe.

Leonardo’s version goes beyond illustration: the geometric construction, multiple superimposed positions of the limbs, and accompanying notes demonstrate his empirical measurement of human proportions and his belief in harmonic correspondences between the body and cosmic order.
