# Counter-cultures in the Cold War

The wave of student, youth, and social movements in the 1960s–1970s that challenged the bipolar Cold War order, consumerism, authority, and the military-industrial complexes on both sides of the Iron Curtain.

From Leffler/Westad Vol. II (Suri Ch. 22): These rebellions were transnational, linked by opposition to the "structure of great power politics," Vietnam War, nuclear arms race, and domestic conformity. They influenced détente by pressuring leaders (e.g., US anti-war movement, European New Left, Eastern dissidents precursors).

Included New Left, counterculture (hippies, music), feminist and civil rights extensions, and protests in West, East, and Third World.
