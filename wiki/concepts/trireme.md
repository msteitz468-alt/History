# Trireme

The premier warship of the Classical Greek world: a fast, oared galley with three banks of oars (hence tri-), a bronze ram, and a crew of ~170 (mostly rowers) + marines. Highly maneuverable for ramming tactics rather than boarding. Central to Athenian naval power (Delian League/Athenian Empire), the Persian Wars (Salamis), and the Peloponnesian War (Sicilian expedition, naval arms race with Sparta/Corinth).

Key features: light construction, skilled citizen or mercenary crews, sophisticated command and signals. Maintenance and manning were major state burdens. Later superseded by larger polyremes in the Hellenistic period.
