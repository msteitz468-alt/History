# Battle of Hamburger Hill (1969)

Ten-day battle (10–20 May 1969) by the 101st Airborne Division to seize Dong Ap Bia (Hill 937) in the A Shau Valley from the NVA 29th Regiment.

## Narrative

Multiple assaults against heavily bunkered, jungle-covered high ground. "Hugging" tactics by NVA defenders negated some US firepower advantages. US eventually took the summit with heavy use of air and artillery. The hill was evacuated days later.

## Causal Analysis

`caused_by:` Post-Tet emphasis on disrupting NVA sanctuaries and main-force units in I Corps.

`contributed_to:` Public perception of pointless casualties.

## Consequence Analysis

`produced:` Domestic controversy and pressure on the Nixon administration. Contributed to narrative of attrition without strategic payoff.
