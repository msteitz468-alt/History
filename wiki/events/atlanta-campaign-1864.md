# Atlanta Campaign (May–September 1864)

Sherman's successful campaign to capture Atlanta, crippling the Confederacy's war effort in the West.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/atlanta-campaign-1864]].

Sherman used flanking maneuvers to force Johnston back, then defeated Hood's aggressive attacks. Atlanta fell on September 2.

## Causal Analysis

Superior Union numbers, logistics, and Sherman's operational skill vs. Confederate command changes and defensive strategy.

## Consequence Analysis

Major political victory for the Union; enabled the March to the Sea and further destruction of Confederate resources.
