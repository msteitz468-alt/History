# SpaceX Falcon 1 Fourth Launch Success (2008)

After three consecutive Falcon 1 failures (2006–2008), the fourth launch on 28 September 2008 from Omelek Island (Kwajalein Atoll) reached orbit. First privately developed liquid-fuel rocket to do so. Occurred days after Lehman collapse amid simultaneous Tesla/SpaceX funding crises. Validated the company and unlocked NASA contracts.
