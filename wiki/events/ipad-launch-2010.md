# iPad Launch (2010)

On 27 January 2010, Steve Jobs unveiled the iPad as the device for the post-PC era. Larger multi-touch tablet positioned between iPhone and laptop. Launched April 2010; sold one million units in under a month.

## Historiography

Based primarily on Isaacson's authorized biography, supplemented by
extensive contemporary product journalism and Apple's own public statements; as a recent
corporate/product event it has not yet been the subject of independent academic
historiography, and the main interpretive question in existing accounts (Apple's product
narrative vs. critics who saw the iPad as a "closed," locked-down platform) reflects
industry commentary rather than settled historical debate.
