# Battle of Kiev (1941)

Enormous encirclement battle in Ukraine during Operation Barbarossa. After initial advances, Hitler overruled his generals and ordered Army Group Center to divert panzers south to assist Rundstedt's Army Group South. The resulting pocket around Kiev trapped four Soviet armies. Enormous material and human losses for the Red Army; significant delay to the drive on Moscow. Classic example of Hitler's operational meddling and the tension between economic/ideological objectives and pure military concentration.
