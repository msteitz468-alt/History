# Hunnic Migrations and the "Great Wandering of Peoples"

The arrival of the Huns in eastern Europe c. 370 CE triggered a chain reaction of migrations and invasions that profoundly reshaped the Roman world and created the political map of early medieval Europe. Under leaders like Attila, the Huns dominated much of central Europe for a generation before their power collapsed after Attila's death (453).

## Causal and Consequence Analysis
Not a single "invasion" but a cascade: Huns displace Goths and Alans → Goths enter Roman territory (376) → Visigothic revolt and sack of Rome (410) → Vandals cross to Africa (429) → Attila's campaigns (447–452) → final deposition of Romulus Augustulus (476).
