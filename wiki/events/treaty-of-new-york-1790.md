# Treaty of New York (1790)

## Narrative

The first major treaty under the new Constitution between the US and a major Native nation. Negotiated by Henry Knox with Creek leader Alexander McGillivray. Provided solemn US guarantee of Creek territorial integrity; included public and secret articles on trade and status. Quickly undermined by settler influx.

## Causal Analysis

Produced by the new federal policy articulated by Knox (tribes as foreign nations with prior occupancy rights) and Washington's desire for orderly western expansion without immediate war. Contributed to by the power vacuum after the Revolution and Georgia's aggressive claims.

## Consequence Analysis

Revealed the limits of federal power against demography, state interests, and land hunger. Served as test case for the "tragic" failure to achieve just accommodation with Native Americans. Parallel to slavery compromises as a foundational moral/political failure.
