# War of 1812

U.S.-British war (1812–1815) often called the "Second War of Independence." Triggered by maritime rights disputes and frontier pressures. Inconclusive militarily (burning of Washington, successful defense of Baltimore, Jackson's victory at New Orleans after peace signed) but politically transformative for the young republic.
