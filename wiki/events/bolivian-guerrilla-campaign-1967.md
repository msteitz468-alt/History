# Bolivian Guerrilla Campaign (1966–1967)

Che Guevara's final foco expedition in Bolivia (Ñancahuazú region). Entered clandestinely Nov 1966; isolated by lack of Bolivian CP support, poor logistics, and rapid counterinsurgency (Bolivian Army + US Green Berets/CIA). Reduced by ambushes and desertions; Che wounded and captured 8 Oct 1967 near La Higuera, executed 9 Oct. Detailed in Anderson from diaries and participant interviews.

## Historiography

Anderson's account, based on interviews with Bolivian participants (including Vargas Salinas) and the 1997 exhumation details, is the most detailed non-official narrative. Discrepancies persist among officers and CIA accounts on the exact sequence and order to execute. The event is central to both the defeat of foco theory in practice and the global amplification of the Che myth.
