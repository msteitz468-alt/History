# Battle of Nashville (December 15–16, 1864)

Decisive Union victory that destroyed the Army of Tennessee.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-nashville-1864]].

Thomas attacked and routed Hood's army over two days.

## Causal Analysis

Thomas's superior numbers, preparation, and coordination vs. Hood's depleted and demoralized force.

## Consequence Analysis

Effectively ended the war in the Western Theater.
