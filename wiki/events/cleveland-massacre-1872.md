# The Cleveland Massacre

Over roughly six weeks in early 1872, [[actors/rockefeller-john-d|Rockefeller]]'s [[actors/standard-oil|Standard Oil]] — armed with preferential secret freight rates negotiated through the South Improvement Company scheme — bought out or absorbed the great majority of competing oil refiners in [[places/cleveland|Cleveland]], acquiring some 22 of 26 rivals. Rockefeller presented competitors with a choice: sell out (often for Standard stock or cash) or be crushed by transport-cost disadvantages they could not match. The episode established Standard's regional supremacy and became the template for its later national consolidation; it also entered muckraking lore (via [[actors/tarbell-ida|Ida Tarbell]], whose family was among the affected Oil Regions interests) as the defining act of Rockefeller's predation.
