# Fall of France (May–June 1940)

Rapid German conquest of France and Low Countries using innovative mobile warfare.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/world-wars-era/second-world-war/fall-of-france-1940]].

German forces bypassed Maginot Line via Ardennes, encircled Allied armies in north, leading to Dunkirk and French surrender.

## Causal Analysis

Allied complacency, poor coordination, German combined arms (Panzers + air + infantry), Manstein plan, Guderian's drive.

## Consequence Analysis

Britain isolated but defiant; US involvement accelerated; Germany turned east; end of Third Republic.
