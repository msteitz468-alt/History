# Solvay Conferences: Einstein-Bohr Quantum Debates (1927, 1930)

**Einstein's repeated thought-experiment challenges to the Copenhagen interpretation of quantum mechanics at the 1927 and 1930 Solvay Conferences in Brussels.**

## Narrative

1927 Solvay: Einstein (with Planck, Lorentz) vs young Turks (Heisenberg 25, Dirac 25, Pauli 27, etc.). Einstein proposes thought experiments (electron through slit + shutter; position/momentum measurements) to show quantum mechanics does not give complete deterministic description. Bohr refutes each overnight using uncertainty relations. "Einstein, I’m ashamed of you" (Ehrenfest). "We rode to and fro" debating on streetcar (Bohr).

1930 Solvay: Einstein's photon-box + precise clock thought experiment. Bohr shaken but refutes using Einstein's own GR (gravitational redshift on clock after photon escape changes box mass/position). Einstein concedes calculation but not interpretation.

## Consequence Analysis

Bohr group gains confidence ("now be sure of our ground"). Einstein never fully convinced; "I admire... but I believe that the restriction to statistical laws will be a passing one." Leads to EPR 1935. "No more profound intellectual debate has ever been conducted" (C. P. Snow).
