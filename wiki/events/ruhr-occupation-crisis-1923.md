# Ruhr Occupation Crisis (1923)

The Franco-Belgian military occupation of the Ruhr industrial district from
January 1923 to enforce reparations deliveries, met by German
government-financed "passive resistance" — the trigger that tipped chronic
inflation into the [[hyperinflation-1923|hyperinflation catastrophe]].

## Narrative

Per Evans ([[evans-coming-of-third-reich-2003]]): "So French and Belgian
troops occupied Germany's leading industrial district, the Ruhr, in January
1923 in order to seize the missing coal and force the Germans to fulfil their
obligations under the Treaty." Berlin proclaimed passive resistance,
financing non-cooperating workers and officials by printing money; the
struggle was called off toward the end of September 1923, when
[[gustav-stresemann|Stresemann]] negotiated withdrawal in exchange for
guaranteed reparations payments — the opening of the "fulfilment" policy that
led to the [[dawes-plan-1924|Dawes Plan]].

## Causal Analysis

- caused_by: German arrears in gold-priced coal deliveries under
  [[treaty-of-versailles-1919]] — themselves unaffordable because of currency
  decay (a feedback loop with [[hyperinflation-1923]]).
- caused_by: Poincaré's enforcement-first security policy.

## Consequence Analysis

- produced: the terminal phase of [[hyperinflation-1923]] (dollar from 17,000
  marks in January to 353,000 by July 1923); the threat of starvation in the
  occupied zone was "very real" as passive resistance crippled transport.
- contributed_to: a "massive wave of nationalist outrage" through German
  universities — students prominent in resistance agitation — and the
  radicalized autumn of 1923 that produced the
  [[beer-hall-putsch-1923|Beer-Hall Putsch]].
- produced: [[dawes-plan-1924]] and Franco-German de-escalation (Locarno).

## Historiography

Evans's framing makes the crisis the hinge of early Weimar: both consequence
and amplifier of the reparations-inflation trap, and the incubator of the
nationalist wave of 1923. Fuller diplomatic treatments (Schuker, McDougall)
are not yet ingested.
