# Challenger Disaster Investigation 1986

The presidential commission (Rogers Commission) investigation into the January 28, 1986 explosion of the Space Shuttle Challenger 73 seconds after launch, which killed all seven crew members. Richard Feynman served as a member. His appendix to the final report and his televised demonstration (O-ring in ice water) highlighted the temperature dependence of the seal failure and NASA’s unrealistic probability estimates (“one in 100,000”). His conclusion: “For a successful technology, reality must take precedence over public relations, for nature cannot be fooled.”
