# Ramanujan Elected Fellow of the Royal Society (1918)

In 1918 Srinivasa Ramanujan was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society — the first Indian so honored for scientific work. The election was the direct result of the papers he had published since arriving in England and of G. H. Hardy’s persistent advocacy. It was a formal recognition that the “clerk from Madras” had entered the first rank of world mathematicians. The same year he was also elected a Fellow of Trinity College.
