# Founding of SpaceX (2002)

Elon Musk founded Space Exploration Technologies (SpaceX) in June 2002 after selling PayPal shares. Explicit goal: reduce cost of space access by factor of ten and make humanity multi-planetary (Mars). Early team included Gwynne Shotwell. Developed Falcon 1 from scratch in El Segundo/Hawthorne.
