# Siege of Khe Sanh (1968)

Prolonged siege of the Khe Sanh Combat Base by multiple NVA divisions, January–April 1968. One of the largest concentrations of US airpower in the war.

## Narrative

NVA encircled the base and assaulted outlying hills. USMC garrison reinforced; resupply by air under fire. Massive B-52 and tactical air campaign plus artillery broke repeated assaults. Operation PEGASUS (1st Cav) relieved the base in April. The base was later evacuated as part of force redeployments.

## Causal Analysis

`contributed_to:` NVA Tet deception plan (fixing US forces in north). US over-focus on Khe Sanh as potential main effort (per Willbanks intel analysis).

## Consequence Analysis

`contributed_to:` Tet psychological impact (base under siege while cities attacked). Demonstrated limits of isolated bases without secure LOCs. Airpower proved decisive in siege defense.
