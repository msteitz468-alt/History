# Aktion T4 (1939–1945)

The Nazi "euthanasia" program — codenamed **T4** after its Berlin headquarters at Tiergartenstrasse 4 — was the systematic murder of institutionalized psychiatric patients and disabled people, authorized by [[adolf-hitler|Hitler]] in a secret note backdated to 1 September 1939. Documented for the wiki from [[evans-third-reich-at-war-2008|Evans, *The Third Reich at War* (2008)]].

## Narrative

Per [[evans-third-reich-at-war-2008|Evans (2008)]]:

**Origins.** Hitler's eugenic intent long predated the war (1929 Nuremberg speech: removing "70,000 to 80,000 of the weakest" of a million annual births would "perhaps actually be an increase in [Germany's] strength"). The 1933 sterilization law reached 360,000 victims by 1939 (see [[nazi-racial-hygiene-program-1933-1939]]); Hitler told Reich Doctors' Leader Wagner in 1935 he would implement killing in wartime, "when the whole world is gazing at acts of war and the value of human life in any case weighs less in the balance." The trigger was a father's 1939 petition to kill his severely disabled infant son: Hitler sent Karl Brandt to Leipzig to arrange it (done 25 July 1939), then tasked Brandt and Philipp Bouhler of the Führer Chancellery with a full program. His authorization — backdated to 1 September 1939 to tie it rhetorically to the war — was a private note, not a decree, empowering named doctors to grant "a merciful death" to the "incurable." Killings of institutionalized patients in annexed Poland and Pomerania by regional leaders actually *preceded* the signed order (`preceded_by`, not `caused_by`, in strict chronology).

**Children.** Compulsory registration of "malformed" newborns from 18 August 1939 (2 RM per report to doctors and midwives); three Chancellery assessors marked forms "+" for death; ~5,000 children killed in some 30 pediatric "clinics" by starvation or Luminal in the food, the age limit creeping up to 16. Director Hermann Pfannmüller of Eglfing-Haar, displaying a starving child to a visitor: "With this one, for example, it will still take two to three days."

**Adults — Aktion T4 proper.** Run from Tiergartenstrasse 4 by Viktor Brack (alias "Jennerwein") under Bouhler, medical direction under Werner Heyde. SS chemist Albert Widmann — briefed that the victims were "beasts in human form," the phrase Evans takes for his chapter title — designed the shower-disguised carbon-monoxide gas chamber demonstrated at Brandenburg prison (late 1939/January 1940) before Bouhler, Brandt, Conti, Brack, and the Stuttgart police official Christian Wirth: 8 patients gassed, lethal injection having proved too slow. Six killing centers followed — Brandenburg, Grafeneck, Hartheim, Sonnenstein, Bernburg, Hadamar — killing **~80,000** by gas by August 1941 (the program's own statistician's total; ~200,000 institutional victims by 1945 counting the decentralized continuation). Selection was assembly-line arbitrary: assessors like Pfannmüller processed 121 forms a day, Josef Schreck 15,000 in 1940 — "neither man can have spent more than a few seconds" per life; the operative criterion was capacity for work. Jewish patients were killed categorically from April 1940 (~1,000; Pfannmüller reported his institution "Jew-free"). The deception apparatus — grey buses, sham showers, falsified death certificates with a "pin map" to avoid suspicious clustering of causes, urns of mixed ashes, pensions collected past death — did not fool everyone: patients cried "Here come the murderers!" as the buses arrived. An eyewitness at the Hadamar peephole: "The way they died was so full of suffering that one cannot speak of a humane killing." Staff culture was brutal and drunken — Hartheim's party for the 10,000th cremation featured a gassed victim's body laid out with flowers in a mock ceremony. Wirth to new staff: "Anyone who does not keep quiet will go to a concentration camp or be shot."

**Protest and the halt.** Judge Lothar Kreyssig declared the transfers illegal and was compulsorily retired; pastors Braune and Bodelschwingh of Bethel resisted (the 19 September 1940 bombing of Bethel, blamed by Goebbels on British "infanticide," was hinted by an informant to have been German — an allegation Evans leaves unresolved); Bishop Wurm and the Catholic hierarchy protested privately; the Vatican decreed against direct killing of innocents (2 December 1940). The turning point was **Bishop Clemens August von Galen's sermon of 3 August 1941** in Münster, naming the program and warning: "the way is open to the murder of all unproductive people . . . the murder of all of us, when we become old and weak." Printed as a pastoral letter, broadcast by the BBC, dropped as Allied leaflets — in Evans's judgment "the strongest, most explicit and most widespread protest movement against any Nazi policy since the beginning of the Third Reich." Bormann wanted Galen hanged; Hitler and Goebbels deferred revenge "until the war was over." Hitler ordered adult gassing halted on **24 August 1941** — but the quota of 70,000 was already met, child killings continued, and murder shifted to the camps (the "14f13" selections) and to starvation and overdose in ordinary institutions, continuing to 1945. Propaganda kept working the theme: the film *I Accuse!* (August 1941, scenes inserted by Brack) reached 18 million viewers to mixed SD-reported reception.

**Significance.** Evans's through-line: T4 stopped where it did because its victims were "closely bound into the central networks of German society," unlike Jews and Roma; the lesson Hitler drew was never again to put such an order in writing. Its euphemisms, gas chambers, and personnel (Wirth, Stangl, Eberl, the "specialists in extermination by gassing" Himmler explicitly redeployed east in autumn 1941) passed directly to [[operation-reinhard-1942|Operation Reinhard]] — the institutional and methodological incubator of [[the-holocaust]].

## Causal Analysis

T4 was `caused_by` the [[nazi-racial-hygiene-program-1933-1939|prewar racial-hygiene program]] (compulsory sterilization from 1933) and `enabled` by the outbreak of war, which supplied both cover and the regime's rationale of freeing hospital resources. It `produced` the gassing personnel and technology of [[operation-reinhard-1942|Operation Reinhard]].

## Historiography

Evans builds on Burleigh and Friedlander; the key documents are Hitler's backdated authorization, the T4 statistical records, and postwar trial testimony (Widmann, Becker). Interpretive weight rests on (1) the continuity thesis — T4 as the Holocaust's technical and personnel incubator, which Evans affirms for method and staff while warning elsewhere (2015) against treating it as causally sufficient; and (2) what the Galen episode shows about the regime's sensitivity to public Catholic protest — and, by contrast, the absence of any comparable protest over the Jews. One flagged allegation (the Bethel bombing's authorship) remains unestablished.

## Continuation after the "halt" — Evans (2008), Part 5

The August 1941 stop ended only the gas-chamber phase inside the Reich. **"Special Treatment 14f13"** sent T4 doctors into the camps from September 1941 to select the sick, weak, and disabled for gassing at Bernburg, Hartheim, and Sonnenstein — ~20,000 killed (Dr. Friedrich Mennecke's letters describe "selecting" Jewish inmates at Buchenwald as "a purely theoretical task" with invented diagnoses: "fanatical German-hater and asocial psychopath"). In the asylums, killing continued by starvation and overdose: Kaufbeuren's "basic diet," Pfannmüller's Eglfing-Haar "hunger houses," and Hadamar's second phase (4,422 of 4,817 patients dead, August 1942–March 1945), including 40+ healthy "mixed-race" children killed in April 1943 and mentally ill Polish and Soviet forced laborers and their infants (68 children under three at Kelsterbach). In the occupied East the [[einsatzgruppen]] shot, gassed, and starved ~10,000 psychiatric patients. Church protest continued muted — Bertram suppressed a draft bishops' letter in 1941; Pius XII's *Mystici Corporis* (June 1943) condemned the killings in veiled terms; a September 1943 pulpit condemnation was broad but practically ineffective.
