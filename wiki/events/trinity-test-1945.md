# Trinity Test (16 July 1945)

The first detonation of a nuclear weapon, a plutonium implosion device code-named "the gadget," at the Alamogordo Bombing Range in New Mexico. Directed by J. Robert Oppenheimer for the Los Alamos laboratory under the Manhattan Project. Yield ~20 kt. Oppenheimer later recalled quoting the Bhagavad Gita: "Now I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds."
