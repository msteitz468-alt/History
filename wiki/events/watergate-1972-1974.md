# Watergate Scandal (1972–1974)

Break-in at DNC headquarters and subsequent cover-up leading to President Nixon's resignation (August 1974). Major legitimacy crisis; starting point for many narratives of post-1970s distrust and polarization. (Stub for Kruse/Zelizer *Fault Lines*.)
