# United States Presidential Election of 1864

Wartime presidential contest between incumbent [[actors/lincoln-abraham|Abraham Lincoln]] (National Union / Republican) and [[actors/mcclellan-george-b|George B. McClellan]] (Democrat). Ellsworth (*Midnight on the Potomac*, 2025) ties Lincoln's summer political near-death to the military turn after Atlanta and Mobile Bay — "Daylight" after the dark months of Overland casualties and Early's raid.

## Narrative

By August 1864 many Republicans doubted Lincoln could win; casualty lists from the Wilderness–Cold Harbor–Petersburg fighting and Early's threat to Washington fed Peace Democrat hopes. Capture of Atlanta (Sherman) and Mobile Bay (Farragut) reversed momentum. Lincoln won a clear Electoral College victory with strong soldier-vote support; Andrew Johnson of Tennessee was elected vice president on the Union ticket.

- **contributed_to by:** [[events/american-civil-war-1861-1865]] military outcomes in late summer 1864; party coalition management.
- **enabled:** Final-year push to victory and constitutional abolition without a peace-candidate presidency.

## Historiography

Classic question: how close was Lincoln to defeat before Atlanta? Ellsworth follows the "near-run then rescued by arms" popular synthesis. Political historians nuance soldier voting, state machines, and Democratic platform contradictions (war candidate on a peace-tinged platform).


## Ellsworth Detail (*Midnight on the Potomac*, 2025)

Summer "Darkness": Weed told Lincoln reelection was "an impossibility"; Greeley and Swett echoed doom. **23 Aug 1864 blind memorandum** (cabinet signed unread): "exceedingly probable that this Administration will not be re-elected" and duty to cooperate with President-elect to save the Union between election and inauguration.

**Daylight:** Atlanta fell **2 Sept**; Mobile Bay ~10 days earlier; Frémont withdrew; factions united—Sherman "thrown the President a lifeline." 15 Oct Lincoln penciled 117–114 electoral estimate. War Department mobilized soldiers' votes (Dana). **8 Nov** wet Election Day; Company K under White House windows voted Lincoln 65–McClellan 0; around midnight key states looked safe. Franchise was overwhelmingly white male.

Parallel: Confederate Canada terror plans for Election Day multi-city arson collapsed; **25 Nov** NYC Greek-fire attack on ~19 hotels still went forward (largely failed when windows left shut).
