# Battle of Ia Drang (1965)

First sustained battle between US forces (primarily 1st Cavalry Division airmobile) and regular North Vietnamese Army regiments in the Ia Drang Valley, November 1965.

## Narrative

US 1/7 Cav air assaulted into LZ X-Ray and became heavily engaged with elements of the NVA 66th and 33rd Regiments. Intense close combat with decisive US artillery and air support. Subsequent movement of 2/7 Cav to LZ Albany resulted in a costly ambush. The campaign demonstrated both the power of airmobility and the lethality of NVA main-force units when they chose to stand.

## Causal Analysis

`caused_by:` NVA strategic decision to move regiments south to counter US buildup and threaten GVN control in the Highlands. US commitment of 1st Cav to spoiling attack.

`contributed_to:` Validation of airmobile concept in US doctrine; confirmation to Westmoreland that large-unit search-and-destroy could produce favorable exchange ratios.

## Consequence Analysis

`produced:` Template for subsequent US operations relying on heliborne insertion + firepower. `contributed_to:` NVA shift toward guerrilla/main-force mix and sanctuaries. Psychological boost for US but also illustration of costs of meeting NVA regulars.
