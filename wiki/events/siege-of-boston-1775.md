# The Siege of Boston (1775–1776)

> **⚔️ Staff-college tactical analysis:** see **[[hubs/warfare/age-of-expansion/american-revolutionary-war/siege-of-boston|Siege of Boston — Operational Analysis]]** in the warfare hub for the full order of battle, the phased conduct of the action, and the principles-of-war assessment.

The eleven-month investment of British-held Boston that opened the **[[american-revolution|
Revolutionary War]]** and gave [[washington-george|Washington]] his first command. After
[[battles-of-lexington-and-concord-1775|Lexington and Concord]], New England militia penned General
Gage's garrison inside the city; the bloody check at [[battle-of-bunker-hill-1775|Bunker Hill]]
(June 1775) confirmed the stalemate.

## Narrative

Washington arrived at Cambridge on 2 July 1775 to find a "ramshackle" provincial army with no
sanitation, drill, or supply system, and set about forging a true **[[continental-army|Continental
Army]]** — chains of command, discipline, and administration. Congress diverted him into the
disastrous winter invasion of Canada under **[[arnold-benedict|Benedict Arnold]]**, which "came to
grief before the walls of Quebec." The deadlock at Boston broke when **[[knox-henry|Henry Knox]]**
hauled the guns captured at Ticonderoga overland through the winter; on the night of 4–5 March 1776
the Americans seized and fortified **Dorchester Heights**, training artillery on the city. Perhaps
"reminded of Bunker Hill," [[howe-william|Howe]] declined to assault and evacuated — the last
British troops leaving on **17 March 1776**. Washington, who had wanted a bloody, war-shortening
victory, privately called the bloodless win a "disappointment."

## Causal Analysis

- `caused_by:` the spontaneous **militia mobilization** after Lexington and Concord.
- `contributed_to:` Knox's artillery feat and the seizure of **Dorchester Heights**, which made the
  British position untenable.

## Consequence Analysis

- `produced:` the **first major American strategic success** and the liberation of a major city.
- `produced:` a tested Continental Army and command structure that Washington carried into the
  [[battle-of-long-island-1776|New York campaign]].
