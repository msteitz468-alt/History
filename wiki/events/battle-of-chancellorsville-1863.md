# Battle of Chancellorsville (30 April–6 May 1863)

**War hub:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/american-civil-war|American Civil War War Hub]] · **Tactical analysis:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-chancellorsville-1863|Battle of Chancellorsville — Tactical Analysis]]

## Narrative

Hooker crossed the Rappahannock and Rapidan to turn Lee's left. Lee responded with a daring division of his army, sending Jackson on a long flanking march. Jackson struck the Union right flank (Eleventh Corps) with devastating effect. Subsequent fighting around the Chancellor house and at Salem Church. Jackson mortally wounded by friendly fire.

## Causal Analysis

Lee's calculated risk in dividing forces against a superior enemy succeeded due to Hooker's loss of nerve after initial success and excellent Confederate staff work and march discipline.

## Consequence Analysis

Major Union defeat; loss of Jackson changed Confederate command structure for the remainder of the war in the East.
