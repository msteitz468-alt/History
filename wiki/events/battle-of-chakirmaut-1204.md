# Battle of Chakirma'ut (1204)

At **Chakirma'ut** on the Sa'ari steppe, **[[genghis-khan|Chinggis Khan]]** encircled and destroyed
the **Naiman** under Tayang Khan — the culminating battle for unification of Mongolia before the
quriltai of 1206.

## Narrative

After defeating the Kereit (1204), the Naiman of western Mongolia, augmented by steppe allies
including **Jamuqa**, marched against the Mongols in May 1204 despite lean post-winter horses.
Chinggis pinned the Naiman against the mountains, advanced in caragana/nagur/chisel formations,
and completed a **double envelopment** with the dörben noqas (Jebe, Qubilai, Jelme, Sübedei) and
shock tribes Uru'ut and Manqut. The Naiman fled to a mountain top; night escape attempts ended in
mass death over cliffs. Tayang Khan was captured; Güchülüg escaped briefly before submission.
Jamuqa was betrayed by his men and executed at his own request (bloodless trampling in carpet).

## Causal Analysis

- `caused_by:` Naiman perception that "there cannot be two rulers on earth" after Mongol rise.
- `produced:` unified Mongolia under Chinggis Khan; `enabled:` the 1206 quriltai and decimal army.

## Consequence Analysis

- `produced:` the **[[mongol-empire|Mongol state]]** and the campaigns beyond Mongolia.
- `analogous_to:` later Mongol encirclements (Mohi 1241, Kalka feigned retreat).
