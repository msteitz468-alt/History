# Founding and Early Years of Tesla (2003–2004)

Tesla Motors founded July 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning to build high-performance electric sports cars. Elon Musk invested $6.5M in Series A (2004), became chairman, and later took operational control. Early focus on the Tesla Roadster using Lotus Elise chassis and AC Propulsion-derived powertrain.
