# Battle of Gettysburg (1–3 July 1863)

**War hub:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/american-civil-war|American Civil War War Hub]] · **Tactical analysis:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-gettysburg-1863|Battle of Gettysburg — Tactical Analysis]]

## Narrative

Lee invaded Pennsylvania. After Stuart's cavalry was out of touch, Lee encountered Union forces at Gettysburg. Three days of fighting: 1 July (initial clash and Union retreat to ridges), 2 July (assaults on left and right flanks incl. Little Round Top, Wheat Field, Devil's Den), 3 July (Pickett's Charge against the Union center).

## Causal Analysis

Lee sought decisive battle; Meade chose strong defensive ground on the fishhook ridge line.

## Consequence Analysis

Lee's army suffered irreplaceable losses and retreated. Combined with Vicksburg surrender, marked the "high water mark" of the Confederacy.
