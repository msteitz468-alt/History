# Ramanujan’s Voyage to England (1914)

In March–April 1914 Ramanujan sailed from Madras to London. E. H. Neville had been sent by Hardy as emissary and lecturer; his additional task was to bring Ramanujan to England. The decision required overcoming family resistance, fears of ritual pollution, and the practical difficulties of wartime travel. Ramanujan arrived in a Europe already at war. The voyage marks the transition from the isolated Indian years to the Cambridge collaboration that made his work internationally visible.
