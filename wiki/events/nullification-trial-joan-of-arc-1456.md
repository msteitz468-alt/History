# The Nullification Trial of Joan of Arc

The Nullification Trial of Joan of Arc (also known as the Rehabilitation Trial) was opened in 1455 and concluded on 7 July 1456. Commissioned by Pope Callixtus III at the behest of Charles VII and Joan's family, the trial nullified the 1431 condemnation.
