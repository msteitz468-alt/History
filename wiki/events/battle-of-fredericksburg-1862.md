# Battle of Fredericksburg (11–15 December 1862)

**War hub:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/american-civil-war|American Civil War War Hub]] · **Tactical analysis:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-fredericksburg-1862|Battle of Fredericksburg — Tactical Analysis]]

## Narrative

Union Army of the Potomac under Maj. Gen. Ambrose E. Burnside attempted to cross the Rappahannock River at Fredericksburg and assault Confederate positions on the heights behind the town. Main fighting 13 December. Confederate victory under Lee.

Grounded in source: "Army of the Potomac (Union) under Burnside crossing the Rappahannock, 13 December."

## Causal Analysis

Burnside sought a quick victory before winter; Lee chose strong defensive ground on Marye's Heights and adjacent ridges.

`preceded_by: [[battle-of-antietam-1862]]` (per chronology in source phases)

## Consequence Analysis

Union suffered severe losses in frontal assaults. Strengthened Confederate defensive doctrine in the East. Led to Burnside's removal and the "Mud March."
