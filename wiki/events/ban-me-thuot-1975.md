# Ban Me Thuot (1975)

See [[events/fall-of-saigon-1975]] and [[events/vietnam-war]]. Warfare hub analysis in [[hubs/warfare/cold-war/vietnam-war/vietnam-war]].

## Narrative

After Phuoc Long success, PAVN focused Campaign 275 initial effort on Ban Me Thuot (Darlac Province, MR II) "where a successful attack would give the North Vietnamese another province and provide staging areas and avenues of approach for a future follow-on attack to seize Pleiku."

PAVN plan ("blossoming lotus" developed by Dung vs French 1952): diversionary attacks (968th Div on outposts north/west of Kontum/Pleiku), sever routes (Route 19 4 Mar; Route 21 5 Mar; Route 14 8 Mar), then main attack on city center. ~75-80k troops (5 main force infantry divisions + tanks/artillery/AA) vs ARVN one regiment (53rd from 23rd Div) + Ranger group + RF/PF.

Battle opened 0200 10 Mar: 320th Div (Dung's old unit) from north took ammo depot and light airstrip by mid-afternoon. F-10 and 316th elements from south hit sector HQ. "the ARVN, demonstrating a long-standing inability to coordinate fire support, dropped an artillery round intended for the attacking PAVN tanks on the sector command post, knocking out communications..." By nightfall most of center lost; flame throwers used next day. Command post overrun 11 Mar; senior officers captured.

ARVN counterattacks failed. 21st Ranger Group reached outskirts but Brig. Gen. Le Van Tuong halted them "to protect the evacuation of his wife and children by helicopter." Later airlifted 44th/45th Regts (only partial due to CH-47 maintenance/parts; dwindled to one helo) without artillery/tank support; stalled. Tuong lightly wounded 16 Mar and evacuated to Nha Trang hospital. "A perfect opportunity to relieve Ban Me Thuot was lost." Remaining positions overrun 17-18 Mar; Phuoc An airfield lost 18 Mar.

Thieu (11 Mar) decided truncation but ordered Phu to reoccupy "at all costs" — setting up withdrawal disaster.

## Causal Analysis

- `caused_by: [[phuoc-long-1974]]` (emboldened PAVN; demonstrated ARVN weakness and US inaction).
- `contributed_to:` ARVN misperception (Phu saw Ban Me Thuot as secondary); poor fire support coordination; leadership prioritizing family evac.
- PAVN `produced:` isolation then center attack.

## Consequence Analysis

- `produced:` loss of entire Darlac Province and key Highlands hub.
- `produced: [[fall-of-saigon-1975]]` (triggered Thieu truncation decision and II Corps destruction on Route 7).
- `contributed_to:` loss of half South Vietnam in weeks; morale collapse.
- "the South Vietnamese troops surprised us, too, because they became so disorganized so quickly."
