# September 11 Attacks (2001-09-11)

Al-Qaeda terrorist attacks on World Trade Center, Pentagon, and Flight 93. ~3,000 killed. Transformed US foreign and domestic policy. (Stub; may be updated from prior ingests.)

## Historiography

The 9/11 Commission Report (2004), built on access to classified intelligence records,
testimony from officials, and captured al-Qaeda communications, is the authoritative
primary account, though the Commission itself noted gaps where agencies withheld or
destroyed material (e.g., CIA interrogation tapes destroyed in 2005) and some underlying
intelligence remains classified. Scholarly and journalistic debate has focused on the
extent of prior intelligence-failure versus unavoidable surprise, the role of Saudi
government or citizens in facilitating the plot (a live and only partially resolved
question following the 2021 declassification push and related lawsuits), and the
long-run causal weight of the attacks in reshaping US foreign policy, civil liberties law,
and the "Global War on Terror." A significant fringe "truther" literature disputing the
official account exists but lacks credibility within mainstream historical scholarship.
