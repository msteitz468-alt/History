# Battle of Midway (June 4–7, 1942)

Decisive US Navy victory that halted Japanese expansion in the Pacific.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/world-wars-era/second-world-war/battle-of-midway-1942]].

US carriers, alerted by intelligence, ambushed Japanese fleet; sank four Japanese carriers for one US loss.

## Causal Analysis

US codebreaking (Magic), dive bomber tactics, Japanese overconfidence and divided forces, luck with "the miracle at Midway."

## Consequence Analysis

Shifted balance of power; US took initiative in Pacific; major morale and strategic victory.
