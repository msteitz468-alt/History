# The Battle of Monmouth (28 June 1778)

> **⚔️ Staff-college tactical analysis:** see **[[hubs/warfare/age-of-expansion/american-revolutionary-war/battle-of-monmouth|Monmouth — Tactical Analysis]]** in the warfare hub for the full order of battle, the phased conduct of the action, and the principles-of-war assessment.

The proof that **[[valley-forge-1777|Valley Forge]] had remade the army**. When the
[[franco-american-alliance-1778|French alliance]] forced Britain onto the defensive,
[[clinton-henry|Clinton]] evacuated Philadelphia and marched 10,000 men across New Jersey to New York;
[[washington-george|Washington]] — urged on by [[greene-nathanael|Greene]],
[[steuben-friedrich|Steuben]], and [[lafayette|Lafayette]] over cautious councils of war — followed
with 12,000 to strike the column.

## Narrative

The attack opened in confusion. **Charles Lee**, who had pulled rank over Lafayette to take the
forward command he had opposed, attacked without a plan, lost control, and fell into a disorderly
retreat. Washington arrived, **furiously relieved Lee on the spot**, and rallied the line — and, unlike
the routs of 1776, "the retreating troops halted and instantly obeyed his commands." The Continentals
then **held against Cornwallis's regulars in the open**: "Steuben's drill served them well," Knox and
Greene enfiladed the British advance with artillery, and repeated assaults failed in fierce heat
(Hamilton and Burr had horses shot under them). Clinton withdrew after dark, leaving the field to the
Americans. Losses were roughly 500 British to 350 Americans.

## Causal Analysis

- `caused_by:` the **strategic reversal of 1778** — the French alliance forcing Clinton's retreat from
  Philadelphia — and Washington's resolve to contest it.
- `contributed_to:` the **Valley Forge training** that made a stand-up fight survivable.

## Consequence Analysis

- `produced:` confidence that the Continentals "could fight British regulars toe-to-toe" — Lengel's
  campaign turning point.
- `produced:` the consolidation of Washington's authority: **Lee's court-martial** and [[gates-horatio|
  Gates]]'s eclipse removed his last serious rivals, fixing the continuity of command that "would prove
  decisive for American victory."
