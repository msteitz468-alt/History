# Return to Apple (1997)

In late 1996 Apple, then under CEO Gil Amelio and facing insolvency, purchased NeXT for approximately $429 million (mostly stock) to acquire its technology and, crucially, Steve Jobs. Jobs returned first as advisor, then interim CEO (“iCEO”) in July 1997 after Amelio’s departure. The return is the pivot point of Isaacson’s narrative: the “Second Coming.”

**Narrative**: Jobs found Apple’s product line bloated (dozens of models with no clear identity). He immediately began culling products to four quadrants (consumer/pro desktop/laptop) and launched the “Think Different” campaign. The 1998 iMac revived the company’s fortunes.

**Causal Analysis**: Apple’s crisis created the necessity; NeXT purchase gave Jobs the vehicle and leverage; his product instincts and force of personality supplied the direction.

**Consequence Analysis**: Within three years Apple was profitable again. The return set in motion the iPod, iPhone, and iPad eras and the modern Apple design identity.
