# Human Experimentation in the Camps (1941–1945)

Per [[evans-third-reich-at-war-2008|Evans (2008)]], "Deadly Science." Recorded at full fidelity; this material is graphic by nature and is not softened.

## The Programs

- **Dachau — decompression (Sigmund Rascher, February–May 1942):** up to 300 simulated high-altitude experiments for the air force, including deliberate "terminal experiments" on men Rascher called "race-defiling, professionally criminal Jews"; 70–80 prisoners killed; results screened for the Air Ministry.
- **Dachau — freezing (Rascher, from June 1942):** 50–60 inmates immersed in ice water in flight gear; 15–18 dead by October (average time to death ~70 minutes); presented to 95 medical scientists at Nuremberg (26–27 October 1942) — no one objected. (Rascher and his wife were later executed by the SS over her kidnapped "children.")
- **Seawater experiments (Schröder/Beiglböck, 1944):** 40 Roma prisoners fed only seawater preparations; maddening thirst, men licking mopped floors.
- **Sulphonamide wound experiments (Gebhardt/Grawitz, from July 1942):** ordered after Heydrich's death from wound sepsis; Polish women prisoners at Ravensbrück ("the rabbits") had legs cut open, muscle crushed, glass, wood, and bacteria sewn in, bones smashed with hammers; parallel Dachau gangrene experiments on 40 Polish priests (12 dead). Results presented at a May 1943 conference with non-consent openly acknowledged.
- **Buchenwald — typhus vaccines (from December 1941):** 537 prisoners deliberately infected across nine trials; 127 died.
- **Mauthausen — "Eastern Nutrition" (1943):** a cellulose paste devised at the top (Hitler, Brandt, Himmler); of 150 fed it exclusively for six months, 116 died.
- **Sachsenhausen — hepatitis (Gutzeit/Dohmen, 1943–44):** 11 Jewish boys selected from the Auschwitz ramp and infected.
- **Chemical weapons:** phosphorus burns (Buchenwald), mustard gas (Sachsenhausen, Natzweiler, Neuengamme — deaths "in many cases with the knowledge of Hitler himself"), phosgene (four Soviet POWs killed).
- **Auschwitz — Josef Mengele:** noma studies on Roma children in which treatment was deliberately withdrawn to watch the disease recur; the twin program for Verschuer and the Kaiser Wilhelm Institute (selection at the ramp — "twins out!" — chemical injections causing deafness and death, killings for comparative autopsy, ~15% experimental mortality by one estimate, and no reliable method even of confirming twinship); heterochromic eyes excised and shipped to Berlin; methylene blue injected into children's eyes to "Aryanize" their color. Mengele also ran ramp and ward selections, drew a height line on a children's-block wall to select for gassing, and killed by phenol injection.
- **Auschwitz — mass-sterilization experiments:** X-ray and injection methods tested on women and men with a view (discussed, never scaled) to sterilizing millions of "racially inferior" people.
- **Neuengamme — tuberculosis (Kurt Heissmeyer):** 20 Jewish children aged 5–12 infected with virulent TB; on 20 April 1945, to destroy the evidence, the survivors were taken to the Bullenhuser Damm school, injected with morphine, and hanged one by one.

Commerce ran through it: Bayer bought women prisoners from the SS at 700 RM each for drug testing; a 1942 Justice Ministry ruling gave anatomy institutes executed prisoners' bodies without family consent until storage overflowed with "massive deliveries of corpses."

## Historiography

Evans's ethical verdict is explicit: even the minority of experiments that followed contemporary scientific protocol and reached reputable journals were criminal — "it would still have been unethical had the subjects participated in it of their own free will, given the fundamental moral commitment of medicine to preserve life and not to end it." The Nuremberg Doctors' Trial (1946–47) produced the Nuremberg Code; the complicity of elite institutions (Kaiser Wilhelm Institutes, university medicine) took German scholarship decades longer to confront.
