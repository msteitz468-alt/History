# Battle of Mantinea (418 BCE)

Major land battle during the Peloponnesian War. Spartan army under King Agis II defeated a coalition of Argos, Mantinea, Athens, and others. Despite some tactical confusion on the Spartan side, superior hoplite discipline and numbers carried the day. The battle demonstrated the limits of the Peace of Nicias and Spartan willingness to use force to maintain its position in the Peloponnese.
