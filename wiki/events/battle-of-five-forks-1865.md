# Battle of Five Forks (April 1, 1865)

Union turning movement that broke Lee's right flank.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-five-forks-1865]].

Sheridan's cavalry and Warren's infantry routed Pickett's command.

## Causal Analysis

Sheridan's aggressive combined arms attack vs. isolated Confederate position and poor command (Pickett absent).

## Consequence Analysis

Forced Lee's evacuation of Petersburg/Richmond and the final retreat to Appomattox.
