# Battle of Franklin (November 30, 1864)

Devastating Confederate defeat in a frontal assault on Union entrenchments.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-franklin-1864]].

Hood attacked Schofield's prepared lines; the Army of Tennessee was shattered.

## Causal Analysis

Hood's aggressive but poorly planned assault vs. strong Union defenses.

## Consequence Analysis

Led directly to the destruction of the Confederate army at Nashville.
