# Battle of Chickamauga (19–20 September 1863)

**War hub:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/american-civil-war|American Civil War War Hub]] · **Tactical analysis:** [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-chickamauga-1863|Battle of Chickamauga — Tactical Analysis]]

## Narrative

Rosecrans maneuvered Bragg out of Chattanooga but Bragg concentrated and counterattacked along Chickamauga Creek. Two days of heavy fighting; Confederate breakthrough on 20 Sep nearly routed the Union army, saved by Thomas ("Rock of Chickamauga").

## Causal Analysis

Bragg achieved surprise concentration; Union command issues led to gap in line.
