# La Cabaña Executions (1959)

Revolutionary tribunals and executions of Batista-era officials, police, and alleged war criminals held at the La Cabaña fortress in Havana in early 1959, with Ernesto "Che" Guevara as commander. Part of the immediate post-victory consolidation; documented by Anderson as the moment Che served as "Supreme Prosecutor."

## Historiography

Anderson presents La Cabaña as the point where Che functioned as revolutionary enforcer ("Supreme Prosecutor"). Official Cuban accounts frame the tribunals as justice for Batista crimes. Critics highlight summary procedures and the human cost of consolidation. The episode contributed to Che's dual international image (idealist and executioner). Collection now anchors the event via this source.
