# Operation Shingle (Anzio, January–May 1944)

The amphibious landing at Anzio intended to bypass the German Gustav Line defenses in southern Italy.

## Logistics Dimension

One of the most demanding sustainment challenges of the theater: maintaining a large force on a narrow, exposed beachhead under constant artillery and air attack for months. The operation tested the maturity of Allied port and beach logistics, medical evacuation, and the ability to reinforce and supply under fire.
