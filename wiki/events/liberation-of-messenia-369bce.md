# The Liberation of Messenia, 370–369 BCE

## Narrative

In the winter of **370/69 BCE**, in the aftermath of **[[battle-of-leuctra-371bce|
Leuctra]]**, **[[epaminondas]]** led a great Boeotian-and-Peloponnesian army into
**Laconia** — the first hostile force in living memory to ravage the Eurotas valley and
threaten the unwalled town of **Sparta** itself. Then he carried out the act that
[[lewis-cah-v6-1994|CAH Vol. VI]] (Ch. 7, Roy) calls "**worst of all by far for Sparta**":
he moved into **Messenia, liberated it from Spartan control, and founded the city of
Messene** below Mount Ithome — returning to the descendants of the **helots** the land
their ancestors had worked in bondage since the Archaic **Messenian Wars** (see
[[sparta]]). Tellingly, the bitterly partisan **Xenophon omits the whole episode** from
his *Hellenica*; it survives mainly through Diodorus.

## Megalopolis and the Arcadian ring

The liberation was paired with the consolidation of a hostile northern frontier. Out of
the anti-Spartan rising in Arcadia grew the **Arcadian League**, and around **368** its
new **federal capital Megalopolis** ("the Great City") was founded by the synoecism of
many small Arcadian communities — a deliberate strategic counterweight planted on
Sparta's border. Together, free **Messene**, **Megalopolis**, and a democratic **Argos**
and **Elis** boxed Sparta into Laconia.

## Significance

This was the single most consequential act of the [[thebes-hegemony-371bce|Theban
hegemony]] and arguably of the whole fourth century in the Peloponnese. Spartan power had
always rested on the **helot-worked estates of Messenia**, which fed the full-time
warrior **Spartiates**. "Deprived of these resources for the support of Spartiates,
Sparta could not hope to recover her former strength." Where [[battle-of-leuctra-371bce|
Leuctra]] had broken Sparta's army, the liberation of Messenia broke its **economic and
demographic base** — permanently. Sparta would spend the rest of the century in a futile
quest to recover Messenia, refusing every Common Peace that recognized Messenian
independence (down to 362/1), and thereby isolating itself further.

## Consequence Analysis

caused_by: the collapse of Spartan military prestige at [[battle-of-leuctra-371bce|
Leuctra]] and the Theban invasion led by [[epaminondas]]. produced: the **permanent
decline of Sparta** (loss of the helot/Messenian base); the independent state of
**Messene**; **Megalopolis** and the Arcadian League; and a ring of hostile neighbours
that confined Sparta to Laconia for good.
