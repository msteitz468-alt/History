# Second Bull Run (Second Manassas)

Major Confederate victory in northern Virginia that cleared the way for Lee’s invasion of Maryland.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-second-bull-run-1862]].

Pope’s Army of Virginia was outmaneuvered by Lee. Jackson captured supplies and held a defensive position; Longstreet delivered a devastating flank attack on the second day.

## Causal Analysis

Lee’s audacious division of his army and use of interior lines. Pope’s overconfidence and failure to account for Longstreet’s arrival.

## Consequence Analysis

Boosted Confederate morale and allowed the first invasion of the North. Led directly to Antietam.
