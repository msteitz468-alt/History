# Battle of Cold Harbor (May 31–June 12, 1864)

Disastrous Union assaults against entrenched Confederate positions.

## Narrative

See detailed tactical analysis at [[hubs/warfare/long-19th-century/american-civil-war/battle-of-cold-harbor-1864]].

Grant later admitted the June 3 assault was a mistake.

## Causal Analysis

Strong Confederate entrenchments; Union frontal attacks without adequate preparation.

## Consequence Analysis

Led to the decision to cross the James River for Petersburg.
