# Rome — Civilization Portal

> One civilization, twelve centuries, three constitutions. This portal ties together Rome's
> **periods, actors, events, places, and processes** into a single narrative arc — from a Latin
> hill-town to a Mediterranean empire to the twin afterlives of Byzantium and the Latin West.

## The arc

### 1. Regal & early Republic (753–264 BCE)
[[foundation-of-rome-753bce|Foundation (trad. 753 BCE)]] → the [[roman-kings|Roman kings]] → the
[[roman-republic|Republic]]. Survives the [[gallic-sack-of-rome-390bce|Gallic sack (390 BCE)]],
then conquers the peninsula ([[roman-expansion-italy|expansion in Italy]],
[[romanization-of-italy|Romanization of Italy]], [[roman-commonwealth-formation-338bce|the 338 BCE settlement]]).
The **Struggle of the Orders** and the constitution of [[concepts/principate|magistracies]] take shape.

### 2. Mediterranean conquest (264–133 BCE)
The [[first-punic-war-264-241bce|First]], [[second-punic-war-218-201bce|Second]]
([[battle-of-cannae-216bce|Cannae]]; [[scipio-africanus|Scipio Africanus]]), and
[[third-punic-war-149-146bce|Third Punic Wars]] destroy Carthage; the
[[roman-syrian-war-192-188bce|Syrian War]] breaks the Hellenistic kingdoms.
Rome becomes a Mediterranean hegemon — see the controversy on the
[[nature-of-roman-imperialism|nature of Roman imperialism]].

### 3. The Republic's crisis & fall (133–27 BCE)
The [[agrarian-crisis-roman-republic|agrarian crisis]] and the
[[tiberius-gracchus|Gracchi]] ([[gaius-gracchus|Gaius]]) open a century of civil strife:
[[marius|Marius]] vs. [[sulla|Sulla]] ([[sullan-civil-war-83-82bce|civil war]],
[[sullan-proscriptions-82bce|proscriptions]]) → the alliance and rivalry of
[[pompey|Pompey]], [[crassus|Crassus]], and [[julius-caesar|Caesar]]
([[caesar-gallic-war-58-50bce|Gallic War]], [[roman-civil-war-49-45bce|civil war]],
[[julius-caesar-assassination-44bce|the Ides]]) → [[mark-antony|Antony]] vs. Octavian, decided at
[[battle-of-actium-31bce|Actium (31 BCE)]]. → [[fall-of-roman-republic|Why the Republic fell]].

### 4. The Principate & high empire (27 BCE–235 CE)
[[augustus|Augustus]] founds the [[concepts/principate|Principate]] — monarchy in republican dress.
[[tiberius|Tiberius]], [[nero|Nero]], then the "Five Good Emperors" ([[trajan|Trajan]]'s greatest
extent, [[hadrian|Hadrian]]'s consolidation). [[battle-of-teutoburg-forest-9ce|Teutoburg (9 CE)]]
fixes the Rhine limit. Empire-wide systems: [[roman-imperial-trade|imperial trade]],
[[codification-of-roman-law|Roman law]], [[roman-citizenship-and-belonging|citizenship]] (universalised 212 CE),
and the provinces — [[roman-gaul|Gaul]], [[hispania-roman|Hispania]], [[roman-africa|Africa]],
[[roman-britain|Britain]], [[roman-egypt|Egypt]], [[roman-judaea|Judaea]], the [[roman-greek-east|Greek East]].

### 5. Crisis, recovery & Christianization (235–395 CE)
The third-century crisis; recovery under [[aurelian|Aurelian]] and the systematic refounding by
[[diocletian|Diocletian]] (the Tetrarchy, [[late-roman-administration|late administration]],
[[late-roman-army|late army]]). [[constantine-the-great|Constantine]]
([[battle-of-the-milvian-bridge-312|Milvian Bridge]]) and the
[[christianization-of-the-roman-empire|Christianization of the empire]] reorient the state.

### 6. Division & the two afterlives (395–600 CE)
[[battle-of-adrianople-378|Adrianople]] → [[sack-of-rome-410|410]] →
[[fall-of-the-western-roman-empire-476|fall of the West (476)]] and the
[[post-roman-transformation-of-the-west|post-Roman transformation]]. The East endures as the
[[eastern-roman-empire-425-518|Eastern Roman]] / Byzantine state; [[justinian|Justinian]]'s
reconquest and [[codification-of-roman-law|legal codification]] are Rome's longest echo.
