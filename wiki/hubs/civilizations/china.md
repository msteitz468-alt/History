# China — Civilization Portal

> The world's longest continuous political-cultural tradition. This portal threads China's
> **dynasties, thinkers, and transformations** into one arc — from Neolithic villages and the Shang
> oracle bones to the Communist revolution — organized around the recurring rhythm of unification,
> fragmentation, and reunification under the "dynastic cycle." The exhaustive auto-list is the
> **Bases view** below.

## The arc

### Formation — Bronze Age (to 256 BCE)
[[early-agriculture-china|Early agriculture]] and [[anyang|Anyang]] → the [[shang-dynasty|Shang]]
(oracle bones, the first Chinese writing) → the [[zhou-dynasty|Zhou]] and the Mandate of Heaven.

### The classical breakthrough — Warring States & the Hundred Schools
The [[warring-states-period-475-bce|Warring States]] period: political fragmentation but
intellectual explosion. [[confucius|Confucius]] ([[mencius|Mencius]]), the Legalist [[han-feizi|Han Feizi]],
and the Daoists [[zhuangzi|Zhuangzi]] and [[liu-an|Liu An]] — the philosophical foundation of all later China.

### First empire — Qin & Han
[[qin-shi-huang|Qin Shi Huang]] and the [[qin-dynasty|Qin]] unify the realm (221 BCE);
the [[han-dynasty|Han]] consolidate it — [[han-wudi|Emperor Wu]]'s expansion, [[sima-qian|Sima Qian]]'s
history, the Confucian state — interrupted by [[wang-mang|Wang Mang]]'s usurpation.

### Reunification & golden ages — Sui, Tang, Song
The [[sui-dynasty|Sui]] reunite after centuries of division; the [[tang-dynasty|Tang]] reach a
cosmopolitan zenith — broken by the [[an-lushan-rebellion|An Lushan Rebellion]] — and the
[[song-dynasty|Song]] preside over a commercial and technological revolution.

### Conquest dynasties & late empire — Yuan, Ming, Qing
The Mongol [[yuan-dynasty|Yuan]] ([[kublai-khan|Kublai Khan]]); the [[ming-dynasty|Ming]] and the
[[zheng-he-voyages|Zheng He voyages]]; the Manchu [[qing-dynasty|Qing]] at its height, then strained by
the [[late-victorian-famines-1876|famines]] and rebellion.

### The long crisis & revolution (1839–1976)
The [[opium-wars|Opium Wars]] → the [[taiping-rebellion|Taiping]] and [[boxer-rebellion|Boxer]]
rebellions → the [[self-strengthening-movement|self-strengthening movement]] → the
[[fall-of-the-qing-1911|fall of the Qing (1911)]] → the [[republic-of-china-1912|Republic]],
[[warlord-era|warlord era]], and [[kuomintang|Kuomintang]] → the [[chinese-communist-revolution|Communist revolution]]
and [[maoist-china|Mao's China]] ([[mao-zedong|Mao Zedong]], the [[great-leap-forward|Great Leap Forward]],
the [[cultural-revolution|Cultural Revolution]], the [[sino-soviet-split|Sino-Soviet split]]).
