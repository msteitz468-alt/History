## The arc

### Bronze Age & the Dark Age (to 800 BCE)
The [[coming-of-the-greeks-1900bce|coming of the Greeks]] and [[mycenae|Mycenaean]] palace society
([[aegean-scripts|Linear B]]) → collapse → the [[greek-dark-age|Greek Dark Age]] and the
[[ionian-migration-1050bce|Ionian migration]].

### Archaic renaissance (800–500 BCE)
The rise of the polis: [[hellenic-identity|Hellenic identity]], [[panhellenic-games|panhellenic games]],
[[delphi|Delphi]], [[greek-religion-and-mythology|religion and myth]]; [[greek-tyranny|tyranny]]
(and [[western-greek-tyranny|in the west]]); [[archaic-greek-economy|economy]] and
[[archaic-greek-material-culture|material culture]]; the rival models of [[athens|Athens]] and [[sparta|Sparta]]
(the [[spartan-agoge|agoge]]).

### The Classical zenith (500–323 BCE)
The [[greco-persian-wars-499bce|Greco-Persian Wars]] ([[ionian-revolt-499bce|Ionian Revolt]],
[[battle-of-marathon-490bce|Marathon]], [[battle-of-salamis-480bce|Salamis]],
[[battle-of-plataea-479bce|Plataea]]) and the [[freedom-of-the-greeks|"freedom of the Greeks"]] →
[[athenian-democracy|Athenian democracy]], the [[delian-league|Delian League]], and
[[athenian-drama|drama]] → the [[peloponnesian-war|Peloponnesian War]] ([[fall-of-athens-404bce|404 BCE]]) →
the contested fourth century ([[corinthian-war-395bce|Corinthian War]],
[[liberation-of-thebes-379bce|Thebes]], [[battle-of-leuctra-371bce|Leuctra]]).
Thinkers: [[plato|Plato]], [[aristotle|Aristotle]]; historians [[herodotus|Herodotus]] and [[thucydides|Thucydides]].

### Macedon & the Hellenistic world (359–31 BCE)
[[philip-ii-of-macedon|Philip II]] and the [[league-of-corinth|League of Corinth]] → [[alexander-the-great|Alexander]]
→ the [[hellenistic-world|Hellenistic kingdoms]], [[hellenistic-monarchy|monarchy]], the
[[hellenistic-polis|Hellenistic polis]], [[koine-greek|koine Greek]], and [[hellenistic-science|science]] —
absorbed by Rome through the [[first-macedonian-war-214-205bce|Macedonian Wars]] and the [[roman-greek-east|Greek East]].

## Cross-sections

Read the Greek world thematically rather than chronologically:

- **The polis & politics:** the citizen city-state was Greece's defining invention — from
  [[greek-tyranny|tyranny]] through [[athenian-democracy|Athenian democracy]] and Spartan oligarchy
  ([[spartan-agoge|the agoge]]) to the [[greek-federalism|federal leagues]] and the
  [[hellenistic-polis|Hellenistic polis]] under monarchy.
- **Philosophy & science:** the [[presocratics|Presocratics]] → the [[sophists|Sophists]] →
  [[plato|Plato]] and [[aristotle|Aristotle]] → the schools of the [[hellenistic-science|Hellenistic age]],
  Greece's deepest legacy to later thought.
- **Drama, art & the games:** [[athenian-drama|Athenian drama]] · [[classical-greek-art|classical art]] ·
  the [[panhellenic-games|panhellenic games]] that bound the Greeks across their political divisions.
- **Religion & identity:** [[greek-religion-and-mythology|myth and religion]], [[polis-religion|civic cult]],
  the oracle at [[delphi|Delphi]], and the [[hellenic-identity|sense of Hellenic identity]] forged against Persia.
- **Language & economy:** the unifying [[koine-greek|koine]] of the Hellenistic world; the
  [[archaic-greek-economy|economy]] of colonization, coinage, and trade; and war as a way of life
  ([[hoplite-warfare|the hoplite]], [[greek-warfare|Greek warfare]] — see [[hubs/themes/warfare|Warfare]]).
