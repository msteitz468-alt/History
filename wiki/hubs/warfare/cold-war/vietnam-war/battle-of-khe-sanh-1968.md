# Siege of Khe Sanh — Tactical Analysis

> **Narrative & full sources:** [[events/battle-of-khe-sanh-1968]] · **War hub:** [[hubs/warfare/cold-war/vietnam-war/vietnam-war]]

High-resolution analysis of the 1968 siege. Grounded in Wawro Ch. 15 (Range 04) + cross-sources. (See source page for full verbatim extraction.)

## 1. Strategic Context and Objectives

**US (Westmoreland / Lownds / 26th Marines):** Hold the combat base (Western Anchor); lure NVA into kill zone for unprecedented air/artillery; prevent thrust into I Corps/populated areas; avoid Dien Bien Phu repeat. "The side which can keep its artillery intact will win the battle."

**NVA (Giap / 304th, 325C + supporting):** Isolate and overrun base or force humiliating withdrawal; fix US reserves away from cities for Tet; achieve battlefield spectacular and psychological win.

## 2. Terrain, Weather, and Intelligence

Rugged plateau near DMZ; Route 9 LOC; hills 881/861/558/950 for observation/FSBs. Monsoon/fog limited air ~40% even in dry season. NVA used trenches/saps, AA, long-range arty from Laos. Lang Vei overrun Feb 7 by tanks (first communist armor in South).

## 3. Order of Battle and Deployment

**US/ARVN:** ~6,000 Marines (26th Regt reinforced) + 1,000 ARVN Rangers at base (designed for ~1,000); hill positions (881S, 861 etc.); 46 guns (5 bty) + 4x 175mm Army; massive air (USAF/USMC + B-52s). Daily supply ballooned 50→185 tons.
**NVA:** ~20,000–40,000 (304th/325C Divisions + regts); heavy arty/rockets/mortars/AA; siege trenches.

## 4. Course of the Battle — Phases

1. **Buildup / opening (Jan 21+):** NVA encircled; heavy barrage (ammo dump cook-off 11k rounds); hills contested. Marines held key high ground.
2. **Main siege / air campaign (NIAGARA):** B-52s 2,602 sorties / 75k+ tons (twice all other I Corps/DMZ); tac air 184 sorties/day avg; sensors (Muscle Shoals acoustic/seismic); "super gaggle" resupply under fire; C-130s as "mortar magnets." Arty "Be Generous" (159k outgoing vs 11k incoming).
3. **Lang Vei overrun + Tet pressure (Feb 7):** 2 NVA bns + 12 tanks overran Green Beret camp; Lownds refused relief (ambush fear); Westmoreland ordered helos. Khe Sanh fixation diverted US attention.
4. **Climax assaults + relief (Feb 29–Apr):** NVA Feb 29 night attacks repulsed by massed fires + diverted B-52s. Operation PEGASUS (1st Cav) relieved April. Base later abandoned.

## 5. Outcome and Casualties

**US:** 205 KIA Marines + ~1,670 WIA during siege.
**NVA:** Bodies 1,602 (perimeter) or MACV internal est ~5,500 (3,500 air/arty + 2,000 ground); higher claims to 15k.
**Result:** US held; massive firepower broke assaults; base abandoned post-relief as no longer critical. NVA failed to achieve Dien Bien Phu.

## 6. Critical Decisions

- Lownds: stand-fast on hills and base; "Be Generous" arty.
- Westmoreland: Niagara priority + reinforcement to 5 bns; later abandonment.
- NVA: committed large force without ability to close or sustain under fires.

## 7–8. Principles of War + Modern Parallels

Mass/Economy via overwhelming fires (honoured by US); Maneuver limited by weather/AA for both; Objective achieved tactically but base abandoned; Security challenged by isolation/fog. Parallels: modern contested logistics, sensor-fused fires, air dominance in siege defense.
