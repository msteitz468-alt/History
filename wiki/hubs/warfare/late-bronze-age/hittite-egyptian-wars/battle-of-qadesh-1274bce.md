# Battle of Qadesh – Tactical Analysis

**Narrative overview**: [[events/battle-of-qadesh-1274bce|Full event page]]

This page provides a structured, professional-level (West Point / military academy style) analysis.

## Strategic Context and Objectives

**Egyptian (Ramesses II)**:
- Recover lost territories in Syria/Amurru and restore Egyptian prestige after Hittite gains.
- March north in four divisions to seize Qadesh.

**Hittites (Muwatalli II)**:
- Defend southern holdings and crush the Egyptian invasion.
- Ambush the strung-out Egyptian army.

## Terrain and Environment

- City of Qadesh on the Orontes.
- Hittites concealed large force behind the city and used the river for surprise crossing.

## Order of Battle and Deployment

**Egyptian**:
- Divisions marched in column, widely separated.
- Ramesses with Amun division reached Qadesh first and camped.

**Hittites**:
- Chariotry hidden, forded the river to hit the Re division in flank, then the Amun camp.

## Course of the Battle – Phases

1. **Hittite Chariot Attack**:
   - Smashed Re division and broke into Egyptian camp.

2. **Egyptian Rally**:
   - Ramesses counterattacked with guard.
   - Arrival of Ne'arin task force and Ptah division turned the tide.

3. **Hittite Withdrawal**:
   - Muwatalli held infantry back; fighting ended at night.

## Outcome and Immediate Consequences

- Tactical draw.
- Hittites retained Qadesh; Egypt lost prestige, leading to later treaty.

## Analysis

**Hittite Success**:
- Excellent deception and ambush.
- Use of chariots for shock.

**Egyptian Failures**:
- Poor intelligence and over-extended march.
- Divisions not mutually supporting.

## Lessons Learned

- **Intelligence**: Critical failure by Egyptians.
- **Combined Arms / Reserves**: Timely arrival of reinforcements saved the day.
- **Caution in Advance**: Separated columns vulnerable.
