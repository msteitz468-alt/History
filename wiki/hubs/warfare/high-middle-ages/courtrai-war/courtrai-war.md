# Courtrai War (Franco-Flemish, 1302+)

War hub for the conflict culminating at the Battle of Courtrai (Golden Spurs) and continuing through subsequent actions.

**Key battle analyses:**
- [[hubs/warfare/high-middle-ages/courtrai-war/battle-of-courtrai-1302|Battle of Courtrai — Tactical Analysis]]

**Narrative:** See [[events/battle-of-courtrai-1302]].

Post-Courtrai actions (per DeVries): Arques (1303) showed Flemish circular defensive formation (goedendags/pikes; "bowl or crown") vs. French flanking preference after Courtrai trauma; ambiguous result with heavy Flemish losses in loose-order contingent. Mons-en-Pévèle (1304) saw Courtrai-style line + wagon fort (wheels removed); initial success and near-killing of Philip IV, but heat, partial withdrawal, and French counter (or disputed flight per Annales) led to rout and effective end of the independence movement (treaties of Marquette 1304, Athis-sur-Orge 1305). Illustrates limits: success when holding defensive stand; vulnerability when order broke or force divided. (DeVries range 02)

Material primarily from Verbruggen and DeVries (infantry tactics continuity/evolution/limits).
