# Biographies Hub — Detailed Lives

> The wiki maintains two deliberately high-resolution analytic zones: the Warfare Hub (tactical/operational) and this Biography Hub (life conduct, decisions, and analytic assessment of historically significant individuals). All other `actors/` pages remain at normal summary resolution.

**Summary pages live at `wiki/actors/[person-slug].md`.**  
**Detailed analytic biographies live here (`hubs/biographies/[period]/[person-slug].md`).**

See the locked standard: [[hubs/biographies/templates/biography-analysis-template|Biography Analysis Template]].

## Selection Criteria

Detailed biographies are created only for:
- Individuals whose actions produced civilizational, epochal, or trans-regional consequences, **or**
- Subjects of one or more high-quality dedicated biographical sources already in the collection that supply rich primary material (letters, memoirs, contemporary witnesses, the subject's own writings) justifying the investment in depth.

They are **not** created for every actor. Ordinary or composite figures, or cases with thin source bases, stay at summary level.

## How These Pages Are Built

- Main-thread scaffold decides significance + pre-establishes names (per CLAUDE.md Biography Hub + Ingest Step 1).
- For dedicated biographical monographs: Section Plan in the source page explicitly flags ranges/chapters for the detailed bio.
- Subagents extract grounded claims (decisions, quotes, bias notes, counterfactual implications) from their exclusive line ranges.
- Main reconciliation writes the full detailed page using the template, adds reciprocal links, and updates the summary actor page.
- Always: 0 broken links; template compliance; source attribution in tables and quotes.
