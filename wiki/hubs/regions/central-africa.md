## Key threads
- The [[kingdom-of-kongo|Kingdom of Kongo]] — the Atlantic-facing state most engaged with Portugal
  and the slave trade.
- The [[luba-lunda|Luba and Lunda]] states of the interior savanna.
- The [[congo-free-state|Congo Free State]] — Leopold II's colony and one of the most violent
  episodes of [[colonial-rule-in-africa|colonial rule]].

## Collection note
Pre-colonial Central Africa is sparsely covered; the [[bantu-expansion|Bantu expansion]] supplies
the deep background. See [[hubs/regions/sub-saharan-africa|Sub-Saharan Africa]] for the continental frame.
