## The long imperial spine
- **Neolithic origins:** [[early-agriculture-southwest-asia|first farming]] · [[catalhoyuk|Çatalhöyük]] ·
  [[jericho|Jericho]] · [[agriculture-and-urbanism|agriculture & urbanism]].
- **First states:** Sumer and Akkad → see [[hubs/regions/mesopotamia|Mesopotamia]].
- **Imperial age:** the [[neo-assyrian-empire|Neo-Assyrian]] and [[neo-babylonian-empire|Neo-Babylonian]]
  empires; the [[bronze-age-collapse-1200bce|Bronze Age Collapse]] (snapshot: [[synchronic-1200bce|c. 1200 BCE]]).
- **Persia & Hellenism:** the [[hittites|Hittites]] of [[hubs/regions/anatolia|Anatolia]]; Achaemenid and

  ([[zenobia|Zenobia]]); the long Roman–Persian wars.
- **Islam:** [[rashidun-caliphate|Rashidun]] → [[umayyad-caliphate|Umayyad]] → Abbasid [[baghdad|Baghdad]];
  the [[seljuk-empire|Seljuks]], [[crusader-states|Crusader states]], [[saladin|Saladin]], the [[mamluk-sultanate|Mamluks]].
- **Ottoman & modern:** the [[ottoman-empire|Ottoman Empire]] → [[tanzimat-reforms|Tanzimat]] →
  [[fall-of-the-ottoman-empire-1922|its fall]] → the [[colonial-partition-of-the-arab-world|partition of the Arab world]],
