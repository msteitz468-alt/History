## The Sahelian empires
[[trans-saharan-trade|Trans-Saharan trade]] underwrote a succession of gold-and-salt empires:
[[ghana-empire|Ghana]] → [[takrur|Takrur]] → [[mali-empire|Mali]] ([[sundiata-keita|Sundiata Keita]],
[[mansa-musa|Mansa Musa]]) → [[songhay-empire|Songhay]] ([[ahmad-baba|Ahmad Baba]], ended by the
[[moroccan-invasion-of-songhay-1591|Moroccan invasion of 1591]] and the [[western-sudan-arma-pashalik|Arma pashalik]]) ·
[[kanem-bornu|Kanem-Bornu]] · the [[hausa-city-states|Hausa city-states]] · the [[mossi-states|Mossi states]].
Early urbanism: [[jenne-jeno|Jenne-jeno]]; deep roots in the [[nok-culture|Nok culture]].
