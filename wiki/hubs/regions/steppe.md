- **The Mongols:** [[genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]] and the [[mongol-empire|Mongol Empire]] —
  the [[mongol-army|Mongol army]], the [[mongol-conquests-13th-century|conquests]],
  [[kublai-khan|Kublai Khan]], and the [[golden-horde|Golden Horde]].
  *Secret History* (de Rachewiltz, ll. 1232–4000) grounds origins: wolf/doe crossing Tenggis to Burqan Qaldun/Onan source (ll. 1239-1247); Alan Qo'a, Bodončar raids and clan formations on Tünggelik/Onan (ll. 1323-1781); Yisügei-Hö’elün abduction and Qutula on Onan/Qorqonaq (ll. 1989-2147); Temüjin's birth at Deli’ün Boldaq, foraging, Tergüne Heights, Onan stream escape, Senggür/Kelüren camps, Burqan Qaldun flight (ll. 2153-3702). These establish the Onan-Kherlen-Burqan heartland and tribal dynamics of the steppe polity.
- The underlying way of life: [[pastoral-nomadism|pastoral nomadism]].

**Verification anchors from *Secret History* (de Rachewiltz indexes ll. 17896–22035)**: Burqan Qaldun "sacred mountain, original home of the Mongols" (ll. ~18184+); "Onan River... see also Mother Onan" (ll. ~18660); numerous steppe place/people entries (Kelüren, Qara Qorum, etc.) with § refs provide high-utility citation grounding for steppe hub. Name/subject indexes confirm core geography and institutions of the Mongol steppe polity. Updated.

**Additional from Secret History (de Rachewiltz, ll. 7001–11000)**: Naiman campaign on "southern slopes of the Altai" (ll. 10489); "Ürünggü River", "Lake Kišil Baš", "Tamir River", "Orqon", "Naqu Cliff", "Qaradal Source", "Erdis River" (various ll. 7289-10700+) illustrate active geography of unification wars. "Sa’ari Steppe" camp (ll. 7387 etc.); Onggirat submission east of Tüngge Stream (ll. 8458-8452). `part_of` steppe political arena; `preceded_by` tribal rivalries; `produced` Mongol dominance across core steppe territories (Naiman, Merkit remnants). Loyalty networks (Badai/Kišiliq warning ll. 7917-7935) and unit mobility (Uru’ut/Mangqut ll. 8026+) `contributed_to` control of steppe hub. Grounded in range; prior preserved.
