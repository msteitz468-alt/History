# Empire & Collapse — Thematic Hub

> Empires are history's largest political machines, and their failures are among its most studied
> events. This hub gathers the great imperial systems alongside the collapses, sacks, and falls that
> ended them — and asks the recurring question of whether decline is endogenous (overreach, fiscal
> exhaustion, elite fragmentation) or exogenous (invasion, climate, disease). The exhaustive
> auto-list is the **Bases view** below.
