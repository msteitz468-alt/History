# Religion & Belief — Thematic Hub

> Religion is where cosmology, political legitimacy, law, art, and identity meet. This hub tracks
> the **rise and spread of the major traditions**, the recurring dynamics — conversion, reform,
> syncretism, persecution, the entanglement of church and state — and the historiographical problem
> of writing about belief from the outside. The exhaustive auto-list is the **Bases view** below;
> the curated threads are the spine.
